How Apprentice Wage Estimates Work

For 151 Inside Wireman locals without a verified current starting wage, we show a broad regional model estimate. It is not a wage quoted by a training center. Each local page explains why its pay remains unverified, including outdated sources, uncertain program matches, unavailable sites, and unconfirmed centers.

What the Range Means

We trained on 92 locals with one verified Inside Wireman wage schedule each. The model adjusts those rates using 2024 regional cost-of-living data and, where available, verified wages from the same state. It weights metro areas across each local's Inside jurisdiction, using a state price index where county mapping is unavailable. The displayed range extends about $6/hr on either side of the prediction, rounded outward to whole dollars.

In a harder test that withheld each state in turn, 90% of verified-local errors were within $5.32/hr. This is a historical test on locals that publish wages, not a 90% guarantee for a local that does not. Actual pay can fall outside the displayed range.

Some estimated locals had no verified training wage from their state. For those, the model relies on regional price data and verified wages elsewhere; the local page says so.

What It Leaves Out

Cost of living cannot reveal a local's negotiated agreement, wage zone, starting period, or future raise. The published wages used for training have different effective dates. Two locals with multiple wage zones were left out of model training rather than averaged together; their published rates are displayed as verified ranges. Benefits and journeyman rates are excluded. Where the official source is stale, ambiguous, or unavailable, the model range has no direct program wage evidence. A verified current rate always takes priority over a model estimate.

Sources

The wage inputs are individually linked official JATC or local program schedules. Geography uses U.S. BEA regional price parities and U.S. Census metro definitions. Model checked 2026-10-16. Check the linked training center for its actual current pay before relying on an estimate.
