Local Union 113, of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, has been proudly representing the skilled electrical workers of Colorado Springs and the Pikes Peak region for over 125 years.

## ORGANIZE YOUR  WORK PLACE

IBEW, Local Union 113 is a labor union committed to advancing the ideals of the Labor Movement. Our members uphold the belief that every working individual deserves equal wages and benefits. Without collective action, both wages and working conditions are likely to persist unchanged. Federal laws and boards safeguard your right to establish a union in your workplace.

ABOUT US

## NEED HELP? LOOK NO FURTHER THAN IBEW

Together with the Southern Colorado Chapter of NECA, IBEW Local 113 delivers the region's highest-trained, most productive electrical workforce — committed to quality, value, and safety on every job.

### Partnership for the Future

IBEW Local Union #113 and the Southern Colorado Chapter of the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) are dedicated to creating an environment in which our partnership can flourish — built on a shared vision, shared risk taking, common objectives, mutual respect, trust, and a team approach. Partnering helps us create avenues to accommodate the needs of our customers and community.

### Our Vision

IBEW Local Union #113 and the Southern Colorado Chapter of NECA shall lead the electrical industry by excelling in all future opportunities. This cooperative effort is committed to customer satisfaction and safety.

### Our Mission

To be recognized as the leader in the electrical industry. Through partnering, we provide the best quality product and value to the customer, utilizing the highest trained and most productive workforce.

Commercial and industrial electrical work

- Health & welfare
- Pension
- Annuity

### Journeyman Lineman

Outside line construction and maintenance

- Full journeyman rate
- Health & welfare
- Pension
- Annuity

Residential electrical construction

- Competitive rate
- Health & welfare
- Pension
- Training

### VDV Technician

Voice, data, and video installation

- Competitive rate
- Health & welfare
- Pension
- Training

### Apprentice

Percentage of journeyman rate based on year

- Progressive increases
- Health & welfare
- Training provided

# APPRENTICESHIP & TRAINING

Earn while you learn. IBEW Local 113 represents two apprenticeship paths — Inside (Wireman) and Outside (Lineman). Choose your path below to learn how to get started.

## CHOOSE YOUR PATH

There are two apprenticeship programs. Pick the one that fits the career you want. Each is run by its own training center — that's who you apply to.

[Inside\\
**Apprentice Wireman** \\
Commercial, industrial and residential electrical work — the electricians who wire buildings.\\
Learn how to apply](https://ibew113.com/apprenticeship#inside) [Outside\\
**Apprentice Lineman** \\
Overhead and underground power lines — the linemen who build and maintain the electrical grid.\\
Learn how to apply](https://ibew113.com/apprenticeship#outside)

Inside — Wireman

## Apprentice Wireman

Inside Wiremen are the electricians who install and maintain the wiring, controls and electrical systems in commercial, industrial and residential buildings. The Inside apprenticeship is a paid, earn-while-you-learn program combining on-the-job training with classroom instruction, run by the Colorado Springs Electrical JATC.

![Colorado Springs Electrical JATC](https://ibew113.com/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fapprenticeship%2Fcsejatc-logo.webp&w=640&q=75)

### Classifications

Trades you can train for through this apprenticeship.

- Inside Wireman



Install and maintain the wiring, controls and electrical systems in commercial, industrial and residential buildings.

- VDV / Telecommunications Installer Technician



Install and service low-voltage voice, data and video systems — structured cabling, networks and fiber.


### How to Apply

Applications are handled by the training center below — not the union hall. Contact them directly to get started.

Colorado Springs Electrical JATC

414 West Pikes Peak Ave, Colorado Springs, CO 80905

[(719) 473-1781](tel:7194731781) [John@CSEJATC.org](mailto:John@CSEJATC.org) [csejatc.org](https://www.csejatc.org/)

Monday – Friday, 8:00 AM – 5:00 PM (closed 12:00 – 1:00 PM)

The JATC is a partnership between IBEW Local 113 and NECA. Contact John DeLuke, Training Director, to apply directly through the JATC office or website during open enrollment.

[Visit csejatc.org](https://www.csejatc.org/)

Outside — Lineman

## Apprentice Lineman

Outside Linemen build and maintain the overhead and underground power lines that make up the electrical grid. The Outside apprenticeship is run by MSLCAT — the Mountain States Line Constructors Apprenticeship & Training program — serving a multi-state region. The closest training center to Colorado Springs is in the Denver area (Brighton, CO).

![MSLCAT — Mountain States Line Constructors Apprenticeship & Training](https://ibew113.com/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fapprenticeship%2Fmslcat-logo.avif&w=640&q=75)

### Classifications

Trades you can train for through this apprenticeship.

- Journeyman Lineman



Build and maintain overhead and underground power lines — the backbone of the electrical grid.

- Line Equipment Operator



Operate the cranes, diggers and heavy equipment used in line construction.

- Substation Technician



Construct, wire and maintain the substations that step power up and down across the grid.

- Traffic Signal Technician



Install and service traffic signals, street lighting and intelligent transportation systems.


### How to Apply

Applications are handled by the training center below — not the union hall. Contact them directly to get started.

MSLCAT — Mountain States Line Constructors Apprenticeship & Training

[(801) 562-2929](tel:8015622929) [office@mslcat.org](mailto:office@mslcat.org) [mslcat.org](https://www.mslcat.org/)

Monday – Friday, 7:30 AM – 4:00 PM Mountain Time

MSLCAT is Utah-based and serves a multi-state area within the IBEW 8th District. Apply directly through MSLCAT — the closest facility to Colorado Springs is the Denver-area (Brighton) training center.

[Visit mslcat.org](https://www.mslcat.org/)

## HELMETS TO HARD HATS

Helmets to Hard Hats is a national program that connects transitioning active-duty military, veterans, National Guard and Reserve members with career training in the building and construction trades — including the IBEW/NECA electrical apprenticeship. It's free to join, and the skills and discipline you built in the service translate directly to a career in the trade.

Veterans interested in an electrical career are encouraged to explore Helmets to Hard Hats, then apply through the JATC (Inside — Wireman) or MSLCAT (Outside — Lineman) as described above.

[Visit helmetstohardhats.org](https://www.helmetstohardhats.org/)

## BROTHERHOOD FUND

A voluntary, members-only fund that provides a beneficiary with immediate cash in a member's time of need. Open to all brother and sister members in good standing of IBEW Local 113.

### A Little History

In 1961, a group of Local 113 members began working toward a way to protect their brothers' and sisters' beneficiaries in their time of need — something that would provide immediate cash within roughly 48 hours. In late October 1962 a plan was approved and the Brotherhood Fund was born as a voluntary fund.

As the needs of the membership grew, the by-laws were amended over the years by member vote to keep the fund sustainable and cover administrative costs. Beyond inexpensive insurance, helping one another in times of need is what the Brotherhood Fund is truly about.

Contribution and join amounts are set by member vote and are listed on the current application. Contact the hall to confirm today's figures.

Members Only

Open to members in good standing of Local 113.

Immediate Support

Provides beneficiaries with cash quickly in a time of need.

# ORGANIZE YOUR WORKPLACE

Are you a non-union electrical worker who wants better wages, benefits, and working conditions? Bring IBEW Local 113 to your shop. We'll help you and your coworkers organize — confidentially and at no cost to you.

## WHY JOIN THE IBEW?

### Better Wages

Union electricians earn significantly more than their non-union counterparts.

### Comprehensive Benefits

Health insurance, dental, vision, and retirement plans that protect you and your family.

### Ongoing Training

Access to world-class training programs and continuing education at no cost.

### Job Security

Collective bargaining agreements protect your employment rights.

### Brotherhood

Join a community of skilled professionals who support each other.

### Fair Treatment

Grievance procedures ensure workplace disputes are resolved fairly.

## HOW ORGANIZING WORKS

### Initial Contact

Reach out to our organizing team. All conversations are confidential.

### Build Support

Connect with coworkers who share your interest in better conditions.

### Authorization Cards

Employees sign cards indicating their desire for union representation.

### NLRB Election

The National Labor Relations Board conducts a secret ballot election.

### Negotiate Contract

After winning, we negotiate a collective bargaining agreement.
