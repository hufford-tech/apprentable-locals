**Welcome to the career opportunity that could change your life!**



**PLEASE DO NOT APPLY UNLESS YOU ARE SURE WHAT YOU ARE APPLYING FOR!**



\\*\\* It's recommended that you read the " [Applying for our Programs](http://www.electricaltc.org/index.cfm?zone=/unionactive/view_page.cfm&page=Application20Information)" link before starting the online process.







\\*\\* Wage Schedules [Click Here](http://www.electricaltc.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_page.cfm&page=Wage20Schedules)







\*\*ONLINE APPLICANTS\*\*

**Step 1.** You will need to create a new account.

**Step 2.** You will receive an email with a link to finish creating your account.

**Step 3.** You will receive an additional email once your account has been approved.



  - Follow the link to log in, then click on the "Application" tab.
  - You will need to select the program in which you are applying for: _**INSIDE or TELECOMMUNICATIONS**_

**Step 4.** Follow instructions to start the application and to pay a $25 application fee.

**Step 5**. You will need to have your High School or College transcripts and your VALID Drivers license or VALID State ID in a separate digital file available to upload during the application process. Acceptable formats can be PDF or a clear quality .JPG

**Step 6.** You will need to complete all parts of the application process (not all at one time) and you will be made aware when you have completed it.

Please create a new account by **[CLICKING HERE](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/electricaltc-org/login/accountCreate.php)**.

New applications are no longer being accepted for the Inside Electricians Apprenticeship Program or  the Teledata Apprenticeship for the School year of 2026/2027
