# Journeyman Resources

Under the Journeyman tab you can find information about CEU classes that are offered. We offer CEU classes in 3 different methods: traditional classroom, paper correspondance and online correspondence. The in person classes can be scheduled through the tradeschool link on this page and are hosted at our facility in Pocatello. Paper correspondence materials can be picked up from our facility and returned when completed.
