TradeSchool

## Log Hours with TradeSchool.

TradeSchool is the JATC's official platform for tracking your on-the-job training (OJT) hours. Your login credentials are provided by the JATC office upon enrollment.

- Log OJT hours after each shift or work week
- View running totals toward 8,000 hours (Inside Commercial Wireman) or 6,000 hours (Telecom)
- Record details on work performed and skills practiced
- Access from any device — desktop or mobile app
- Monitor your advancement standing through each 6-month period

**Stay consistent.** Hours sync directly with the JATC's reporting system. Falling behind on OJT entry can delay advancement to the next period.

Tools & Books

## Pay for required tools and books.

Required textbooks, toolkits, and supplies for your apprenticeship are available through the JATC training center. Payments are processed securely through our portal.

- Required tool list with approved brands and specs provided at orientation
- Textbooks issued each semester through the JATC training center
- Secure payment by check, money order, or ACH bank transfer

Questions about required materials? Call the JATC office at [**(217) 544-3479 Ext. 229**](tel:+12175443479,229).

ProTech

## Pay your IBEW dues online.

IBEW Local 193 union dues can be paid conveniently online through the ProTech member portal. Keeping dues current is required to maintain good standing as an apprentice.

- Log in to ProTech with your member credentials
- Make payments and manage your dues online
- Secure online payment through the ProTech member portal
- View your full payment history and dues receipts online

**Protect your place.** Maintaining good standing preserves your position in the apprenticeship and all IBEW membership benefits. Questions about your account? Call the IBEW Local 193 hall at [**(217) 544-3479 Ext. 229**](tel:+12175443479,229).

[Log In to ProTech →](https://lms.protechskillsinstitute.org/login/index.php)
