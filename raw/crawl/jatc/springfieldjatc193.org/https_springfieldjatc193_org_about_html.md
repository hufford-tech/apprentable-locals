Our Mission

## Training the best workforce in the industry.

The mission of the NECA-IBEW 193 JATC is to provide the electrical industry with the most highly trained and skilled workforce possible.

We produce elite trade professionals by delivering comprehensive apprenticeship programs that cover every aspect of work in the electrical sector. Our hands-on and online curriculum combines rigorously structured on-the-job learning with advanced theoretical classroom education.

By partnering apprentices with the best contractors and IBEW journeymen, we provide unparalleled real-world experience under proper supervision and guidance. Our instructors are seasoned professionals who stay current with the latest techniques, safety protocols, and National Electrical Code standards.

Who We Are

## 120+ years of electrical training excellence.

IBEW Local 193 was founded in 1901 to protect the rights of electrical workers, and our JATC was established to train the next generation. For over 120 years, we have been driving excellence in electrical education across Central Illinois.

Today our state-of-the-art Springfield Training Center offers two elite programs — the **4-Year Inside Commercial Wireman** apprenticeship and the **3-Year Telecommunications Technician** apprenticeship. Both combine paid on-the-job hours with classroom instruction tailored to the specialty, and graduates enter the electrical field as highly skilled IBEW journeymen.

Our instructors are seasoned professionals who train using the latest techniques and National Electrical Code standards. Our curriculum is continually updated to cover emerging technologies and innovations.
