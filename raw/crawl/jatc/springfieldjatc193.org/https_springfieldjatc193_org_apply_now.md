# Start Your Career

Applications are accepted year-round. No experience required — just commitment and the right qualifications.

Step By Step

## The application process.

1

### Pick Up Application

Visit the JATC training center at 3150 Wide Track Drive, Springfield, IL — Mon–Fri 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM.

2

### Pay Fee

A non-refundable $25 application fee — cash, check, or money order.

3

### Submit Materials

Return the completed packet with transcripts, a copy of your driver's license, a copy of your birth certificate, and DD-214 if applicable.

4

### Aptitude Test

Take the NJATC electrical aptitude test — a passing score is required to advance.

5

### JATC Interview

Qualified applicants complete a structured interview with the committee.

6

### Begin Your Career

Pass the physical and drug screen, get placed with a contractor, and start earning.

**Applications accepted year-round.** Call [(217) 544-3479 Ext. 229](tel:+12175443479,229) with any questions about applying.


Eligibility

## Applicant qualifications.

To be eligible for the NECA-IBEW 193 JATC apprenticeship program, you must meet these minimums.

✓

#### Age 18 or older

You must be at least 18 years old at the time of application.

✓

#### HS Diploma or GED

High school diploma, GED equivalent, or a two-year Associate Degree or higher.

✓

#### One Year of Algebra

1 year of high school algebra, one post-HS algebra course, or the NJATC Online Tech Math Course.

✓

#### Aptitude Test

Pass the NJATC electrical aptitude test.

✓

#### Pass a Physical

Complete a physical exam prior to enrollment.

✓

#### Pass a Drug Screen

Complete a drug screening prior to official enrollment.

**No prior experience required.** The apprenticeship is designed to train you from the ground up.

Documents Needed

## What to bring to your application.

1

#### Application Form

Complete the form and submit by the posted deadline.

2

#### High School Transcripts

Official transcripts showing graduation, GED certificate, or two-year Associate Degree or higher.

3

#### Proof of Algebra

Transcript or course record documenting algebra completion (see qualifications above).

4

#### Driver's License

Bring a copy of your valid driver's license.

5

#### Birth Certificate

Bring a copy of your birth certificate.

6

#### DD-214 (Veterans)

To verify military training and experience if applicable.

7

#### $25 Application Fee

Non-refundable — cash, check, or money order accepted.

8

#### Professional References

Contact information for educational or professional references.
