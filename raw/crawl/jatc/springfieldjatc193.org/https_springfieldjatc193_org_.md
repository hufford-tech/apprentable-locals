## Choose your trade. Earn your future.

The NECA-IBEW 193 JATC partners with IBEW signatory contractors to train the next generation of electrical professionals. Pick the track that fits your goals.

Inside Commercial Wireman

### Commercial & Industrial Electrician

Train to install, maintain, and troubleshoot complete electrical systems — power distribution, lighting, controls, motors, PLCs, and automation. Start at ~45% of journeyman scale with annual raises across six periods.

- 8,000 hours supervised on-the-job training
- 720 hours NJATC-approved classroom instruction
- National Electrical Code & blueprint reading
- Graduate as an IBEW Journeyman Electrician

[Program Details →](https://springfieldjatc193.org/apprenticeship-program)

3-Year Program![Low-voltage data racking installation](https://springfieldjatc193.org/images/data-racking.jpg)

Telecommunications

### Low-Voltage Electrician

Specialize in the low-voltage systems that power modern business: structured cabling, fiber optics, security, audio/video, and building automation. Fast-track into a fast-growing trade.

- 6,000 hours supervised on-the-job training
- 480 hours classroom instruction
- Fiber, CATV, alarm, and data network systems
- Graduate as an IBEW Journey-Level Technician

Why IBEW 193

## Benefits that start day one.

### Earn While You Learn

Paid on-the-job training from your first day. No tuition, no debt, real wages.

### Regular Raises

Pay increases at each period as you progress toward journeyman scale.

### Full Health & Pension

Health insurance, pension, and 401K through the NECA/IBEW Trust Fund.

### No Student Debt

JATC sponsors all classroom instruction and training materials.

### Brotherhood

Join 800,000+ IBEW members across the U.S. and Canada with travel rights.

### Job Security

Work exclusively for IBEW signatory contractors with fair, safe conditions.

### Advancement

Advance to Foreman, GF, or specialize in fiber, fire alarm, or renewables.

### Lifetime Career

Your IBEW Journeyman card is recognized and respected nationwide.
