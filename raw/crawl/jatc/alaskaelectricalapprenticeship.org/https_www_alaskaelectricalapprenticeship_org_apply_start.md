## Qualifications

Familiarize yourself with the AJEATT's qualifications, structure, duration, training components, and what the apprenticeship entails. The program includes a combination of on-the-job training and classroom instruction. Make sure you pick the apprenticeship program that is right for you, this is a lifelong career.

### Qualifications

You must meet all of the following requirements to qualify for interview:

- Be a minimum of 18 years of age at time of selection.
- Have a valid drivers' license.
- Have a high school diploma or state issued GED certificate.
- Meet our minimum math requirement through one of the following:
  - Two semesters of high school algebra 1 (or higher-level algebra) with a passing grade of 'C' or higher, or
  - one post high school algebra course with a passing grade, or
  - a certificate showing completion of the SkillsPrep Tech Math course , or
  - the State of Alaska WorkKeys math test (at any State of Alaska Job Center) with a '6' or higher, or
  - NOTE: Tree trimmer applicants do not need to meet the math requirement.

### Required Items

You must be able to submit all the following items by the application deadline to qualify for interview:

- Copy of your drivers' license
- OFFICIAL transcripts for high school and post high school education and training, including GED records if applicable.
  - Official transcripts are transcripts delivered to the AJEATT directly from the school, whether electronically or by mail. You can mail or hand deliver transcripts if they are sealed by the issuing school. You may request the issuing school to email transcripts if they contain verifiable digital certificates (such as through Adobe).
- Copy of high school diploma or GED certificate. High school transcripts are sufficient if they show that you successfully obtained a diploma.
- Documentation showing that you meet the minimum math requirement.
- A full individual driving record obtained within 90 days of your application date.
- $25 application fee.

### Do any of the following apply to you?

- You have worked at least 2,000 hours performing tree trimming, inside wireman (commercial or residential electrical construction), outside lineman, or telecommunications work
- You have been honorably discharged from service with the United States military in the last 5 years

If so, you may qualify for interview without meeting all of the above requirements. Please email the school for more information.
