## Inside Wireman / Electrician

An electrician is a skilled tradesperson who installs, maintains, and repairs electrical systems in various settings, including residential, commercial, and industrial environments. Here’s a breakdown of what their work typically involves:

- **Installation**: Set up wiring, outlets, switches, circuit breakers, and other electrical components based on blueprints and technical diagrams.
- **Maintenance**: Perform routine checks and upkeep on electrical systems to ensure efficiency and safety.
- **Repair**: Troubleshoot and diagnose electrical issues, then carry out repairs or replace faulty components.
- **Upgrading Systems**: Modify existing systems to accommodate new technology or increased power needs, including installing new circuits and panels.
- **Safety Compliance**: Ensure all work adheres to local building codes and safety regulations, including obtaining permits and conducting inspections.
- **Customer Interaction**: Communicate with clients to understand their needs, provide estimates, and explain the work being performed.
- **Specialized Skills**: Focus on specific areas such as industrial systems, home automation, or renewable energy, requiring additional training and expertise.
- **Traffic/Street Light Systems**: Repair, modify and build traffic control systems and street light maintenance and repair.

In essence, electricians play a crucial role in ensuring that electrical systems are safe, reliable, and efficient, supporting the functioning of homes, businesses, and various types of infrastructure.
