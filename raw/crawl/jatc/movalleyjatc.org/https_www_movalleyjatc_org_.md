# REACH YOUR POTENTIAL IN ELECTRICAL CONSTRUCTION

You have the potential to succeed in an exciting career that offers outstanding benefits, ongoing education, and an invaluable skillset.

​

Our apprenticeship programs allow you to earn a high wage while gaining the experience and knowledge you need to succeed as a journeyman lineman, traffic signal technician, substation technician, or cable splicer.​

## Apprenticeship Programs

### Lineman Program

Climb High

Our lineman program will prepare you to maintain overhead & underground powerlines, substations, switchyards, etc., with on-the-job training and enhanced with related classroom instruction.

### Substation Technician Program

Light the Way

Our substation technician program will give you the tools you need to maintain, operate, and repair substations & switchyards. Become fluent in reading blueprints, get hands-on training with state-of-the-art equipment, and learn a variety of complex electrical skills that will allow you to thrive in this field so you can "keep the lights on" for businesses/institutions/homeowners.

### Traffic Signal Technician Program

Green Means "Go"

Our traffic signal technician program will teach you the essential skills of electrical construction to prepare you for installing/maintaining/repairing traffic signal control devices & computerized communication cables, along with developing special circuits/sequences to modify signal operations and assisting in traffic pattern development.

### Cable Splicer Program

Wired Right

Our cable splicer program will teach you to be a versatile & highly skilled professional in the electrical construction industry. You'll learn to work with everything from powerlines and substations to the distribution and transmission lines. Over the course of this program, our talented instructors will ensure that you can get the job done correctly and safely.

## Midwest Line College

Welcome to Midwest Line College, our 13-week-long pre-apprenticeship program that provides entry-level training in the outside electrical industry

​

At MLC, we recognize the importance of practical skills in

linework. That's why our program goes beyond traditional classroom learning, incorporating extensive hands-on training in our state-of-the-art pole yard. Here, students engage in real-world scenarios, learning the intricacies of equipment handling and safety protocols under the guidance of experienced instructors.
