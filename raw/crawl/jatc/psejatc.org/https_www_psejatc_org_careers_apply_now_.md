# Apply Now

## Applications Status Notice - May 1, 2026

## Applications are closed

##### As of May 1, 2026, Puget Sound Electrical JATC has stopped accepting new applications.

Applications that were started prior to the May 1st deadline will be processed as usual. Testing and interviews for applicants who have an application in process will continue.

At this time there is no set date for reopening applications. Puget Sound Electrical JATC will provide a minimum of 30-days public notice in accordance with Washington State Labor & Industries requirements, prior to reopening applications.

We will continue to update this website as new information becomes available.

## Start Your Career

Interested in a career in the electrical industry that is rewarding and fulfilling? We have the opportunity and the program for you.

The Puget Sound Electrical JATC provides quality classroom instruction paired with hands-on learning in our labs. Combine the classroom education with the on-the-job experience you will receive from the finest IBEW/NECA electrical contractors and journey-workers in the area, and you will have more than just a job, you will have a lifelong career.

As an apprentice you will have excellent medical, dental, and vision benefits for you and your family.  You will be able to start building for your retirement through the pension and 401(k) programs.  Best of all, you will be part of an industry that provides and supports the workers of the NECA/IBEW electrical family.
