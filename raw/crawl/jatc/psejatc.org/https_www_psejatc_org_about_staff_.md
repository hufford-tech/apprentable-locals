## Board of Trustees

PSEJATC is led by a Board of Trustees with four Management Trustees and four Labor Trustees. They meet monthly to manage JATC finances and handle issues involving apprentices. Their goal is to help the PSEJATC apprenticeship in maintaining a standard of excellence in the electrical industry.

![](https://www.psejatc.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/rectangle-skyline.jpg)
