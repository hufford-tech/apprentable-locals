# How to Become an Electrician Trainees (ET) Through IBEW 569

Becoming an Electrician Trainee with IBEW Local 569 is the first step toward entering the electrical industry and beginning a path toward apprenticeship.

Our process ensures new workers enter the field safely, legally, and with strong union support.

![Electrician Trainee learning in a shop calss.](https://etiedu.org/images/assets/heros/hero-wm06.jpg)

## Four Step Process

## Step 1: Sign the Entry-Level Books at the IBEW 569 Hall

Visit the [IBEW Local 569](https://www.ibew569.org/ "Tap here to visit the IBEW 569 website. Opens in new browser tab") union hall in persson with your valid driver's license and social security card to sign Entry-Level Books.

**IBEW Local 569**

4545 Viewridge Ave

San Diego CA 92123

[Get Directions](https://maps.app.goo.gl/RudVn3WrrzExXCG97 "Tap here to get directions to the union hall. Open in a new browser tab.")

These entry-level positions include:

- Material Handler
- Construction Wireman (CW)
- Street Light Tech
- Sound Tech

You do not need experience to sign the books — just a willingness to learn and work safely.

## Step 2: Get Dispatched to an Entry-Level Job

Once you pick up a job, you will be dispatched to a signatory contractor.

This is the best way to get real jobsite exposure and begin learning the trade.

## Step 3: Enroll in Electrician Training Classes at ETI

Once you are a member, you must enroll in state-approved training classes through the Electrical Training Institute.

ETI provides the hands-on and classroom training required for you to legally perform electrical work.

Our curriculum teaches safety, tools, basic electrical theory, jobsite conduct, and prepares you for future apprenticeship opportunities.

## Step 4: Register With the State as an Electrician Trainee (ET)

California law requires you to register as an Electrician Trainee with the [Division of Apprenticeship Standards (DAS).](https://www.dir.ca.gov/das/ "Tap here to learn more about the Division of Apprenticeship Standards. Opens in a new browser tab.") To register, you must submit:

- ET Registration Application
- Proof of ETI enrollment
- $25 fee to the state

Once registered, you are legally allowed to perform electrical work under the supervision of a State-Certified Electrician.

### Why This Process Matters

This pathway protects new workers by ensuring they receive:

- Proper training
- Legal status to work
- Supervision by certified electricians
- A safe entry point into the union electrical industry

It also creates the strongest foundation for applying to the **Inside Wireman Apprenticeship** at ETI.
