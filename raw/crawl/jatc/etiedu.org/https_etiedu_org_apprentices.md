## Frequently Asked Questions

[When do classes start?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

January, May, and September. Check your [**TradeSchool**](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/sdett-org/login/index.php "Tap here to log into TradSchool. Opens in a new browser tab.") account for class schedule.

[Where can I find my class schedule?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

Log onto your [**TradeSchool**](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/sdett-org/login/index.php "Tap here to log into TradSchool. Opens in a new browser tab.") account to view your class schedule.

[What happens if I miss a class?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

The apprentice will need to schedule a make-up class in [**TradeSchool**](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/sdett-org/login/index.php "Tap here to log into TradSchool. Opens in a new browser tab.").

[How do I make up missed class hours?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

The apprentice is responsible for scheduling make-up classes in their [**TradeSchool**](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/sdett-org/login/index.php "Tap here to log into TradSchool. Opens in a new browser tab.") account.

[How do I advance to the next pay period?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

A combination of class hours and on the job training hours.

[What happens if I fall behind?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

Contact the [**Training Center**](https://etiedu.org/contact "Tap here to get our contact info.").

[What happens if I am laid off?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

Check in at the IBEW 569 Hall and contact the [**Training Center**](https://etiedu.org/contact "Tap here to get our contact info.").

[Who do I contact if my phone number or email changes?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

You can make changes in your [**TradeSchool**](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/sdett-org/login/index.php "Tap here to log into TradSchool. Opens in a new browser tab.") account.

[Where can I find apprentice forms and documents?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

On [**Moodle**](https://sdett-moodle.sdett.org/moodle/ "Tap here to visit the Moodle website. Opens in a new browser tab.") or your [**TradeSchool**](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/sdett-org/login/index.php "Tap here to log into TradSchool. Opens in a new browser tab.") account.

[What tools or books do I need for class?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

Refer to class syllabus.

[How are on-the-job hours tracked?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

It is the apprentices’ responsibility to log on the job training hours in [**TradeSchool**](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/sdett-org/login/index.php "Tap here to log into TradSchool. Opens in a new browser tab.").

[Who should I contact with dispatch or contractor questions?](https://etiedu.org/apprentices)

Contact Gail at the [**Training Center**](https://etiedu.org/contact#gail "Tap here to contact Gail at the Training Center.").
