# Maintaining Your Electrician Trainee Card (California)

## Why Your Electrician Trainee Card Is Important

In California, anyone performing electrical work who is not, yet a certified journeyperson or an apprentice must hold a current Electrician Trainee (ET) card issued by the California Department of Industrial Relations (DIR).

![Electrician Trainee on a roof about to use a ladder to get down.](https://etiedu.org/images/assets/heros/hero-wm04.jpg)

**At IBEW Local 569 and the Electrical Training Institute (ETI), maintaining your trainee card is required to:**

- Work legally in the electrical trade
- Have your on-the-job hours count toward apprenticeship or certification
- Remain eligible for union employment
- Stay in good standing with state regulations

Working with an expired trainee card is illegal and can result in removal from a job site, fines, and delays.

## What Is an Electrician Trainee Card?

An Electrician Trainee card allows individuals to work in the electrical trade while enrolled in a state-approved training program, such as the IBEW Local 569 / ETI Electrician Trainee or Apprenticeship Program.

If you are working in the field and are not yet a certified electrician, this card is required by the State of California.

[Apply for or renew your \\
\\
Electrician Trainee card](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/ecu/ElectricalTraineeRenewRegistration.pdf "Tap here to download the application from PDF. Opens in a new browser tab.")

## How to Maintain Your Electrician Trainee Card

To keep your Electrician Trainee card active, all three of the following must be completed every year:

### 1\. Remain Enrolled at ETI

You must be actively enrolled in a DIR-approved electrical training program, such as the Electrical Training Institute (ETI) affiliated with IBEW Local 569.

If you are not enrolled in an approved program, your trainee card is not valid, even if you are working.

### 2\. Complete Required Classroom Instruction

The State of California requires Electrician Trainees to complete a minimum number of classroom hours (150) each year to remain eligible for renewal.

At ETI:

- Classroom hours are tracked by the training center
- Hours must be completed before your trainee card expires
- Incomplete hours may prevent renewal

Important: Working hours alone do not keep your trainee card active — classroom instruction is mandatory. It is YOUR responsibility to maintain your ET card.

### 3\. Renew Your Trainee Card Annually with the DIR

Electrician Trainee cards must be renewed every year through the California DIR.

You are responsible for:

- Monitoring your expiration date
- Submitting your renewal on time
- Paying the required state renewal fee

Your employer does not renew this for you.

It is the trainee’s responsibility to maintain a current card.

[Renew your trainee card](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/ecu/ElectricalTraineeRenewRegistration.pdf "Tap here to download the Renew Registration from PDF. Opens in a new browser tab.")

### Common Issues We See

- Letting a trainee card expire
- Falling short of required classroom hours
- Waiting too long to renew
- Assuming someone else is handling the process

Any of these can delay your entry into the apprenticeship or interrupt your ability to work.
