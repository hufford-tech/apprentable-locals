## Your path to a fulfilling, union electrical career

When you join a union electrical apprenticeship, you’re not just learning a trade — you’re stepping into a career with power, purpose and a union family that has your back from day one.

Here’s what your journey will look like.

### Apply to the Electrical Training Institute Apprenticeship Program

Become part of a brotherhood and sisterhood committed to skill, safety and solidarity. Your journey begins the moment you decide you deserve a career with real dignity and real opportunity.

### Earn While You Learn

Union training means you never have to choose between paying your bills and building your future. You’ll get paid from day one while training for a lifelong union trade, because a real career shouldn’t put you in debt.

### Learn From the Best

Classroom instruction gives you the technical skills and tools to succeed. You’re learning from instructors who’ve spent years in the field and believe in passing the trade on the right way, the safe way, the union way.

### Experience Strength Through Solidarity

Enjoy the union difference -better pay, strong benefits, a good retirement, workplace protections and more. Learn of the power that comes from standing together as a member of the IBEW.

### Become a Journeyman Electrician

Once you complete your training, you’ll earn more than the title of Journeyman Electrician. You’ll earn stability, pride and a future built on union protections, competitive pay and the power of standing together with thousands of IBEW professionals. These are skills and relationships that will benefit you the rest of your life, no matter where you go.

### Work Hard. Live Well.

Join a trade where you take out of it what you put into it. This is not just a job - it’s a career. It’s the next step in the rest of your life.

Fair wages, good benefits, a comfortable retirement, a union family and training in skills from the best for a career that will last a lifetime.

What’s stopping you?

## Frequently   Asked Questions

[Who can apply to the program?](https://etiedu.org/)

Applicants must meet minimum age, education and eligibility requirements. Specific application requirements are listed for each program:

- [Inside Wireman](https://etiedu.org/applicants/inside-wireman/application-requirements-and-process "Tap here for Inside Wireman application requirements")
- [Sound & Communication](https://etiedu.org/applicants/sound-communication/application-requirements-and-process "Tap here for Sound & Communication")
- [Intelligent Transportation Systems](https://etiedu.org/applicants/intelligent-transportation-systems/application-requirements-and-process "Tap here for Intelligent Transportation Systems application requirements")

[Do I need prior electrical experience?](https://etiedu.org/)

No prior electrical experience. Training is designed to start from the fundamentals and build skills over time.

[Is this a paid apprenticeship?](https://etiedu.org/)

Yes. Apprentices earn wages while completing on-the-job training and classroom instruction.

[How long does the apprenticeship take to complete?](https://etiedu.org/)

Program length varies, but most apprenticeships range from four to five years.

[How much does the program cost?](https://etiedu.org/)

Most costs are covered by the apprenticeship program.
