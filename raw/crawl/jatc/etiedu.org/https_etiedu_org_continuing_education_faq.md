# General Information

[Where do I sign up for the General Electrician state exam?](https://etiedu.org/continuing-education/faq)

You can obtain a copy of the exam application in person at our Training Center, or obtain it online at [https://www.dir.ca.gov/DLSE/ECU/ECUCertforms.htm](https://www.dir.ca.gov/DLSE/ECU/ECUCertforms.htm "Tap here to visit the DIR website. Opens in a new browser tab.").

[Are there classes available to help me study for the exam?](https://etiedu.org/continuing-education/faq)

In-person exam prep classes may be offered as continuing education classes each semester.

[Do I need to be a member of IBEW 569 to take continuing education classes?](https://etiedu.org/continuing-education/faq)

Classes are available to members of IBEW Local 569 (Journeyman, Apprentices, CWs, CEs), travelers from other locals (only if they are currently working with a Local 569 contractor), NECA members and retired Local 569 members.

[How many hours of continued education are required to renew my General Electrician State Certification?](https://etiedu.org/continuing-education/faq)

You are required to complete 32 hours of continuing education by an approved training facility within 3 years of receiving your certification. For further information refer to the DIR website [https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/ecu/electricaltrade.html](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/ecu/electricaltrade.html "Tap here to visit the DIR website. Opens in a new browser tab.").

[How do I get proof of my continuing education hours?](https://etiedu.org/continuing-education/faq)

For a copy of your transcript for classes taken at the Electrical Training Institute, email us at [info@sdett.org](mailto:info@sdett.org) or call us at (858) 569-6633.

[How do I renew my General Electrician State Certification?](https://etiedu.org/continuing-education/faq)

You can obtain a copy of the renewal application in person at our Training Center or obtain it online at [https://www.dir.ca.gov/DLSE/ECU/ECUCertforms.htm](https://www.dir.ca.gov/DLSE/ECU/ECUCertforms.htm "Tap here to visit the DIR website. Opens in a new browser tab."). Please keep in mind that you can renew your license up to one year prior to the expiration date.
