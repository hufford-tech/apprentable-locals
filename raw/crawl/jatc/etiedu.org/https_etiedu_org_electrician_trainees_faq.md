# General Information

[What is an Electrician Trainee?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

An Electrician Trainee is someone working toward state electrician certification who is enrolled in approved education but is not yet an apprentice or journeyman.

[What is the difference between an Electrician Trainee, an Apprentice and a Journeyman?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

- **Electrician Trainee:** Entry-level worker who is enrolled in state-approved electrical education (such as ETI) and registered with the State of California but is not yet accepted into the IBEW apprenticeship.Trainees may work under the direct supervision of a state-certified electrician while completing at least 150 classroom hours per year and gaining experience.
- **Apprentice:** Formally accepted into the IBEW Local 569 / ETI apprenticeship program, combining paid on-the-job training with structured classroom instruction. Apprentices earn scheduled wage increases, receive benefits, and progress through the program while working toward state certification.
- **Journeyman:** A state-certified electrician who has completed required education, accumulated 8,000 hours of supervised work experience and passed the California certification exam. Journeymen work independently, supervise trainees and apprentices, and are full members of IBEW Local 569.

[Do I need an Electrician Trainee card to work in California?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

Yes, anyone performing electrical work for a C-10 contractor must be a certified electrician, registered apprentice, or registered Electrician Trainee.

[What is ETI’s role in the Electrician Trainee program?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

ETI provides state-approved electrical education.

[How many classroom hours are required each year?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

Electrician Trainees must complete at least **150 classroom hours per calendar year** in a state-approved program to remain active.

[Read more approved curriculum info](https://www.dir.ca.gov/t8/296_2.html)

[Can Electrician Trainees work on the jobsite?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

Yes, registered Electrician Trainees may work under the direct supervision of a state-certified electrician.

[Does the Electrician Trainee card need to be renewed?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

Yes, Electrician Trainee registration must be renewed annually with proof of continued enrollment and education. Educational transcripts must be obtained from ETI prior to mailing in renewal.

[Does ETI automatically register me for classes every year?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

No. It is your responsibility to maintain your ET card and register for classes.

[How does the Electrician Trainee path lead to the IBEW Local 569 apprenticeship?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

Many trainees gain experience through ETI and Local 569 signatory contractors before applying and transitioning into the IBEW Inside Wireman apprenticeship.

[Why is maintaining Electrician Trainee status important?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

Active registration ensures work is legal, hours count toward certification, and trainees remain eligible for advancement.

[Where can Electrician Trainee or Electrician status be verified?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

Status can be verified through the **[California DIR certification search database.](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/ecu/ElectCert/electcertsearch.asp "Tap here to visit the California DIR certification search database. Opens in a new browser tab.")**

[How do I register as an Electrician Trainee with the State of California?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

You must enroll in a state-approved training program, complete the Electrician Trainee registration application, submit proof of enrollment, and mail it with the required fee to the California DIR.

[Read more](https://www.dir.ca.gov/dlse/ecu/ElectricalTrainee.htm)

[What happens if my Electrician Trainee card expires?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

If your card expires, you may not legally perform electrical work and any hours worked while inactive do not count toward certification.

[Do I need to apply for the apprenticeship to be an entry level worker / Electrician trainee?](https://etiedu.org/electrician-trainees/faq)

No. You do not need to apply for the apprenticeship to be an entry level worker.
