# Find Your Place in Local 569

IBEW Local 569 is built on more than training and employment—it is built on brotherhood, sisterhood and the strength of members standing together. Our committees offer opportunities for members to connect, lead and support one another. Whether through mentorship, advocacy, outreach or fellowship, these groups give members a meaningful way to be part of something bigger than themselves.

![Electrician Trainee working with a drill on a ladder.](https://etiedu.org/images/assets/logos/ibew-logo.png)

![Electrician Trainee working with a drill on a ladder.](https://etiedu.org/media/yootheme/cache/1f/ibew569-logo-1fab541d.png)

## Alianza

**Meeting:** Last Friday of the month · 4:30 PM

**Union Hall**

(followed by social hour at Kilowatt Brewery @ 6:00 PM)

Alianza is a subcommittee of the Electrical Workers Minority Caucus (EWMC). It engages Latino communities throughout San Diego and Imperial Counties by building community relationships, supporting business opportunities, and strengthening connections with local elected officials.

**How to Join:**

Attend a meeting or email the committee contact to get connected.

**Contact:** [info@ewmc569.org](mailto:info@ewmc569.org) (Attn: Alianza)

[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/alianza569_sdiv/)

## Electrical Workers Minority Caucus (EWMC)

**Meeting:** 1st Saturday of the month - 10:00 AM

**Virtual & Union Hall**

The EWMC promotes diversity, inclusion, and equal opportunity within IBEW Local 569 while strengthening our union through education, advocacy, and member engagement.

**How to Join:**

All members are welcome. Attend a meeting or reach out by email for meeting access.

**Contact:** [info@ewmc569.org](mailto:info@ewmc569.org)

[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/EWMC569)

[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ewmc569?igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==)

## Legislative Committee

**Meeting:** 2nd Tuesday of the month - 4:00 PM

**In person**

The Legislative Committee focuses on political advocacy, legislative priorities, and candidate engagement. Members help guide Local 569’s political action efforts.

**How to Join:**

Attend a meeting or contact the committee chair for more information.

**Contact:** Cori Schumacher [cschumacher@ibew569.org](mailto:cschumacher@ibew569.org)

## RENEW (Reach Out and Engage Next-Gen Electrical Workers)

**Meeting:** First Friday of the month - 5:30 PM

**Virtual & Union Hall**

RENEW encourages younger members to become active in the union by focusing on leadership development, education, and building strong relationships within Local 569.

**How to Join:**

Show up to a meeting or email the committee to get added to meeting updates.

**Contact:** [renewibew569@gmail.com](mailto:renewibew569@gmail.com)

[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/RENEWSanDiego)

[Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/renew569?igsh=NTc4MTIwNjQ2YQ==)

## Retirees Club

**Meeting:** Quarterly - 10:00 AM

**Union Hall**

The Retirees Club keeps retired members engaged through community service, political action, picket support, and social activities.

## United Sparkies

United Sparkies unites women and allies in the electrical industry to promote mentorship, leadership development, community outreach, and recruitment and retention of women in the trade.

**How to Join:**

Attend a meeting or email the group to learn about upcoming events and ways to get involved.
