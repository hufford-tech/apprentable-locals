# Monthly Work Report

Submit your required Apprentice Monthly Work Report.

Your Monthly Work Report is due to the Sacramento Electrical Training Center on the 1st of the month following the work month, it is considered late if received after the 10th of the month. Click below to fill out your Monthly Work Report online.
