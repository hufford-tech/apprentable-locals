# Frequently Asked Questions

You’ve got questions? We’ve got answers. Interested in becoming an Inside Wireman? Check out our Frequently Asked Questions (FAQs) below for more info. Your question is still not answered? [Feel free to reach out](https://www.340jatc.org/contact "Click here to contact us.").

## General   Information

[What is the Inside Wireman Apprenticeship Program?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

**It is a 5-year systematic program of learning**. Apprentices can obtain all the necessary knowledge and skills to become a Journeyman Inside Wireman.

**8000 Hours of On-The-Job Training**. Apprentices train to proficiency in all skills required to install, maintain, and troubleshoot various systems associated with electrical installations.

**1000 Hours of Related Supplemental instruction** (minimum) – Apprentices will study theories relating to all job tasks important to the trade. Classroom, hands-on lab, and online training help to reinforce skills learned while doing on-the-job training.

[What type of work does an Inside Wireman Apprentice do?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Inside Wiremen, among other things, install and maintain all electrical conduits, wires, cables, motor controls and panelboards found in commercial and industrial facilities.

[I would like additional information on what the Apprenticeship program is about.](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Our website is the best place to learn about the Inside Wireman Apprenticeship program. If you still have questions, contact us by calling (916) 646-6688 or emailing [adminstaff@340jatc.org](mailto:adminstaff@340jatc.org "Click here to email the office.").

[Where is the program offered?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Our Training Center is located at 2836 El Centro Road in Sacramento, CA. The jurisdiction for our job sites covers 18 counties around Sacramento, Butte, and Shasta.

[Is the Apprenticeship full-time or part-time?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

As an Apprentice, you will be working full-time, completing your on-the-job training and related supplemental instruction.

## Application Process   and Requirements

[What are the steps of the application process?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

**Step 1: [Complete an application](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/application-requirements-and-process "Click here for more info on how to apply.")** and submit all requirements

**Step 2:** Pass the Aptitude Test

**Step 3:** Attend an Oral Interview

**Step 4:** If selected, attend Orientation

[Where and when can I apply?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Applicants [Apply Online](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/application-requirements-and-process "Click here for more info on how to apply.")

For additional assistance, applicants may also stop by the JATC to apply between 8:30 am and 4:00 pm Monday – Friday.

[What do I need to fulfill the algebra requirement?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Show evidence of successful completion of one full year (2 semesters) of High School Algebra or one semester of College Elementary Algebra with a minimum grade of “C” or equivalent or provide evidence of having successfully completed the Electrical Training ALLIANCE Tech Math Course (available online at [http://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/Learning/OnlineTechMath](http://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/Learning/OnlineTechMath "Click to visit the Electrical Training ALLIANCE Tech Math Course web page.")). Pre-Algebra does **not meet this requirement.**

[What if my transcripts indicate that I completed a Math course but does not state Algebra? (Ex. Math A or Math 1)](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Your transcripts must indicate that you completed an algebra course or any course above Algebra 1. If your transcripts specify that you completed a Math course, you must attach an additional letter from the school stating the description of the Math course. The description must specify that the Math course consisted of Algebra 1.

[Will my Bachelor’s Degree fulfill the algebra requirement?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

No. An Associates, Bachelors, or Master’s degree will only qualify for the diploma requirement. You must submit Official Transcripts from the school where you completed the algebra course to fulfill the algebra requirement (If you transferred schools, the credits transfer but not the courses; therefore, new transcripts won’t show your Algebra completion).

[What If I don’t have the algebra requirement?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

One option is to enroll at your local college and complete an Algebra course. Or the second option is to complete the Electrical Training ALLIANCE Tech Math Course (available online at [http://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/Learning/OnlineTechMath](http://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/Learning/OnlineTechMath "Click here to visit the Electrical Training ALLIANCE website.")).

[Where am I in the application process?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

If you have successfully applied, you will be notified by email of the next step in the application process. For further information, contact the Sacramento ElectricalTraining Center by calling (916) 646-6688 or email at [adminstaff@340jatc.org](mailto:adminstaff@340jatc.org "Click here to email the office.").  See our [application flow chart](https://www.340jatc.org/images/pdf/IW-Application-Flow-Chart.pdf "Click here to download our application flow chart.").

[What if I don’t have official sealed transcripts?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Only official sealed transcripts are accepted. Copies are not accepted and will not fulfill the requirement. The Sacramento Electrical Training Center must have an official transcript on file for you to be a complete applicant.

[What if I have foreign transcripts?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Foreign transcripts are accepted as long as they are translated into English.

[What if I have electrical construction industry experience?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Individuals who have accumulated a minimum of two thousand (2,000) hours of electrical construction work experience must provide the JATC with the proper documentation that defines their prior experience under a C-10 licensed contractor. Documentation must be comprised of official documents, such as payroll records or official statements on contractor’s letterhead. The JATC will examine all documentation submitted before qualification is acknowledged.

Fill out the following Hour Summary Worksheet and submit it and all necessary documentation to the JATC at [adminstaff@340jatc.org](mailto:adminstaff@340jatc.org "Click here to email the office."), mail it to 2836 El Centro Road, Sacramento, CA or drop it off at that address.

[Hour Summary Worksheet](https://www.340jatc.org/images/pdf/2000HourRuleSummaryWorksheet.pdf "Click here to download our 2000 + Hour Summary Worksheet.")

[What’s next after I submit 2,000 hours of experience?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Upon submission of industry-related experience, your qualifying hours may be applied to your apprenticeship program.

[I have a lot of experience and would like to bypass the Apprenticeship Program and become a Union Journeyman Inside Wireman.](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Contact IBEW Local 340 at (916)927-4239 to inquire about joining the Union.

[What if I have a learning disability?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Please notify the Sacramento JATC prior to being scheduled for the aptitude test. Reasonable accommodations will be made.

[Do I need a driver's license when I apply?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Yes, you will need a valid driver’s license when applying.

[What does the JATC consider as an **official** transcript?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

We require your transcript to have a school stamped **official transcript** on the outside of the envelope and never been opened.

[What if my school sends my transcript to me directly?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

If you DO NOT open the envelope and it meets the requirements on the above question, you may either bring it to our office or mail it to our office.

## Aptitude Test

[What’s next after meeting all minimum requirements?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

You will be scheduled to take the aptitude test battery. Once an aptitude test battery date has been established, you will be notified of the time, date, and location via email.

[How often are aptitude tests scheduled?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Aptitude tests are scheduled as needed.

[What does the aptitude test consist of?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

The aptitude test consists of reading comprehension and algebra.

[How can I prepare for the aptitude test?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Once you have been confirmed for the test, you will get a confirmation email that provides a link for some sample algebra and reading comprehension questions. You can view those questions here [Sample Questions](http://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/SamplePage/PreparingfortheTest "Click here to view the sample questions."). If you need further help with algebra studies, we recommend visiting [www.khanacademy.org](http://www.khanacademy.org/ "Click here to visit the Khan Academy website.").

[How long is the aptitude test?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Please plan on spending approximately 3 hours at the JATC Training Center on your scheduled test day.

[What if I already tested?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

If you receive an invite and have already taken the aptitude test within the last 2 years, please notify our office so we can obtain your score. If your score qualifies for our JATC, you will be moved to the next step in the application process: the oral interview.

[What if I can't make it to the aptitude test?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

You must provide written documentation ([adminstaff@340jatc.org](mailto:adminstaff@340jatc.org "Click here to email the office.")) stating why you won't be able to attend. Please submit documents no later than 72 hours prior to your scheduled test date. You may be given a 1-time excuse from the test based upon Sacramento JATC approval and re-scheduled for a later date.

[What if I am running late to the aptitude test?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Unfortunately, late arrivals will not be permitted into the test. You may be given a 1-time exception for taking the test at a later date.

[How will I be notified of my test result?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

If you completed the computer-based version of the test, you will receive an email within 2 business days of taking the test.

If you completed the paper version of the test, the results can take several weeks to receive. You will receive an email containing your results within 2 business days of us receiving them.

## Oral Interview   and Ranking

[What happens after I receive an email stating that I passed the test?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

You will be scheduled for an oral interview once an interview date has been established. You will receive an interview notification by email indicating the time, date, and location of your oral interview.

[Can I interview again?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

You may submit a request for an additional interview after ninety (90) days from your previous interview date. You must submit documentation as evidence that you have gained at least 450 hours of electrical construction work experience or successfully completed two (2) or more post-secondary, trade-related classes, which you feel may enhance your chances for possible selection into the apprenticeship program. In order to obtain a re-interview, you must contact the Sacramento JATC and complete an official Applicant Re-Interview Request Form. The JATC shall review such a request to determine if another interview would be warranted based on the information submitted.

[What happens after I interview?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

You will receive an email within 2 business days of your interview date with your score.

[How long will I be on the rank list?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Your score is valid for up to two (2) years. Please keep in mind all ranked applicants may not be selected, and selections are not always made immediately following the interview. Very often, several months may pass before actual selections are made.

## Selection and   Orientation

[How will I be notified if I have been selected?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Upon selection, you will be contacted by the phone number we have on file. Please keep your contact information up to date with the Sacramento JATC at all times.

[What happens after I accept the offer of Apprenticeship?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

If you accept your offer of Apprenticeship, you will receive an email providing you with a drug screen requisition form and the time and date of your orientation at the Sacramento Electrical Training Center. An electronic orientation packet will also be attached to the email.

[What if I cannot attend the scheduled orientation?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Unfortunately, your application will be closed as orientation is required for the day/date we provide you. We would move forward with the next candidate on the Rank list.

[What is the cost of the program?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

There are no tuition or registration fees; however, you will be required to purchase your own hand tools in accordance with the apprentice tool list.

[If selected, will I receive advanced placement if I submitted 2,400 hours of experience?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

Advanced placement is possible. You must request to have your experience assessed in writing in order to be scheduled for the skills assessment exam. This can only be requested by applicants who have prior formal classroom electrical experience. The Training Director will determine eligibility based on proof of completion of prior coursework.

[When are new classes brought in?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

The needs of the electrical construction industry drive applicant selection. Therefore, registration dates for new Apprentices vary.

[When will I begin related supplemental instruction "classroom training"?](https://www.340jatc.org/applicants/inside-wireman/faqs)

At the beginning of the next school year, which starts in early fall. Apprentices are notified by email of their class dates and times.
