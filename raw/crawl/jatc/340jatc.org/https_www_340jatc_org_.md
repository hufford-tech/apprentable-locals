## We Keep the   Lights on

[![Video about Sacramento Electrical Training Center](https://www.340jatc.org/media/yootheme/cache/e4/home-video-e4122a0e.jpg)](https://www.340jatc.org/#js-0)

![Video play button](https://www.340jatc.org/images/assets/icon-play.svg)

**Be a part of the UNION DIFFERENCE.** Joining a Union means better pay, better benefits, better retirement and a better life. Learn with the best to be the best. Being part of a Union is like being part of a family — your Union brothers and sisters have your back. Join the Union family today!

**EARN while you LEARN.** Apprentices earn money while learning from experienced journeymen on the jobsite once they’re accepted into the program.

**RECEIVE COLLEGE CREDIT.** Apprentices must also complete five weeks of schooling each year. learning from [American River College](https://www.340jatc.org/american-river-college "Click here to read more ARC advantages. ") professors while earning college credit.(American River College is Sacramento Electrical Training Center's Local Educational Agency).

**LEARN FOR FREE:** There are no tuition or textbook costs for students in the program.
