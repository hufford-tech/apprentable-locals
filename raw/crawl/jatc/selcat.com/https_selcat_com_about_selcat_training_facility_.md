**SELCAT Training Facility**

**SELCAT** services Alabama, Arkansas, Florida, Georgia, Louisiana, Mississippi, North Carolina, South Carolina and Tennessee with its **_32,000-square-foot training facility_** with smart classrooms, labs, dorms, and administrative offices.

Located at:

90 Newnan South Industrial Drive

Newnan, GA 30263
