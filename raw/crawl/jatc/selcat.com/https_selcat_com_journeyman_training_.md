# Journeyman Training

## **Journeymen Get More with SELCAT!**

[![](https://selcat.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/xJOURNEYMAN-IMG_0585-e1537385106661-1024x560.jpg.pagespeed.ic.l-2XKNva7X.webp)](https://selcat.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/JOURNEYMAN-IMG_0585.jpg)

_Better Pay. Better Jobs. Better Healthcare. Better Retirement._

**Journeyman Linemen: Get Upgrade Training** **SELCAT** is also committed to developing and providing skills upgrade training to keep the Journeyman-level workforce as current and up-to-date as possible in the newest technologies. SELCAT offers advanced courses for Journeyman level workers such as: EICA Crane Certification Courses, OSHA 10 Hour ET&D, First Aid, CPR, Splicing, Advanced Transformer 3, Train-The-Trainer, and many more! Contact our office for info or check out our calendar for upcoming classes.

**SELCAT** and the **National Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee (N.J.A.T.C.)** constantly identifies new training needs and designs appropriate training courses to help SELCAT meet local training need requirements.
