# SELCAT Mission

_[![](https://selcat.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/xMISSION-IMG_0382-e1537384084566-1024x570.jpg.pagespeed.ic.KslEku58Aq.webp)](https://selcat.com/wordpress/wp-content/uploads/2018/09/MISSION-IMG_0382.jpg)_

_“The Southeastern Line Constructors Apprenticeship & Training Program is focused on keeping the Southeast outside electrical industry supplied with the necessary force of highly skilled and qualified workers.”_

**SELCAT** is committed to administering, on a local level, the standardized Lineman training program of the National Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (N.J.A.T.C.) to educate members of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (I.B.E.W.) and the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA). In doing so, SELCAT provides the regional Outside Electrical Construction Industry with the most highly trained workforce possible.

In addition, **SELCAT** trains various utility corporations and municipalities.

> **SELCAT develops true craftsman!** In addition to providing on-the-job skill training, **SELCAT** also makes sure that apprentices and Journeymen receive trade-related classroom and upgrade training that creates workers who take pride in their craftsmanship.
