**Every applicant must fill out an application form electronically, accurately and completely responding to all questions and items listed in the application.  Any application not properly filled out or not containing all information required**

**will not be processed.**

**Each applicant must meet the following minimum qualifications and will be required to provide evidence satisfactory to the JATC at the time of submitting their application:**

​

- **Each applicant must be: (i) a high school graduate, or (ii) have a Certificate of High School Equivalency or GED, or (iii) have a two-year Associate Degree (or its equivalent) or higher.** **(If equivalent education was obtained outside of the United States, the applicant will be required** **to provide appropriate documentation).** ​


**​**

- **Each applicant must have successfully completed: (i) one full year of high school algebra (or its equivalent) with a grade of “C” or better, or (ii) one semester of post-high school algebra (e.g., Adult Education, Continuing Education, Community College, etc.) with a grade of “C” or better, or (iii) the electrical training ALLIANCE’s online Tech Math course.**


**​**

- **Each applicant must provide an official transcript for high school and post high school education and training.  All GED records must be submitted if applicable.  (Only official copies or electronic parchment through schools are accepted).**


**​**

- **Any applicant who is a veteran must submit a DD-214 to verify military training and/or experience if they wish to receive consideration for such training/experience.**


**​**

- **Each applicant must be able to work legally in the United States and provide a valid social security number.  (Copy of social security card required).**


**​**

- **Each applicant must present evidence of a valid driver’s license.   (Copy of driver's license required).**


- **Each applicant will be required to take the Aptitude Test prepared by the electrical training ALLIANCE. Applicants must receive a minimum passing score of “4”.**


**​**

- **Prior to being indentured, applicants selected from the pool of interviewed applicants must be at least 18 years of age.**


**​**

- **Prior to being indentured, applicants selected from the pool of interviewed applicants will be required to provide results of a physical examination and a drug test as decided by the JATC.****​** **​** **​**


**Residency: Applicants must have resided in the jurisdiction of the committee**

**one year prior to making application.**

**​KY:  Boyd, Carter, Elliott, Floyd, Johnson, Lawrence, Magoffin, Martin, Morgan, Pike, and Rowan Counties.**

**OH:  Gallia and Lawrence Counties.**

**WV:  Cabell, Lincoln, Logan, Mason, Mingo, and Wayne Counties.**​​
