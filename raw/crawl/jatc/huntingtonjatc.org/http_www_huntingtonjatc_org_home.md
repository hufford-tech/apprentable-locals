The Huntington Electrical JATC is an affiliate of the electrical training ALLIANCE (formerly the NJATC).

The electrical training ALLIANCE developed uniform standards that are adopted and used nationwide to

select and train thousands of qualified men and

women each year.

Over the years, we have continually trained the best craftsperson in the industry without cost to the taxpayers.

Students in the electrical training ALLIANCE training programs earn while they learn by doing apprenticeships.

Apprentices receive excellent wages, paid health benefits, pension, guaranteed wage advancement, and an

employment atmosphere with safe working conditions.

The Huntington Electrical Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee is comprised of a Committee of Trustees.

The Committee is made up of six members:

three management members that represent the

West Virginia-Ohio Valley Chapter of NECA,

and three labor members that represent

Local Union 317 of the International Brotherhood of

Electrical Workers.

The Huntington Electrical JATC is located at

1816 Madison Avenue in Huntington, West Virginia.
