Apprentices attend class one day for 9 hours every other week. While attending classes, apprentices will sit in a classroom environment with fellow year apprentices. Many classes have a mixed environment of traditional classroom instructions, self studying, and in lab training.

## [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.trmqvwa595ja)    FAQ's

### [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.d754gcbh5vlp)    How long is the apprenticeship?

The Apprenticeship is 4 years long. Related Training Instruction (RTI) is 720 hours, with a minimum of 8,000 hours of On-the-Job Training.

### [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.ei0gy4untyp8)    When do we accept applications?

We accept applications every day through our online portal.

### [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.gt47x7946o3z)    What is the starting Salary?

Currently,  the starting salary for a first-year apprentice is $18.15 for inside wireman, $21 for WDW.

### [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.o65rqyxq69k)    What are the Health Benefits?

During Apprenticeship, apprentices are given single health coverage, however, upon completion of the apprenticeship Journey Workers receive family coverage as part of their package.

WDW has health plans offered you can choose to participate in post wages.

### [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.d7pc9xouu0hs)    Are there any Retirement Options?

Inside Wireman apprentices have the ability to receive up to three retirements.  Starting from day one, apprentices receive the National Electrical Benefit Fund (NEBF), then when the apprentice reaches the third year of school they will receive an annuity. When an apprentice joins the Union they will receive the Pension Benefit Fund (PBF).

WDW Apprentices can choose to participate in a 401K with employer offering matching contributions. That percentage is subject to the current CBA.

### [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.ctphvw43nuwu)    Is there tuition?

The Electrical Training Alliance of Central Florida is a Tuition free school. However, students will need to buy course materials.

### [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.luwy0z9qf7mf)    What is the cost of course materials?

Apprentices can expect to pay up to $1100 a school year for course materials.

### [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.aabm4d4bfgt5)    Are there scholarships or grants?

The Electrical Training Alliance of Central Florida works hard to try and receive grants and scholarships for apprentices. Currently, apprentices may apply for second, and third year grants. The Electrical Training Alliance of Central Florida is not the ones that award the grants. Awarding of the grants is dependent on selection from the Grantor.

### [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/apprenticeship/general-information\#h.ncv5dg5ch2f7)    Are there any classes an applicant can take to prepare for the aptitude test?

The Electrical Training Alliance offers a course through their online training platform. You may go to [https://www.skillsprep.org/login/index.php](https://www.skillsprep.org/login/index.php).

Course fees may apply.
