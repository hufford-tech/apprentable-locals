# PROGRAMS

# [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/programs\#h.r5mg938dmxea)    Learn more about our Programs

Our 4-year electrical apprenticeship will require you to complete four years of related electrical training with approximately 720 hours of classroom time. You will also work for one of our employers to earn 8,000 hours of on-the-job training. Apprentices work full time during the week (OJT), and one day every other week (2 times a month) you attend classroom training from 7AM-4PM.

While on the job, apprentices may find themselves working on elevated platforms, confined spaces, and a variety of shifts. Shifts are determined from the job site requirements

## [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/programs\#h.yl41nkg4m3d6)    Inside Wireman

The Apprentices working in the Inside Construction program can follow the general description of work performed on the job. Inside Wiremen install conduit, electrical wiring, fixtures, and electrical apparatus inside commercial buildings and in a multitude of industrial settings. Inside Wiremen must use many different kinds of tools, ranging from simple one and two-hand tools (such as pliers, screwdrivers, and cable-cutters) to power assisted tools like electric drills and cable pullers. They occasionally operate heavy equipment, work from elevated positions, and enter confined spaces.

## [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/programs\#h.j7qy1zi0d7x)    INSTALLER TECHNICIAN

Our Telecommunications apprenticeship will require you to complete 2 1/2 years of related electrical training with approximately 225 hours of classroom time per year. You will also work for one of our employers to earn 4,800 hours of on-the-job training. Apprentices work full time during the week (OJT) and one afternoon come to class for 6hrs of Classroom related training.

## [Copy heading link](https://www.etacfl.org/programs\#h.p0e9uzvwdsbl)    Walt Disney Maintenance

The Apprentices at Walt Disney World respond to power outages, work on rewiring equipment, run conduit for fixtures, respond to trouble tickets, inspect electrical components, install or rewire lighting, and work with the newest in animation and electronic technology.

As an apprentice for the Walt Disney Company, you can expect to work in the resorts for the first two years. In the third and fourth years of apprenticeship, The Disney Company tries and rotates you into more technical fields of the Parks or Specialty locations.

The field service position may include work from elevated platforms and confined spaces throughout the Walt Disney World Parks and Resort property.

[Additional Information about the WDW program](https://www.etacfl.org/programs/walt-disney-world-joint-apprenticeship-program)
