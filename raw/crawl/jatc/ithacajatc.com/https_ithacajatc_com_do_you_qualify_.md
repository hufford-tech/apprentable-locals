Do You Qualify?

To meet minimum qualifications for apprenticeship, an applicant must:

a. be a at least 18 years of age.

b. be a high school graduate or have a GED.

c. have completed and passed one year of basic algebra

d. possess a valid drivers license.

e. receive a qualifying score on the aptitude test

Prior to being accepted into the program, individuals selected from the pool of interviewed applicants will be required to provide results of a physical examination and a drug/alcohol test as decided by the IEJATC.

APPLICATION PROCESS

The Ithaca Electrical JATC conducts interviews twice yearly - in January and in June. The deadline for submitting required documents is December 1st for an opportunity for a January interview; May 1st for an opportunity for a June interview.

If you are interested in applying to the apprenticeship program, fill out an online application ( [forms page](https://ithacajatc.com/forms/)).

After submitting your application form, you are also required to submit the following documentation via email to [TrainingDirector@IthacaJATC.com](mailto:TrainingDirector@IthacaJATC.com). These documents may also be sent via U.S. mail or dropped off in person at 134 Cecil A. Malone Drive, Ithaca, NY 14850.

- Recent resume
- Three personal letters of reference. These letters should be from people who can speak to your character and ability to succeed in the apprenticeship program

After you are offered a position in our electrical apprenticeship program, you will need to submit the following documentation prior to being indentured into the program. You should have these documents readily available to avoid a delay in entering the program.

- High School and College transcripts; these should be official transcripts and may be emailed/sent directly from the institution
- Copy of your GED, if applicable
- Any other documentation for certifications or training tfor which you were are given credit during the interview process
- If you are a U.S. Military veteran, you will need to submit a copy of your DD214 and register with the Helmets to Hardhats program at [https://helmetstohardhats.org](https://helmetstohardhats.org/)

When you have completed all the steps listed, and if you meet the minimum requirements, you will be scheduled to take an aptitude test at our Training Center.
