## ABOUT US

Electricity powers the world. As a Lima Electrical JATC apprentice, you will learn the skills you need to build and maintain the complex electrical systems that power refineries, chemical plants, manufacturing plants, industries, hospitals, schools, data centers, commercial buildings and more. The Electrical Training Alliance was created over 70 years ago and has developed into what perhaps is the largest apprenticeship and training program of its kind. As an electrician apprentice, you will work and learn on actual job sites and earn a paycheck and benefits from day one. Best of all, this training is provided at NO COST to you!
