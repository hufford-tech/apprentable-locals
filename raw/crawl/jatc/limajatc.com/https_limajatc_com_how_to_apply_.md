# HOW TO APPLY

**Applications are taken by appointment. Please call 419-229-2775 to schedule an appointment.**

**You may also fill out the [online application](https://limajatc.com/online-application/).**

The following are the minimum requirements to apply for apprenticeship with the Lima Area Electrical Joint Apprenticeship committee:

1. You must be at least 17 years of age to apply to the program and at least 18 years of age before July 1st.
2. You must be a high school graduate or have obtained a GED certificate from an accredited institution.
3. You must live in one of the following counties:

- Allen
- Auglaize
- Hardin
- Logan
- Mercer
- Shelby
- Van Wert
- Wyandot (western half)

In-person applications must be completed at the Lima Area Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee office.  **Applications are not permitted to leave the building.**
