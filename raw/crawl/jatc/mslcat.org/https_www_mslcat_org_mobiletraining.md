## MOBILE TRAINING

As the Country’s population increases, so does the demand for power. Increase in Green Energy puts a tremendous load on our aging infrastructure. The demand for power is greater now than it ever has been and increasing every day.

​

This increase in power delivery means more Apprentices, more Lineman, more work and unfortunately, more accidents. Statistics show that most accidents can be prevented, this prevention is a combination of strong Safety Culture and Training. A strong Safety Culture and Training are our goals for our industry to help eliminate some of these human-related accidents.

​

Mountain States JATC recognizes the need to offer high-level training to any IBEW member in the Mountain States Region. We have a strong vision to make a difference in work methods and safety culture. We strive to provide the best training in the industry using proper work methods while following all OSHA standards.

​

Please take the time to review the current course options but not limited to. If you see a need for a particular type of training not listed or want to vary the length of a current course, please contact us.

​

These courses are offered to ANY IBEW MEMBER, Contractors, Utilities, COOP’s, Municipalities, THIS IS FOR YOU! We have 3 state of the art training centers to offer indoor and outdoor training courses, Helena, Montana, Tooele, Utah and Lochbuie, Colorado. At this time, we can only offer the Transmission courses at our Helena facility. OR let us come to you! We are completely mobile, offering most of our courses at your location, whether it be at your training yard or at on one of your projects.

​

Please help us ELIMINATE accidents in our Industry, we want to hear from you, help us build a strong Safety Culture with the highest level of Training offered in the industry.

Below are some tasks that could be done in the field but are not limited to.

​

Distribution Hotstick

20-40 hrs

Course Description: learn hotstick techniques on various structures, tie conductor in with hot sticks and aluminum ties, change out cross arm with hot stick, energized dead end insulator change out, cut in flying bell, wire splicing

These are some of the basic hot stick techniques but we are not limited to only training with these techniques. Willing to accommodate any other task that would be needed, please inquire.

Temporary Protective Grounding (EPZ)

4-8 hrs

Course Description: stay up to date on all of the OSHA regulations pertaining to EPZ grounding, classroom EPZ presentation with tabletop simulator (4 hrs) go outside in the afternoon and demonstrate EPZ scenarios (8 hrs)

Hotstick Care, Maintenance and Testing, Ground Set Testing

3 hrs

Course Description: discuss how hotsticks are made and how to maintain and test them, discuss what portions of hotsticks are insulated and what portions are not insulated, discuss ground set testing, things to look for and the importance of maintaining the ground set

Transmission Hotstick

20-40 hrs

Course Description: go over how to calculate wire weight and tensions, the importance of knowing the weights and tensions of what you are picking, insulator change out on suspension string, dead end string and running angle, cross arm change out on H frame, change out hot structure if time and equipment allows

These are common tasks for live line or hot stick transmission work but are not limited to. Willing to accommodate any other task that would be needed, please inquire.

​

Rubber Glove Work Methods

8 hrs

Course Description: use proper techniques for rubber glove work

Fall Protection

8 hrs

Course Description: stay up to date on all of the OSHA regulations on fall protection for ladder work, baker board work, leaving the pole on H frame and tower work

Tower Rescues

Level 1: Ground Based Rescue

16 hrs

Introduction

Course Description: This class will introduce the equipment and rigging methods used to rescue an  individual from a tower while rigging from the ground. This will include classroom instruction and hands-on ground-based training.

Level 2: Structure Based Rescue

8 hrs

Prerequisite: Level 1 Tower Rescue or demonstrated competency/trade experience

Course Description: This class will build on the skills of the rescuer being ground based to being on the structure. This will include some classroom training, but mostly hands-on tower-based training.

Level 3: High Risk Rescue

8 hrs

Prerequisite: Level 1 and 2 Tower Rescue or demonstrated competency/trade experience

Course Description: This class will build on the skills of the rescuer being ground/structure based to rescuing the victim by placing them in their system and lowering both to a safe position on the ground. This will include high angle rigging and rescue to avoid violating minimum approach.

​

Rope Access Barehand

80 hrs

Prerequisite​​​: Level 1, 2, and 3 Tower Rescue or demonstrated competency/trade experience

Course Description: This class will prepare the learners to utilize ropes and rigging to access and work energized lines. This is an extremely high level skill that requires competent and dedicated individuals to be trained to perform this type of work.

​

Bucket and Pole Top Rescue

8 hrs (dependent on number of participants)

Course Description: bucket and or pole top rescue

Confined Space Training and Rescue

8 hrs (dependent on number of participants)

Course Description: teach the proper way to prepare a confined space before entering, proper PPE, proper rescue equipment and rescue training
