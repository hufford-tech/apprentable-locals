# The first steps toward an electric career are only a few clicks away!

As a SOTX apprentice, you’ll GET PAID while you learn your craft in our five-year program. This type of opportunity isn’t gifted to just anybody. You’ll need to pass our entrance exam and will be required to pay a $28 application fee.

Please make sure you are familiar with the process before continuing to the application. The videos below will walk you through the application steps.

[Start Application](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/sotxjatc-org/login/accountCreate.php)

### Step One

Create a secure account [tradeschoolinc.com](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/sotxjatc-org/login/accountCreate.php).

![](https://www.sotxjatc.org/img/application-proces-1-no-vid.jpg)

### Step Two

Confirm email address. May take up to 24 hours.

### Step Three

Enter your new account information.

### Step Four

Login to complete payment and application form.

### Application

To start electrical training you must first complete and submit an application form. You can complete an application online or in person. Supporting documentation (see below) will be required in order to complete your application. Documents may be uploaded; however, original documents must verified in order to complete application.

[QUALIFICATIONS FOR APPRENTICESHIP](https://www.sotxjatc.org/assets/files/documents/Notice%20of%20Apprenticeship%20Opportunity%20-%20Technician.pdf)

**YOU MUST BE AT LEAST 17 YEARS OLD TO APPLY & 18 YEARS OF AGE TO PARTICIPATE IF SELECTED.**

Documents needed to verify the minimum requirements are, but not limited to:

1. **Valid** **driver’s license** or **state issued photo ID**
2. **Certified copy** of your **birth certificate** and/or **Social Security Card**
3. **Official copy of your high school transcript** showing successful completion of Algebra I
4. **Diploma, Transcript, or GED** showing High School graduation or equivalent
5. **Form DD214** (military veterans only)
6. **$28 application fee**, payable online
