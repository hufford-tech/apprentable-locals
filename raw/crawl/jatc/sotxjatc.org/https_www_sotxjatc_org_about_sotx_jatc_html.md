# About SOTX JATC

![SOTX Training Center](https://www.sotxjatc.org/assets/images/about-horizontal.png)

Since 1946 we have been the standard bearer of trade education excellence. A true “earn while you learn” program. Apprentices gain trade skills, knowledge, and licensure through both “On the Job Training” and structured classroom training, all while earning outstanding wages and excellent benefits.

**Electricians Apprenticeship:** A structured four year program, electrican apprentices attend class two days a month, September through June. In addition to related classes, students participate in on-the-job training (OJT). Each apprentice is assigned to, and employed by, a qualified electrical contractor. Through OJT, students will earn full time wages and benefits while also gaining valuable trade experience. Upon completion, apprentices will have a minimum of 8,000 of on the job experience, 720 classroom hours, and a State of Texas Electrical License. Student’s also receive college credit through A.C.E.

**Technologies Apprenticeship:** A structured three year program, technologies apprentices attend class Tuesday evenings, September through June. In addition to related classes, students participate in on-the-job training (OJT). Each apprentice is assigned to, and employed by, a qualified technologies contractor. Through OJT, students will earn full time wages and benefits while also gaining valuable trade experience. Upon completion, apprentices will have a minimum of 6,000 of on the job experience, 528 classroom hours, and trained for BICSI Technician Certification. Student’s also receive college credit through A.C.E.

**Under the strict training standards of the Electrical Training ALLIANCE, the South Texas Electrical J.A.T.C. is committed to:**

■ Providing “earn-while-you-learn” electrical and technologies education & training

■ Recruiting and training the best qualified electrical and technologies workforce

■ Providing continuing education and upskill training for electricians and technicians

■ Maintaining progressive training facilities and educational resources

■ Providing on-the-job-training opportunities

■ Developing qualified subject matter experts and trade professionals

■ Labor and Management working together
