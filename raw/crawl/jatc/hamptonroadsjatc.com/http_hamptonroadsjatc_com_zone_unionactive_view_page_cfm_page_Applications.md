## **Apply to the Hampton Roads JATC Apprenticeship**



The Hampton Roads JATC accepts applications **from December 1 until April 3o**



👉 **Please read ALL information below before starting your application.**


Failure to follow instructions may result in delays or rejection.



* * *



## ⚠️ **Before You Begin**



**DO NOT register for this website unless:**



  - You have been **accepted into the apprenticeship program**, OR
  - You are currently working out of our union hall as a **Journeyman, CE, or CW**

❗ **Website registration is NOT the application.**

If you attempt to register as a member, your request will be **rejected**.

* * *

## 📌 **Applicant Responsibility**

It is your responsibility to:

  - Read and understand all instructions
  - Submit all required documents
  - Follow all directions exactly

The JATC will **not be responsible** for incomplete or incorrect applications.

📞 Questions (after reading everything):

**Phone:** 757-875-1744 (Mon–Fri, 8:30 AM–4:00 PM)

📧 **Email:**apprenticeship@ibew1340.com _(Subject: Application)_

* * *

## 📝 **How to Apply**

1. Complete the **online application**
2. Upload **ALL required documents**
3. Wait for application review and verification
4. You will be contacted to submit payment


      ⚠️ **Do NOT bring payment until notified**
5. Once complete, you will be scheduled for the next **aptitude test**

* * *

## 📂 **Required Documents**

All documents must be uploaded before your application can be submitted.

  - Valid **Driver’s License**

    _(Not required for Construction Wireman applicants)_
  - **Birth Certificate**
  - **Social Security Card**
  - **Transcripts (REQUIRED — not diplomas)**from:

    - High School
    - College
    - Technical Schools
  - **GED Applicants:** Must include **GED test scores**
  - **Driving Record** (dated within 30 days)
  - **DD-214** (if applicable – veterans only)
  - **Employment History / Resume**

* * *

## 💲 **Application Fee**

  - $25.00 (non-refundable)
  - Must be paid by:
    - Cashier’s Check **or**
    - Money Order
  - Payable to: **Hampton Roads JATC**

❌ No credit cards

❌ No debit cards

❌ No personal checks

* * *

## ✍️ **Important Entry Tip**

Use proper capitalization when entering your information:

  - John Doe
  - Hampton, Virginia
  - Colony Drive

* * *

## 🚫 **Incomplete Applications**

Your application **cannot be submitted** until:

  - All documents are uploaded
  - All fields are completed correctly
