# EARN WHILE YOU LEARN.

The Inside Wireman program is an apprenticeship program approved by the state of California that combines on-the-job and classroom training. This is a tution-free program that pays you while you work and provides you with the necessary education to have a rewarding career.

Related training – approximately 200 hours per year

Earn While You Learn

Length of Apprenticeship Program = 5 years

No cost or fees for Program/Training to you

[LEARN MORE ABOUT OUR PROGRAM](https://sjeti.org/applicants/)
