## Code of Excellence.

Our profession is uncompromising: there’s no room for error or mistakes. The Code of Excellence is a program designed to cultivate the best construction workers while showing customers and contractors that IBEW members perform the highest quality and quantity of work, use their skills and abilities at full capacity, and execute safe and productive practices. All IBEW members will be required to sign off on Code of Excellence agreements.

Watch the video for a detailed explanation of the Code of Excellence and its impact on the industry at large.
