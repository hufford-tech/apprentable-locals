# Officers and Staff

Our team is here to provide you the best training possible! We strive to make your educational experience and on the job training the most comprehensive and up to date in partnership with the National Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (NJATC), International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) and the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA). A joint venture indeed!  A team brought together by mutual interest in order to bring the best electrical training and education through a Registered DOL Apprenticeship program and offered to the construction industry throughout east central Illinois and west central Indiana.
