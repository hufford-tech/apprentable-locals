# Before You Apply

Below is some information that you need to know prior to applying for an apprenticeship.

1. We accept applications all year.
2. There is a $30.00 application fee.
3. In order to apply, you must:
1. Be a High School graduate or have a GED
2. Have completed a year of Algebra
3. Have a driver's license.
4. Residential applicants must contact the JATC Office.
5. Applicants applying after November 1st may not complete the application, testing, and interview processes in time to be eligible for a possible apprenticeship offer the next Spring.

Learn more about the [application requirements.](https://thejatc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/THEJATC-Application-Requirements-as-of-10-1-2025.pdf)
