# HOW TO APPLY

### Inside Wireman - Telecommunications Technician

For all areas, requirements are:​

- Must be 17 or older (18 at start of program)

- Be a graduate from high school or have a G.E.D.

- Must have passed one year of high school algebra or one semester of college algebra.

- Must have a valid driver's license


Here are the ways to apply for the Dubuque Electrical Apprenticeship

​​

- You can print a "Request for an Application Form" and a Q&A flyer at the links below.

- You can pick up a request form in person Monday through Friday, 8:30 AM - 4:00 PM at the IBEW Local 704 office located at 1610 Garfield Ave, Dubuque, IA 52001.

- Application request forms can also be mailed individually by contacting our Training Director, Greg Willging at [willginggr@nicc.edu](mailto:willginggr@nicc.edu)
