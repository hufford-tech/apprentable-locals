# Application Process

[Home](https://ibewlocal5jatc.org/) » [Become an Apprentice](https://ibewlocal5jatc.org/?page_id=26) » Application Process

The IBEW Local 5 Registered Apprenticeship Program is a five-year program that combines on-the-job training with classroom instruction from accredited professionals.

## ELECTRICIANS APPRENTICESHIP

IBEW Local Union #5, and the Western PA Chapter N.E.C.A

J.A.T.C

5 Hot Metal Street, Suite 100, Pittsburgh, PA, 15203

**APPRENTICESHIP OPPORTUNITIES for those who meet the following requirements**

**are hereby announced by the Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (JATC)**

**of the IBEW Local Union #5 and the Western PA Chapter of N.E.C.A.**

01. Must be at least 18 years of age.
02. Must be a high school graduate or hold a GED.
    - NOTE – High school seniors who are 17 or 18 years of age, may apply before graduation and take the entrance test. If you attain a qualifying score, you will not be eligible to interview until you are 18 years of age.
03. Must have received credit for the successful completion of at least one secondary or post secondary Algebra course.
04. Applicants must be willing to take any tests given by or on behalf of the JATC.
05. When an apprenticeship is offered, the applicant must submit to a physical and


    substance testing, and a test for color blindness. These tests will be administered by a doctor selected and paid for by the JATC.
06. Prior to selection for apprenticeship, with the applicant’s concent, the JATC shall undertake a background check of the applicant.  The cost of the background check will be paid by the JATC.
07. Must be a resident of one of the following counties in Pennsylvania for at least one year


    prior to application: Allegheny, Armstrong, Bedford, Blair, Butler, Cambria, Cameron,


    Centre, Clarion, Clearfield, Elk, Fayette, Fulton, Greene, Huntingdon, Indiana, Jefferson,


    McKean, Somerset, Venango, Washington, and Westmoreland.
08. All applicants must have a valid Pennsylvania Driver’s License for the entire time they


    are in the Apprenticeship Program.
09. There is a $25.00 Nonrefundable Processing Fee.
10. The recruitment, selection, employment, and training of apprentices during their


    apprenticeship shall be without discrimination due to race, color, religion, national


    origin, sex, or non-job-related disabilities. The sponsor shall take affirmative action to


    provide equal opportunity in the apprenticeship and will operate the apprenticeship


    program as required under Title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 30, and the


    Equal Employment Opportunity Regulations of the Commonwealth of Pennsylvania.
