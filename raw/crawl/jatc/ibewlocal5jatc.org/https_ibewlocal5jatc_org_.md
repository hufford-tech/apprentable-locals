## ABOUT US

The Western Pennsylvania Electrical Training Center serves as a training ground for the electricians of Local 5. The training center offers both apprenticeship training courses, as well as journeyman upgrade courses.

[Learn More](https://ibewlocal5jatc.org/about-us)

![Pittsburgh Electrical JATC Apprenticeship Program](https://ibewlocal5jatc.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/HomePage-Img2.png)

### CURRENT APPRENTICES

Apprentices go to work during the day, earning a livable wage and benefits, and attend night classes a few times a week through an earn while you learn model.

[Learn More](https://ibewlocal5jatc.org/current-students/)

![Pittsburgh Electrical JATC Apprenticeship Program](https://ibewlocal5jatc.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/HomePage-Img3.png)

### JOURNEYMEN

As our industry evolves, our workforce needs to do the same. That is why we provide training that allows journeymen to stay up-to-date on technologies.

[Learn More](https://ibewlocal5jatc.org/journeymen/)
