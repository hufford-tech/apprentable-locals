# Application Information

Application Information[CantonJATC](https://cantonjatc.org/author/cantonjatc/)2026-07-17T10:00:21-05:00

## **Application Process Update: Temporary Pause**

The Canton Electrical JATC is currently updating our application system to make the process smoother and faster for everyone.

**What you need to know:**

- **Temporary Pause:** We are temporarily pausing the intake of new applications while we transition.

- **Timeline:** We expect to take approximately **two months** to complete though this is tentative.

- **Stay Tuned:** This page will be updated as soon as the new-and-improved process is live!


We apologize for any inconvenience this may cause, but we are confident these major improvements will be worth the wait.

### **Want to be notified when applications reopen?**

Please fill out our [**NOTIFICATION FORM**](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSczfizNusNVDA4_x4N9NDO7mBS0_t_N2UGcMr_klH0aW27PXg/viewform?usp=publish-editor) with your name and email address. We will only use this information to alert you when the new application process is ready for you.

### Application Process Update

We’re still finalizing a few details of our updated application process, but we wanted to share where things stand.

**Good news: our minimum requirements have not changed.**

#### Minimum Requirements

To apply, you must:

Be **18 years of age** by July 1 of the current year

Be a **high school graduate** or have a **G.E.D.** by July 1 of the current year

Have completed **one full credit** of High School or College Algebra with a passing grade

Be capable of **passing a drug screen** if accepted

Provide **proof of a valid driver’s license**

**Pay the $45 application fee** to begin the application
