# [Continuing Education](https://www.statewidelea.org/continuing-education/ "Permanent Link: Continuing Education")

Please note: Power Limited Technician, NICET and CCNA prep classes may only be attended by IBEW members working for contractors signatory to the Minnesota Limited Energy agreement.

Continuing education classes can be attended by individuals not working for a signatory contractor for a fee.

Fees for IBEW members not working under the LEA agreement:

2 hour = $60

4 hour = $62.50

Fees for General public:

2 hour = $95

4 hour = $100

Individuals not employed by a contractor signatory to the Limited Energy collective bargaining agreement are not eligible to attend prep or certification classes. (PLT prep. NICET prep or CCNA certification prep)

All classes are scheduled for Zoom unless otherwise noted.

100 percent attendance is required to qualify for continuing education credit. Each day must be attended. Partial credit cannot be given.

Please bring your NEC Code Book to all classes.

**Click on the class title or the class on the calendar to register.**

**\*Class for July, August, and September will be posted once the NEC 2026 code updates have been adopted.\***

### Continuing Education

Our classes are approved by the [Minnesota Department of Electrical Licensing](http://www.doli.state.mn.us/CCLD/LicElectricalPersonal.asp) and qualify to meet the requirements for continuing education to maintain/renew the Power Limited Technician License.

Members and contractor employees signatory to the Minnesota Limited Energy Agreement are eligible to register for classes at a reduced cost.
