### Continuing Education

Our classes are approved by the [Minnesota Department of Electrical Licensing](http://www.doli.state.mn.us/CCLD/LicElectricalPersonal.asp) and qualify to meet the requirements for continuing education to maintain/renew the Power Limited Technician License.

Members and contractor employees signatory to the Minnesota Limited Energy Agreement are eligible to register for classes at a reduced cost.
