# Laid off? Here’s what to do.

### **Contact the JATC**

within 1 business day of being laid off by calling us at 763-497-0072 or emailing a copy of your Separation Notice to [office@mplsjatc.org](mailto:office@mplsjatc.org).

### **Sign the Out-of-Work Book**

on your next scheduled class day. See Lisa in the admin office to sign.

### **If another job is immediately available**,

we will let you know and would then send a referral to the 292 hall for you to pick up.

### **If another job is NOT immediately available**,

you will be placed on the Out-of-Work List according to your layoff date. Be available during JATC office hours of Monday through Friday, 8am to 4pm, for a potential call to return to work.

### If added to the Out-of-Work List, file for unemployment

at [https://www.uimn.org/](https://www.uimn.org/). When it asks if you’re a student or in school, say no. This question is referencing full-time college students, not apprentices. When it asks if you are looking for work, say yes. Being on the Out-of-Work List with us means that you are currently looking for work with one of our contractors once a call becomes available for you.

### If you would like to check your number on the Out-of-Work List,

contact Lisa at 763-307-8574 or [lkopitzke@mplsjatc.org](mailto:lkopitzke@mplsjatc.org). You can also check your number in-person at the front desk during school.
