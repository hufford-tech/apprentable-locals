# Contractors

**Manpower Requests**

Use the following form to request apprentice manpower. The completed form should be emailed to [office@mplsjatc.org](mailto:office@mplsjatc.org).

[![Manpower Request Form](https://mplsjatc.org/wp-content/plugins/document-gallery/src/assets/icons/pdf.png)Manpower Request Form](https://mplsjatc.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/Manpower-Request-Form-Fillable-7.1.21-rev2.pdf)

**Hiring Pre-Apprentices (Pre-Apprentice Hiring is Currently Frozen)**

The following packet details how to hire a Metro, Residential, or 12-County Pre-Apprentice.

[![2025 Pre-Apprentice Info for Contractors](https://mplsjatc.org/wp-content/plugins/document-gallery/src/assets/icons/pdf.png)2025 Pre-Apprentice Info for Contractors](https://mplsjatc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/05/2025-Pre-Apprentice-Info-for-Contractors.pdf)

**Evaluation Samples**

Evaluations are now digital and are sent out by email quarterly for apprentices and bi-monthly for pre-apprentices. They are also sent out upon layoff. Below are samples of the questions asked on evaluations.

[![Apprentice Evaluation](https://mplsjatc.org/wp-content/plugins/document-gallery/src/assets/icons/pdf.png)Apprentice Evaluation](https://mplsjatc.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/11/Apprentice-Eval-2019-FILLABLE.pdf)
