### What are applicant Info Sessions?

Info Sessions are held on the first Wednesday of each month at the JATC. Each session includes an informational overview of the apprenticeship program and application process.  Info Session attendees will have the opportunity to participate in a question-and-answer forum. Info Sessions last approximately 60 minutes.

You are welcome to bring a parent or other guest with you to the Info Session.  Please be sure to include the total number of people attending when scheduling.

### When are JATC Info Sessions held?

**Info Sessions** are conducted the **1st** **Wednesday** of each month at the JATC located at **13100 Frankfort Pkwy NE,** **St. Michael, MN, 55376.** You must pre-register to attend.

### How do I register to attend an Info Session?

Please email Sara Bode at [sbode@mplsjatc.org](mailto:sbode@mplsjatc.org) to register for the Info Session you would like to attend. Please include the following information in your request.

- Name
- Info Session date you would like to attend
- Total number of individuals attending in your party
- Phone Number

If an Info Session is full, you will be contacted with additional options. Call [763.307.8562](tel:7633078562) with any questions.
