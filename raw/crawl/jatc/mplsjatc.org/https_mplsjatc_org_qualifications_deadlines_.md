#### Application deadlines are outlined at the bottom of this page.

The FALL application period is June 16th – September 30th, 2026.  Applications **OPENED** by September 30th and COMPLETED WITHIN THE 30-DAY APPLICATION WINDOW will be eligible for the NOVEMBER 2026 GAN Aptitude Test.  Click APPLY ONLINE to begin your application after reviewing link – _**[Navigating the Application Process](https://mplsjatc.org/applicants/)**_.

**Minimum Requirements to Apply**

- Complete the on-line application and all requirements within 30 days of opening your application. Applications are accepted year-round.
- Be a minimum of 17 years of age to apply. You must be at least 18 years of age to be hired.
- Must possess a valid driver’s license.
- Be a high school graduate or have a GED Certificate. If you have a GED, you must provide a copy of the score report from the Department of Education.


\*If you have not yet graduated high school, you may submit an on-track letter from your advisor and provide a copy of your diploma after graduation. Official transcripts are not required. Copies of your documents must be uploaded directly through the GAN Personal Experience Form (PEF) portal.
- Show evidence of successful completion of one full credit of algebra (or higher math) with a passing grade of C or better; or one post high school algebra course with a passing grade of C or better (i.e. Continuing Education, College Course, Trade School Course, etc.); or provide documents confirming successful completion of the Online Tech Math Course through Electrical Training Alliance available at [www.electricaltrainingalliance.org](https://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/Learning/OnlineTechMath)

**Meeting the minimum application requirements is just the first step. Because the selection process for the Local 292 Apprenticeship is highly competitive, many applicants choose to improve their application score by gaining experience in the following key areas:**

- Electrical construction trade school
- Pre-apprentice electrical work experience
- Electrical construction Pre-apprentice certificate programs >125 hrs
- Electrical Training Alliance online [Interim Credentials course](https://www.skillsprep.org/login/index.php)
- Electrical Construction or IBEW letter(s) of recommendation
- Review [GAN Human Resources Aptitude Test study guide](http://ganhumanresources.com/study-guide-1)

### Application Deadlines

GAN Aptitude Testing is held four times per year at the JATC. To be eligible for a specific test date:

- Applications must be started by the OPEN BY date.
- Applications must be fully completed and verified by within the applicants 30-day application window.


The application process may take a week or longer to complete.  Give yourself plenty of time to successfully complete your application and for it to be verified. Please review the information on link – _**[Navigating the Application Process](https://mplsjatc.org/applicants/)** and h_ ave your required documents ready before starting your application.
