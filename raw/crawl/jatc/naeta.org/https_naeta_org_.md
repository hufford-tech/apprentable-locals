## Our Mission

Educate our students to become the most productive, highly-trained, and highly-skilled workforce in the Electrical Industry.

### Skill

We provide hands-on training and development of practical skills needed for electrical work.

### Knowledge

Our curriculum includes both classroom instruction and on-the-job training to build a strong foundation of electrical theory and practices.

### Attitude

Our training program aims to instill professionalism, safety consciousness, and a positive work ethic among trainees/students.

The North Alabama Electrical Training Alliance Apprenticeship Program consists of 4 years of classroom instruction and 8,000 hours of on-the-job training.

### Apprenticeship

As an apprentice, you will work year-round for electrical contractors and attend classroom sessions during the academic year. As you learn by doing, you are paid directly by the contractor with whom you are employed. While progressing through the training, you receive guaranteed promotion and pay raises.
