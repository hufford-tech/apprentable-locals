## About the     NJATC

The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) and National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) have devoted thousands of hours, untold human energy, and millions of dollars toward ensuring quality training for their apprentices and journeymen. This bilateral commitment is what brought about the founding of the NJATC in 1941.

Local programs affiliated with the NJATC have trained over 300,000 apprentices to journeyman status and have demonstrated the most cost-effective way to train qualified craft workers. Those in NJATC training programs are not a burden to the taxpayers because they earn while they are learning. Each year, participants in the NJATC programs pay more than six million dollars in taxes. This is truly a model program as it takes nothing from the taxpayers while training some of the most productive workers in the world, who thereby pay tremendous dollars to the country. Skill, knowledge, and attitude are the main focus in all of the NJATC’s training programs.
