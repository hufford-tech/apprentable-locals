## North Alabama Electrical Training Alliance

# Pay Rates

Apprentices are paid on a scale based on a Journeyman Wireman’s pay and depend on what year they are in the program.

[Call Us for More Info](tel:+12563839507)

## Benefits of Joining Our Training Alliance



#### Hands-On Training

Gain practical experience with real-world electrical projects under the guidance of experienced professionals.



#### Industry-Recognized Certification

Earn certifications that are respected and valued by employers across the electrical industry.



#### Competitive Pay

Receive a competitive wage while you learn, with incremental increases as you progress through the program.



#### Comprehensive Curriculum

Our curriculum covers all essential aspects of electrical work, ensuring you are well-prepared for any challenge.



#### Career Advancement Opportunities

Our program opens doors to advanced positions and specialized roles within the electrical field.



#### Supportive Learning Environment

Benefit from a community of mentors and peers who are committed to your success.



#### Flexible Learning Options

Balance your training with other commitments through our flexible scheduling options.



#### Strong Industry Connections

Leverage our partnerships with leading companies for job placements and networking opportunities.
