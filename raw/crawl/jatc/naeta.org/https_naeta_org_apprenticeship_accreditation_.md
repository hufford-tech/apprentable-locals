## NAETA's Accreditation and Educational Excellence

# Empowering Electricians Through Recognized Programs

Explore how our accredited programs and partnerships with leading educational bodies enhance your career in the electrical trade.

## Our Accreditation Partners

The North Alabama Electrical Training Alliance proudly collaborates with esteemed accreditation bodies to ensure our programs meet the highest standards. Our apprenticeship programs are evaluated by the American Council on Education (ACE), providing opportunities for college credit. We are also registered with the Department of Labor’s Board of Apprenticeship Training (BAT), adhering to their rigorous guidelines. Additionally, our programs are approved by the Veterans Affairs Department, offering educational benefits under various U.S.C. Chapters.

## Accreditation

### American Council on Education

The American Council on Education (ACE) has thoroughly evaluated our apprenticeship programs, recognizing their academic value. Participants in the Inside Apprenticeship Program can earn up to 60 semester credit hours, while the Outside Apprenticeship Program offers 25 semester credit hours. These credits can significantly advance your educational journey. For more information on applying for college credit, please contact us today!

### Board of Apprenticeship Training

### Department of Labor

The North Alabama Electrical Training Alliance Apprenticeship Program is officially registered with the Department of Labor’s Board of Apprenticeship Training (BAT). This registration ensures that our programs adhere to the highest standards of apprenticeship training, aligning with federal guidelines and regulations. By maintaining this registration, we provide our apprentices with a structured and recognized pathway to becoming skilled electricians, benefiting from a curriculum that meets national standards. Our registration with the Department of Labor signifies our commitment to quality education and training. It allows us to offer programs that are not only comprehensive but also compliant with industry requirements, ensuring our graduates are well-prepared for their careers in the electrical field.

## Veterans Educational Benefits

### Approval for Veterans Training

The North Alabama Electrical JATC is proud to be approved by the Veterans Affairs Department and the Alabama Department of Postsecondary Education. This approval allows us to offer instruction and job training under the provisions of 38 U.S.C. Chapters 30, 32, 36, and Chapter 1606. These chapters provide educational benefits to veterans, helping them transition into rewarding careers in the electrical industry. Our programs are designed to support veterans in gaining the skills and knowledge necessary to succeed in their new roles.

## Supporting Our Veterans

By participating in our programs, veterans can take advantage of educational benefits that cover tuition and training costs, making it easier to pursue a career in the electrical trade. Our commitment to supporting veterans is reflected in our comprehensive training programs that cater to their unique needs and career goals.

Are you interested in earning college credits through our apprenticeship programs? The North Alabama Electrical JATC offers opportunities for participants to apply for college-level credits, thanks to evaluations by the American Council on Education (ACE). To find out more about how you can benefit from these opportunities, we encourage you to reach out to us today. Our team is ready to assist you with the application process and provide all the information you need.
