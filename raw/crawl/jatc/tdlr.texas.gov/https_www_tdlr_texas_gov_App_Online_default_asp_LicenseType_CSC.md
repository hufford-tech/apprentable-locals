| **Notice:** If you experience difficulty and are unable to apply/renew online, you must mail an application/renewal form and fee to TDLR <br>Credit Cards currently accepted: **Visa, MasterCard, Discover, American Express**<br> **ADDITIONAL FORMS REQUIRED:**<br> **The online application/renewal does NOT include the required medical forms. Your license will NOT be issued until all applicable forms are submitted and approved. Please see the below link to the required forms:**<br> **[Combative Sports Forms and Publications](https://www.tdlr.texas.gov/sports/sportforms.htm#contestants)**<br>**1\. BOX-LIC-032-E which includes:**<br>**. Authorization to release medical records**<br>**. The comprehensive medical examination must also include proof that within      the last six months, the applicant has been tested for and is free of the      Hepatitis C virus antibody and the human immunodeficiency virus     (HIV)antibody, and that the applicant is not acutely or chronically infected      with the Hepatitis B virus by testing the Hepatitis B surface antigen.**<br>**. Proof of having passed an ophthalmologist medical examination (eye exam)      within the previous six months**<br>**. If you are 36 & older, you must submit a favorable EKG & EEG**<br>**2\. Contestants 17 years old (not yet 18 years old) must provide a notarized     Parent Consent Form Box-035-N-E.**<br>**3\. Drug Test Authorization Form BOX-034-N-E.**<br>*** * *** |
