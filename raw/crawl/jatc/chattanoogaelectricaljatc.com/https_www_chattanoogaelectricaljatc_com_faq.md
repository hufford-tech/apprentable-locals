# FAQS

## HOW DO I APPLY TO THE SCHOOL?

Click the [Applicants Tab](https://www.chattanoogaelectricaljatc.com/book-online) on our website and follow the steps to create your TradeSchool account and fill out your application.

## WHERE ARE YOU LOCATED?

We are located at 3924 Volunteer Drive Chattanooga, TN 37416.

We are next door to the IBEW 175.

## WHEN DO I SCHEDULE AN APTITUDE TEST?

After you have completed your application, we will send you an email containing the details of your aptitude test.

## WHAT IS ON THE APTITUDE TEST?

The aptitude test is half high school algebra and half reading comprehension.

## WHAT DO I NEED TO SCORE ON THE APTITUDE TEST?

You need to make a minimum score of a "4" to qualify for an interview.

## WHEN DO I FIND OUT IF I HAVE MADE IT INTO THE APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAM?

We send our acceptance emails in the spring.
