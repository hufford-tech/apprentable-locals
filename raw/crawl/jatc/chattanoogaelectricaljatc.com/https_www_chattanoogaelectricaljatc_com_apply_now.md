# HOW TO BECOME AN APPRENTICE

If you want more information about the school, schedule a free Orientation and Tour!

1\. Check to see if you are [Qualified](https://www.chattanoogaelectricaljatc.com/qualifications).

2\. Click this link to [request an account](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/chattanooga-jatc-com/login/index.php).

3\. You will receive a confirmation email within 48 business hours that your account has been accepted,

4\. Log in and complete the INSIDE application (under the "Applications" Tab). You will also need to upload:

- A valid drivers license.

- An official copy of your \*COMPLETED\* high school transcripts showing that you completed Algebra 1 or GED test scores. You must upload a scanned a PDF; screen shots and pictures of transcripts will NOT be accepted.
  - High School Diplomas ARE NOT accepted, you must have the Transcript that shows your completion date and a credit for Algebra 1.​

  - If you cannot scan a copy of your transcripts, they can be mailed to 3924 Volunteer Drive Chattanooga, TN 37416.

\*After we process your application, you will receive an email telling you when to come back to take the aptitude test\*

5\. Take the aptitude test. \*\*You must make a qualifying score of a '4' to be considered for the interview round.

6\. Complete an oral interview with the committee of the school.

7\. Wait to see if you have been accepted! \*\* All applicants will be informed by e-mail in the spring whether or not they have made it into the school.

​

\\* if you have already passed the Aptitude Test or have 4,000 hours of on-the-job training, please email [aqueen@chattjatc.com](mailto:aqueen@chattjatc.com)\*

​

If you are interested in going out to work as a Construction Wireman, please email [Jobs@chattjatc.com](mailto:Jobs@chattjatc.com).
