# APPRENTICESHIP BOOK STORE

We are working hard with Mayer Electric to provide easy access to tools for our apprentices. We currently offer two tool bundles and individual tools. After you purchase, you can come pick up your new tools at the Apprenticeship School.

WE DO NOT SHIP ANY ITEMS.

​

During the month of August, Apprentices will be able to purchase their books from this website.

Please do not purchase these unless you are an indentured apprentice with Chattanooga Electrical Apprenticeship.

​

Apprentices, please bring proof of purchase to the school to redeem your books.
