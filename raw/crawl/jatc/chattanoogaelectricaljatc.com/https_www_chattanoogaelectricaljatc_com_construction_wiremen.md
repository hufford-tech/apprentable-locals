# CONSTRUCTION WIREMEN

## The Construction Wiremen (CW) Program is an alternative path to the traditional apprenticeship and is designed to clearly define and provide ways and means for growth and development that permits advancement to the next level within the program. This could ultimately lead to Journeyman Wireman status provided one completes the minimum course work and certifies on craft proficiency examinations (written and hands-on) examinations.  ​  Participating in the CW Program will not in any way prohibit you from being accepted into the Apprenticeship Program.   If you are interested in going out to work at as CW, please email Jobs@chattjatc.com.   Minimum wage for CWs is $15.00 per hour.  ​  Click [HERE](https://www.chattanoogaelectricaljatc.com/_files/ugd/d0bd00_7e9c6c410ee442b18f055e8fe11bbe2a.docx?dn=CW%20PROGRAM%20ADVANCEMENTS.docx)to see how to advance in this program.
