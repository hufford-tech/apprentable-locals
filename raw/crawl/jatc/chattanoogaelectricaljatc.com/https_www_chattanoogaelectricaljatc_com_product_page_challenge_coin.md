# Challenge Coin

```
The Chattanooga Electrical JATC’s first Challenge Coin! This coin features Ella, a service dog, with a 988 dog tag. 988 is the phone number for the suicide prevention hotline. It also features the phrase “it’s okay to not be okay”. We chose these two aspects of the coin to show our school’s determination to educate those about the mental health crisis currently plaguing the electrical construction industry and also to provide support to all who need it.
```
