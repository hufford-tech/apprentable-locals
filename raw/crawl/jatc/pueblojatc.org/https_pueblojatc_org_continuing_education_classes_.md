# Continuing Education Classes

**I.B.E.W. Members: $40.00 (online payment is $45.00) \| Nonmembers: $120.00 (online payment is $130.00)**

_**You must bring a 2023 NEC Code Book and a Calculator.**_

_**Must have 3 people to hold a class, maximum of 15. Members must provide their current dues receipt at time of registration, and everyone must provide your Colorado State electrical license.**_

**PAYMENTS**

**NO CASH OR PERSONAL CHECKS**

You must pre-pay seven days before the date of the class to be registered for a class, all payments are NONREFUNDABLE.

Credit and debit cards will be taken for payment by phone 719-561-8000 ext. 18 or in person at the JATC Monday through Friday 8:00-12:00 and 1:00-5:00pm.

**ATTENTION MEMBERS:** You can also register and pay online for our Continuing Education classes by [logging into TradeSchool](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v4/pueblojatc-org/login/index.php). Follow the instructions for creating an account. If you have taken classes with us before and we have your current email address you already have an account in TradeSchool. Simply enter your email address and reset your password and follow the instructions. If you have any trouble or questions, please call the office at 719-561-8000 ext. 18

Call the JATC at 719-561-8000 ext. 18 to register for all classes or send an e-mail to [dan@pueblojatc.org](mailto:dan@pueblojat.org) . Remember to include your name, phone number, and the class you want to register for.

# ABOUT IBEW

International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local #12 In Pueblo, Colorado has Labor Roots as far back as 1882 and was Chartered on February 24, 1900 to Further the plight of Average American Labor to find equal footing with the corporate capital world. Tradesman and Labor Workers have built this community and contributed to success of Southern Colorado and will strive to make this community better in the future.

# ABOUT NECA

NECA is the voice of the $130 billion electrical construction industry that brings power, light, and communication technology to buildings and communities across the U.S.

Today NECA contractors strive to be solution-providers for their customers, and their industry expertise benefits everyone working on an electrical construction project.
