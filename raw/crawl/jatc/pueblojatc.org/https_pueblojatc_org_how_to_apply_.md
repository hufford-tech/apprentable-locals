# How to Apply

After you submit all required documentation to **applications@pueblojatc.org** you will be contacted by email within 5-7 business days with instructions on completing the application online. Please send all your documents in one email.

Once you complete the application you will be scheduled for the mandatory aptitude test. The test will consist of basic algebra functions and reading comprehension. You will be emailed sample questions after completing the application online. If you think you need additional assistance with the aptitude test, an online prep course is available at [ElectricPrep.com](http://electricprep.com/).

### **Inside Wireman Program Required Documents:**

- Current valid driver’s license
- Birth certificate
- Social Security card
- High school diploma or G.E.D. (unless exempt)\*
- Official transcripts verifying one full credit of an algebra course (unless exempt)\*. GED scores do NOT meet this criteria. You may substitute SkillsPrep.org Online Learning System Tech Math course for an Algebra credit.
- Copy of DD214 (If you were in the military)
- If already completed, Electrical Training Alliance Interim Credential completion certificate, (contact the office, (719) 561-8000 extension 18, to learn more about this certificate)
- Once all of the required documents have been submitted to **applications@pueblojatc.org** and reviewed, you will receive an email with instructions on how to access the online application. If you have any questions or to check on the status of your documents, please call (719) 561-8000 extension 18.

# ABOUT IBEW

International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local #12 In Pueblo, Colorado has Labor Roots as far back as 1882 and was Chartered on February 24, 1900 to Further the plight of Average American Labor to find equal footing with the corporate capital world. Tradesman and Labor Workers have built this community and contributed to success of Southern Colorado and will strive to make this community better in the future.

# ABOUT NECA

NECA is the voice of the $130 billion electrical construction industry that brings power, light, and communication technology to buildings and communities across the U.S.

Today NECA contractors strive to be solution-providers for their customers, and their industry expertise benefits everyone working on an electrical construction project.
