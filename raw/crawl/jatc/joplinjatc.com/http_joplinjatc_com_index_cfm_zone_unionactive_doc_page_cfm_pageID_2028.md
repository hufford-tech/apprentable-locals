| The NJATC apprenticeship is a 5 year program in which you work a full time job, and attend school one night a week.  Interviews are done as apprentices are needed to fulfill the needs of our contractor partners.  Many of the questions you have can be answered by clicking the **FAQ** tab on the main menu bar. Below is a listing of the basic requirements needed to be eligible for an interview with our committee.  If you meet these requirements, gather the documentation listed, download and print the application at the bottom of the page, and submit it to the training director with the $20 application fee.  If you are selected for an interview, you will be notified by mail of the time and date.  Download and print the handout below as a guide through the interview and selection process. Good luck!<br>Basic Requirements:<br>1.  High school diploma or G.E.D.
