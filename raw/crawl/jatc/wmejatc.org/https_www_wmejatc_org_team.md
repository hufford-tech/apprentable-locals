## Our Team

![Screenshot 2025-12-17 at 2.59.36 PM.png](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/7e5ec3_a6ce877a47d44332b959f38d3556dc83~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_365,h_369,al_c,q_85,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/Screenshot%202025-12-17%20at%202_59_36%E2%80%AFPM.png)

### David Kitchen

Training Director

Dave started his electrical career in the Naval Nuclear Power Program (1988-1994). He became a licensed Master Electrician in 2000. In 2003, Dave completed the National Training Institute Instructor Program at the University of Tennessee. Dave is a Certified Red Cross 1st Aid/CPR Trainer, OSHA 10/30 Safety Trainer, NFPA 70E Trainer, and has taught all 5 years of the NJATC Curriculum.

![Screenshot 2025-12-17 at 2.59.36 PM.png](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/7e5ec3_a6ce877a47d44332b959f38d3556dc83~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_365,h_369,al_c,q_85,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/Screenshot%202025-12-17%20at%202_59_36%E2%80%AFPM.png)

### Ally Owen

Office Manager

Ally graduated from Davenport University with a Bachelor's degree in Business Administration. Her past experiences  in customer service, financial institutions, and as an AmeriCorps member are beneficial when working directly with the Training Director.

![Screenshot 2025-12-17 at 2.59.36 PM.png](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/7e5ec3_a6ce877a47d44332b959f38d3556dc83~mv2.png/v1/fill/w_365,h_369,al_c,q_85,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/Screenshot%202025-12-17%20at%202_59_36%E2%80%AFPM.png)

### Bryce Butler

Training Instructor

Bryce completed the program in 2016 and was our outstanding graduate. Since completing the program, he has worked as a Licensed Journeyman for 8 + years. He has been attending various trainings for over 7 years to prepare as an instructor. Bryce also served on the JATC Committee before taking on the Training Instructor position.
