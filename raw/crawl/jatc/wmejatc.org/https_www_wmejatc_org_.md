## Get Paid to do It

## At West Michigan JATC, you’ll earn while you learn—and graduate without student debt.

## Our 4-year apprenticeship program combines 180 hours of classroom instruction each year with hands-on training alongside experienced electricians. You’ll get paid from day one, with full healthcare and employer-paid retirement benefits.
