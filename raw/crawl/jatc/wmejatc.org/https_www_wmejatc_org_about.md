# About

The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) is the world’s largest electrical union, representing skilled professionals across the electrical and telecommunications industries. We stand for workers’ rights, safety, and opportunity on every job site.

The National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) represents union electrical contractors who are committed to delivering high-quality work—on time, on budget, and backed by a deep investment in their workforce.

Together, IBEW and NECA created a national gold standard for apprenticeship training: the Electrical Training Alliance (formerly the NJATC), established in 1941. This powerful partnership ensures every apprentice receives top-tier education and hands-on experience to meet industry demands and launch a rewarding career.

We don’t just power buildings—we power futures.

## Our Mission

To provide comprehensive training and education to the apprentices and journeymen of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local Union 275 and the National Electrical Contractors Association, West Michigan Division, Michigan Chapter—ensuring the electrical construction industry is supported by the most highly trained and skilled workforce available.

Together, IBEW and NECA created a national gold standard for apprenticeship training: the Electrical Training Alliance (formerly the NJATC), established in 1941. This powerful partnership ensures every apprentice receives top-tier education and hands-on experience to meet industry demands and launch a rewarding career.
