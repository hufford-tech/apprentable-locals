# Apply

# A quick Guide on how to

## Apply

Our four year apprenticeship begins with the application process.

- ### Step 1




You will need to create an account on our online application portal provided in the link at the bottom of the page. If you have any questions, you can contact the Apprenticeship office at 205-849-5522 or email [corey@bejatc.org](mailto:corey@bejatc.org).



At this point, you have a decision to make. Some people don’t want to go to school right now and just want to work. We have a path for that called the [CW/CE program](http://bejatc.org/#cw-ce-program). Those that want to pursue the [four-year registered apprenticeship program](https://bejatc.org/#cw-ce-program) have a few more steps to take.

- ### Step 2




Once you have completed your application and turned in all of the required documentation, you will be eligible to go to work with one of our union contractors and begin your on the job training.

- ### Step 3




Once all of your documents have been approved you will move into a test pool and your application status will change to **Need Test**, once this happens you will just need to wait on an email with a date and time for the aptitude test. We work down a list of eligible candidates by submission completion date. Once you receive the email you must respond to confirm your seat, if you do not respond within 72 hours your spot will be given to the next available candidate.

You can get practice exams at Electric Prep. Click the button to visit the website.



[ELECTRICAL PREP](https://www.electricprep.com/)

- ### Step 4




Once you have successfully completed the aptitude test, you will be scheduled to interview with the apprenticeship selection committee. These interviews normally occur in July and are the last step before you begin your formal classroom training. If this is the path you want to choose, scroll down to Enroll Today.


## Items needed to apply:

- A completed application
- High School Transcript or GED w/scores not a copy of your Diploma
- Valid Driver’s License
  - Must be a valid Alabama Driver’s License – not a state-issued ID
- Social Security Card

You will receive further information about your aptitude test date and time after completion of application and all documents have been verified.

## Basic Requirements

1. Must be at least 18 years of age.
2. Must be a high school graduate or have a GED.
3. Minimum math – must have completed one full year of high school algebra with a passing grade or one post high school algebra course with a passing grade.
4. Must have qualifying score on the NJATC aptitude test.
5. Must provide official transcript or transcripts for high school and post high school education showing courses and grades.
6. Must have and maintain a valid driver’s license.
7. Must pass a drug screen test.

_All applications will be received without regard to race, color, religion, national origin, or sex._
