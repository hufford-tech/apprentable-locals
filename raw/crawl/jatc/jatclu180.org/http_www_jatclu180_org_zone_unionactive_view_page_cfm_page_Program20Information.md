| **[Applicant Qualifications](http://www.jatclu180.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_article.cfm&HomeID=97227&page=Program20Information)** |
| |     |
| --- |
|  | |
| **INSIDE/Commercial Wireman** ~ 5 Year Program<br>At time of application: <br>**MINIMUM REQUIREMENTS ~ MUST PROVIDE PROOF:**<br>• At least 18 Years of Age (CA Drivers License, Birth Certificate or other Proof of Age)<br>• High School Graduation or G.E.D. (Valid Certificate or Diploma)<br>• Photo ID (ex: Valid-CA Drivers License)<br>• Proof of Algebra - Sealed (unopened) Official Transcript with **_One FullYear_**(2 semesters) of High School Algebra with passing grade = “C” or better. Pre-Algebra does NOT count. <br>• All Information MUST be presented in English when filing the application.<br>_For any interpretation of foreign educational documents use available link: Academic & Prof. Intl. Eval._<br>**QUALIFYING EXAMS:**<br>• Applicants meeting minimum requirements and completing application will be notified and scheduled to take a 2-part aptitude examination, consisting of Reading Comprehension and Mathematics<br>• Applicants who pass the aptitude test will be notified and then scheduled for an ORAL INTERVIEW<br>• The combination score (written and oral interview) will place the applicant in the pool of eligible candidates<br>**APPLICATIONS:**<br>• Applications must be made _**in person**_ and _**at our facility**_.<br>• Only individuals present at application process with _**all required documents**_ will be allowed to apply. |

| **Instructions for the Sample Test**<br>**As part of the selection process, you may be required to take an aptitude test battery designed to determine whether you possess the abilities that will help you succeed within the electrical construction industry.**<br>**The sample questions provided are similar to those on the actual tests, allowing you to know what to expect on the day of your test session.**<br>**You should read the sample questions on each test carefully and then examine each of the responses. Only one answer is correct for each question. Choose the response that you think is correct. Check your answers with the provided sample test answer key to see if you responded correctly.**<br>**If you find some of the sample items to be difficult for you, you may want to review material that is similar to the sample items.**<br>**Download:**<br>[![](https://server5.unionactive.com:8484/icons/filetypes/pdf.gif) Sample Reading Comprehension.pdf](http://www.jatclu180.org/docs/Sample%20Reading%20Comprehension.pdf)<br>[![](https://server5.unionactive.com:8484/icons/filetypes/pdf.gif) Sample Test Answer Key.pdf](http://www.jatclu180.org/docs/Sample%20Test%20Answer%20Key.pdf)<br>[![](https://server5.unionactive.com:8484/icons/filetypes/pdf.gif) Sample Algebra and Functions.pdf](http://www.jatclu180.org/docs/Sample%20Algebra%20and%20Functions.pdf) |
