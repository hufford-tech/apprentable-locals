| What is the JATC

|     |
| --- |
|  |

**Solano-Napa Counties**

**Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee**

**Electrical Apprenticeship**

We are a jointly sponsored organization of the IBEW (International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers)

and NECA (National Electrical Contractor Association). Our formed JATC (Joint Apprenticeship Training

Committee) responsibility is to recruit, train and educate individuals to meet the ever increasing needs of

electrical industries in Solano and Napa Counties.

At our facility located in Napa County, we train apprentices for Industrial-Commercial Inside Apprenticehship 5 Year Program. The Solano-Napa JATC is California State Certified and United States Federal Certified Training Falicity.

We offer technical upgrade classes and extended education classes for apprentices, journeymen and contractors. These classes will allow individual participants to keep current on the most progressive technology for our future electrical needs.

Our building is equipped with individual classrooms, professional computer lab, complete hands-on

shop area, conference rooms and large upper level meeting hall, all for the benefit of keeping everyone well informed and up to date.

Driving Instructions to our location:

720 Technology Way Napa, CA 94558
