# IBEW Local 743 TRAINING

# THANK YOU FOR YOUR INTEREST IN IBEW 743 JATC APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAM.

## Application Intake Now Closed

Once your **application and transcript** are received, you will be notified of your **aptitude test date**.

### Transcript Requirements

To qualify for the aptitude test, your **high school, GED, or college transcript** must show a **passing grade in Algebra**.

- Transcripts may be **uploaded directly** during the application process
- If you do not have your transcript, you may request your school send it directly to us

**Email:** jenng@ibew743.org

**Mail:**

IBEW LU743 Reading Joint Apprenticeship

20 Morgan Drive

Reading, PA 19608

**Both the application and transcript are required**. Applications without a qualifying transcript will not be processed.

* * *

## Before You Apply

You must live within **IBEW Local 743’s jurisdiction** to be considered.

- Click **“About”** at the top of this page
- Select **Jurisdiction Map**
- Enter your home address and confirm it falls within the shaded area

* * *

## How to Apply

1. Click **Apply Now**
2. Review the pre-requisites
3. Click **Submit Application Online at TradeSchool**
4. Complete and submit your application

You will receive an **automatic confirmation email** once submitted.

**Important:**

- You cannot submit your application without uploading your transcript.
- If your transcript is sent directly to the apprentice office, it will be added to your account once received, and you will be notified that your application is complete.

* * *

### Reminder

An **official transcript** showing a passing grade in Algebra is required.

If the transcript is not received, your application will be **archived and not processed**.
