# Journeymen Upgrade

![IBEW 743 JATC - Apprenticeship Program](https://ibew743.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/09/IMG_0871-copy.jpg)

Please register for the following OSHA 10 / 30 classes by emailing Ed Bernitsky at [edb@ibew743.org](https://ibew743.org/journeymen-upgrade/edb@ibew743.org) or call 610-777-8388.  Both classes will be held at our Training Center at Local Union 743.

- _**OSHA – Classes will be scheduled throughout the year.  If interested please contact Ed or register in TradeSchool if you currently have an account. 10 Hour Construction Outreach Class** **will continue**_ _**for those who are interested for an additional 20 hours to obtain an OSHA 30 card**_

In addition to providing quality apprenticeship training, IBEW 743/NECA Electrical Training Alliance has a  commitment to providing upgrade training to keep the Journeyman-level workforce as up-to-date as possible, while using the newest technologies. The IBEW 743/NECA Electrical Training Alliance works directly with equipment manufacturers and technology developers of a variety of tools, equipment and supplies, to search for the most up-to-date information available. After identifying a new training need, the IBEW 743/NECA Electrical Training Alliance designs an appropriate training course and offers this training to their membership.
