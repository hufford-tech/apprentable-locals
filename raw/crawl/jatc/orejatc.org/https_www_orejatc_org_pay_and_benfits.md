# pay and benefits

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/11062b_407e13c69b8041af88725714590d991af000.jpg/v1/fill/w_589,h_124,al_c,q_80,usm_0.33_1.00_0.00,enc_avif,quality_auto/11062b_407e13c69b8041af88725714590d991af000.jpg)

                    CLA Wage Rate         Journeyman Wireman                          $33.00

​

Apprentice Rates

1st  Period                         0-1000 hours                       55% of JW                        $18.15

2nd Period                        1000-2000 hours                 60% of JW                        $19.80

3rd Period                         2000-3500 hours                 68% of JW                       $22.45

4th Period                         3500-5000 hours                 76% of JW                       $25.10

5th Period                         5000-6500 hours                 83% of JW                       $27.40

6th Period                         6500-8000 hours                 93% of JW                       $30.70

​

​

​

Fringe Benefits

Health & Welfare (Medical, Dental, Vision etc)        $8.70/hour             100% paid by contractor

Retirement Fund  (begins at hour 1)                           $10.07/hour           100% paid by contractor

NEBF Pension                                                                       3% of Gross

Training  - JATC                                                                    2% of Gross

CALM Program                                                                     $0.04/hour

​

​

All of the 270 Apprentices receive full family coverage

              insurance as part of the negotiated package.

     This benefit kicks in once the apprentice has worked

      140 hours per month for 2 consecutive months.

​

                     All of the 270 Apprentices receive retirement benefits

with the first hour they work.

So, from day one on the job, their contractor will contribute $8.97 per hour which does not come out of your  hourly rate.
