# Path to  Apprenticeship

Path toApprenticeshipPath toApprenticeshipPath toApprenticeship

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/11062b_407e13c69b8041af88725714590d991af000.jpg/v1/fill/w_589,h_239,al_c,q_80,usm_0.33_1.00_0.00,enc_avif,quality_auto/11062b_407e13c69b8041af88725714590d991af000.jpg)

1\. Click this link to request an account:

You will be sent an email link to verify your email.

     If you do not complete that step, we are not able to create you an account.

Previous applicants will just need to reset your password

2\. You will receive a confirmation email when your account has been accepted.

This could take up to 48 business hours.

3\. Log in and complete the "Inside" application.

​    You are allowed 30 days to submit a complete application.

​

4\. UPLOAD all required documents: ​

- Resume as a PDF

- Official High School transcripts (not your diploma) or GED scores as a PDF

- Proof of one credit in algebra - this should be on your transcripts

- Valid Driver's License

- If you are a Veteran, you can upload your DD214 as a PDF

- If you have industrial/commercial experience - upload paystubs and/or notarized letter as a PDF


​

5\. Take the Aptitude Test

​

Aptitude tests are scheduled once we have a group ready to test. Once you are scheduled, you will receive

     an email with your scheduled date and time. It is imperative that you attend your scheduled session or call the

     office to make other arrangements.

​

6.  Oral Interview with the JATC committee

​

Applicants who receive a qualifying score of 5 or higher will receive

       an email with their interview date and time.

       Your score on the interview will place you on our ranked list for possible selection for 12 months.

Please check your account often. We communicate by email. There is a message tab on your online account that will also show your messages. It is the applicants responsibility to make sure their application is complete.

You have 30 days to submit a completed application from the date you start it.

​

Uploaded documents must be in PDF format.

​

If you have previously applied, you should have an account--you will just reset your password.

​

If you have industrial/commercial electrical experience, you can email [apply.orejatc@gmail.com](mailto:apply.orejatc@gmail.com)

to see if you qualify to not take the aptitude test.

​

If you have taken the aptitude test within the last 2 years, please email [apply.orejatc@gmail.com](mailto:apply.orejatc@gmail.com)

and we will retrieve those scores.

​

If you do not show for a scheduled aptitude test, you will not be rescheduled to take it again for 3 months

and will have to reapply to the program.

​

If you do not show for a scheduled interview, you will not be rescheduled for an

interview until 6 months past your recent application.

​

If you have any questions, please reach out by email [apply.orejatc@gmail.com](mailto:apply.orejatc@gmail.com) or call the office (865)483-9955.
