# Frequently Asked Questions

Please select your question category from our most frequently asked questions.

#### OJT - Record Book (Blue Book)

1Who do I contact if I have questions about my OJT Record Book or need a report of my employer hours?

Please email info@norcal-jatc.com.

2When should I submit my OJT Record Book?

You must submit your OJT Record Book every time you reach 1,000 work hours. Your book must be signed by your job supervisor.

3How can I submit my OJT Record Book?

You can submit your completed OJT Record Book to our office at: 3033 Alvarado St., San Leandro, CA 94577

You may:

- Mail it to our office.
- Submit it in person during office hours (8am-4pm, Monday - Friday).
- Submit it in person after office hours by dropping it in the silver mailbox located in front of the building.

* * *

#### Apprentice Classes

1What should I do if I cannot attend my scheduled class?

Use the [Contact Us Form](https://www.norcal-jatc.com/contact-us) as soon as possible to explain your situation and request rescheduling (based on availability).

Please Note: Documentation is required for any absence to be considered EXCUSED.

- Absence due to illness – Please provide a doctor’s note or positive COVID-19 test result.
- Absence due to accident – Please provide a tow receipt, police report, or similar documentation.
- Absence due to travel plans – Please provide an Airline ticket or hotel booking confirmation.
- Absence due to Wedding or Funeral – Please provide documentation showing service dates.
- Absence due to childcare issues – Please provide a letter from your child’s daycare or a signed handwritten note from the caretaker stating the dates care was unavailable.

2Who do I contact with questions about my travel per diem check?

Please email info@norcal-jatc.com.

* * *

#### VDV State Certification Application

1How can I request a copy of my DAS-1 Form and DIR Letter?

Please email info@norcal-jatc.com.

2Who should I contact with questions about the VDV State Certification Application?

Please contact the Electrical Certification Unit at (510) 286-3900.

#### Medical or Dental Benefits

1Who do I contact for questions about my medical or dental benefits?

Please contact UAS at (408) 288-4400.

#### Withdrawing from the Apprenticeship Program

1What should I do if I no longer wish to be an Apprentice?

If you decide to resign from the program, you must contact the Training Coordinator and submit a written resignation letter. Your resignation is subject to review and approval by the Sound and Communication Sub-JATC Committee.

#### About Us

The Northern California Sound and Communication Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee helps to provide a highly trained and skilled workforce for apprentices in the low voltage electrical industry in Northern California. It also provides ongoing instruction for Installer/Technicians.
