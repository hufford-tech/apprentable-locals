# NorCal S&C JATC Career Opportunities

The Northern California Sound and Communication Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (NorCal S&C JATC) is always seeking skilled professionals to help strengthen our training program.

If you are an experienced Sound and Communication Installer/Technician and are interested in joining our team as a **part-time or full-time** **instructor**, we invite you to apply. Please review the qualifications below, then submit both your resume and a cover letter. Once received, we will review your information and send you a confirmation email. Qualified

resumes will be kept on file for future opportunities.

**Important:** If you are interested in becoming anapprentice, please apply directly through our [online application portal](https://www.norcal-jatc.com/applicants/apply-online-2/).

### Minimum Qualifications

- Comprehensive experience in the Sound & Communication VDV Industry
- Excellent communication skills, both verbal and written
- Above average ability to use a computer in daily operations
- Ability to multi-task
- Willingness to travel throughout the region to serve the needs of the membership
