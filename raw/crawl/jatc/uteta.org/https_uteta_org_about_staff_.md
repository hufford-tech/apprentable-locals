## Board of Trustees

The Utah Electrical Training Alliance is led by a Board of Trustees consisting of four NECA representatives and four IBEW representatives. The Committee meets monthly to oversee financial matters, review apprentice disciplinary issues, and provide strategic direction. Together, they ensure the UETA JATC apprenticeship program upholds the highest standards of excellence within the electrical industry.
