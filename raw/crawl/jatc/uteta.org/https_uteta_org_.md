## Apprenticeship Programs

Join more than 800 apprentices who are building their futures through the Utah Electrical Training Alliance (UETA). We offer two specialized career pathways that allow students to focus on the area of the electrical industry that best matches their interests and career goals. Our comprehensive training programs provide the education, hands-on experience, and industry support needed for long-term success.

### Inside Wireman

Plans, lays out, modifies, repairs, tests, and installs electric wiring, equipment, and apparatus for power, light, communications, and control systems in residential and commercial buildings and industrial plants; uses electrician’s hand tools, various electrical testing devices, and soldering and welding equipment.

### Telecommunication

While the Inside Wireman is installing the conduit and power feeders on a project, the Installer Technician is working beside the Wireman, installing the network of low voltage cabling that is used for video, voice and data or other low voltage signaling.
