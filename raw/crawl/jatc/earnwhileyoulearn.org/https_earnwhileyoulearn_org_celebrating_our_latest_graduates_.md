The Electrical JATC of Southern Nevada proudly celebrated its 2025 graduation ceremony, honoring the newest class of Inside Wireman and Installer Technician apprentices. The event marked the successful completion of a rigorous multi-year program blending classroom education with real-world jobsite experience. Graduates were recognized for their hard work, determination, and commitment to the electrical trade. Family members, instructors, union leaders, and industry partners joined in celebrating this important milestone. From mastering electrical codes and blueprint reading to gaining hands-on experience with renewable energy systems and data center infrastructure, these graduates are prepared to lead in Nevada’s growing electrical workforce.
