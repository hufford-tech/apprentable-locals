# Forge Your Electrical Future

#### Become a Skilled Electrician with EJATC

EJATC has been training top-notch electricians since 1947. Join our apprenticeship program and start your career today.

[Learn More](https://earnwhileyoulearn.org/about-us/)

![](https://earnwhileyoulearn.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/DSF8431-1-scaled-1-1024x459.webp)

**Welcome to the Electrical Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee of Southern Nevada (EJATC of Southern Nevada),** where your journey to becoming a skilled electrician begins.

Our comprehensive apprenticeship programs combine on-the-job training with classroom instruction, allowing you to earn while you learn. Join us today and take the first step toward a successful career in the electrical industry.

### Apprenticeship Programs

Our apprenticeship programs offer in-depth training in various specializations, ensuring you gain the skills needed to excel in your career. From Inside Wireman to Installer Technician, our programs are designed to provide you with hands-on experience and theoretical knowledge.

Learn more about the opportunities awaiting you.

[Application Information](https://earnwhileyoulearn.org/apprenticeship-and-application/)

## Empowered by the Apprenticeship

Hear from our apprentices, graduates, and instructors about how our programs shape confident, skilled professionals through high-quality training and hands-on experience—transforming lives, careers, and futures in the electrical field.

You grow as a person while building a strong future for your family. This program offers more than a career—it offers a lifestyle, a community, and a path to becoming the best in the trade while growing into a well-rounded individual.”
