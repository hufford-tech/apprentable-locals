# Discover Your Future in Electrics: ETISN Open House and Career Fair

August 14, 2024 \- August 17, 2024

![](https://earnwhileyoulearn.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/b1.jpg)

### Event Overview:

ETISN invites prospective students, parents, and industry professionals to an exciting Open House and Career Fair. This event will showcase the Electrical Training Institute of Southern Nevada’s top-notch apprenticeship program and provide valuable insights into the thriving electrical industry.

### Event Highlights:

- **Campus Tours:** Explore state-of-the-art training facilities and classrooms.
- **Meet the Instructors:** Connect with experienced electrical professionals who are passionate about teaching.
- **Industry Showcase:** Network with local electrical contractors and learn about career opportunities.
- **Live Demonstrations:** Witness electrifying demonstrations of electrical skills and techniques.
- **Informational Sessions:** Attend workshops on financial aid, career paths, and apprenticeship details.
- **Student Testimonials:** Hear firsthand accounts from current apprentices about their experiences.
