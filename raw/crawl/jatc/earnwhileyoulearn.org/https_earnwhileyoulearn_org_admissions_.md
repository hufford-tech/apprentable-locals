### Member and Apprentice Classes

The Electrical JATC of Southern Nevada offers a wide range of specialized classes exclusively for our members and apprentices. These courses are designed to strengthen your skills, expand your knowledge, and keep you up to date with the latest technologies, codes, and industry standards.

Whether you’re currently an apprentice building your foundation or a journeyman looking to grow in a specific area of the trade, our continuing education opportunities are here to support your development.

Our training center is committed to supporting lifelong learning and career growth. Whether you’re preparing for new responsibilities on the job, seeking certifications, or simply staying sharp in your trade, the Electrical JATC is here to help you succeed every step of the way.
