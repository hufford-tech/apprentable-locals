# Apprenticeship and Application

## Apprenticeship Overview

At the Electrical Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee of Southern Nevada (EJATC of Southern Nevada), we offer comprehensive apprenticeship programs to provide you with the skills and knowledge needed for a successful career in the electrical industry.

###### Our programs include:

### Inside Wireman

- **Requirements:** Applicants must be at least 18 years old, possess a high school diploma or GED, hold a valid driver’s license and social security number, and successfully complete both an aptitude test and an interview.
- **What You Will Learn:** You’ll learn the fundamentals of electricity, wiring methods, electrical code, and jobsite safety. Training includes conduit bending, blueprint reading, motor controls, and advanced systems like fire alarms and solar. .
- **Duration:** Apprentices must complete 8,000 hours of on-the-job training and successfully fulfill all related instructional requirements, including a minimum of 720 hours of classroom instruction.
- **Benefits:** Our apprenticeship program allows you to earn while you learn. You’ll receive competitive wages and benefits while gaining hands-on experience in the electrical trade.
- **Career Paths:** After graduation, you’ll join the workforce as a highly skilled professionals with opportunities to work for IBEW-NECA contractors both locally and nationwide. You can advance into roles such as foreman or project manager, become an instructor, or even start your own business.

### Installer Technician

- **Requirements:** Applicants must be at least 18 years old, possess a high school diploma or GED, hold a valid driver’s license and social security number, and successfully complete both an aptitude test and an interview.
- **What You Will Learn:** You’ll learn how to install, test, and maintain technology systems like voice, data, video, fire alarms, and security. Training covers cabling, system integration, safety, and industry standards.
- **Duration:** Apprentices must complete 6,000 hours of on-the-job training and successfully fulfill all related instructional requirements, including a minimum of 640 hours of classroom instruction.
- **Benefits:** Our apprenticeship program allows you to earn while you learn. You’ll receive competitive wages and benefits while gaining hands-on experience in the electrical trade.
- **Career Paths:** After graduation, you’ll join the workforce as a highly skilled professionals with opportunities to work for IBEW-NECA contractors both locally and nationwide. You can advance into roles such as foreman or project manager, become an instructor, or even start your own business.

## Steps for Successful Apprenticeship Application

1. **Review Requirements:** Ensure you meet all the prerequisites listed in the apprenticeship overview.
2. **Complete the Application Form:** Fill out the online application with your personal information and educational background.
3. **Submit Supporting Documents:** Upload your high school diploma, GED, transcripts, and other required documents.
4. **Schedule an Aptitude Test:** Once your application is complete, you will be scheduled for an aptitude test.
5. **Panel Interview:** Successful candidates will be invited for an interview to discuss their qualifications and interest in the program.
6. **Receive Admission Decision:** Applicants will be notified of their admission status and next steps.
