### [Discover Your Future in Electrics: ETISN Open House and Career Fair](https://earnwhileyoulearn.org/event/discover-your-future-in-electrics-etisn-open-house-and-career-fair/ "Discover Your Future in Electrics: ETISN Open House and Career Fair")

PixelVega
Dhaka, Dhaka

ETISN invites prospective students, parents, and industry professionals to an exciting Open House and Career Fair. This event will showcase the Electrical Training Institute of Southern Nevada's top-notch apprenticeship program and provide valuable insights into the thriving electrical industry.

[![](https://earnwhileyoulearn.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/JATClogo-final-hor.webp)](https://earnwhileyoulearn.org/)

EJATC is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to training skilled electricians. We provide hands-on experience and classroom instruction.
