## History of EJATC

Inception was February 6th, 1947 when chartered by the Nevada State Apprenticeship Council (NSAC) and the United States Department of Labor Office of Apprenticeship, the Electrical Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee of Southern Nevada (EJATC of Southern Nevada) has been committed to excellence in electrical training. Our journey began with a mission to provide aspiring electricians with top-notch education and hands-on experience. Over the years, our programs have evolved to meet the changing demands of the industry. The first graduation class of 2 was in 1952, over the last 70 years we have graduated over 2,000 apprentices.

Our rich history is filled with milestones, dedicated instructors, and successful graduates who have shaped the electrical landscape of Nevada.

## Why Join an IBEW-NECA Apprenticeship?

At EJATC, we provide high-paying job opportunities and ensure a steady pipeline of skilled workers for electrical and technical projects. Our apprenticeships are more than just training programs; they are pathways to rewarding careers. Joining an IBEW-NECA apprenticeship means gaining access to excellent benefits, continuous learning, and the potential to advance into roles such as foreman, project manager, or even starting your own business.

### Interesting Facts About Our Apprenticeship Programs

At the Electrical JATC of Southern Nevada, we’re proud to offer a tuition-free apprenticeship programs that combines classroom learning with real-world experience.

- **Earn While You Learn**: Apprentices receive competitive wages and benefits while completing on-the-job training.

- **No Tuition**: The apprenticeship is completely tuition-free. However, apprentices are responsible for small costs such as curriculum books, basic hand tools, and work gear.

- **Hands-On Experience**: Apprentices work on high-profile projects across commercial, industrial, and residential sectors.

- **Career-Ready Graduates**: Most apprentices secure employment immediately after completing the program.

- **Union-Backed**: A partnership between **IBEW Local 357** and **NECA** ensures strong contractor connections and job placement support.

- **Specialized Certifications**: Opportunities include fiber optics, fire alarm systems, solar, EV charging, and much more.

- **Proven Success**: With over 70 years of training experience, we’ve helped shape the workforce that powers Southern Nevada.
