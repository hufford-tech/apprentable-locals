## Outreach

EJATC is committed to giving back to the community through various outreach programs. We

engage in educational initiatives, career fairs, and community projects to inspire and support the next generation of electrical professionals

#### Our Outreach Programs:

### School Presentations:

Helping students discover rewarding careers in the electrical trade, through education, hands-on experiences, and access to real opportunities.

### Community Workshops:

Promoting electrical apprenticeships that empower our community with pathways to rewarding, skilled careers.

### Industry Partnerships:

Collaborating with electrical professionals for real-world training.

### Veteran Support:

Offering support to veterans through specialized programs like VEEP and Helmets to Hardhats.

[![](https://earnwhileyoulearn.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/JATClogo-final-hor.webp)](https://earnwhileyoulearn.org/)

EJATC is a non-profit 501(c)(3) organization dedicated to training skilled electricians. We provide hands-on experience and classroom instruction.
