# Applicant Qualifications

**In order to apply for our program, an applicant must:**

- 18 years of age at time of indenture (You can apply at 17 but cannot be indentured until 18).
- Be a high school graduate or have a  NYS GED.
- Have successfully completed one year of high school, college or post secondary algebra. (Freshman Math 1, Course 1, or Sequential Math 1).
- Must live within the Local Union Jurisdiction for at least 1  year (Albany, Schenectady, Saratoga, Schoharie, Rensselaer, Montgomery, Warren, Hamilton, Washington, Fulton, Columbia, a small portion of Otsego, and a portion of Greene County.
- Must be in good physical and mental health.
