#### **Troubleshooting:**

**_Canvas_**

**Issue:** I am unable to login to Canvas

**To Do:**

- If you have a **@bostonjatc.org** email, [click here](https://bostonjatc.instructure.com/login/saml) to log in

  - Try a different browser Edge/Chrome
  - Reset your Office 365 password if needed

- If you use a personal email to access Canvas:
  - Clear your browsers temporarily, cache and cookies and restart your browser
  - Try a different browser Edge/Chrome
  - Email [support@bostonjatc.org](mailto:kelsy@bostonjatc.org) if you forgot your password

**_NJATC LMS (Learning Management System)_**

**Issue:** I am unable to login to the LMS

**To Do:** [Click here](https://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/10/LMS-Instructions-9.2.21.pdf) and follow instructions to reset password

**_Office 365_**

**Issue:** I am unable to login to my Office account

**To Do:**

- Make sure you are using your **@bostonjatc.org** email
- Reset your password on the login page if needed
- Clear your browsers temporary, cache and cookies and restart your browser
- Use Microsoft Edge as your browser

**Issue:** How do I use MS Word, Excel, Outlook and Teams apps?

**To Do:** Information and How-To’s can be found [here](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365)

**_Hardware/Windows_**

**Issue:** My JATC laptop is not powering on, keeps crashing and other related hardware and Windows 10 issues

**Issue:** Stolen or missing laptop

**Issue:** Your account has been hacked or possible virus on the laptop

**To Do:** Call 1-401-272-6688 7AM-7PM M-F
