#### **Apprentice Lay Offs:**

Apprentices must [![](http://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/Click-here.jpeg)](https://bostonjatc.com/apprentice-reporting-layoff/) to notify the JATC that they have been laid off the **DAY they are laid off.**

If you wish to file for unemployment, [click this link](http://www.mass.gov/lwd/unemployment-insur/).

**Important info you will need when filing your unemployment claim:**

JATC Apprentices and CWs are **not considered part-time or full-time students**. Answer the questions accordingly.

Does your union have a hiring hall?   YES

Does your union restrict you from seeking work outside your hiring hall?  YES

Agent Information is: Robert Sheehan, 617-436-3710, rsheehan@ibew103.com

Additional information about unemployment assistance from the Commonwealth of Massachusetts is available **[here](http://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/2020/04/Unemployment-information-from-Commonwealth-of-Mass..pdf).**

[Unemployment FAQs for Union Members](https://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/Unemployment-FAQs-for-Union-Members.pdf)

#### **Change of Address:**

To update or confirm your contact information, please log in to your online account: [https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/ibeweducation-com/login/index.php](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/ibeweducation-com/login/index.php)

NOTE: Any time you have an address change, you must also update your address with Local 103 Business Manager’s Office: (617) 436-3710, Trust Funds: (617) 288-5999 and Modern Assistance Program: (617) 774-0331 as well.

#### **Apprentice On-site Parking Info:**

- This [map link](http://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/Parking-Access-Map_Appr.pdf) provides guidance on where you can and cannot park on campus.
- We suggest parking in the lot across from the Union Hall if you’re commuting south with departing traffic.
- There is no parking in the 170 Freeport St. JATC staff lot, nor is entering or exiting permitted via the 170 lot for staff safety reasons.
