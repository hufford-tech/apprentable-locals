# Master Electrician

### **The following information is geared towards Local 103 Members who are preparing to apply for a Master Electrician license:**

**Summary of documents you will need to apply for your license:**

1. **A recent passport-style photo**
2. **High school diploma or equivalency**
3. **Electrical-industry education 150-hour certificate (Form 225)**
4. **15-hour code update certificate**
5. **CORI Authorization Form**

**Additional documents some people will need:**

- **Military veterans should submit a DD-214 or DD-215.**
- **Those who wish to apply for a testing accommodation will need to submit an Accommodation Request and supporting documentation.**

* * *

****Details on required documents:****

**1\. A recent passport-style photo**

- A recent photo of yourself taken indoors from the shoulders up with a plain background.
- You may take a selfie on your phone but look directly at the camera lens, not at the screen.

* * *

**2\. Your high school diploma, equivalency, or transcript**

- If you cannot locate it, ask your high school to email you a transcript.

* * *

**3\. Electrical industry education 150-hour certificate (Form 225)**

- Please email **Contact@bostonjatc.org** to request your certificate. Allow 1-2 weeks for processing.

* * *

**4\. 15-hour Code Update Certificate**

- If you completed your code update online, the completion certificate was emailed to you.
- If you took the in-person code update, certificates are available for download under “CEU Training” from the [**JATC portal**](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/ibeweducation-com/login/index.php) upon completion of the course.

* * *

**5\. CORI Authorization Form**

- Fill out the **[CORI Authorization Form](https://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/DOL-CORI-Authorization-Form.pdf)**.

* * *

### Optional Additional documentation:

- **Veterans and Military Spouses – s** **ubmit a copy of discharge form (DD214 & DD215) or[Military Spouse Affidavit](https://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/Military-Spouse-Affidavit-1.pdf)** to receive benefits of the Valor ACT and **5 points exam** **credits.** Disregard this step if you are not a Veteran.
- **Fill out the [Request for Accommodation Form](https://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Request-for-Accommodation.pdf) and include along the required supporting documentation, if you wish to request special testing accommodations.**

* * *

### AFTER YOU GATHER ALL DOCUMENTS ABOVE:

[![](https://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/Click-here.jpeg)](https://test-takers.psiexams.com/mael)

To create a PSI account, upload your documents and submit your application to PSI!

* * *

### AFTER YOU SUMBIT YOUR APPLICATION TO PSI:

1. It can take **up to 90 days** for final approval to take the exam.
2. Check your status anytime under “My Application”

* * *

### **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:**

#### How can I apply for an accommodation during the exam?

Click [Here](https://bostonjatc.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/06/Request-for-Accommodation.pdf) to fill out the Request for Accommodation Form and include it, along with the required supporting documentation, with your Application for Exam.

#### Will I get extra points on the exam for being a Veteran?

Applicants who qualify as a veteran per M.G.L. c. 4, § 7, clause forty-third must **submit a** **copy of discharge form (forms DD214 and DD215)** to receive benefits of the Valor ACT and **5 points exam** **credits**.
