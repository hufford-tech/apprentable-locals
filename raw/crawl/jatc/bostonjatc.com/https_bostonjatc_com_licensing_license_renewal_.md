# License Renewal

## **FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS:**

* * *

#### **Do I need to upload my code update certificate when I renew my license?**

**No.** In fact, licensees should **NOT** upload their code update certificate upon renewal. The upload feature in the ePlace portal is meant for those who check NO to any of the statements they must agree to, or those who must report a legal matter or complaint against their license.

#### **Do I need to _do_ my code update before I renew my license?**

Yes.

#### **How do I renew my license?**

Click **[Here](https://elicensing21.mass.gov/CitizenAccess/Login.aspx)**to log into the ePlace portal and pay with a credit card before **July 31, 2025.**

Those who are renewing their license for the **first time**, will need a record ID and authorization code which should have been mailed, but it can also be looked up [**here**.](https://elicensing21.mass.gov/DOLAuthCodeApp)

If you experience any difficulty with renewal, contact the Help Desk at **844-733-7522** or [**ePLACE\_helpdesk@state.ma.us**](mailto:ePLACE_helpdesk@state.ma.us)

#### **What if the option to Renew is not available when I log in?**

1. Make sure you clicked **“Manage My Licenses, Permits & Certificates”**
2. Make sure you have [**Linked**](https://elicensing21.mass.gov/CitizenAccess/Cap/CapApplyDisclaimer.aspx?Module=License&CAPType=License/Record%20Link/NA/NA&isSubAgencyCap=Y&agencyCode=MASS&createRecordByService=YES) your license.
3. Call the Help Desk at 844-733-7522 to ask why you don’t have the option to renew.
4. If the reason is related to **[tax compliance](https://www.mass.gov/administrative-procedure/ap-613-requesting-a-certificate-of-good-standing-andor-tax-compliance-release-of-professional-or-health-profession-license-or-waiver-of-corporate-excise-tax-lien?_gl=1*7hy36r*_ga*MTQ4NTA5OTk5LjE3NDQxMjY4NTI.*_ga_MCLPEGW7WM*czE3NDc2NjEzNDUkbzEkZzEkdDE3NDc2NjEzNjAkajAkbDAkaDA.)**, contact the Department of Revenue at 617-887-6400 then request a certificate of good standing from [**MassTaxConnect**](https://mtc.dor.state.ma.us/mtc/_/).

#### **What if there is a hold on my license due to taxes or child support I owe?**

1. Call the Department of Revenue at **617-877-6400** and set up a payment plan.
2. Request a certificate of good standing from [**MassTaxConnect**](https://mtc.dor.state.ma.us/mtc/_/).
3. Provide the certificate of good standing to the Board of State Examiners of Electricians at electricians.board@mass.gov.

#### **Why does it seem like the code update I took is on an old code book?**

The National Electrical code is updated and published **every three years**.

The **2023** edition is the most current. It will be next published in 2026.

Massachusetts licensees have until _July 31 of the last year of the code cycle_ to complete their Mandatory Continuing Education (15-hour code update and 6-hour related instruction).

#### **How can I confirm that my license is current?**

Click **[Here](https://elicensing21.mass.gov/CitizenAccess/GeneralProperty/PropertyLookUp.aspx?isLicensee=Y)** to look up your license status.

* * *

#### **What if I don’t want to do my code update _online_?**

The JATC has in-person classes also.

Or you could opt to take a course with another provider at your own expense.

Please click [**Here**](https://licensing.reg.state.ma.us/public/el_ceu/el_list_ceu_providers.asp) to view a list of **Massachusetts** code update providers.

Please click[**Here**](https://www.oplc.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt441/files/inline-documents/sonh/education-providers.pdf) to view a list of **New Hampshire** code update providers.

#### **How long is the online code update course?**

You have 15 hours of class to complete, over a period of three months from the date of registration. The deadline for completing the Continuing Education for License renewal is July 31, 2025.

#### **How can I do the additional 6-hours?**

A 6-Hour course is available upon registering for the online course.

_237 CMR 17:01 Continuing Education Requirement: (c) Of the required 21 clock hours, six clock hours shall be in areas chosen by the licensee for professional development in the subjects of Electrical Code, business, law, first aid, safety, Building Code, and related topics. **Said instruction need not be offered by a Board approved Provider.**_

#### **Is there a test during the online code update class?**

There are 5-10 questions at the end of each module. They are multiple-choice and it is non-fail. If you get a question wrong, you may retry to answer correctly.

#### **Does the class cover NH licenses?**

NH Licensees will need to complete one brief additional module to meet the requirements for NH. If you have a NH license and do not see the NH module in your account after logging in, please contact the Support Department at M.E.P. Con. Ed., LLC **508-746-3724** or **support@mepconed.com.**

#### **Does the class cover Systems Technician & Systems Contractor Licenses?**

C&D Licensees will need to complete one brief additional module to meet the requirements for renewal. If you have a C or D license and do not see the C&D module in your account after logging in, please contact the Support Department at M.E.P. Con. Ed., LLC **508-746-3724** or **support@mepconed.com.**

#### **What if I am retired and don’t have a Union Card Number?**

Your Union Card Number is the same when you are retired as when you were an active member. If you can’t remember it – contact the Financial Office at 617-436-5150.

#### **What do I need to do upon finishing the class?**

As long as you filled out your Certificate information (your electrical license numbers) with the appropriate license # and letter classification, your course hours will automatically be submitted on your behalf. You will need to wait until the year 2025 to pay for your license renewal.

#### **Is the operation of the online course easy for individuals not used to using computers?**

Yes! Once you have completed the registration process the course is fully geared towards ease-of-use. The auto-play, auto-save, fully narrated course allows for simple login and resume of course play with a few button clicks. The course can be completed over time, allowing users to login and logout as often as they wish. The course is also fully compatible with computers, tablets and smart phones. Also, there is phone and email support for anyone who may be having issues or unsure of the process.

#### **When do I have to do my code update?**

The deadline to complete the required continuing education for the 2023 Code Cycle for MASSACHUSETTS licenses is **July 31, 2025**.

#### **Do I need to retake the exam to reinstate my license if it expired more than one renewal cycle ago?**

Yes, per the Board regulation your license can only be reinstated by retaking the respective exam. To begin the reinstatement process:

1\. Complete a 15 hour code class on the current code cycle.

2\. Complete a [**Request For Reinstatement Form**](https://www.mass.gov/doc/application-for-reinstatement-of-expired-licensure/download) and email it to [electricians.board@mass.gov](mailto:electricians.board@mass.gov)

3\. The Board will reply and advise you on the next steps.
