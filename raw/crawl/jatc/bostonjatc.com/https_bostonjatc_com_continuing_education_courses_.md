# Continuing Education Courses

**Journeyman & Technician Continuing Education**

IBEW Local 103 and the JATC are proud to offer continuing education opportunities for both Journeyman and Technician members at our Training Center.

**Course Registration Is Now Open**

Explore our current course offerings and find classes that fit your schedule and professional goals.

**Popular Courses Include:**

- Code Update (NEC)
- Master License Prep
- OSHA Safety Training

**Ready to Register?**

Browse available classes and secure your spot through our online registration system. Classes fill quickly, so early registration is encouraged.

👉 [**View Classes & Register**](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/ibeweducation-com/login/index.php)
