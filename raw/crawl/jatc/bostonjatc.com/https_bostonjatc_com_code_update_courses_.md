**Online Code Update Frequently Asked Questions:**

**How do I renew my license?**

**All Electrical and Systems licenses in Massachusetts expire on July 31, 2028. Renewal will begin in approximately April 2028.** For more information about Renewing your license click **[HERE](https://bostonjatc.com/licensing/license-renewal/)**

**What if I do not want to do my code update _online_?**

We expect to have in-person classes available for registration beginning in spring 2026.

Or you could opt to take a course with another provider at your own expense.

Please click [**Here**](https://licensing.reg.state.ma.us/public/el_ceu/el_list_ceu_providers.asp) to view a list of **Massachusetts** code update providers.

Please click[**Here**](https://www.oplc.nh.gov/sites/g/files/ehbemt441/files/inline-documents/sonh/education-providers.pdf) to view a list of **New Hampshire** code update providers.

**How long is the online code update course?**

You have 15 hours of class to complete, over a period of three months from the date of registration. The deadline for completing the Continuing Education for License renewal is July 31, 2028.

**How can I get my code update certificate?**

If you completed the course online during the 2023 code cycle, you can get your certificate in one of the following ways:

1. Your certificate was emailed to you from MEP Con Ed upon completion of the course – please search your emails for it.
2. [Log into your MEP Con Ed account](https://jatc.mepconed.com/novusiii/application/login/) and click on “certificates” to download a copy.
3. Contact MEP Con Ed directly at **508-746-3724** or **[support@mepconed.com](mailto:support@mepconed.com)** to request a copy.

**How can I do the additional 6-hours?**

A 6-Hour course is available upon registering.

_237 CMR 17:01 Continuing Education Requirement: (c) Of the required 21 clock hours, six clock hours shall be in areas chosen by the licensee for professional development in the subjects of Electrical Code, business, law, first aid, safety, Building Code, and related topics. **Said instruction need not be offered by a Board approved Provider.**_

**Is there a test during the online code update class?**

There are 5-10 questions at the end of each module. They are multiple-choice and it is non-fail. If you get a question wrong, you may retry to answer correctly.

**Does the class cover NH licenses?**

NH Licensees will need to complete one brief additional module to meet the requirements for NH. If you have a NH license and do not see the NH module in your account after logging in, please contact the Support Department at M.E.P. Con. Ed., LLC **508-746-3724** or **support@mepconed.com.**

**Does the class cover Systems Technician & Systems Contractor Licenses?**

C&D Licensees will need to complete one brief additional module to meet the requirements for renewal. If you have a C or D license and do not see the C&D module in your account after logging in, please contact the Support Department at M.E.P. Con. Ed., LLC **508-746-3724** or **support@mepconed.com.**

**What if I am retired and don’t have a Union Card Number?**

Your Union Card Number is the same when you are retired as when you were an active member. If you can’t remember it – contact the Financial Office at 617-436-5150.

**What do I need to do upon finishing the class?**

As long as you filled out your Certificate information (your electrical license numbers) with the appropriate license # and letter classification, your course hours will automatically be submitted on your behalf. You will need to wait until the year 2028 to pay for your license renewal.

**Is the operation of the online course easy for individuals not used to using computers?**

Yes! Once you have completed the registration process the course is fully geared towards ease-of-use. The auto-play, auto-save, fully narrated course allows for simple login and resume of course play with a few button clicks. The course can be completed over time, allowing users to login and logout as often as they wish. The course is also fully compatible with computers, tablets and smart phones. Also, there is phone and email support for anyone who may be having issues or unsure of the process.

**When do I have to do my code update?**

The deadline to complete the required continuing education for the 2026 Code Cycle for MASSACHUSETTS licenses is **July 31, 2028**.

**Do I need to retake the exam to reinstate my license if it expired more than one renewal cycle ago?**

Yes, per the Board regulation your license can only be reinstated by retaking the respective exam. To begin the reinstatement process:

1\. Complete a 15 hour code class on the current code cycle.

2\. Complete a [**Request For Reinstatement Form**](https://www.mass.gov/doc/application-for-reinstatement-of-expired-licensure/download) and email it to [electricians.board@mass.gov](mailto:electricians.board@mass.gov)

3\. The Board will reply and advise you on the next steps.

#### **Electrical Business License Information**

The MA Electrical Board (BSEE) has issued every electrical and systems business entity a new “A1” business license number in accordance with 237 CMR 16.03. This license will need to be renewed along with your master contractor license in 2022 and every three years thereafter. The fee for this new license is the same as a master license renewal. The BSEE has **not** set a date for when company vehicles and advertising must reflect the business license number in lieu of the master license number. We will continue to keep you posted on changes and news updates.

If you received an **electrical business license** in the mail, click [**Here**](https://www.mass.gov/doc/electrical-business-license-faqs/download) to read the Frequently Asked Questions List issued by the Board of State Examiners of Electricians.
