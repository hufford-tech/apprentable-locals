- **Issue**: I am unable to login to Canvas

- If you have a @bostonjatc.org email, [click here](https://bostonjatc.instructure.com/login/saml) to log in
  - Try a different browser Edge/Chrome
  - Reset your Office 365 password if needed
- If you use a personal email to access Canvas:
  - Clear your browsers temporarily, cache and cookies and restart your browser
  - Try a different browser Edge/Chrome
  - Email [support@bostonjatc.org](mailto:kelsy@bostonjatc.org) if you forgot your password
- Issue: I need to get in touch with my instructor
- Use the Canvas “Inbox” feature to contact your instructor about class-related things.

## **Student Device Frequently Asked Questions:**

- **Where can I get help using Windows and Office 365 applications such as Word and Excel?**
  - Refer to [https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365](https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/microsoft-365)

- **What do I do if my computer is frozen, or will not turn on?**
  - Ensure the computer is fully charged. Hold the power button down for 30-60 seconds. After that time, release and press the power button again. If it won’t turn on Call 1-401-272-6688 7AM-7PM M-F

- **What do I do if Windows keeps freezing / crashing?**
  - Call 1-401-272-6688 7AM-7PM M-F

- **What happens if my laptop is lost or stolen?**
  - Send an email ASAP to: [support@bostonjatc.org](mailto:support@bostonjatc.org)

- **What happens if my laptop is physically damaged and won’t turn on?**
  - Call 1-401-272-6688 7AM-7PM M-F
- **What do I do if I forgot my Office 365 account password?**
  - On the Office.com login page, click “Forgot my password”

- **Why can’t I login to Canvas?**
  - Use the following link: [https://bostonjatc.instructure.com/login/saml](https://bostonjatc.instructure.com/login/saml)

- **What should I do if my Office 365 account is blocked?**
  - Call 1-401-272-6688 7AM-7PM M-F

- **What do I do if I can’t login to Netacad.com (Telecom only)?**
  - Make sure you are using your @bostonjatc.org email
  - Click forgot password on the login page to reset it

- **Can I install software for personal use?**
  - No – Refer to Student Technology Policy outlined in the 2021-2022 Rules & Regulations \[page 38\]

- **What internet browser should I use?**
  - Microsoft Edge is the recommended browser for Canvas and Office 365
  - Google Chrome can be used as well

- **Can I access my @bostonjatc.org email on my smartphone?**
  - Yes. You will need to download the Microsoft Outlook app from your phone’s app store
  - Or configure your phone’s native mail app to connect to Office 365

- **Can I forward my @bostonjatc.org email to personal email account?**
  - While it is possible to forward mail, the JATC will only be corresponding with you through you @bostonjatc.org account which you are required to review periodically
  - Missed messages because of forwarding cannot be used as an excuse
  - Refer to Student Technology Policy outlined in the 2021-2022 Rules & Regulations \[page 38\]

- **Can I use my laptop for personal use outside of the JATC?**
  - Refer to Student Technology Policy outlined in the 2021-2022 Rules & Regulations \[page 38\]

- **Can I use my laptop on the jobsite?**
  - Refer to Student Technology Policy outlined in the 2021-2022 Rules & Regulations \[page 38\]

- **What happens if my laptop is damaged (non-warranty) and needs to be replaced?**
  - There is a 1-time accidental damage coverage on the laptop – determined by Dell
  - Call 1-401-272-6688
  - Additional replacements are at the cost of the apprentice
  - Refer to Student Technology Policy outlined in the 2021-2022 Rules & Regulations \[page 38\]

- **What happens if my laptop is lost or stolen?**
  - Replacement is at the cost of the apprentice
  - Refer to Rules & Regulations for Technology Policy

- **If my laptop is damaged or lost, can I bring in my personal laptop to use in class?**
  - No – Refer to Student Technology Policy outlined in the 2021-2022 Rules & Regulations \[page 38\]
  - Devices under repair by Dell or Envision will be handled on a per-case bias

- **Are there any special care instructions for the laptop?**
  - Don’t leave in extreme temperatures (hot/cold) ex: your car or outside
  - Use the provided sleeve for protection
  - Do not leave the laptop in locations where it can be easily stolen
  - You shouldn’t use standard cleaners on the screen.
  - Dry, nonabrasive cloth
  - Special computer screen cleaners

- **Who can I reach out with general questions not outlined here?**
  - Send an email to [support@bostonjatc.org](mailto:support@bostonjatc.org)
