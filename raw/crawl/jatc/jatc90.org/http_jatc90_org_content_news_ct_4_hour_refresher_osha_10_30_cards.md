# CT 4 Hour Refresher for OSHA 10 / 30 Cards

- [Announcements](http://jatc90.org/category/news/announcements "")

**OSHA  CT OSHA** **10/30** **Refresher Class for Licensed Electricians**

This class is for licensed electricians who already have an OSHA 10/30 card.

Thursday January 26, 2023 5:00 pm to 9:00 pm.

**CT 4hr Refresher Course only** \*\*\*\*

Please contact the JATC at 203-265-3820 for more information or to reserve your spot.

**Class space is limited.**
