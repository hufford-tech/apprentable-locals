**Event Details:**

**Every 2nd Thursday of the Month at 4:00 PM**

**Location:** **ETI 6023 S. Garfield Ave, Commerce, CA 90040**

EMPOWER is an initiative run by the Electrical Training Institute. The program, known as the ETI Mentorship Program Offering Women Extra Resources, is dedicated to recruiting, retaining, and supporting women in IBEW Local 11. Our mission is to create a supportive environment where women can thrive in the electrical trade.

EMPOWER hosts monthly meetings designed to equip women with essential skills, knowledge, and inspiration. These meetings, held every second Thursday at 4:00 PM, provide attendees with a unique blend of practical workshops, informative sessions, and opportunities for networking. From Milwaukee Tool Company demonstrations of power tool safety features to Casa Treatment Center for Women discussing the effects of alcohol on women's health, to origami workshops designed to enhance spatial reasoning, each meeting is crafted to help women gain the information, skills, and encouragement needed to join and stay in our local. We have had powerful guest speakers, including Yvonne Wheeler, the first female president of the LA Federation of Labor, Keith Edwards, International President of the Electrical Workers Minority Caucus, and Sue Doro, author of several books on being a woman in the trades. Sue's Bluejean Pocketwriters Workshop was particularly moving and inspirational.

Each meeting also offers a free dinner, a raffle for attendees, and a grand raffle prize drawn at our December meeting. The diversity of topics covered at our events—from tradeswomen’s legacy and food prep strategies to understanding learning styles and addressing mental health stigmas—ensures that there is something valuable for everyone.

As we move forward, we continue our commitment to hosting enriching and diverse events. We are excited about upcoming sessions, including presentations from different companies selling electrical equipment such as Southwire and Siemens, which will help with learning about the gear involved and safe usage for our work.

EMPOWER’s monthly meetings are more than just gatherings; they are opportunities for continued learning and support. By joining us, you can learn, connect with like-minded women, and grow in the electrical construction career path. For more information or to RSVP, please contact us at EMPOWER@laett.com or call 323.221.5881 X 3310. Join EMPOWER and become part of a vibrant community dedicated to empowering women in the electrical trade.
