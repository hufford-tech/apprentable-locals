Apprenticeship is the age-old method of teaching new workers the construction craft. With the loss of vocational education in most schools, Apprenticeship Prep programs have sprung up around the state using the Multi-Craft Core (MC3) curriculum approved by the National Building Trades that gives participants a leg up in understanding the crafts, gaining safety certificates, and being able to prepare for a successful apprenticeship. To learn more and find good programs near you, [**click here**](https://cabuildingtrades.org/issue/apprenticeship-and-pre-apprenticeship-mc3/) to visit our apprenticeship website. |
