| **Innovative Solutions for the Salesforce Hiring Crunch** |
| --- |
| **Hosted by:** | **Jewish Vocational Services and the San Francisco Office of Economic and Workforce Development** |

| **Event information** | The Salesforce job market is hot, and the world’s leading CRM platform is continuing to explode. According to IDC, Salesforce and its broader ecosystem will create nearly 2 million jobs over the next five years. Burning Glass says that jobs requiring Salesforce skills are growing faster than the overall market, and these jobs pay more. Employers are competing hard to lure top talent. And the talent isn’t always sticky. Will there be enough skilled workers to meet the need? What can a hiring manager do? Come hear from a panel of experts about a new hiring solution that Bay Area employers are using to meet their tech talent needs - the apprenticeship. Historically confined to the trades, apprenticeships have emerged as an innovative way for local employers to find, grow and retain diverse tech talent. Discussion Topics: Trends in Salesforce hiring solutions The new Salesforce Business Analyst Apprenticeship program The employer experience - managing tech apprenticeships The benefits and challenges of tech apprenticeships Join us for refreshments and a conversation that will change the way you think about hiring.
