# Classes Offered

## For Applicants

If you do not meet the math requirement with which to apply, you can take the **Tech Math** online course at [www.skillsprep.org](https://www.skillsprep.org/login/index.php). Once you’ve completed this course, please mail a copy of the completion certificate with your application packet. This will satisfy the math requirement. (PLEASE NOTE: once you’ve clicked the “Register for Free” button to create an account, make sure to select the “I’m not affiliated” option. The other option does not apply to apprenticeship applicants.)

## For Journeymen

Below is a list of some of the JOURNEYMAN CONTINUING EDUCATION classes that we offer through our training center.

- CPR / First Aid

- OSHA 10

- NFPA 70E

- Foreman Development

- Confined Space Awareness Training

- Taping & Cable Splicing

- Forklift / GradAll training and certification

- Scissor / Boom Lifts training and certification

- California Advanced Lighting Control Training Program (CALCTP)… a 50 hour class (not including the 10-12 hours of online coursework) that will only be open to those who have completed their online pre-requisite coursework at: [bit.ly/IBEW234CALCTP](http://bit.ly/IBEW234CALCTP)

- Electrical Vehicle Infrastructure Training Program (EVITP)


Please refer to the [Calendar](https://www.tricountyjatc.org/calendar) for class dates and times. Sign up for classes by calling the Training Center at (831) 633-3063 during regular office hours (Monday – Thursday, 12pm – 5pm).
