### Muncie Electrical Jatc

The Muncie Electrical Joint Apprentice is a training program sponsored by the
National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) and the International
Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW). It's a local chapter of the broader
electrical training ALLIANCE. The program provides apprenticeship training for
individuals seeking careers as electricians, offering both on-the-job training
and classroom instruction.
