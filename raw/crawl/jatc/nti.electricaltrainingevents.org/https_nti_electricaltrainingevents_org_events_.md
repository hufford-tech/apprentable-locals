# Events

NTI offers various events during the week. All attendees have the opportunity to participate in each of our special events.

![](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/events_1.jpg)

## Events

[**Opening Ceremony** \\
\\
Join us for the Opening Ceremony and celebrate this week-long journey of learning, networking, and camaraderie.](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/events/opening-ceremony/) [**Welcome Reception** \\
\\
Immediately following Opening Ceremony, join your friends and colleagues for food, fun, and fellowship.](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/events/welcome-reception/) [**Trade Show** \\
\\
Come see the industry’s latest innovations at this year’s NTI Trade Show.](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/tradeshow/) [**A.J. Pearson Charity Golf** \\
\\
Spend some time on the green and contribute to a great cause. Morning and afternoon tee times are available.](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/events/golf-tournament/) [**Sporting Clay Shoot** \\
\\
Union Sportsmen’s Alliance presents a Wednesday afternoon shooting clays at Island Lakes Shooting Range.](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/events/clay-shoot/) [**Block Party** \\
\\
Experience Ann Arbor’s famous Main Street with live music, great food, and friendly game competition.](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/events/block-party/) [**TD/ATD Reception** \\
\\
Join your fellow Training Directors, Assistant Training Directors for a special welcome reception with food and drinks.](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/events/td-atd-reception/) [**Graduation** \\
\\
The NTI Graduation Ceremony will celebrate the culmination of a journey.](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/events/graduation/) [**Worship Service** \\
\\
Sunday at the G3 Center. Service begins at 7:00 am. Coffee and Donuts available.](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/events/worship-service/)

## INTERESTED IN COMING TO NTI?

Ensure your place at the upcoming National Training Institute by joining IBEW, NECA, and the _electrical training ALLIANCE_ at Eastern Michigan University and Washtenaw Community College. We encourage you to register early to benefit from the early registration rate and secure your spot for the class of your choice. Join us for a week of training, seminars, and networking.
