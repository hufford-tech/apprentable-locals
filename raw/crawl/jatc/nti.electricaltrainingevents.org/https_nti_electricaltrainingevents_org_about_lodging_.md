Reservations will be taken by EMU through this [**link**.](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLSdmdeSwrcK16n4rXz1Vxzkg6r5BF5tKA2QfrLbKAnfYz95R1w/viewform)

### Reservation Cancellation

Before canceling any hotel rooms at any property, please contact Denise Jenkins, [dj@etalliance.org](mailto:dj@etalliance.org). There might be another JATC that could use those rooms.

The _electrical training ALLIANCE_ and Destination Ann Arbor work to arrange the best housing options for NTI Participants. A variety of lodging is offered to fit your needs and budget.

Attendees are responsible for making their own housing arrangements directly with the hotel.

The _electrical training ALLIANCE_ inspects each property and hotel rates are guaranteed. Be sure to mention the NTI room block code to receive a discounted rate at participating hotels. _Room availability is limited, so don’t wait to make your reservation!_
