# Opening Ceremony

### Sunday 7/26

Plan to attend the Opening Ceremony, marking the commencement of a week dedicated to learning, networking, and collaboration. The event will take place at the Rynearson Football Stadium. During the ceremony, we will highlight the week’s scheduled activities, honor Outstanding Apprentice Graduates, and announce recipients of the Outstanding Apprentice Graduate Training Partner Scholarships.
