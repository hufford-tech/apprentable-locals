### [Training](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/)

Since 1990, NTI has been offering World Class Training for Instructors, Apprentices, Journey-Level Workers, JATC Committee Members, Training Directors, and Local Union and Chapter Officers.

[Learn More](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/)

## INTERESTED IN COMING TO NTI?

Ensure your place at the upcoming National Training Institute by joining IBEW, NECA, and the _electrical training ALLIANCE_ at Eastern Michigan University and Washtenaw Community College. We encourage you to register early to benefit from the early registration rate and secure your spot for the class of your choice. Join us for a week of training, seminars, and networking.
