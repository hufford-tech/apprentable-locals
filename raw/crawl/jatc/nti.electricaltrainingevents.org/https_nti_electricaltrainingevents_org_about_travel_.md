## Construction Near Detroit Metro Airport / Ann Arbor Area

Drivers headed to Detroit Metro Airport may want to plan additional time.  There is continued construction in and around the airport area.   [Here’s a link for construction around DTW.](https://i94detroit.org/)

To find out the latest construction projects or view current traffic cams, visit the [Michigan Department of Transportation Construction Map](https://mdotjboss.state.mi.us/MiDrive/map). If you click on the MDOT map – in the upper left corner there are icons (construction cone, camera, etc.) if you click on those icons the information you need will show up on the map.

## Airline Information

When making flight arrangements for NTI, the closest airport serving the most passenger airlines is [Detroit Metropolitan Airport (DTW)](http://www.metroairport.com/). This airport services nearly all major airlines and is approximately 25 miles east of Ann Arbor or about a half-hour car ride.

Getting from the airport to your hotel is easy and convenient.  You can grab a cab, arrange one of the shuttles or pick-up a rental car, keep in mind parking on campus is very limited.

* * *

## Car Rental Agencies

There are several on-airport agencies available including Alamo, Avis Rent-A-Car, Budget, Dollar, Enterprise, Hertz, National, and Thrifty.  To see the complete information [click here](https://www.metroairport.com/to-from-dtw/rental-cars).
