# Office Manager / Staff Group

**“OM Group”** is for JATC / AJATC Office Manager and/or Administrative Staff who assist the Training Director with all aspects of running an Apprenticeship Program.

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Sunday**<br>1:00 pm – 5:00 pm | **Monday**<br>7:30 am – 5:00 pm | **Tuesday**<br>7:30 am – 11:45 am |

Participants wanting to attend both OM and C Group Sessions must register for SC Group-B (Thursday-Friday) and select the OM Group box to add to your schedule.
