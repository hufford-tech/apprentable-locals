# About

NTI is an annual week of training offering a variety of education and training opportunities to meet the demands of the Electrical Industry. Attend NTI to learn new skills, network with peers, and hear from experts.

[Information Booklet](https://indd.adobe.com/view/07da85f1-17a3-45ef-a7a6-00a3ec6821ad)

## Training

The National Training Institute was started in 1990, then known as the “Instructor Training Institute,” to professionally train educators instructing IBEW Apprentices and Journeymen. Since then, the Instructor Training Institute has evolved into the NTI of today, offering World Class Training for Instructors, Apprentices, Journey-Level Workers, JATC Committee Members, Training Directors, and Local Union and Chapter Officers.
