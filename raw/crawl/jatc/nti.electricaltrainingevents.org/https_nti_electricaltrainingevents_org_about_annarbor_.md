The Greater Ann Arbor Area offers a wide array of activities and things to do while you are in town! Catch a play, take in a live musical performance, and explore one of the most outstanding art collections on a college campus.

Those looking for recreational activities have a host of options for golfing, hiking, or biking. Three metro parks, plus the largest state recreation area in Michigan’s lower peninsula are close by.

![](http://ntiinfo.wpengine.com/wp-content/uploads/2014/12/annarbor_header.jpg)

### Looking for things to do?

The Ann Arbor Downtown Area is a vibrant downtown area offering a variety of shops, restaurants, and cultural activities.

Favorite spots close to the University of Michigan campus include:

- [University of Michigan Museum of Art](http://www.umma.umich.edu/)
- [University of Michigan Kelsey Museum of Archaeology](https://www.lsa.umich.edu/kelsey/)
- [Ann Arbor Hands-On Museum](https://www.aahom.org/)
- [Kerrytown Market and Shops](http://kerrytown.com/)
- [Wolverine Pickleball](https://wolverinepickleball.com/outdoor-fun/)

Looking for a quiet spot to relax? Several area coffee houses offer complimentary Wi-Fi and a cozy spot to spend time with a good book. You can also try the [Ann Arbor District Library](http://www.aadl.org/) or the [Michigan Union.](https://uunions.umich.edu/munion)

Wanting to venture outdoors? The [University of Michigan Nichols Arboretum](https://mbgna.umich.edu/nichols-arboretum/) [,](http://www.lsa.umich.edu/mbg/see/NicholsArboretum.asp) begun in 1907, is loaded with displays, nature trails, and unique pieces of art. Spend the day enjoying this treasure in the city.

There are numerous city parks surrounding the University of Michigan campus, as well. You can hike, kayak, canoe, golf, and more! Visit the City of Ann Arbor Parks and Recreation [information page](http://www.a2gov.org/departments/parks-recreation/Pages/default.aspx) for further details.
