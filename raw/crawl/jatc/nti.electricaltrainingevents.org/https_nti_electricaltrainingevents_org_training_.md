# TRAINING

The National Training Institute was started in 1990, then known as the “Instructor Training Institute,” to professionally train educators instructing IBEW Apprentices and Journeymen. Since then, the Instructor Training Institute has evolved into the NTI of today, offering World Class Training for Instructors, Apprentices, Journey-Level Workers, JATC Committee Members, Training Directors, and Local Union and Chapter Officers.

![](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/training_1-1-edited.jpg)

## Training Groups

### [I Group](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/igroup/)

I Group classes make up the instructor training program taught by Professional Educators from colleges and universities across the country. These Professional Education classes are offered at four levels, or years. If an instructor has taken all four years of the Instructor Training Program and would like to continue training in the I Group, NTI offers a three-year Advanced Studies Professional Education program.

[Learn More](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/igroup/)

![](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/I-Group_01.jpg)

### [T Group](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/tgroup/)

Participants who will be taking only technical training courses will be in the T Group. Technical courses are taught by manufacturers and their representatives, and cover the theory and knowledge of the topic being taught. This will help instructors in the classroom with knowledge of a topic, but will not provide teaching skills or tips.

[Learn More](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/tgroup/)

### [C/SC Group](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/cscgroup/)

The C Group consists of JATC Committee Members, Local Union and Chapter Officers, and Training Directors. A combination of general presentations and small group breakout sessions will allow the participant to choose their own areas of interest. The SC Group is an abbreviated form of C Group designed for participants who cannot attend the entire week of NTI.

[Learn More](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/cscgroup/)

![](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/training_3.jpg)

### [OAG Graduate Group](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/oagroup/)

The Outstanding Apprentice Graduate Group is a program designed for outstanding apprentice graduates. These individuals will be recognized individually during the Opening Ceremony of NTI, and will each be presented with an Outstanding Achievement Certificate, as well as gifts from _electrical training ALLIANCE_ Platinum Award winning Training Partners.

[Learn More](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/oagroup/)

### [O Group](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/ogroup/)

Individuals involved in the outside line construction or utility industries, and anyone interested in these industries should register for O Group. This program guarantees participants a better understanding of the opportunities available in the outside industry and what the _electrical training ALLIANCE_ is doing to help prepare the workforce to meet the growing demands of this sector.

[Learn More](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/ogroup/)

### [Office Manager / Staff Group](http://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/omgroup/)

OM Group is for JATC / AJATC Office Manager and/or Administrative Staff who assist the Training Director with all aspects of running an Apprenticeship Program.

[Learn More](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/omgroup/)

### [Training Director Development Group](http://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/tdgroup/)

The Training Director Development program equips electrical apprenticeship Training Directors with essential skills and cultivates a strong relationship with the _electrical training ALLIANCE_. This multi-year program enhances retention, fosters camaraderie among participants, and awards a prestigious certificate. It focuses on continuous education, preparing Directors to lead apprenticeship training committees effectively, with curriculum that empowers them in their roles and contributes to their apprenticeship program’s success.

[Learn More](https://nti.electricaltrainingevents.org/training/tdgroup/)
