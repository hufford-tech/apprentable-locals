### Attending NTI

This year’s NTI will be supported by the etA+ mobile app. This is an app that supports industry events.

**Please download the etA+ mobile application before arriving, if possible.** You will find helpful information there, and we will use the app to communicate with you throughout the event.

**etA+ Mobile Application Access**

To access the agenda for NTI, download the etA+ mobile app from the Apple App Store or Google Play Store (Search your app store for “etA Plus”).

Direct links are here as well:

etA+ on Apple App Store: [https://apps.apple.com/us/app/eta-plus/id6478505230](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/eta-plus/id6478505230)

etA+ on Google Play Store: [https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.combobulate.etaplus](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=io.combobulate.etaplus)

- **Step 01:** Visit the website or download the mobile app from your app store.
- **Step 02:**Register to create your account.

  - You must use the email address used for your registration. Then, you will automatically gain access to the event content.

  - The application uses multi-factor authentication to verify your account for security. When you register, you will receive an email with a code to verify your account access.

  - After you verify your email address, you can enter a phone number if you prefer to receive future login codes via text message (or you can skip this step).
- **Step 03:**Access the Event

  - Click “Access NTI” on the “NTI” event item.

  - If the application does not recognize you as an event attendee, you will be asked to enter your badge ID. This ID will also be provided to you at registration.

Please note: We are still adding content to the app for this year’s NTI, but we encourage attendees to download the app prior to arriving so that you don’t need to download it once you arrive. As soon as we complete adding the content for the event, it will automatically update for you on the app.
