## Public Safety & Community Trust

A Safer City- Improving Quality of Life - A Stronger Community

![IMG_8516 copy.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/b047f6_5775a57fc8514b6ebe0d0551b8355afa~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_923,h_355,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/IMG_8516%20copy.jpg)

Jacky’s Public Safety agenda includes:

- Pomona residents deserve high-quality first responder services, including making the reopening of Fire Station 181 in District 2 a top priority for the community.

- Public safety, fire response, and coordination of emergency medical services and resources to respond quickly and effectively.

- Building strong neighborhood partnerships, focusing on high-need areas, addressing traffic and pedestrian safety.

- Addressing Human Trafficking and the sex work around “The Blade”, to improve the overall quality of life across Pomona.

- A city where residents can trust that when they call for emergency services, they will receive prompt and reliable response times.
