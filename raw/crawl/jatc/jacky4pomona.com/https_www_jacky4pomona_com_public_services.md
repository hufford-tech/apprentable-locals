## Public Services

As taxpayers, we deserve strong, reliable services from every agency that serves our city—not paying more and getting less in return.

![DSC_4333.JPG](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/6ec04d_60082ce72732442dbe75753cf9f2b84c~mv2.jpg/v1/crop/x_0,y_634,w_5568,h_2143/fill/w_923,h_355,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/DSC_4333_JPG.jpg)

Jacky’s Public Service's agenda includes:

- Parks and recreation facilities with clean, accessible restrooms that are consistently staffed and maintained.

- Using our tax dollars responsibly to repair, clean and pave streets, ensure streetlights are fully functional, and invest in new lighting where it’s needed most.

- The public library is a vital resource and the backbone of our community—it should always be fully funded and accessible to all.
