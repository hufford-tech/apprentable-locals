## Jacky Supports Our Youth

A community’s future depends on the youth; safe spaces and real opportunities help our youth thrive and stay on track.

![Library Event 002.jpg](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/6ec04d_8ebb46a8c60b440880c2781171180fe2~mv2.jpg/v1/crop/x_0,y_85,w_960,h_369/fill/w_923,h_355,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/Library%20Event%20002.jpg)

Jacky’s agenda for Supporting Our Youth includes:

- Providing culturally inclusive, free programming accessible to all of our youth, keeping them busy and engaged in positive activities, helps build a stronger, more connected community.

- Our youth need safe, welcoming city spaces—such as well-maintained parks and recreation centers—supported by systems that ensure consistent supervision and community engagement.

- Working together for our youth with local schools, nonprofits, and churches benefits the entire community—because when families thrive, the whole city does too.

- Investing in our youth early strengthens families, improves public safety over time, and creates better long-term outcomes for the entire Pomona community.
