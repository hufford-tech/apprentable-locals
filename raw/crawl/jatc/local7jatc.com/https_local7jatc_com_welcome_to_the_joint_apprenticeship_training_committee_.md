# [Welcome to the Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee](https://local7jatc.com/welcome-to-the-joint-apprenticeship-training-committee/ "Permalink to Welcome to the Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee")

**Training**

The JATC’s goal is to provide the highest quality training to those interested in a career in the electrical industry. We provide classroom training and on the job training. You get paid while you learn!

**What Do Local 7 Electricians Do?**

Inside Wireman are electrical workers who install the power, lighting, controls and other electrical equipment in residential, commercial, and industrial buildings.

Installer Technitians specialize in the installation and maintenance of voice data and vidio systems.

**How Can I Become An Apprentice?**

The requirements for becoming an apprentice are:

1\. Minimum age 18

2\. High School Education

3\. One Year of High School Algebra

4\. Qualifying Score on an Aptitude Test

5\. Drug Free

Seminars are held from September to February explaining the program, its requirements and application process for those who are interested in becoming an apprentice. Please check the calandar to the left for seminar dates.
