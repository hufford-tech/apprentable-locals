## Application Information

Applications will be accepted Monday through Friday at our office located at:

1620 NW Gage Blvd, Topeka, Kansas between the hours of

8:00 - 12:00 a.m. and 1:00 - 4:00 p.m.

or by appointment.

If you live in the Parsons area, you may apply either in Topeka or by appointment in Parsons.

* * *

_**Documentation required to be eligible to take the aptitude test:**_

1. A non-refundable application fee of $25.00 is required to receive an application.

2. We need to see originals of your birth certificate, drivers license, social security card, transcript of high school grades or GED Certificate
3. We will make copies and return your origional documents

**_All applicants must meet the following minimum requirements to be eligible for an interview:_**

1. Age – Applicant must be a minimum of 18 years of age.

2. Education – Applicant must be a high school graduate or have a GED and a minimum of one (1) year of Algebra with a passing grade.
3. Aptitude – Applicant must receive a high score on the aptitude test.
4. Physical Condition – Applicant must be able to perform essential functions of electrical construction work.
5. Documentation – Applicant must complete and turn in all documentation within 20 days of receiving application to be considered complete.

All applicants must reside within the Local Union #226 jurisdiction.  The Committee will grant all applicants who meet the basic requirements an interview.  The eligible applicants will be notified by mail of the date, time and place to appear for their interview.  If an applicant fails to appear at the scheduled time, the Committee will consider the applicant is no longer interested in the program.  Apprentices will be selected in the order of applicants ranking resulting from the rating at their interview.

The Topeka Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training committee admits students of any race, color, national origin to all the rights, privileges, programs, and activities generally accorded or made available to students at the school.  It does not discriminate on the basis of race, color, national and ethnic origin in administration of its educational policies, scholarship and loan programs and other school-administered programs.
