## TOOLS & SUPPLIES REQUIRED

###### The following is the list of tools all apprentices are required to have on-the-job. No exceptions.    There may be  local suppliers that will have pre-assembled electrical apprentice tool sets ready for purchase at a discounted cost.

- (1) pair of side cutting pliers (8" or 9")

- (2) pairs of channel lock pliers or equal

- (1) six foot folding rule or a tape measure

- (1) non-pointed pocket knife

- (1) 4 inch flat screwdriver

- (1) 4 inch Phillips screwdriver

- (1) 6 inch, square shank, flat screwdriver

- (1) small level

- (1) sharpened pencil

- (1) hacksaw frame

- (2) wire strippers (6" and 12")

- (2) crescent wrenches (6" and 12")

- (1) tool box or bag to carry your tools
