# OUR CLASSES

Specialty trade skills mean nothing without a solid foundation of core skills. Our classes allow

students to specialize in their trade while still becoming a well-rounded electrician.

[![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/576deef6725e2552c36444f5/67696ff0-bed1-441e-aef7-f0c601e81c4e/56fa323e-f751-4a20-ae4f-40a3784f3b94.jpg)](https://www.sav-electricaltrainingalliance.com/5-year-program)

### 4 YEAR APPRENTICESHIP PROGRAM

We offer classroom instruction and PAID on the job training including: Electrical Power and Lighting Systems, Power Distribution Systems, Backup Power Generation and Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs)

[![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/576deef6725e2552c36444f5/f5d8c22a-c1f7-475b-b35d-5b1626aac85d/IMG_0580.jpeg)](https://www.sav-electricaltrainingalliance.com/10-3-classes)

### OSHA 10 & 30 CLASSES

OSHA 10-and-30 hour Construction outreach training classes are available to promote safe and efficient electrical construction workplace practices.

[![Lima01.jpg](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/576deef6725e2552c36444f5/1467082156516-KCX7ZHZF0EUDCYZ7KVLW/Lima01.jpg)](https://www.sav-electricaltrainingalliance.com/cpr)

### CPR CERTIFICATION

While you can’t predict when an emergency will occur, you can be prepared. In less time than you think, Savannah Electrical Training Alliance can give you the vital knowledge and skills you’ll need to respond to a life-threatening situation with confidence.
