# OSHA 10 & 30 CLASSES

* * *

## THE BASICS

The 10-hour class is intended for entry level workers, while the 30-hour class is more appropriate for supervisors or workers with some safety responsibility. Through this training, OSHA helps ensure that you are more knowledgeable about workplace hazards and your rights, and contribute to our nation's productivity.
