#### Dakotas Electrical Apprenticeship

The finest electrical workers receive their training through the Electrical Training Alliance of [**NECA**](https://www.dakotasneca.org/) and the [**IBEW**](https://www.ibew.org/) creating a unique model of educational partnership that sets the standard for excellence in the industry. This collaboration produces the best-trained and most up-to-date electrical apprentices and journeyworkers in the country, ensuring that our graduates are not only skilled but also well-prepared to meet the demands of an evolving field.

In a world that relies increasingly on electricity, there’s no substitute for excellent training. The Dakotas Electrical Apprenticeship program is designed to ensure that skilled labor is always available in the rapidly growing field of electrical contracting. Our comprehensive training covers a wide range of essential topics, including telecommunications wiring, fiber optics, fire alarms, programmable logic control, power quality, safe job practices, and the National Electrical Code. Apprentices also receive hands-on experience that prepares them for real-world challenges, making them invaluable assets to their employers. To view our areas covered, [**click here.**](https://dakotasjatc.org/geographical-areas-covered/)

With an impressive completion rate of over 85%, our apprentices successfully finish the program and embark on meaningful careers as highly paid, skilled, journey-level electrical workers. This achievement speaks volumes about the quality of our training and the commitment of our participants. Don’t delay your dreams—discover how you can build a rewarding career in the electrical construction industry today! Join us at Dakotas Electrical Apprenticeship and illuminate your future!
