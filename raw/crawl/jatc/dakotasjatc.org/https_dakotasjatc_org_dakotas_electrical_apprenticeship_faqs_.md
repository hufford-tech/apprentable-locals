#### Getting Started & Applying

The Dakotas Electrical Apprenticeship offers a simple, guided application process for anyone pursuing a career as an electrician. With year-round applications and an easy online portal, you can start whenever you’re ready. This section covers how to apply, qualification requirements, and where to learn more.

### [How do I apply to become an apprentice?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

Becoming an apprentice with the Dakotas Electrical Apprenticeship is a straightforward, step-by-step process. Select **“ [Learn More](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/dakotasjatc-org/login/index.php)”** on our website and create your applicant account. After we verify your information, you’ll receive instructions to log in, finish your application, and move through each required step. Your applicant dashboard will outline everything clearly.

### [Are there application deadlines?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

No. **We accept applications year-round**, and apprentices are selected throughout the year based on contractor and industry needs. You can apply at any time.

### [Are there any prerequisites to apply?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

There are no formal prerequisites, but applicants must meet the minimum qualifications listed on our website. If you’re unsure whether you qualify, **[contact us](https://dakotasjatc.org/contact/)**—we’re happy to help you understand your next steps.

### [How can I learn more about the apprenticeship program?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

Explore our website at [**dakotasjatc.org**](http://dakotasjatc.org/) or our [**Applicant Information**](https://dakotasjatc.org/applicants/) page for detailed program information. For personalized questions, call our office at [**(701) 297-5934**](tel:7012975934).

#### Jobs, Hiring & Employment

As a multi-employer program with more than 75 participating electrical contractors across North and South Dakota, the Dakotas Electrical Apprenticeship provides strong job availability. This section explains job openings, hiring demand, acceptance timelines, and how job placement works within the program.

### [Are there apprenticeship job openings?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

Yes. We work with **more than 75 contractors**, and when you apply with us, you apply to the full network. Construction activity varies, but contractors consistently need new apprentices throughout the year.

### [How does job placement work for apprentices?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

If you are selected into the program, you do not need to find a job on your own. We place apprentices with participating employers in our multi-employer system, ensuring steady work opportunities as you progress.

### [When are apprentices selected?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

Selections occur **year-round**, depending on workforce needs, project schedules, and contractor demand.

### [What is the acceptance rate?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

There is no fixed acceptance rate. Selection is based on regional workforce needs, which change throughout the year. Some applicants are placed quickly; others may remain on the eligibility list for up to one year.

#### Training, Classes & Experience

Our USDOL-registered apprenticeship blends hands-on learning with structured classroom instruction. This section explains class requirements, transfer credits, and travel expectations, ensuring applicants understand what training will look like.

### [Can previous electrical coursework or experience transfer into the program?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

We evaluate all prior electrical training and experience. Many electrical programs within the Dakotas region transfer into the related instruction portion of apprenticeship. Contact our office to confirm whether your coursework qualifies.

### [Can I attend apprenticeship classes without being employed by a contractor?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

No. Apprentices must be employed by a participating contractor to attend classes. If you need help identifying employers, contact your nearest **IBEW Local Union**.

### [How much travel is required as an apprentice?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

Travel depends on contractor needs and project locations. Most apprentices travel **100–200 miles** within the Dakotas, either daily or weekly. Some contractors provide vehicles, while others require the use of your personal vehicle, depending on job needs and availability.

#### Career Path & Work Expectations

Electricians work in a variety of environments, and an apprenticeship gives you experience across residential, commercial, and industrial settings. This section helps applicants understand what electricians do and clarifies the difference between inside electrical work and outside line (lineman) work.

### [What do electricians do, and is this the right career for me?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

Electricians work in diverse environments—from homes and commercial buildings to data centers, industrial facilities, and utility projects. Apprentices may install underground wiring, pull cable, set panels, or assist with building-automation systems. Our USDOL-registered apprenticeship exposes you to the full spectrum of electrical work so you can build your skills and find the specialty that fits you best.

### [How do I become a lineman?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

The Dakotas Electrical Apprenticeship covers **inside electrical work only**. For lineman (outside line) careers, contact the appropriate **IBEW Outside Line Apprenticeship Program**.

#### Costs, Pay & Benefits

The Dakotas Electrical Apprenticeship is an earn-while-you-learn program, offering competitive wages, consistent raises, and excellent benefits. This section explains typical costs and outlines how pay increases as you advance.

### [Are there costs associated with apprenticeship?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

Yes, but they are minimal compared to traditional education. Apprentices will need:

- Personal workwear
- Basic hand tools
- A laptop for coursework
- Textbooks (new or used)

Only annual digital courseware subscriptions are billed separately.

### [What are the pay and benefits for apprentices?](https://dakotasjatc.org/dakotas-electrical-apprenticeship-faqs/\#)

Apprentice wages increase with each step of the program, ensuring a competitive starting wage and regular raises. Apprentices also receive excellent health insurance and retirement benefits. Rates vary by location and are determined through collective bargaining. For specifics, contact our office or your local **IBEW Union**.
