# Welcome to Butler Warren Co. EJATC!

![singlePostImage](https://www.hamiltonohiojatc.org/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmedia.linkedunion.com%2Fapps%2Fibew648jatc%2Fnews%2FPhoto%2520May%252030%25202025%252C%25208%252029%252041%2520PM.jpg&w=3840&q=75)

At Butler Warren Co. EJATC, we are dedicated to training the next generation of skilled electricians and telecommunication workers. Our apprenticeship programs provide a structured pathway for individuals seeking a rewarding career in the electrical or telecommunications industry. Through a combination of hands-on experience and classroom instruction, apprentices gain the knowledge and expertise needed to succeed.

As a partnership between the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW Local Union 648) and the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA), our program ensures that apprentices receive high-quality training, competitive wages, and real-world job experience. With experienced instructors, state-of-the-art training facilities, and a commitment to safety and excellence, we prepare apprentices to become highly skilled professionals in the field.

Whether you're just starting your journey or looking to advance your skills, our apprenticeship program offers the support and resources you need for a successful career. Join us and take the first step toward a bright future in the trade.
