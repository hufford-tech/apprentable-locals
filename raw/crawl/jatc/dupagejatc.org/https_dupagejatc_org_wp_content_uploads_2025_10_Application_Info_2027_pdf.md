## APPLICATION INFORMATION 2027

DuPage County JATC will be accepting applications online starting on 10/1/25 through September 30, 2026 for the class that will start in 2027.

## You must APPLY ONLINE-Click the link found on our website dupagejatc.org

There are two training programs available: Commercial Electrician and Telecommunications Electrician.

## APPLICATION FEE: $20 fee for each program. Paid online

An Aptitude Test will be given in November 2026 to those with completed applications. The Aptitude Test is two parts: Part I is Algebra. Part II is Reading Comprehension. Test scores range from 1-9. Your score must be a 4 or higher to be interviewed in January or February 2027.

After the interview, your name will be placed on an eligibility list. Selection from that list will be determined by the number of job openings. Work will begin in spring 2027. Classes will start in fall 2027.

DuPage County JATC will not discriminate against apprenticeship applicants or apprentices based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex (including pregnancy and gender identity), sexual orientation, genetic information, or because they are an individual with a disability or a person 40 years old or older. DuPage County JATC will take affirmative action to provide equal opportunity in apprenticeship and will operate the apprenticeship program as required under Title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations, part 30.

# APPLICATION INSTRUCTIONS
(Application Period: October 2025 - September 2026)
Please read all instructions carefully!

I understand that it is my responsibility to provide all of the following information. I further acknowledge that I must submit all required documents to the JATC by October 31, 2026.

## YOU MUST SUBMIT ITEMS 1-4:

1. Upload a copy of your valid Driver's License. If you are driving on a ticket, submit a copy of the ticket AND State I.D.

2. Upload a copy of your birth certificate (minimum of 17 years of age).

3. Upload transcript(s) for high school AND post high school education and training. All GED records must be submitted if applicable.

4. If not already provided on transcripts submitted, provide evidence of successful completion of one full year of high school Algebra I or one post high school Algebra I equivalent. (Minimum "C" average required for Commercial (Inside) and Telecommunication programs.)

Letters of recommendation, personal merit citations, military training as listed on a DD-214, achievement awards, etc. may also be provided. These items must be received by October 31, 2026.

• Photocopied transcripts, outdated transcripts, transcripts identified as unofficial, or transcripts in unsealed envelopes, WILL NOT be accepted.

• Mailing Address: DuPage County JATC, 28600 Bella Vista Pkwy, Suite 1500, Warrenville, IL 60555

## INCOMPLETE APPLICATIONS WILL NOT BE PROCESSED!

## PLEASE NOTE:
All applicants who meet minimum requirements and have a completed application by the deadline will be scheduled for the aptitude test. You will receive an email with the date and time of your aptitude test. This information will also be available in your Trade School account.

There is an online study guide available at ElectricPrep.com. There is a fee for this study guide. Another helpful website is: http://www.electricianapprenticehq.com/how-to-join-ibew-apprenticeship/

Online math course for those unable to meet the math requirement for apprenticeship programs: www.skillsprep.org
There is a fee for this course. You can choose not to purchase the textbook and/or calculator. (These items can be found online at a lower price)

In order to qualify for an oral interview, an applicant must obtain a minimum score of "4" or higher, using the electrical trade's aptitude test developed and validated by the American Institutes for Research. The number of qualified applicants interviewed may be determined by selecting the top aptitude scorers equaling 500% of the anticipated positions available. You are not guaranteed an interview.

Prior to being indentured, applicants selected from the pool of interviewed applicants must be at least 18 years of age.
Prior to being indentured, applicants selected from the pool of interviewed applicants will be required to provide results of a physical examination and drug tests, which include testing for marijuana, as determined by the JATC.
