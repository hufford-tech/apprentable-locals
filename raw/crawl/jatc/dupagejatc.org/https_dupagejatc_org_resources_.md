# To Make It.

We want to see you succeed, and make sure you make it. So when you’re part of the brotherhood, there are many resources available to you. If you have questions or need assistance in getting started, just reach out to the IBEW Local 701, PowerForward DuPage, the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, or the Electrical Training Alliance. We all want to see you succeed. We all want to you to MAKE IT.
