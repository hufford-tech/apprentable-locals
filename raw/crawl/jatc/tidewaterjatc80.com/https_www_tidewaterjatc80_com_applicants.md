# Earn While You Learn – Become an Electrician and Join the Union Family!

You're about to take a life-changing step toward a fulfilling career. As an electrician, you'll play a vital role in powering homes, businesses, and entire communities.

By joining the Union, you're becoming part of a strong, supportive brotherhood and sisterhood that has your back. Enjoy great pay, excellent benefits, and a career that provides for you and your family.

The Tidewater Training Center offers a top-notch electrical apprenticeship designed to prepare you for success. Our mission is to train skilled, professional electricians who can meet the electrical construction demands of today ... and tomorrow.

Everything you need to start your journey is right here. Take your first steps to becoming a union electrician!
