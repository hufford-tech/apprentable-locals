## General   Information

[What is the Inside Wireman Apprenticeship Program?](https://www.tidewaterjatc80.com/applicants/faqs)

**It is a 4-year systematic program of learning.** Apprentices can obtain all the necessary knowledge and skills to become a Journeyman Inside Wireman.

**8000 Hours of On-The-Job Training.** Apprentices train to proficiency in all skills required to install, maintain, and troubleshoot various electrical systems on commercial and industrial job sites.

**720 Hours of Related Supplemental instruction (minimum):** Apprentices will study theories related to all job tasks essential to the trade. Classroom, hands-on lab, and online training help reinforce skills learned during on-the-job training.

[What type of work does an Inside Wireman Apprentice do?](https://www.tidewaterjatc80.com/applicants/faqs)

Electrical power and lighting systems, power distribution and metering systems, motor control, building automation systems, energy management systems, backup power generation systems, and power supply systems.

[Is the apprenticeship full-time or part-time?](https://www.tidewaterjatc80.com/applicants/faqs)

The apprentices work full time during the week and attend evening classes on Monday and Wednesday for 3-hour sessions.

[What states and cities are in your jurisdiction?](https://www.tidewaterjatc80.com/applicants/faqs)

**Virginia:** Cities of Chesapeake, Norfolk, Portsmouth, Suffolk, and Virginia Beach. Counties of Accomack, Brunswick, Greensville, Lunenburg, Mecklenburg, Northampton, and Southampton

**North Carolina:** Counties of Camden, Chowan, Currituck, Dare, Gates, Pasquotank, Tyrrell, and Washington.

[Will I receive any health or pension benefits?](https://www.tidewaterjatc80.com/applicants/faqs)

Yes. IBEW Local Union #80 has negotiated the following Benefits:

- Family Health Insurance Coverage at no cost to employee
- 3% gross payroll Company contribution Retirement Pension (all apprentices & journeymen)
- 7% gross payroll Company contribution Retirement Pension (2nd year and above)
- $1 per hour Company contribution Annuity Pension (2nd year and above)
- National Pension Retirement Plan (When an apprentice becomes a member of IBEW Local Union # 80)
- Credit Union Savings Plan: 4% of gross weekly payroll is automatically deducted and deposited into your personal credit union account. (All apprentices and journeymen).

[Are there any out-of-pocket expenses to be an apprentice?](https://www.tidewaterjatc80.com/applicants/faqs)

Yes. All apprentices are responsible for paying an annual registration fee. The fee for each school year is $1,250.00 to be paid at the time of acceptance into the program. Apprentices are also responsible for their own tool kits, which averages about $250. Durable work clothes and steel-toe boots are required as well.

[Do I need my own tools?](https://www.tidewaterjatc80.com/applicants/faqs)

Yes, you will need to provide your own tools. Per Section 3.15 of the Collective Bargaining Agreement, all employees covered by this agreement shall provide themselves with and keep in first-class condition a kit of tools consisting of:

- Tool Pouch & Belt (electrician's pocket type)
- Screwdrivers: Straight type; Small, Medium & Large Phillips type #1 & #2
- Diagonal Cutting Pliers
- High Leverage Side Cutters
- Long or Needle Nose Pliers
- Two (2) pairs of Channel Lock Pliers (or equivalent)
- 6-foot Wood Rule (metal tape measures not allowed)
- Knife (pocket type)
- Torpedo Level
- Hammer (16OZ. straight claw)
- Voltage Tester (Non-Contact)
- Hacksaw Frame
- Pencil
