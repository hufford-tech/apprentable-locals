You've Got Questions...

We've Got Answers!

How do I begin the application process?

**You will need to apply online from** [**APPLICATION PAGE**](https://www.wcpaejatc.org/how-to-apply/)

- Provide necessary information
- If you are currently doing electrical work, let us know (you might receive credit for the aptitude test)

What is the aptitude test?

- This is both a reading and a math test.
- It is a timed test
- Both parts are graded, then combined for an overall test score which is graded on a scale of 1-9
- You must receive a 4 or higher to qualify for an oral interview
- If you receive a 3 or less, in order to try again you must reapply
- 6 months must pass before you can take the aptitude test again.

What do I need to do if I qualify for an interview?

If you qualify for an interview

- Appear 10 minutes before your scheduled interview
- Dress appropriately
- Pay attention fo the interview questions
- Ask questions, if you have any
- You will be notified whether or not you have been accepted into our apprenticeship program
