Upon Completion

Upon passing the 5th year in the classroom and completing 8000 hours of OJT, you would become a Journeyman Wireman status. At this point, you would be awarded IBEW Journeyman papers, as well as a Pennsylvania State certificate qualifying you as a Journeyman Wireman.

Your certifications means that at the end of your five-year apprenticeship program, you will be a certified electrician capable of performing all aspects of the electrical field.

You will receive training in

- Residential, Commercial & Industrial Electrical Work
- Electrical Building Systems
- Data Systems & Fiber Optics
- Safety
- Fire Alarm
- Security
- Nurse Call & Other Systems
