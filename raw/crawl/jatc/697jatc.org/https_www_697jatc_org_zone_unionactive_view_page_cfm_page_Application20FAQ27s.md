| Application FAQ's



|
|




**Q - How much will this program cost me?**



**A -** Once an applicant becomes an apprentice, the cost to the apprentice is negligible, roughly $350.00 annually for the cost of books.



* * *



**Q - Can I continue to work at my current job while completing the apprenticeship?**



**A -** 10,000 hours of **O** n-the- **J** ob **T** raining is required to complete the apprenticeship. Participating contractors provide OJT. Apprentices earn a wage while performing the OJT portion of their training. You will be expected to attend full time employment at your OJT assignment.



* * *



**Q - How much will I be paid while being trained?**



**A -** Inside Wireman Apprentices start the program earning 32.5% of what an Inside Journeyman worker earns: $16.90 per hour (1/6/2026). Raises are earned at pre-determined intervals throughout the 5 years.



Telecommunications Apprentices start the program earning 40% of what a Telecommunications Journeyman worker earns: $16.60 per hour (1/6/2026). Raises are earned at pre-determined intervals throughout the 3 years.



* * *



**Q - Are classes held in the evenings?**



**A -** Apprentices attend class one day every other week, for 8 hours each day. Apprentices do not receive payment for the time spent in class. Some classes may require evening attendance.



* * *



**Q - How often are Apprentice classes taken?**



**A -** Apprentices are selected from the list of ranked applicants based on the need of the electrical industry.



* * *



**Q - Am I required to live in Lake County, Indiana?**



**A -** While the majority of your training will be within the borders of Lake County, there is no requirement to live within the county.
