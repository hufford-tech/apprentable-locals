**Welcome to the career opportunity that could change your life!**



**PLEASE DO NOT APPLY UNLESS YOU ARE SURE WHAT YOU ARE APPLYING FOR!**



\\*\\* It's recommended that you read the " [Applying for our Programs](https://www.82jatc.com/?zone=/unionactive/view_page.cfm&page=Applying20for20our20Programs)" link before starting the online process.



\*\*ONLINE APPLICANTS\*\*

**Step 1.** You will need to create a new account.

**Step 2.** You will receive an email with a link to finish creating your account.

**Step 3.** You will receive an additional email once your account has been approved.



(Approvals happen during  normal business hours)



  - Follow the link to log in, then click on the "Application" tab.
  - You will need to select the program in which you are applying for: _**INSIDE or TELECOMMUNICATIONS**_

**Step 4.** Follow instructions to start the application and to pay a $20.00 application fee.

**Step 5**. You will need to have your high school or college transcripts and your VALID Driver's license in a separate digital file available to upload during the application process. Acceptable formats can be PDF or a clear quality .JPG

**Step 6.** You will need to complete all parts of the application process (not all at one time) and you will be made aware when you have completed it.

Please create a new account by **[CLICKING HERE](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/ibew82-org/login/index.php)**.
