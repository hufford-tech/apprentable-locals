Incomplete documentation will result in closure of your file.<br>  - No documents will be accepted outside of a scheduled appointment.<br>**Step III – Testing Requirements**<br>  - All assigned testing dates are **mandatory**.<br>     <br>     <br>  - Failure to attend will result in closure of your file.<br>## IMPORTANT APPOINTMENT GUIDELINES<br>To ensure a smooth and fair scheduling process, please carefully follow the guidelines below for call-in appointments.<br>  - **Start Time:** All call-in appointments begin promptly at **8:00 AM** on designated call-in days.<br>  - **Scheduling Process:** Appointments must be scheduled **by phone only** on the designated call-in day.<br>  - **No Early Appointments:** Appointments cannot be scheduled in advance. <br>  - **No Alternative Scheduling Processes:** Walk-ins, voicemails, and emails will not be accepted for appointment scheduling.<br>  - **Refer to the Calendar:** Always consult the [official calendar](https://www.ocett.org/Application%20Interview%20Testing%20Calendar%202026%20v5.pdf) for specific call-in dates.<br>Thank you for your understanding and cooperation. |  | |
