The Legal Services Plan offers you legal services you may need for real estate transactions, name changes, document reviews, wills and other legal matters. The Plan provides high-quality legal advice, counseling and representation. Whatever you discuss with your lawyer will be held in strict confidence. This benefit is based on contributions made on your behalf by your employer. These services are provided only in the states of New York, New Jersey, Connecticut and Pennsylvania. All benefits are considered taxable income to the eligible participant and the Plan will report the value of the benefit to the IRS on a W-2 form.

## Plan Overview

Below are the Legal Services Plan highlights.

[Summary Plan Description](https://www.jibei.org/wp-content/uploads/legal-servcies-plan-of-the-electrical-industry-spd.pdf)

### Eligibility and Participation

To be eligible for the legal services provided by this Plan, you must be:

- An active member of Local Union No. 3 who:A. is currently employed or has been employed, in a classification covered under the Plan and whose Employer is/was required to contribute to this Plan, or has been employed and available for employment in accordance with the rules of the Plan: andB. has been so employed or available for employment for at least the four consecutive years immediately prior to seeking the benefits provided by this Plan
- Effective January 1, 2017, a retired Participant who has retired on a Standard, Early Standard or Disability Pension from the Pension Trust Fund and who was covered by the Plan immediately prior to his/her retirement
- The spouse of an individual eligible under paragraph A or B who is living with and is not legally or in fact separated or divorced from the eligible Employee or Retiree.

### When Eligibility Ends

Your eligibility to receive benefits provided by this Plan ends if:

- You are no longer employed or available for employment by a contributing employer
- A particular benefit is discontinued by the Plan
- The Plan is terminated
- The Trustees determine that you have engaged in conduct which warrants termination of benefits

### Legal Services Benefits

**You’ll receive high-quality legal services.** The following is a sample of the legal services offered by the Plan, as long as they do not arise out of business or commercial ventures:

- Consultations
- Real estate matters
- Landlord – tenant matters
- Adoption
- Wills
- Document review
- Name change
- Elder law
- Estate planning
- Trusts (up to $2500/once per lifetime)

The value of these benefits is considered taxable income for you and will be reported to the IRS by the Plan Administrator accordingly.

### Limitation of Benefits

Legal services will not be provided for:

- Matters arising from commercial or business ventures
- Matters arising out of tax issues
- Matters in the nature of a class action
- Defense of civil matters not expressly covered by the Plan
- Matters arising out of ownership or use of a vehicle
- Any matters arising out of claims against the Plan Administrator, the Trustees, JIB, or any of its related organizations

In addition, the Plan does not pay for any disbursements, expenses, or filing fees incurred with performance of the legal services provided.

### Claims for Benefits

To obtain legal services provided by the Plan, contact the Legal Services Plan office at JIB at (718) 591-2000, Extension 1397. At that time, it will be determined if you are eligible, and whether the services you require are covered by the Plan.

## Frequently Asked Questions

- ### Does the Legal Services Plan have panel attorneys outside of the tri-state area?









No. Panel attorneys are referred to participants who live in New York, New Jersey, Connecticut and Pennsylvania only.

- ### Will the Legal Services Plan cover the cost of the attorney to represent me in court?









No. If you request a panel attorney to represent you in court, the Plan will not cover this expense.

- ### Does the Legal Services Plan handle divorce matters?









No. The Plan does not cover matrimonial or domestic matters.

- ### What costs are covered under the Plan?









The Legal Services Plan covers the panel attorney fee for covered services only. This benefit is taxable to the participant. Therefore, you will receive a W-2 Form at the end of the year. The Plan will not pay for additional costs associated with the legal service provided. For example, if you purchase a house, the Plan will pay the attorney’s fee, but you will be responsible for other associated costs (i.e. closing costs, appraisals, etc.).

- ### What services are available under the Legal Services Plan?









The Legal Service Plan includes the following services:



  - consultations
  - real estate matters
  - landlord/tenant matters
  - uncontested adoption proceedings
  - wills and health care proxies
  - document reviews
  - names changes
  - elder law/estate planning.\*

For more details about these covered services, as well as a list of non-covered services, please refer to the [Legal Services Plan SPD](https://www.jibei.org/wp-content/uploads/legal-services-plan-of-the-electrical-industry-spd.pdf "Legal Services Plan of the Electrical Industry SPD").

\\* The Plan covers a portion of the services only. Please consult your referred attorney or the Plan for covered expenses under the elder law/estate planning benefit.

- ### Can I use the Legal Services Plan for my family members?









The only other family member who is eligible for the Legal Services Plan is the legal spouse who is living with and not separated from the covered participant. Legal Services for children, parents or grandparents are not covered.
