## Electronic Contribution Submission

[Electronic submission](https://remit.jibei.org/) is easier and more efficient — the data is transferred to our system with just a couple of clicks and, you only have to issue one check.  Employers may also elect to process their payment electronically via ACH payment.

Electronic payroll submission requires a password which has already been assigned.
