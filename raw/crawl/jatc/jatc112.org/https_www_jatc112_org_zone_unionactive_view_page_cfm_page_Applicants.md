What is the Electrical Apprenticeship Program?

The finest electrical workers in the country receive their training through the Electrical Training ALLIANCE (ALLIANCE) of the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW). | |     |

[Application Steps](https://www.jatc112.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_article.cfm&HomeID=863746&page=Applicants "Read Full Article")


The Columbia Basin Electrical Training Alliance Inside Wireman Apprenticeship Applications are AVAILABLE as needed throughout the year. Please check our website to get information about our next application window. Applications are submitted online and are available 24-7 during that window. There is a $50.00 application fee payable by credit/debit card at the time of application creation. | |     |

Click above to access the online application link. Create an account by clicking "Create Account" and filling out the form to request a new account. Once your account has been approved, read the online application introduction text, pay the $50.00 application fee with a major credit card or debit card, and complete the online application, including turning in your high school transcript(s). |
