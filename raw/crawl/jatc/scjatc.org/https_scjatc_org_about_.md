# About

## The Sioux City Joint Electrical Apprenticeship Program

The Sioux City Electrical Apprenticeship empowers individuals through education, mentorship, and hands-on experience. Sponsored by the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local 231 and the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA), our program trains skilled electricians ready to excel in a dynamic industry. With our "earn while you learn" approach, apprentices gain real-world experience, graduate debt-free within five years, and enter a high-demand field with excellent pay and benefits.

Beyond [apprenticeships](https://scjatc.org/apprenticeship/), we offer [continuing education for journey-level electricians](https://scjatc.org/continuing-education/), ensuring they stay updated with the latest advancements. Located in Sioux City, Iowa, we are dedicated to building a community of professionals who take pride in their craft and are passionate about innovation and growth.

Whether starting your career or advancing your skills, the Sioux City Electrical Apprenticeship offers the tools, training, and support for a bright future. Join us and take the next step toward a rewarding professional life.
