# Journeyman Continuing Education

## Stay Current, Stay Competitive

The Sioux City Electrical Apprenticeship provides comprehensive continuing education programs designed to help journey-level electricians stay current with industry advancements and maintain their professional credentials. These offerings include courses focused on the latest updates to the National Electrical Code, safety training such as OSHA 10 compliance, and a variety of specialized classes.

By participating in these courses, Local 231 members can enhance their expertise, expand their skill sets, and ensure compliance with state and local regulations. The program emphasizes hands-on training and practical knowledge, preparing participants to meet the evolving demands of the electrical industry. Accessible and relevant, these continuing education options reinforce the commitment of the Sioux City Electrical Apprenticeship to lifelong learning and professional excellence.
