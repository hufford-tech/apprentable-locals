## Providing top-tier training for electrical apprentices and journeymen, backed by IBEW Local 231 and NECA partnerships.

We are a joint electrical apprenticeship training program that provides training for electrical apprentices and also provides CEU training for journey level electricians.

## Apprenticeship Program

![Students working on electrical projects in a classroom.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201000%20923'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The Sioux City Electrical Apprenticeship Program offers a comprehensive five-year "Earn While You Learn" training designed to equip individuals with the skills and knowledge necessary to become licensed journeyman electricians.

## Journeyman Continuing Education

![Students working on electrical projects in a classroom.](data:image/svg+xml,%3Csvg%20xmlns='http://www.w3.org/2000/svg'%20viewBox='0%200%201000%20923'%3E%3C/svg%3E)

The Sioux City Electrical Apprenticeship offers a Journeyman Continuing Education Program designed to help licensed electricians stay current with industry standards, enhance their skills, and maintain licensure requirements.
