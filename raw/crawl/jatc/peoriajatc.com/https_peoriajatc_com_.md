## Your Career Potential

![](https://peoriajatc.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/AdobeStock_352908897.jpg)

### Inside wireman

The Inside Wireman installs and maintains all of the various types of electrical systems found in commercial and industrial facilities.

[Learn More](https://peoriajatc.com/inside-wireman/)

![](https://peoriajatc.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/08/AdobeStock_76486945.jpg)

### residential wireman

The Residential Wireman installs and maintains the electrical systems in homes and other types of residential installations.

[Learn More](https://peoriajatc.com/residential-wireman/)

![A man with a digital tablet working in a server room.](https://peoriajatc.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/AdobeStock_296696957.jpg)

### telecom installer-technician

The Telecommunications Installer Technician installs the network of low voltage cabling that is used for video, voice, and data or other low voltage signaling.

[Learn More](https://peoriajatc.com/telecom-installer/)
