# inside wireman

![Electrician Fixing the Light Inside Remodeled Apartment.](https://peoriajatc.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/07/AdobeStock_352908897.jpg)

The **Inside Wireman** installs and maintains all of the various types of electrical systems found in commercial and industrial facilities. This may include:

Planning & initiating Projects

Providing temporary power

Installing service equipment and complete grounding systems

Raceway planning and installation

Wiring up various forms of motor controls, HVAC systems, and specialized equipment

Receptacle and Lighting system installation

Troubleshooting and repairing of Electrical Systems and components

Fire alarm, data, and security system installation

Building Automation System and Energy Management Systems

Installation of Systems both high overhead and underground

[JATC Entry Flow Chart](https://docs.google.com/presentation/d/12Fw7gUSDZ5gdSDJ6JYUIKrCW5M5_qsnpWDBYSFWglQM/present?slide=id.g6273ed2ef9_0_0)

In order to accomplish these duties, Inside Wiremen employ a variety of tools ranging from one-hand and two-handed tools to power tools such as drills and tuggers. They also are frequently required to operate equipment such as trenchers and aerial lifts.

## COMPENSATION

During their apprenticeship, apprentices are paid at a graduated rate based upon the number of working hours they have logged.
