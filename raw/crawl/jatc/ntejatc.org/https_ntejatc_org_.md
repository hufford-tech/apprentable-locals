The North Texas Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training Center is affiliated the Electrical Training Alliance. We are a team of industry partners that have properly trained hundreds of thousands of men and women to become competent Journeyman Electricians. As a result, these individuals enjoy the benefits of being a part of this extraordinary and exciting industry, including:

- Excellent Wage Rates
- Paid Health Insurance
- Guaranteed Pension Benefits
- Dedicated Training Facility
- Safer Working Conditions
- College Credit for Apprentices
- Advancement Opportunities
- A Career With A Future

Apply online. [Click here](https://ntejatc.org/index.php/applicant) for more information.

### About Us

The North Texas Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee is sponsored by the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) and the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA), to comprise the largest, most comprehensive electrical Journeyman and Apprentice training programs available in this country.
