# Applicant Information

Applicants are required to apply online following the instructions supplied [here](https://ntejatc.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Application-Steps-for-North-Texas-Electrical-JATC2.pdf). **All applications require a $20.00 fee**. This fee will be credited to accepted apprentices who successfully complete Math Refresher and register for 1st Year, 1st Semester.

## MINIMUM QUALIFICATIONS

These minimum qualifications determine eligibility for an interview.

- At least 18 years of age
- High School graduate, have a GED, or 2 year associate degree or higher
- One year of high school algebra, with passing grades (GED algebra not accepted)\* If you do not meet the high school algebra requirement, a Tech Math course from the electrical training Alliance can be used to fulfill this requirement. You can find out more information about the Electrical Training Alliance’s Tech Math Course by [clicking here](https://www.skillsprep.org/login/index.php).
- Driver License (Without restriction B)
- Texas Electrical Apprentice License
- Score a 5 or higher on the NJATC Aptitude Test.
- Physically capable of doing electrical construction work

All applicants who are accepted into the Apprenticeship Program will have a drug screen and maybe required to take a physical exam which will include color blindness.

## REQUIRED DOCUMENTATION

- Copy of Birth Certificate
- Valid Drivers License (Without restriction B)
- Texas Electrical Apprentice License
- Official High School Transcript and/or College Transcript with an associates degree or higher
  - An official high school transcript can be mailed directly to us from your institution.
  - Emailed transcripts are not accepted.
  - Unofficial transcripts are not accepted.
- Social Security Card
- GED Certificate (If applicable.)
- College Transcript (If applicable.)
- Veterans DD214 – Long Form – as proof of service (If applicable)

## Texas Electrical Apprentice License

Go to [www.tdlr.texas.gov/electricians/](http://www.tdlr.texas.gov/electricians/) and click on “Apprentice Electrician” under “Individual Licenses” and enter your information. You will need a debit/credit card to make the $20.00 payment. Your physical license will be mailed to you in a few weeks.

Individuals with criminal records will need to fill out a supplemental information sheet. Please keep in mind that TDLR will take weeks to process your application if you are required to complete the supplemental information sheet.

You will need to upload an image of your Texas Electrical Apprentice License or Temporary Texas Electrical Apprentice License during the application process.

## APTITUDE TEST

After your paperwork has been reviewed and it is confirmed that you meet the qualifications, you will be scheduled for the NJATC Aptitude Test.

The Aptitude Test is made up of two parts:

- Algebra and Functions, and
- Reading Comprehension.

It takes approximately two to three hours to complete the test. To qualify for an interview, you must score a 5 or higher on the NJTAC Aptitude Test.

## INTERVIEW

The Apprenticeship Committee will notify applicants of the date and time of their interviews. Interviews are held at the North Texas Electrical Training Center. The Committee will select the people they feel are the top prospects for the electrical industry. Points that can be taken into consideration at the interview are:

- Educational background,
- Extent of math background,
- Character,
- Interest,
- Cooperativeness,
- Attitude, and
- Personal appearance.

## TERM OF APPRENTICESHIP

The term of apprenticeship is 8,000 hours of on-the-job training divided over 4 years with 180 hours of related classroom instruction each year, plus an 8 week math refresher class.

## ON-THE-JOB TRAINING

Applicants selected will start their on-the-job training as soon as there is a job opening.

Starting salary is $20.33 an hour, with increment raises throughout the program, plus fringe benefits including:

- Free family health insurance,
- Free retirement and
- Free annuity after the first year.

## SCHOOL

Classes are held at the North Texas Electrical Training Center, one day every two weeks from 7:00 AM – 3:30 PM.  After you complete Math Refresher, there are 2 semesters per year starting in January and July of each year. You will receive college credit through Dallas College – North Lake Campus.

## COMPUTER REQUIREMENTS:

Apprentices are required to have a desktop or laptop computer

## COST

Apprentices purchase their books and pay tuition at the beginning of each semester.

Book and course fees averages $1,500 per year

Tuition cost is $316.00 per semester (Out of state is higher)

Total cost over 4 years is approximately $7,200.

NOTE: Costs are approximate – All above costs are subject to change as exact costs are determined.

## Direct Entry

North Texas Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee offers Direct Entry after interview from two different pre-apprenticeship programs. [Veterans Electrical Entry Program (VEEP)](https://in2veep.com/) and the [IBEW-NECA Electrical College.](https://apply.protechskillsinstitute.org/texas)

### About Us
