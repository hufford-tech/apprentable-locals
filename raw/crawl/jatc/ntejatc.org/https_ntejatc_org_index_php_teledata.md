# Teledata

## **Online Teledata Training Courses**

The following five courses are offered to IBEW 20 members holding TeleData classifications. The following courses address the training requirements for Installers level 1-5.

- Level 1 Course – Low Voltage Structured Cabling Systems  (includes Grounding training). **Cost – $288.62**
  - You are required to take this course and have 2,000 hours to become a Level 2 Installer.
- Level 2 Course – Fiber optics. **Cost – $246.98**
  - You are required to take this course and have 4,000 hours to become a Level 3 Installer.
- Level 3 Course – Security (includes Access Control). **Cost – $155.02**
  - You are required to take this course and have 6,000 hours to become a Level 4 Installer.
- Level 4 Course – Audio-Video (A/V and Streaming Technology) **. Cost – $129.24**
  - You are required to take this and have 8,000 hours to become a Level 5 Installer.
- Level 5 Course – Networking Technology. **Cost – $138.74**
  - You are required to take this course, have 10,000 or more hours, and have a BICSI certifcation to advance to Technician.

Each course is self-paced but must be completed in 90 days.  Multiple courses may be taken simultaneously, but it is recommended that no more than 3 are taken at one time.  Each course is stand-alone and has no prerequisites.  Access to a computer and a reliable internet connection is required.

Once registered, books will be ordered.

You can check your hours with the Local Union to see if you have enough hours to advance.
