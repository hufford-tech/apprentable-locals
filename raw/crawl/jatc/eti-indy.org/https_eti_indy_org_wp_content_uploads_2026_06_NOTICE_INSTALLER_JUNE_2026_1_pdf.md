# NOTICE OF APPRENTICESHIP OPPORTUNITY FOR THE POSITION OF INSTALLER TECHNICIAN (TELECOM) APPRENTICE

The Electrical Training Institute of Indianapolis (also known as the Indianapolis Electrical JATC) is 
currently accepting applications for those applicants interested in being considered for the next 
apprenticeship class. Applications may be submitted online at <u>www.eti-indy.org</u> or if you prefer to apply 
in-person, applications are accepted Monday through Friday from 8:00 am to 4:30 pm at the Electrical 
Training Institute of Indianapolis, located at 1751 South Lawndale Avenue, Indianapolis, IN 46241.

Applicants will be charged a $35.00 application fee payable by Visa, MasterCard, or Discover only 
through the online portal. If you prefer to pay by check or money order, you may do so at the Training 
Center. **No cash will be accepted!**

All applicants **must** complete the following:

To meet the minimum qualifications for apprenticeship, applicants must be a minimum of 17 years of age 
at the time of application and be a high school graduate, GED recipient or if graduation is expected by the 
end of the school year for those applicants still enrolled in high school.

1. Provide evidence of successful completion of either one full year of high school algebra with a “C” 
or better or one post-high school algebra course with a grade of “C” or better, though this course 
must be North Central Accredited. All credits for algebra courses must be shown on the transcript.

a. You may also provide evidence of having successfully completed the Tech Math Course to 
satisfy this requirement, which can be found at: https://www.skillsprep.org

2. Provide an official transcript for high school and post-high school education. Transcripts of grades 
and/or GED test scores must be mailed to the apprenticeship office by the high school or place of 
testing in their letterhead envelope or delivered electronically to <u>fsmith@eti-indy.org</u> through the 
use of <u>www.parchment.com</u>, which is a digital credentialing service used by high schools and 
colleges.

3. **Transcripts will not be accepted via fax or from an applicant unless they are submitted in-**
**person with an “Official School Records” label across the back flap of an unopened envelope.**

4. To qualify for the aptitude test, you must provide all transcripts and any other documentation 
required 60 days from the date of your initial application. Failure to do so will result in the 
requirement to restart your application.

5. To qualify for the interview, the applicant must successfully complete the aptitude test developed 
and validated by the American Institutes for Research for the electrical trades industry.

---

6. Prior to being indentured, applicants selected from the pool of interviewed applicants must be at
least 18 years of age, required to provide results of a drug screen as decided by the Electrical
Training Institute, and present evidence that the applicant possesses a valid driver’s license.

The recruitment, selection, employment, and training of apprentices during their apprenticeship, shall be 
without discrimination because of race, color, religion, national origin, sex, or age other than the applicant 
must be at least 17 years to apply and 18 years of age at the time of an indentured offer. The Electrical 
Training Institute of Indianapolis does not, and will not, discriminate against a qualified individual with a 
disability because of the disability of such an individual. The sponsor will take affirmative action to provide 
equal opportunity in apprenticeship and will operate the apprenticeship program as required under 
applicable law and lawful regulations issued there under. Applicants who meet all basic requirements will 
be interviewed by the Electrical Training Institute and all apprentices will be selected in order of their final 
ranking.

**Qualifications:** Must have H.S. diploma or GED certificate, which must be North Central 
Accredited. Proof of 1 year of high school algebra with the grade of a “C” or better
Lower age limit: 18 years old. Upper age limit: None.

**Requirements:** After the applicant meets the above requirements and completes an application, 
the individual will then be scheduled for an aptitude test.

**Program Length:**    3 Year Program consists of:
6,000 on-the-job training hours and 480 classroom hours. 
Probationary Period: first 1,500 hours of on-the-job training.

**Degree:** After completing the 3-year program, apprentices will have had the opportunity to 
earn an Associate of Applied Science Degree from Ivy Tech Community College.

**Wages:** The starting wage for an apprentice after being accepted into the apprenticeship 
program is $20.85 per hour plus benefits and the graduates of the apprenticeship 
program currently earn $37.90 per hour plus benefits.

As an apprentice in this program, you may be required to report for on-the-job training assignments 
anywhere within the employment jurisdiction of the Electrical Training Institute, which includes the 
following Indiana counties: **Bartholomew, Boone, Decatur, Hamilton, Hancock, Hendricks, Jennings,** 
**Johnson, Madison, Marion, Montgomery, Morgan, Putnam, Ripley, Rush, and Shelby.**

Our program is very competitive for the number of people applying versus the number of openings available 
and applicants may be interviewed on an as-needed basis. As our office will notify applicants by written 
correspondence with the time and date of their interview, it shall be the applicant’s responsibility to keep the 
Electrical Training Institute’s office informed of any changes in their basic contact information including 
physical address, email address, and telephone number.
