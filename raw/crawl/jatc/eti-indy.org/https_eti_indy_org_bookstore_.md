# Bookstore

- [Home](https://eti-indy.org/)
- Bookstore

All books and tools are available for pick up during normal business hours (Mon-Fri from 8:00 AM to 4:30 PM). Apprentice books are available on the first day of the scheduled class. Fees must be paid no later than one full week prior to your first day of class.

Apprentices must first log in to TradeSchool to view their assigned courses. Match the course number in TradeSchool to the semester fees below. Apprentices who are completing personalized education plans will receive customized invoices for their specific course fees.

We will not ship books or items. Sales are strictly limited to members and registered students. Email [asmith@eti-indy.org](mailto:asmith@eti-indy.org) with any questions.
