### Our History

The Electrical Training Institute (ETI) in Indianapolis has a rich history dating back to its founding in 1948. Established to address the growing need for skilled electrical workers in post-war America, the institute quickly became a cornerstone of the local and regional electrical industry. As the demand for electricity and electrical services continued to rise, so did the need for well-trained electricians.

From its early days, the ETI in Indianapolis dedicated itself to providing high-quality education and training to individuals aspiring to become electricians. Over the years, the institute evolved with advancements in electrical technology, safety standards, and industry best practices. Its curriculum expanded to cover a wide range of topics, from basic electrical theory to the latest innovations in green energy and automation.

The ETI in Indianapolis has played a pivotal role in shaping the electrical workforce in the region. It has not only produced skilled electricians but has also served as a hub for ongoing professional development and continuing education. The institute's commitment to excellence has led to its accreditation and recognition by industry organizations and regulatory bodies.

Through the decades, the Electrical Training Institute in Indianapolis has adapted to the changing needs of the electrical industry. It continues to serve as a vital institution, training generations of electricians, ensuring safety and quality in electrical work, and contributing to the growth and prosperity of the region. Today, it stands as a testament to the enduring importance of education and training in the electrical field, maintaining its legacy as a key player in the development of high-quality electrical professionals.
