# Tuition & Fees

- [Home](https://eti-indy.org/)
- Tuition & Fees

**Cost of Attendance**

The Electrical Training Institute (ETI) is a jointly managed training, nonprofit organization run by the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) #481 and the Central Indiana Chapter of the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA). Since 1948, both the IBEW and NECA have partnered to train electrical workers in central Indiana.

These organizations cover all tuition costs as an investment in the future of our industry. Apprentices accepted into the ETI receive a full-ride scholarship that covers all their tuition costs. They are responsible for their semester book and learning management system fees.

Participants will leave their apprenticeship training without school loans or debt upon completion.
