## Construction Electrician Program

-
Interested in becoming a Construction Electrician?


-
Construction electricians perform light commercial and residential work. They can attend school for more training as an option. Contact IBEW Local Union #481 for more information.


[Contact IBEW 481](https://www.ibew481.org/employment_application/)

-
Are you a current IBEW Construction Electrician looking to enroll in classes?


-
If you are a current IBEW #481 Construction Electrician, you must either log in to your existing account or create an account to register for classes. Once your account is created, you can then complete the online registration and submit payment through our secure portal. Please note that all classes have registration deadlines.


[Create CE Account](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/eti-indy-org/login/index.php)
