### Application Information

Are you ready to receive a valuable education while earning a good wage and then graduate to a secure career with great benefits?

We accept applications year-round and typically start classes five times per year. Due to the competitive nature of the application process, we recommend you submit your application early to begin the qualification process.

The basic qualifications required of apprenticeship candidates are:

- Minimum of 17 years of age
- One year of high school algebra with a grade of C or better
- Successful completion of aptitude test
- Valid driver’s license

### The Application Process

1

### Create a User Account

You will request to create a user account. Our staff will review your request within 1-2 business days. You will receive an email after we confirm your account.

2

### Complete your Online Application

Click the link in your account confirmation email and begin your application. Our online application process has a nominal $35 fee to cover processing costs.

3

### Submit your Transcripts

You must submit your official transcripts through our secure application portal no later than 60 days after the online application is started.

4

### Submit Additional Documentation

Are you a veteran? Have previous experience? Submit your DD-214 and other documentation through our secure application portal.

5

### Pass our Aptitude Test

We will schedule you to take our in-person aptitude test that includes both math and reading sections.

6

### Interview Process

Our panel of industry experts will evaluate your potential for a career in the electrical industry.
