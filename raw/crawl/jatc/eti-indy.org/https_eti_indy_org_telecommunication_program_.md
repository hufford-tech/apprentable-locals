## Telecommunication Program

[Telecommunication Plan of Study](https://eti-indy.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/10/Plan-of-Study-Fall-25-Telecom.pdf)

-
What type of work do telecommunication technicians perform?


-
Telecommunication technicians are the backbone of modern communication systems, responsible for installing, maintaining, and troubleshooting a vast array of equipment that facilitates global connectivity. They install and maintain cables and wiring systems, such as fiber optics, coaxial cables, and Ethernet cables. This may involve pulling cables, connecting connectors, and testing connections.


-
What are the typical working conditions for an telecommunication technician?


-
Technicians work in a variety of settings, and their working conditions can vary depending on their specific job, location, and the type of work they are involved in. Telecommunications technicians may perform work indoors or outdoors. They can work varied hours to meet project-specific schedules. The physical demands of the job can include climbing ladders, working in confined spaces, lifting heavy equipment, and using hand and power tools.


-
What is the earning potential for a telecommunication technician?


-
Apprentice starting pay is $20.85 per hour. They receive two raises per year. After completing the apprenticeship, they will earn a minimum of $37.90 per hour.


-
How long is the telecommunication apprenticeship program?


-
The telecommunication apprenticeship consists of a minimum of 480 hours of classroom instruction and 6,000 hours of on the job training. The program takes three years to complete. But don't worry, you will be earning a living wage starting on day one of your apprenticeship.


-
Is telecommunication work dangerous?


-
Telecommunication technicians are exposed to hazards, including the risk of falls, abrasions, and injuries from tools and equipment. Safety is a top priority, and they must follow safety protocols and use personal protective equipment.
