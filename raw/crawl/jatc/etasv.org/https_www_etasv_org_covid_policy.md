### Covid Policy

The ETASV will observe the present Covid policies adopted by the County of Santa Clara with regards to what determines a close contact, isolation, and testing. (covid19.sccgov.org/covid19-guidelines)

​

- Students are responsible for their own safety to reduce exposure and ensure consistent attendance.

- A third-party test must be submitted to the school in order for the absence to be considered as Excused.

- A third-party negative test will be required in order to return to school.

- All absences must be made up, consistent absences may require a student to repeat the semester.


UNEXCUSED ABSENCES

​

One (1) Unexcused Absence – One (1) day off of work, (made up at Day school, 8 hours each  day)

Two (2) Unexcused Absences – Five (5) days off of work, (made up at Day school, 8 hours each day) and pay upgrade withheld or revoked for sixty (60) days.

Three (3) Unexcused Absences – Ten (10) days off of work (made-up at Day school, 8 hours each day) and pay upgrade withheld or revoked for ninety (90) days

Four (4) Unexcused Absences - Apprentice will be removed from work and cited at the next Committee Meeting and subject to repeating the semester or having Apprentice Agreement cancelled altogether.
