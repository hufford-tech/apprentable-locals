### RE-INTERVIEW GUIDELINES

What can I do to increase my interview score?

You can request a re-interview provided that:

1) It has been 1 (one) year since your last interview.

2) You have completed 1000 hours of electrical related work or have taken 2 or more electrical or post-secondary trade-related classes.  (SEE BELOW)

3) The request is in writing. [Email](mailto:Judy@etasv.org)Judy to request re-interview and attach proof of #2.

Pursuant to California Division of Apprenticeship Standards, Section VII, Part D, paragraph 14:

Applicants wishing to re-interview must complete at least 1000 hours of work experience with a C-10 electrical contractor, or successfully complete at least TWO or more post-secondary trade-related classes (2 quarters or 1 semester).

​​

Please see the [ETASV POLICY (click here)](https://www.etasv.org/_files/ugd/ff03b5_e0774ce461904c25951f17f59281217f.pdf)regarding Re-Interview Qualifications.
