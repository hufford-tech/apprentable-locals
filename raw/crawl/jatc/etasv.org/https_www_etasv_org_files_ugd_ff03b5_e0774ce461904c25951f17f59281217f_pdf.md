Applicants cannot re-interview any sooner than one year from the original interview date. To qualify for a 
re-interview, the applicant must have either 1) completed two post-secondary, trade-related classes of at 
least one quarter in length, or 2) completed a minimum of 1,000 hours of electrical construction work 
with a C-10 electrical contractor.

## Qualify

1. State registered apprentice

2. State registered electrical trainee

3. Nonunion electrician/apprentice

4. IBEW material handler classification

5. IBEW Sound & Communication (working for a C-10 contractor)

6. Santa Clara County Trades Orientation Program completion certificate

## Do Not Qualify

1. Solar installation

2. Non-union low voltage/telecommunications

3. Labor placement Agency “outsource”

Acceptable forms of proof of hours include:

1. W-2 Reports

2. Employers pay stubs (previous or current)

3. Social Security Hours Report

4. IBEW/NECA print out of hours worked

According to the Executive Secretary for the National Electrical Training Alliance, solar installation is NOT
considered as “electrical construction”.

Definition: <u>Post-secondary education</u> <u>refers to</u> <u>formal education that takes place after high school</u><u>,</u> 
<u>including vocational training, community college programs, and university degrees. It encompasses</u> 
<u>various programs, from associate degrees to advanced graduate and professional degrees.</u>

Online courses taken, other than those from an institution mentioned above, do NOT qualify.

Examples of accepted courses: Algebra I and II, Geometry, Trigonometry, Physics, The Plan Grid 
Construction Course, Electrical Code, HVAC Controls or other <u>Nine-week</u> <u>construction relevant courses</u>.
