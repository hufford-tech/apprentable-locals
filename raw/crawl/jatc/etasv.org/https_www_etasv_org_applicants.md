### APPLICANTS & APPRENTICE INFORMATION

Apprenticeship Information

The ETASV trains men and women in the fields of Commercial Wiremen and Residential Electrician. To achieve proper certification and a deep technical understanding of these careers, you will study under highly qualified professionals as an Apprentice. During your apprenticeship, you are paid a fair wage and benefits. Each apprenticeship has specific requirements and pre-requisites you must obtain before applying.  Watch this great video below to learn more:

Applying for an Apprenticeship

Online applications are open to first three days of the month only. (Maximum 300 applications taken per month) Applications will open at 8:00 AM on the 1st day of the month  Each Apprenticeship has requirements that must be achieved before applying. Click below for requirements and information about each available apprenticeship.
