# Hutchinson Electrical Training Academy Welcomes You

### Training For ACAREERYou Can Take Pride In.

The Hutchinson Electrical Training Academy (HETA) believes that identifying and satisfying the needs of the electrical industry is critical to the future. Training is the basic foundation on which every craftsperson builds a career. Providing the customer with access to the safest, most highly trained workforce is the primary objective of the Hutchinson Electrical Training Academy and Training Committee. We believe providing a superior workforce will not only contribute to the economic development of Kansas, but will build great communities to live and work in.
