Below is the Wage and Benefits Packages for the 6 Levels of Apprenticeship.  First year apprentices start as Level 1 and after the first 6 months of school and 1000 on the job training hours reach level 2.  After completion of the first year of schooling and 2000 on the job training hours reach level 3.  After completion of the second year of schooling and 3500 on the job training hours reach level 4.  After completion of 3rd year of schooling and 5000 on the job training hours reach level 5.  After obtaining 6500 job training hours reach level 6 and once you complete 4th Year and obtain 8000 on the job training hours and successfully passing the Kansas Electrical Journeyman Test reach Journeyman Classification.
