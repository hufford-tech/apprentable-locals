**[Blended Learning](http://blendedlearning.njatc.org/login/index.php)**

Also known as LMS, this is where apprentices can log-in for online instruction and assignments.

**[Bluevolt](https://go.bluevolt.com/default/s/)**

A company unaffiliated with the DJEATC that offers CEU courses for journeymen if we don't have what you need at the right time.

**[Electrical Training Alliance](http://www.electricaltrainingalliance.org/)**

The apprenticeship's national organization, formerly known as the NJATC.

[**SkillsPrep.org Online Learning System**](https://www.skillsprep.org/login/index.php)

For applicants unable to provide a transcript proving they have taken a credit of algebra, the online, self-paced Tech Math course will meet our requirements.
