# How to Apply

### **Applications now open.**

### **If you have any questions, please email [**djeatcapplications@outlook.com**](mailto:djeatcapplications@outlook.com)**

### **Please read below the new application steps.**

## Required Documents to Apply

### Inside Wireman Program Document Submissions:

### **You must submit your documents to **[djeatcapplications@outlook.com](mailto:djeatcapplications@outlook.com)**before applying for the apprenticeship. Please send all the following documents in one email:**

- Current & Valid Driver’s License (Identification cards will not be accepted)
- 2nd Form of ID: Social Security Card, Birth Certificate or Passport
- High School, College or GED Diploma or Transcripts stating your graduation date.
- DD-214 (if applicable)

**Please allow 3-5 Business days for our team to review your submitted documents.**

Once approved, you will receive an email with instructions to start filling out the online application. Your account will need to be approved by the DJEATC team.

**$35.00 to apply – This is non-refundable fee when you start the application online.**

### VDV Program Submissions:

### **You must submit your documents to **[djeatcapplications@outlook.com](mailto:djeatcapplications@outlook.com)**before applying for the apprenticeship. Please send all the following documents in one email:**

### Application & Aptitude Test Process

Once your application has been completed, within a week you will receive an email from GAN Human Resources to start filling out your Personal Experience Form.

**(Important Note: You will have two weeks from when you first receive the email from GAN to get your PEF completed. If your PEF is not completed within two weeks, your application will be disqualified, and you will have to pay another application fee and apply again.)**

When filling out your Personal Experience Form for GAN you will need to have the following documents ready to submit that are related to your experience:

- Employment Verification: Year-End Pay Stub, or W-2, or Company Letterhead
- High School or College Transcripts or Equivalent
- DD-214 Member Form
- Affidavit of Experience
- Letters of Recommendation
- Resume

**Test Components for the GAN Aptitude Test:**

- Paper Folding
- Numerical Computation
- Numerical Reasoning
- Reading Comprehension
- Mechanical Reasoning

**GAN Aptitude Test Study Guide:** [Purchase the study guide](https://ganhumanresources.com/study-guide-1/digital-download-gan-aptitude-test-study-guide) for $20.00.

### Background Check Information (Inside Wireman Program Only)

After you have taken the aptitude test, and if you receive a ranking sufficient to meet selection requirements, a background check will be conducted. Having a criminal record does not automatically disqualify an applicant from being accepted. Every applicant identified by the screening as having been convicted of a felony in the past seven years will be individually assessed by the committee. If the committee determines an applicant unsuitable for the program, they will have 10 days to demonstrate that the exclusion should not apply. [Please see our full Background Check Policy for more information.](https://djeatc68.com/apply/background-checks/)
