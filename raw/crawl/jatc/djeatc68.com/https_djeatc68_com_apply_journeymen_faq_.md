# Journeymen FAQ

**Q: What is my "Tradeschool" account?**

Tradeschool is the name of our database for journeymen and apprentices alike. By logging into Tradeschool, you will be able to sign up for CEUs and other upgrade courses, view past courses that you have taken, and update your contact information.

**Q: How do I set up an account/log in for the first time?**

- If you are new to the DJEATC, click on "Login to Tradeschool" (under the Apprenticeship menu), then "Create Account."
- If you have taken classes through the DJEATC before, and we have your current email address on file, click on "Login to Tradeschool," then "Reset Password."
- You must have an account with a valid email address and IBEW membership # in order to register for classes.

**Q: How do I sign up for upgrade courses?**

First, create or login to your Tradeschool account (see above). At the top of the page, click on "Class Schedule." By default, only classes taking place in the current month appear. To look at classes in upcoming months, click the links on the left side of the page, or search by location or class name. When you find the class you want, click "View" for details. If you would like to register for the class, click the green "cart" icon to add it to your class cart. On the right side of the page, click "Proceed to checkout" and follow the instructions from there.

**Q: How do I pay for upgrade courses?**

Payment is due at the time of registrations (if registering online or over the phone). At that time, you can pay via credit card, Visa, MasterCard, American Express or Discover.

**Q:** **Do you sell code books?**

Yes, we sell the 2023 NEC code book (paperback) for $125, or the hardcover handbook for $230. We also sell tabs for $20.

Yes, we sell the 2026 NEC code book (paperback) for $135, or the hardcover handbook for $240. We also sell tabs for $25.

**Q: Do you sell other books and tools?**

Yes, we sell course textbooks and other resources, such as the Ugly's book ($15). We also have information for ordering leather tool pouches, but we no longer sell these at the office.

**Q: Can I get a copy of a certificate for a class I took in the past?**

We keep photocopies and/or scanned versions of certificates for most upgrade courses in recent years. We can provide a photocopy or emailed version of your certificate free of charge. If you need an original version of your certificate, we will charge $5 for creating a new one.

**Q: Can I get a letter or affidavit verifying that I was an apprentice or stating the number of hours I worked?**

Yes, we can usually provide this information. Please contact the office via phone or email and provide your name, dates that you were in the apprenticeship, and exactly what information you need.

**Q: How many CE Hours do I need to renew my Journeyman's License?**

All electricians with an active license must complete at least 24 hours of CE's during each three-year license period and it needs to be completed before renewing.

**Q: What CE Classes count towards the 24 hour credit?**

Grounding and Bonding, Significant Code Changes, Theory and Calculations, Wiring Methods and Safety Related to the Electrical Industry (NFPA 70E).

**Q: What course is required to take for the 24 hour renewal?**

At least four hours must be completed in the National Electrical Code Changes core competency each license period. The remaining hours may be selected in any combination of the Core Competencies, however no more than four hours will be credited in Safety Related to the Electrical Industry.

**Q: When is the cutoff to register for the CEU Classes?**

The classes closes 48 hours prior to when the class is being held.
