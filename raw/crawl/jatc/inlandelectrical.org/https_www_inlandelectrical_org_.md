## The POWER is in your hands!

**The Inland Empire Electrical Apprenticeship is a joint partnership between the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 73 and the National Electrical Contractors Association.**

**We offer apprenticeship programs for [Residential Electricians](https://www.inlandelectrical.org/apprenticeships/residential/), [Commercial/Industrial Electricians](https://www.inlandelectrical.org/apprenticeships/inside-wireman/) (Inside Wireman), and [Low Voltage Electricians](https://www.inlandelectrical.org/apprenticeships/sound-and-communications-technician/) (Sound and Communications).**

**Let us help you get started on an exciting and fulfilling career in the electrical industry.  [Apply today!](https://www.inlandelectrical.org/how-to-apply/)**
