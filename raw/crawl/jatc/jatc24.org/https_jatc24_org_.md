## Start Training as a Union Electrician or Technician

[IBEW Local 24](https://ibewlocal24.org/) and the [Maryland Chapter, National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA)](https://marylandneca.org/), sponsor unsurpassed training programs for our members and apprentices through our Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (J.A.T.C.).

Our state-of-the-art training facility offers an educational program approved by the Bureau of Apprenticeship, U.S. Department of Labor, and the Maryland Apprenticeship and Training Council.

## Benefits of a Union Apprenticeship

#### Tuition Free

Graduate with no student debt. Books are the only thing you need to purchase for school. Receive nationally recognized Electrical industry training at no cost to you.

#### Retirement Benefits

Enjoy a career knowing that you are saving for your retirement from day one. Our members enjoy 3 pensions and a severance and annuity plan.

#### Competitive Starting Pay

1st year apprentices start at $23.63/hr plus benefits. Our Journeyman currently earn $52.50/hr with increases to wages and benefits annually.

#### Family Health Care

Coverage for both you and your family. Dental, Vision, Health, and Prescription.
