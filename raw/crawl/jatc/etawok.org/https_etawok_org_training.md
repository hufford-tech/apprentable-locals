[**APPRENTICESHIP SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM**](https://etawok.org/our-crew)

The registered Apprenticeship Program is a federally approved, accredited program consisting of 8,000 hours of on-the-job training coupled with 756 hours of classroom education. It is a scholarship program so there is no tuition, but you must be selected for the program and go through a selection process which will include an aptitude test and an oral interview.

[**CONSTRUCTION WIREMAN /**\\
\\
**CONSTRUCTION ELECTRICIAN**](https://etawok.org/cw-ce)

The CW/CE program is designed to provide training to experienced electrical workers that aspire to become Journeyman level electricians, as well as to those who are inexperienced but do not meet the minimum qualifications for the registered Apprenticeship program.

[**CONTINUING EDUCATION**](https://etawok.org/journeymen)

CEU classes are designed for Journeymen Wiremen to remain knowledgeable of industry trends, remain current on licensing obligations, and remain Quality Connected.
