The _electrical training ALLIANCE of Western Oklahoma’s_ Apprenticeship Program is associated with the national Electrical Training Alliance. Our US Department of Labor Accredited program consists of 8,000 hours of on-the-job training and 756 hours of classroom education.

![](https://images.squarespace-cdn.com/content/v1/5629315be4b0126c03388002/1457967593237-0M0ROPSGI0PK242TGWQA/image-asset.png)

The _electrical training ALLIANCE_ of Western Oklahoma is a joint effort by the Oklahoma chapter of the [National Electrical Contractors Association](http://www.okneca.org/) and the [International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 1141](http://www.ibew1141.org/)
