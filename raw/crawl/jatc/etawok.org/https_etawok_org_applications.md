## **HOW TO APPLY FOR OUR SCHOLARSHP PROGRAM:**

# **PLEASE NOTE: EFFECTIVE JULY 1, 2026 THERE WILL BE A $25 APPLICATION FEE**.

**The fee will be due after your account request is approved and is payable by credit card only.**

# Click [**“**](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/woejatc-org/login/index.php) [**HERE**](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/woejatc-org/login/index.php) [**”**](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/woejatc-org/login/index.php) to go to our web portal to create an account and begin a digital application for the registered Electrical Apprenticeship Scholarship Program.

## **Applications are available online at the above link. You will be required to prove that you meet the minimum qualifications for apprenticeship by uploading a valid driver’s license and requesting an official transcript from your high school be emailed directly to us.**

## **After your application has been approved, you will be invited to come to our facility to take an Aptitude Test covering Algebra Functions and Reading Comprehension. If you make the minimum required score on the test, you qualify for an oral interview with the Scholarship Committee. If you need a refresher in Algebra I, we recommend Khan Academy.**

## **Qualifications for Apprenticeship SCHOLARSHIP:**

- **At least 17 years of age to apply**

- **At least 18 years of age to enter program**

- **High school diploma or GED**

- **Completion of at least one full year of high school level Algebra I with a passing grade**

- **Valid state driver's license (not a state ID)**

## **Click** ["HERE](https://etawok.org/s/2026-WOEJATC-Prog-Bro-052626.pdf)" **for detailed information about the Apprenticeship SCHOLARSHIP Program, including pay scales and benefits.**

**This is a PAID, on-the-job training program. You will receive 8,000 hours of verified training with a licensed Journeyman Electrician for a union signatory contractor and 756 hours of classroom time.**

**This is a SCHOLARSHIP program, so there is no tuition, however, you must be selected for the program and you must go through a selection process that includes an aptitude test and an oral interview with the JATC Committee.**

**The first step is to apply for our program and prove you meet the requirements for apprenticeship. Those qualifications are listed above.**
