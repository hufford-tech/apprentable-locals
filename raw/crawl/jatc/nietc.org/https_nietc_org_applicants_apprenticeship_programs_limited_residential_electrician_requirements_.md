# [Requirements](https://nietc.org/applicants/apprenticeship-programs/limited-residential-electrician/requirements/ "Permanent Link: Requirements")

[CURRENT APPLICATION OPENINGS](https://nietc.org/applicants/preparing-to-apply/current-application-openings/)

Please read our [Limited Residential Electrician Program Description and Wages](https://nietc.org/applicants/apprenticeship-programs/limited-residential-electrician/).

- **Apply On Line At:** NECA-IBEW Electrical Training Center ( [Web Services](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/nietc-org/login/index.php)). _The NECA-IBEW Electrical Training Trust admits students of any race, color and national or ethnic origin_.
- **Required at the time of application**
  - Copy of: High School Diploma or GED Certificate (minimum score of 225 if taken prior to 1/1/02, 2250 if taken prior to 12/31/13, or 600 if taken after 1/1/14.
  - Transcripts for Math Requirement _– See Minimum Requirements_
- **Minimum Program Requirements**
  - At least 18 years of age _– proof of age may be required_
  - Documentation of successful completion of one full year of high school mathematics (Transcript Required)
    - OR Equivalent placement test score from a community college or other educational facility indicating a placement level beyond high school level math – Transcript required at time of application
    - OR if submitting a GED taken after 1/1/14, a minimum score of 150 on the Math portion of the test.
    - OR documentation of successful completion of online NJATC math course [www.njatc.org](http://electricaltrainingalliance.org/Learning/OnlineTechMath)
- **The Training Center Will**
  - Notify by email of date and time for interview (if applicable)

**If Accepted:**  Employer requires substance abuse test and background check
