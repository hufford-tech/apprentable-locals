### Entry-level Positions (Material Handling)

Only licensed electricians and registered apprentices may legally perform electrical work. Material Handling is an entry-level position that does not require a license. For information click [Material Handling](https://www.dropbox.com/scl/fi/rueqz0z9qpv59stoo1kik/Material-Handling-2026.pdf?rlkey=qcs8hy7s6kbx7278x3ipas8jz&dl=0)

### Construction Related Volunteer Work

Volunteerism shows your commitment to the community and your ability to work alongside others with tools. This is a great way to build the “hands-on” section of your resume if you do not have prior trade-related experience. Document your hours and request a letter of recommendation if you feel you have earned one.

Below is a list of organizations that perform construction-related volunteer work.

- [Community Energy Project](https://www.communityenergyproject.org/)
- [Hands On Greater Portland](https://www.handsonportland.org/search?sort_c=&sort_o=&opportunity_id=&page=1)
- [Habitat for Humanity](https://www.habitat.org/)
- [Metropolitan Family Services](https://www.metfamily.org/volunteer/)
- [REACH Community Development](https://reachcdc.org/take-action/volunteer)
- [The Rebuilding Center](https://www.rebuildingcenter.org/)
- [Rebuilding Together](http://rtpdx.org/contact)

### Pre-Apprenticeships

Pre-Apprenticeships expose students to all the building trades and help students to become familiar with terminology, hand and power tool use, construction safety, and more. Click [here](https://www.oregon.gov/boli/apprenticeship/Pages/pre-apprenticeship-programs.aspx) for a list of adult and youth pre-apprenticeships that are approved by the Oregon State Apprenticeship and Training Council.

Click [here](https://nietc.org/pre-apprenticeship/) for information about the Electrical Training Alliance Pre-Apprenticeship Program.

### Documentation of Hobbies

Do you like to build and fix things? Do projects around the house? If so, take photos of your work, and add captions describing the work performed.  Before and after photos are great.

You can get project ideas from these websites, or websites that are similar:

- [The DIY Network](https://www.diynetwork.com/how-to)
- [Instructables](http://instructables.com/workshop/projects)
- [eHow](http://ehow.com/home/outdoor-building)

Most communities have tool libraries that allow you to check out tools. An internet search will yield some results. You can also check out [localtools.org](https://localtools.org/), although this site does not list all tool libraries.
