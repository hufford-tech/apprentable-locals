### Veteran Applicants

A military veteran of the United States armed forces may apply during any open office hours, regardless of public application openings provided the below conditions are met.

1. The Applicant must submit all required documentation, found under the _Program Requirements_ section of each [program description](https://nietc.org/applicants/#apprenticeship-programs)
2. The Applicant submits a DD-214 form that documents honorable discharge from the US armed forces, Selected Reserve, Ready Reserve or National Guard within 24 months of the date of application; **– OR –**
3. The Applicant is an active duty military member who is within 180 days of expected separation from service with an honorable discharge. Prior to registration a DD-214 form that documents honorable discharge must be submitted.

Once the qualifying application is submitted the standard application timeline applies.
