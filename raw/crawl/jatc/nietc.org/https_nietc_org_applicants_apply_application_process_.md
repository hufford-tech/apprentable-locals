Application Procedure for the NECA-IBEW Electrical JATC

**Qualify and Apply**

All required documentation, found under the _Program Requirements_ section of each program description, must be submitted to the application. There are no exceptions.

**Aptitude Test**(Inside Electrician and Limited Energy Technician Programs)

If you meet the minimum qualifications you will be scheduled for an aptitude test the following month.

**Interview**

If you receive a qualifying score on the aptitude test or if you have met the requirements for the Limited Residential Electrician Program you will be scheduled for an interview the following month.

**Ranked List**

Your interview score determines your rank on our list of eligible candidates.When we have contractor need for new apprentices, a class is formed in rank order beginning with #1.
