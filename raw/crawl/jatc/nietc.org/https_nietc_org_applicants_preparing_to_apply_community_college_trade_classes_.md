# [Community College Trade Classes](https://nietc.org/applicants/preparing-to-apply/community-college-trade-classes/ "Permanent Link: Community College Trade Classes")

You are here:[Home](https://nietc.org/ "NECA-IBEW Electrical Training Center") 1/[Applicants](https://nietc.org/applicants/ "Applicants") 2/[Preparing to Apply](https://nietc.org/applicants/preparing-to-apply/ "Preparing to Apply") 3/Community College Trade Classes

Community College Trade-Related Classes may be taken to strengthen an application portfolio or to qualify for a [re-interview](https://nietc.org/applicants/preparing-to-apply/preparing-for-the-aptitude-test-and-the-interview/).

This list of [examples of trade-related classes](https://www.dropbox.com/s/5bzhrtpbfm13n4l/Examples%20of%20Trade-Related%20Classes.pdf?dl=0) is provided as a courtesy to our applicants who are interested in applying for a re-interview.

The NECA-IBEW Electrical Training Center does not keep current with college schedules and available courses. It is the applicant’s responsibility to do their own research regarding prerequisites and when courses are offered.

Our Board of Trustees has determined that distance learning courses do NOT count towards a Re-interview. Examples of distance learning schools are Ashworth College and Penn Foster College.
