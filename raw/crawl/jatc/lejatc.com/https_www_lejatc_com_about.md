# About the Lafayette Electrical JATC

The LEJATC was established as a partnership between the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) and International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW).

Its purpose is to train the next generation of electrical workers.  We pride ourselves on producing the best-trained and most skilled electricians in the workforce.  We utilize a 4-year apprenticeship model, which ensures a quality combination of both on the job and classroom training.

We also provide Journeyperson skills refresher classes to ensure that our workforce is up to date with ever evolving technology.
