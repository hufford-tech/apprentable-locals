# Building the Future of Electrical Professionals

Focused. Integrated. Applicable.

Macon Electrical Training Center has been training professionals in the electrical industry since 1942. Our curriculum is designed to provide students with a solid foundation of knowledge and hands-on experience. Our debt-free approach allows students to focus on learning without the stress of accumulating student debt. With earning opportunities available during the program, this ensures that our students are well prepared for their future careers. We also offer quality health benefits and a retirement package to ensure our students' long-term success.
