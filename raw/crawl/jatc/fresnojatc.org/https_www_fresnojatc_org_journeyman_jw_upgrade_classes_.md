# JW Upgrade Classes

[Home](https://www.cceti.org/)&#x39;[Journeyman](https://www.cceti.org/journeyman/)&#x39;JW Upgrade Classes

The Central California ETI hold journeyman upgrade classes regularly. State Licensing for general electricians requires that all wiremen must take 32 hours of continuing education at least once every three years, before your card expires. In-person classes are currently suspended due to the training center being remodeled. there are still options to get your required hours of continuing education.

## **CPR/First Aid**

This 8 hour CPR and First Aid course teaches essential life-saving skills to help individuals respond effectively in emergencies. The course covers how to perform Cardiopulmonary Resuscitation (CPR) on adults, children, and infants, how to use an Automated External Defibrillator (AED), and how to assist someone who is choking. It also includes basic first aid techniques for treating injuries such as cuts, burns, sprains, and fractures, as well as how to recognize and respond to medical emergencies like heart attacks, strokes, and allergic reactions. The goal is to equip participants with the knowledge and confidence to act quickly and appropriately in critical situations until professional help arrives. A red cross certification will be emailed to you. If you desire a physical  certification card a 20$ fee is added.

### Electrical Trainee/Journeyman Electrician Exam Preparation

This course is designed to educate individuals on the fundamental principles of electrical theory, Electrical safe work practices, and the National Electrical Code (NEC) requirements.  This is an excellent class for preparing to sucsessfully take the State Code Test. This Class is ongoing year round. Each class will count for 4 hours towards the 32 hours of required class hours for state license renewal. Any State licensed electrician looking for continuing education hours can attend at will. Please contact Victoria for log in credentials for this ZOOM based class.
