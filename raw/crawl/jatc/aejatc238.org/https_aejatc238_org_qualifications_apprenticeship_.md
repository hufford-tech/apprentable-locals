# APPRENTICESHIP QUALIFICATIONS

#### To qualify for the apprenticeship program one has to meet and/or be able to do the following:

-must be at least 18 years of age

-must be a high school graduate/GED with one full credit in Algebra

-must obtain a minimum score of Aptitude test measuring basic reading & math skills

-must be able to lift, carry and manipulate tools and materials common to the trade

-must be able to read, hear, understand & follow written & verbal instructions

-must be willing to follow instructions & to work cooperatively with others

-must be able & willing to follow safety procedures

-must be able to get to and from sites anywhere in the jurisdiction.

The ETA will accept college or post-high school credit to meet Algebra requirements. One may also complete the NJATC online math course to satisfy the Algebra requirement.

**The Asheville Electrical JATC does not discriminate against any person or persons for race, ethnicity, sex, age, nationality, disability, family status, gender identity, marital status, religion, and/or sexual orientation.**

#### While most work is in, around or near the Asheville City area, apprentices are subject to assignments in the following counties:

Avery, Buncombe, Burke, Cherokee, Clay, Graham, Haywood, Henderson, Jackson, Macon, Madison, McDowell, Mitchell, Polk, Rutherford, Swain, Transylvania, Watauga, & Yancey

Veterans must provide DD214 form.
