# FALL 2026 APPLICANTS: UPCOMING SIGN-UPS & DATES FOR APTITUDE TEST & INTERVIEWS

Below are important updates about the next steps in the application process for the Asheville Electrical Apprenticeship Program. **Please ensure that you have submitted your online application & submitted all supporting documents by May 15, 2026. We do accept applications year round, but to ensure you get the sign-up links for Fall 2026, we ask that you try to get all proper documentation in by that May date.**

### **📝 Aptitude Test & Interview Sign-Up Form**

The sign-up form for the **Aptitude Test and Apprenticeship Committee Interviews** will be sent out to all applicants **summer 2026**. We will reach out prior to the sign-up form to inform applicants on which date and time they can expect to receive said communication. Be sure to check your email and complete the form promptly to secure your preferred time slot.

### **📂 Outstanding Documents – Due by August** 1st

If you have **any outstanding application documents**, please ensure they are submitted **no later than August 1st.** I’ll be reaching out to each individual who still has documents to turn in with details about what’s missing.

If you have any questions about your application, required documents, or the next steps, don’t hesitate to reach out to Joey Moore, JATC Director, at **local238jatc@gmail.com**.

Thank you for your continued interest in the program—we’re looking forward to moving into this next phase with you!
