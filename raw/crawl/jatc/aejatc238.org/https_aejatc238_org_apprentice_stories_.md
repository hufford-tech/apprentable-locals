## Build a Career. Build a Life.

Hear From Our Student & Graduates

![](https://aejatc238.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Bending-Conduit-Full-ClassicFocus.png)

“After graduation you definitely have the skills to be successful at the job.”

— Bryan, Journeyman Electrician, 2014 JATC Graduate

![](https://aejatc238.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Looking-up-Full-ClassicFocus-1.png)

“It’s given me a trade that I’ll know and have for the rest of my life.”

— Erica, 2018 JATC graduate

![](https://aejatc238.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Hard-Hat-Full-ClassicFocus-1.png)

“It has exceeded my expectations. There is no shortage of career paths within out trade that fit everyone’s interests and skill level.”

— Jonathan, Electrical Apprentice

![](https://aejatc238.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Work-Vest-Full-ClassicFocus.png)

“The apprenticeship has changed my life. I was able to buy my first house at 21. I have a bright future ahead of me.”

— Kevin, 2016 JATC Graduate

![](https://aejatc238.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Ice-Chest-Full-ClassicFocus.png)

“Being accepted into this program has opened so many doors for me and my future … No other school or college can touch it, hands down.”

— Dustin, 2018 JATC graduate, enrolled after leaving the Marine Corp

![](https://aejatc238.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/04/Ladder-Full-ClassicFocus.png)

“I had a job as soon as I was out of the Marine Corp. I started out in this industry wiring receptacles and ended up being certified to install vehicle charging stations and solar power. In five years, I earned a respected trade that pays well, a good career, and a pension. It’s very rewarding.”

— Clint, JATC graduate, enrolled after leaving the Marine Corp
