# APPRENTICESHIP APPLICANTS: ASSESSMENT PREP

![](https://aejatc238.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/Application-Process-Rev.png)

We will be moving forward with the next steps in August and will reach out to schedule a time for you to come in for your aptitude test and apprenticeship committee interview. This will be a crucial phase in the selection process, and we are excited to have you participate.

To help you prepare for these assessments, there are several resources and strategies you can use:

- **Practice aptitude tests**

[IBEW Aptitude Test Beginner’s Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=6KxPgSNkmrg)

[Taking the Aptitude Test- Mad Electrician](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=k4QnIBCGGBQ)

[IBEW Electrical Apprenticeship Math Test-Guide](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=BFTZCH4vwxM)

[IBEW-NECA Student Guide-Math](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=JfwCNPM_syI)

- **Review technical and industry-related knowledge**

[Electrician: A Vocabulary Guide for Beginners](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=DEJZ0R-07DQ)

[Introduction to Electricity with Electrical Terms & Vocab](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=sMwONIYZqxM)

[Electricity Explained: Volts, Amps, Watts, Fuse Sizing, Wire Gauge, AC/DC, Solar Power](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=cX4s-bxn4fs)

- **Prepare for interview questions relevant to the apprenticeship**

[\[Apprenticeship\] Electrician Interview- Mad Electrician](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=qCmi3ZZAtP0)

[Electrician Apprenticeship Interview-Sparky’s Journey](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=_v8MN8EFRWA)
