## Maintain Essential Skills

The State of Iowa requires all electricians working within its borders to hold an Iowa State Electrical License that must be renewed every three years. This licensing ensures that as electrical industry technologies, codes, standards, and customer needs rapidly evolve, Iowa electricians stay up-to-date through continuing education. By completing regular training courses, licensed electricians gain and maintain the skills vital for thriving in the electrical trade in today’s fast-changing landscape. The licensing system allows Iowa to guarantee its electricians can safely and expertly serve its residents and businesses.

### Classroom facilities

The Southeast Iowa JATC recognizes the importance of having proper classroom facilities that support effective learning. That’s why the classrooms are thoughtfully designed to promote an environment that is conducive to learning for apprentices. With ample space, comfortable seating, and adequate lighting, apprentices can focus on their lessons without any distractions.

To enhance the learning experience, the classrooms are equipped with state-of-the-art audio-visual aids. These aids, such as projectors and interactive whiteboards, are an essential tool for instructors to deliver their weekly lessons effectively. Instructors can present the material in a way that engages the apprentices and encourages active participation.
