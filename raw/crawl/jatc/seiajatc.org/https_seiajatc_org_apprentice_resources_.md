## Apprentice Reminder

Work reports are due the end of each month and must completed, signed and returned to school the first of the following month.

## Apprentice Evaluation Forms

During the four year program, the apprentice is evaluated by the journeymen wiremen/foreman they work with on the job to determine how they are progressing in learning the skills needed to become a qualified journeymen wireman. When the apprentice is not receiving or lacking in a necessary skill, these reports will help make the apprentice committee aware that adjustments to the apprentice’s training and work environment are needed to be made.
