February 23, 2026

Dear JATC Registered Apprentice Participants & Completers,

Rowan University is proud to partner with the electrical training ALLIANCE (etA) to create new

educational pathways that recognize the advanced training and leadership development within

the Inside Wireman, Outside Lineman and Telecommunications apprenticeship programs.

Through this agreement, Rowan University awards college credit towards the online Bachelor of

Arts in Construction Management and related certificate programs for etA apprenticeship

graduates:

• 65 college credits for completion of the Inside Wireman Apprenticeship Program

• 65 college credits for completion of the Outside Lineman Apprenticeship Program

• 65 college credits for completion of the Telecommunications Apprenticeship Program

This partnership gives etA apprentices and IBEW members a clear, affordable, and flexible path

to a college degree-without leaving the field. Rowan's programs are built for working

tradespeople, with online coursework, union-member tuition discounts, and academic credit for

skills already gained through their apprenticeship.

To learn more about the program, view degree requirements, and access the application, visit

the Rowan-electrical training ALLIANCE landing page here:

https://sites.rowan.edu/educational-partnerships/eta/

We're proud to partner with the etA to expand career pathways in the building trades and to

demonstrate what we've always known: union apprenticeship programs offer education that

matches the rigor of college-level courses.

For more information or to assist members interested in applying their credits, please contact:

Account Executive, North America's Building Trades Union (NABTU}
