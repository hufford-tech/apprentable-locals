**THE FIRST STEPS TOWARD A REWARDING CAREER IS ONLY A FEW CLICKS AWAY!**

**All applicants must apply through the online process.**

**Make sure to read ALL the steps below before creating a TradeSchool account.**

**WE DRUG TEST before job assignments and random testing.**

**Marijuana is included in drug testing**

**Headshot photos will be used as professional photos. Dress accordingly.**

**Step 1.** Create a TradeSchool account:

Click on “create account”, complete the email and password fields. You will receive an email from messaging@secure.tradeschoolinc.com, it may go to your spam folder. Open the email and click on the TradeSchool link. This will allow you to enter your account information and submit it for approval. If you are not a member of Local 666, leave the member # field blank. If you do not hold a classification from Local 666, then please select “unclassified”. (Approval may take several days) Upon receiving your acceptance email from TradeSchool, you will be able to log in and start your online application.

- Save "@rjatc.org" in your email contacts in order to receive emails to your inbox.
- Opt into receiving text messaging.

**Step 2.** Login to TradeSchool using your email and the password you created in step one. Click on the application tab to begin the application process. From the drop-down menu, select a specific program application. Inside Wireman Apprenticeship or Construction Wireman (CW).  The Inside Wireman Apprenticeship School is an acceptance process (at least a 3-month process) and during that process you can go out to work as a Construction Wireman as the work situation allows.  Construction Wireman (CW) is straight to work (no acceptance process) if the work situation permits.  For more details on each program, see the "Programs" tab at the top of this site.

- If you have any current or previous electrical experience with an electrical contractor, upload your W2's, last paystub and/or get the employer to provide your total hours on their business letterhead, you may possibly receive partial credit for those hours towards the 8000 hours required for the Apprenticeship Program.

**Step 3.**  In addition to uploading documents to your TradeSchool application, you MUST contact your former/current school and/or transcript service and ask them to send a digital copy of your transcripts directly to the JATC, copy the JATC on the email request to your school or transcript service by adding our email, **apply@rjatc.org**, on the cc line. Your institution should “reply all” to your request, so the transcripts come directly to our inbox. If the only option your school provides is hard copy mailing, then please have your transcripts mailed to the address below, or you can mail them directly to us unopened and sealed. Once we have verified ALL of your documents, **you will receive an email letting you know that you are eligible for the aptitude test**.

- A $20 processing fee is payable at the time of your aptitude test.
- At this time, there are NO online payment options. You can pay the processing fee at the school office Monday-Friday 9:00am to 4:00pm or on the day of your aptitude test.  (the office is closed for lunch, between 12:00pm to 12:30pm)

Transcript Mailing Address

Richmond Electricians' JATC

7914 Midlothian Turnpike

N. Chesterfield, VA 23235

**You will not be scheduled for an aptitude test until you complete these steps.**
