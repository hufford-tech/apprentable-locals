| Become an Apprentice

|     |
| --- |
| Interested in becoming an electrical apprentice with the Flint/Saginaw Electrical JATC?  You're in the right place!

The first step is to apply to the program that fits your career goals. If you have any questions along the way, please don’t hesitate to contact us.

When applying, please indicate whether you are applying for the **Inside Wireman** program **,** the **Telecommunications** program **,** or **both.**

* * *

**Minimum Requirements**

To apply, complete an application and return it to our office within 30 days of receipt. Along with your application, please submit the following required documentation:

- **Official Birth Certificate**

Applicants must be at least **18 years old** at the time of Department of Labor (DOL) registration.\*
- **Proof of Education**

One of the following is required:
  - Official high school diploma
  - College diploma
  - GED
- **Transcripts**

You must have official transcripts submitted showing:
  - Two (2) semesters of high school Algebra, or
  - One (1) semester of 100-level or college Algebra
  - Grade of C (2.0) or higher

    **Transcripts will only be accepted if:**
  - Emailed directly from the school or a third party (e.g., Parchment) to RRemington@fsejatc.com or
  - Delivered as a sealed paper copy with school letterhead
  - Transcripts uploaded personally by applicants during the application process will not be considered official records.
- **Valid Identification**

Acceptable forms include:
  - State-issued driver’s license
  - State ID card
  - Voter registration card

\*Applicants who are under 18 years of age or currently enrolled in high school are eligible to apply, test, and participate in interviews. A waiver signed by a parent or guardian is required and can be found at the bottom of this page. However, a training assignment will only be issued once documentation of meeting the minimum age requirement and completion of high school or GED is provided.

**Processing Fee:**

There is a $25.00 non-refundable application fee.

The fee is to cover the cost of the aptitude test. If you are applying to more than one program, only one fee and test is required.

Optional Supporting Documents

Applicants are encouraged to submit any of the following to strengthen their application:

- Resume
- Letters of recommendation
- Documentation of hours worked in the electrical industry
- Military discharge papers (DD-214)
- Any additional materials that offer insight into your background and qualifications

* * *

**Application Process**

**Step 1: Submit Application and Documents**

Once your completed application and all required documents are received and verified, you’ll be scheduled—via email—for an aptitude test. Testing is scheduled when a sufficient number of applicants is reached.

_\*Time Frame: Applications are accepted year-round; however, to be considered for next year’s class, application submissions must be received by the end of January with other required documents within 30 days._

**Step 2: Aptitude Test**

- 3-hour test covering basic Algebra and English comprehension
- Administered at the Training Center
- A passing score is required to advance
- If you do not pass, you may retake the test after 3 months by submitting an Aptitude Test Re-Take Form and paying a $25.00 re-test fee

_\*Time Frame: Testing is done when we have enough applicants to fill a classroom.  Approximately once a month._

**Step 3: Oral Interview**

Applicants who pass the aptitude test will be invited to a 15-minute interview with the FSEJATC Committee. Notification of the interview date and time will be sent via mail or email.

_\*Time Frame: Interviews are conducted starting near the end of April._

**Step 4: Program Acceptance**

Applicants accepted into the program must:

1. Complete a background check (Provided by JATC’s expense)
2. Drug Screening
1. Applicants must pass a drug screening as a condition of acceptance.  Failure to comply may result in disqualification.

_\*Time Frame: Selected applicants will be offered admission into the program after interviews are completed. Orientation and expected work assignment will follow their acceptance into the program with classroom instruction starting in September._

**_\*Please note: All time frames are approximate and may shift based on business needs._**

* * *

**Direct Interview Opportunities**

**Based on Military Experience**

You may qualify for a direct interview if you meet any of the following:

- **Active Duty:** Completed at least 3 years of active-duty U.S. military service, with an honorable discharge within 5 years of applying
- **Reserves:** Completed at least 6 years of service in the U.S. military reserves, currently serving or honorably discharged within 2 years of applying
- **MOS Experience:** Completed a technical training school and served at least 2 years in a Military Occupational Specialty (MOS) applicable to commercial/industrial electrical work within 5 years
- **Residency Waiver:** Served overseas or lived on a military installation for at least 2 years—residency restrictions are waived.  Must provide DD-214 or equivalent documentation acceptable to the JATC

* * *

**Based on Industry Experience**

Applicants may qualify based on prior work experience in the electrical construction industry:

- **2,000+ hours** of verified work in any specifically in an electrical construction setting
- **4,000+ hours** of verified work specifically in commercial or industrial electrical construction may qualify for direct entry


(Note: General construction or unrelated electrical experience does not count)

Acceptable documentation may include:

- Tax/payroll records
- Notarized letters from previous employers

If you meet any of the **Direct Interview** criteria, please call the JATC before applying.
