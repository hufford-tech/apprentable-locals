| Apprenticeship

Apprenticeship Info

|     |
| --- |
| _Definition: An apprenticeship is a structured training program in which an individual (the apprentice) learns a skilled trade through a combination of classroom instruction and on-the-job training under the supervision of experienced professionals._

**Earn while you learn!**  At the Flint/Saginaw Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (FSEJATC), apprentices have the unique opportunity to earn a living while they learn. Our programs combine hands-on work experience with rigorous classroom instruction, preparing participants for successful careers in the electrical industry.

**Program Overview**

**Inside Wireman Apprenticeship**

- Duration: 5 years
- Description: Focuses on electrical construction and maintenance in commercial and industrial settings.

**Telecommunications Apprenticeship**

- Duration: 3 years
- Description: Specializes in the installation and maintenance of low-voltage systems including security, data, and voice networks.

Both programs allow the apprentices to attend class **one day a week, every other week,** while working for contractors the rest of the time (contingent on meeting program requirements). Apprentices are paid for their work by the contractors they are assigned to.

* * *

**Job Descriptions**

**Inside Wireman**

Inside Wiremen perform electrical installation, construction, and maintenance in commercial and industrial environments. This includes systems such as power distribution, lighting, fire alarms, fiber optics, transformers, conduit, and security systems. Work locations can include hospitals, factories, schools, and office buildings. The nature of the job is dynamic—one day you might be installing lighting indoors, and the next day working outdoors in variable weather conditions.

**Telecommunications Installer**

These professionals install, maintain, and repair telecommunication systems, including telephone lines, cable, security systems, CCTV, and other wired or wireless systems. Tasks may involve running lines, laying cables, or installing equipment in diverse environments, from office buildings to industrial facilities. Installers work both indoors and outdoors, in all seasons, and are essential to everyday communications and emergency services.

* * *

**Apprentice Responsibilities**

- Perform physically demanding tasks (e.g., climbing, crawling, lifting, using tools, working in tight or elevated spaces).
- Demonstrate manual dexterity and hand-eye coordination.
- Maintain consistent, punctual attendance at both work and classes.
- Complete homework assignments and pass curriculum assessments.
- Adhere to all policies, procedures, and safety regulations of the JATC and job sites.
- Remain drug-free and be able to read, hear, speak, and understand instructions and warnings **in English**.

* * *
