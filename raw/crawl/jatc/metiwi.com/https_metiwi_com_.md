| ## [Why Choose METI?](https://mejatc2025.unionactive.com/index.cfm?zone=/unionactive/view_page.cfm&page=Why20Choose20METI)















Respected. Union-backed. Built to last. We train with the support of [IBEW Local 494](https://www.ibew494.com/) and [NECA Milwaukee Chapter](https://www.neca-milw.org/), giving you access to the highest standards, top wages, excellent benefits, and long-term job security. Whether you prefer a structured classroom setting or learning by doing on the job, METI has a path that fits your strengths and goals.
