| ## **Built by Hard Work. Powered by Opportunity.**

**At METI, success isn’t a slogan — it’s something people live every day. The stories below come from electricians who started where you might be now: curious, motivated, and ready for something better. Through commitment, hands-on training, and the strength of union support, they’ve built careers that offer stability, pride, and real opportunity.**

**These aren’t one-size-fits-all paths. They’re real journeys — shaped by effort, mentorship, and the belief that skilled work deserves respect and reward. Read on to see how METI helps people turn potential into lasting careers — and imagine where it could take you.**
