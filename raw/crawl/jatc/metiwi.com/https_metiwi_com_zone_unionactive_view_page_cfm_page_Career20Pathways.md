| ## Three training programs. One goal: a strong, successful career.<br>At METI, we offer three distinct pathways into the electrical industry — all backed by union strength, employer demand, and real career outcomes. Whether you prefer structured classroom learning or hands-on experience, there's a path here that fits your style and sets you up for long-term success.<br>Each training program prepares you for a different type of electrical work and leads to professional certification — from Journeyman Wireman to Certified Technician — through union-backed jobsite experience and practical training. | |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| |     |     |
| --- | --- |
| ![](https://metiwi.com/images/CP_IC.jpg) | ## INSIDE CONSTRUCTION<br>**Powering everything from schools to skyscrapers.**<br>Commercial and industrial electricians wire and maintain the systems that keep large buildings running. This path prepares you for high-demand work on complex job sites like office towers, factories, and hospitals. Choose from two routes:<br>- **Apprenticeship:** A competitive five-year program that blends classroom learning with paid, on-the-job training.<br>- **CW/CE Training Program:** A self-paced, field-based, earn-while-you-learn route to Journeyman Wireman status with more hands-on learning and less time in class. Motivated participants can achieve Journeyman status in six years. |

| ![](https://metiwi.com/images/RE-PPE-3.jpg) | ## RESIDENTIAL CONSTRUCTION<br>**Lighting the homes and neighborhoods around you.**<br>Residential electricians handle the wiring and service work that keeps houses, apartments, and condos powered safely and efficiently.<br>- **RCW Training Program:** A hands-on training program that leads to Residential Journeyman Wireman status. Learn by doing, earn while you train, and work directly alongside licensed union electricians. |
