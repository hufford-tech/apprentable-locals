| # _Welcome!_<br>#### The **Wyo**ming Statewide Electrical **J**oint **A**pprenticeship and**T**raining **C**ommittee (WYOJATC) is a cooperative effort between the Wyoming Chapter of National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local 322 and Local 415, whose combined jurisdictions cover the entire State of Wyoming.<br>#### The WYOJATC has been providing a professionally trained and highly skilled electrician workforce, as a Department of Labor Registered Apprenticeship Program, since 1965.<br>#### An apprentice completes 4 years of classroom-related instruction in addition to 8,000 hours of PAID on-the-job training (OJT) in the areas of residential, commercial and industrial work.   This fulfills the minimum qualifications to take the State of Wyoming Journey-level Electrician Licensing Exam and receive a Department of Labor nationally recognized completion certificate. | | |
