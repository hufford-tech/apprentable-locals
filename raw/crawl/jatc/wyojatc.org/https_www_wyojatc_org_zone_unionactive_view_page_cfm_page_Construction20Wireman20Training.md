Click the CW Classroom-Related Training Application below to complete the form electronically and then return to either the Wyoming JATC Training Director by email at:   bmorgan@wyojatc.org  or one of the Membership Development Coordinators/Organizers for either IBEW Local 322 or 415.

You must provide a copy of your current State of Wyoming Apprentice License and Proof of a minimum of 2,000 OJT hours with the application.

CWCRT applications must be submitted no later than August 31st of each year to be considered for the upcoming academic year.
