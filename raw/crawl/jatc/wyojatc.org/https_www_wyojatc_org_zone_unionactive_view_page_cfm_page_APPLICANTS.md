[Minimum Requirements to Apply](https://www.wyojatc.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_article.cfm&HomeID=935774&page=APPLICANTS "Read Full Article")


An applicant to the WYOJATC must meet four minimum requirements to apply for apprenticeship.

Applicant must be 18 years old at time of application.
Applicant must show proof of one credit in Algebra I or a higher math class with a passing grade OR you may substitute the SkillsPrep.org Online Learning System math course for the math requirement.  Visit https://www.

[Aptitude Test Information](https://www.wyojatc.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_article.cfm&HomeID=935873&page=APPLICANTS "Read Full Article")


Qualified applicants who have completed the application process will be notified via their email on record with the WYOJATC of the next available Aptitude Test Date, Time and Location.

You must bring a valid Driver’s License with you to the Aptitude Test for Identification purposes.  You must obtain a minimum score of "3" (updated 1/1/26) to advance to the interview process.

[Oral Interview Information](https://www.wyojatc.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_article.cfm&HomeID=935874&page=APPLICANTS "Read Full Article")


Applicants who qualify for an oral interview will be notified of the interview date and time via the email on record with the WYOJATC.   Applicants will be interviewed by a Committee representing both NECA (National Electrical Contractors Association) and the IBEW (International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers). | |     |

[Ranking/Wait List Information](https://www.wyojatc.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_article.cfm&HomeID=935875&page=APPLICANTS "Read Full Article")


Based on the interview, applicants will receive an overall ranking score. Applicants will then be ranked on a list according to this final score. As new positions become available in the apprenticeship program, names will be taken off the list in the order that their name appears.

Applicant names will remain on this list for up to 2 years if interviewed before 1/1/26 and up to 1 year if interviewing after 1/1/26. | |
