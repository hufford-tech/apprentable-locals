Journeyman Training Classes Enrollment Policy

Any participant that enrolls for a class through the WYOJATC and does not attend will have to notify the JATC a minimum of 48 hours prior to the start of the class. Anyone that does not notify the JATC a minimum of 48 hours prior to the class will be sent an invoice for $50 payable to the JATC. |
