**DO NOT CONTACT US IF:**

**You are wanting to become an apprentice:** The information on becoming an apprentice can be found on this website by clicking on the Applicant page then click on and read each section/link. Please also click on the Apprentice page and read each section/link. This will cover most questions you may have.

**You are looking for updates on your application:** There is a 30-day processing period for you to get everything required for us to process your application. During those 30 days, you can check your status by logging into the TradeSchool account you created to see if anything is marked incomplete or has notes in red. We will contact you when it is time for the next step.

**You can’t make it to the Aptitude Test you are scheduled for:** If you can not make it to your Aptitude Test, you will be counted as a No Show and your application will be closed.

**You are looking for information on your ranking:** The JATC does not give updates on Ranks once we have notified you. Please refer to the notification that was sent to you for further information on what the next step will be.
