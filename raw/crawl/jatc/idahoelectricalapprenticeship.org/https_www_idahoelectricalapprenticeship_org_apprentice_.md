# Apprentice Resources

Welcome the apprentice resource page! Here you can find a link to register for classes, log hours or sign up for any available volunteer opportunities.

## School Rules and Policies

You can find all current and active rules and policies in your Apprentice Rules and Policy handbook. These policies are set by the committee with input from the staff. Alternatively, you can download a PDF of the rules and policies below:

[Download Apprentice Rules and Policies PDF](https://www.idahoelectricalapprenticeship.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/Apprentice-Rules-and-Policies-09.01.26.pdf)

### Attendance

Current policies dictate that all missed classroom time must be made up through continuing education classes, volunteer opportunities or correspondence coursework.
