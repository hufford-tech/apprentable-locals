# Journeyman Resources

Under the Journeyman tab you can find information about CEU classes that are offered through the Southwestern Idaho Training Center. We offer CEU classes in 2 different methods: traditional classroom and paper correspondence. The in-person classes can be scheduled through the TradeSchool link on this page and are hosted at the JATC. Paper correspondence materials can be picked up from our facility and returned when completed.
