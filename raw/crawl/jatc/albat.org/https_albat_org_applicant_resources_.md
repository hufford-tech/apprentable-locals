# Applicant Resources

Apprenticeships are an opportunity for individuals to learn a career skill through “hands-on” training, not just reading about it in a book. American Line Builders Apprenticeship Training (ALBAT) apprentices are full-time employees of electrical contractors and earn excellent pay and benefits while developing skills from master craftsmen on the job. These careers are physically and mentally demanding and safety is the utmost importance, which requires focus at all times.
