# Apprentice Resources

The American Line Builders Apprenticeship Training (ALBAT) advantage as an apprenticeship program provides the opportunity for individuals seeking highly-skilled, high-paying jobs and for employers seeking to build a qualified workforce. Our programs are built on providing flexible training that combines job-related technical instruction with structured on-the-job learning experiences.

## Veteran Benefits

The ALBAT Program is federally registered enabling veterans to receive educational benefits while progressing through their apprenticeship. Apprentices that are military veterans may qualify to receive veteran benefits. In order to receive VA Benefits an apprentice must:

- Complete their application through the VA website
- Submit a copy of DD214 and Certificate of Eligibility letter to ALBAT
