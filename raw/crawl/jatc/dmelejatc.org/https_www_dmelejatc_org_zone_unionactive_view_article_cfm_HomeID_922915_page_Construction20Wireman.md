| Please watch the following video to learn how to get started logging hours as a construction wireman. <br>Construction Wireman Login Video - YouTube<br>Tap to unmute<br>[Construction Wireman Login Video](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kC7UsG7j0vk) [Trevor Stevens](https://www.youtube.com/channel/UCF5GJ1FH2Bczp2lzCgWJtPQ)<br>![thumbnail-image](https://yt3.ggpht.com/ytc/AIdro_nU2YD2Z--lTM_gdrvauZL95c14lMizI3TCYnoHVH-z3cvFi5RKknXfib9wghzEocwihg=s68-c-k-c0x00ffffff-no-rj)<br>Trevor Stevens1 subscriber<br>[Watch on](https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=kC7UsG7j0vk)<br>**Recording On-the-job-training (OJT) hours in [Tradeschool](https://secure.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/dmelejatc-org/login/index.php).**<br>The first time you log into TradeSchool you will need to use the email address that you provided to us. <br>Once you have the webpage open, you will need to click on the “reset password” link. It will then prompt you to enter a temporary password, which you will have to enter twice. Once completed you will be prompted to enter a permanent password. Make sure to save this password for future use. <br>After you have successfully logged into TradeSchool, please verify that all of your personal information is correct; spelling of your name, address, phone number, etc.<br>**Work reports**<br>Each month you are required to enter your OJT hours. This on-line form is commonly referred to as a “Work Report”. It is important that you enter your hours accurately and<br>honestly.<br>• The hours will be checked against your hours work as submitted by your contractor.<br>• OJT hours may be reviewed by the committee for possible advanced credit , at your request, after being offered an apprenticeship position.<br>• OJT hours submitted after the 7th of each month will not be considered.<br>It is your responsibility to have your work reports submitted by the 1st of each month. There is a grace period for submitting your work report of 6 days. If work reports are not received by 11:00 pm Central Time on the 7th of each month, they are considered late. The committee will NOT consider credit for hours worked in a month when the work report was late. |
