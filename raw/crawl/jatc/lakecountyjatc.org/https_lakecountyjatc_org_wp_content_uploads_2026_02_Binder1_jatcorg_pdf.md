To all current and retired Local 150 members:

Recently at the October 14, 2015 JATC Committee meeting there was a motion and a second to change the enrollment process for Journeyman classes.

The following statement is how enrollment will be established for all future Journeyman classes:

"All active members seeking employment and not working in conflict or whose position is not adverse or detrimental to the IBEW and its industry Partner, NECA, will pay the class fee that is set within the JATC class bulletin. All other members, the cost of each class is $100.00 non-refundable".

Retirees will be allowed to shadow classes, only if the enrollment is enough to support having a class. A retiree will not be allowed to take an exam or be allowed to receive CEU's for the class he or she will be shadowing.

Any question about this change can be answered by calling the Lake County JATC at 847-566-2200
