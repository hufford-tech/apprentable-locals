###### Since 1937

## OUR HISTORY

![](https://ibew692.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/HISTORY_1-1024x768.png)

- April 1, 1937

Local Union 692 received its charter from the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers on April 1, 1937.

- 29 Electricians

Twenty-nine electricians signed the charter on that day.

### $42/HR
