###### About Us

## WHO WE ARE

![](https://ibew692.net/wp-content/uploads/2023/09/membership-meeting.png)

### 600+

Local Members

FAIRNESS. JUSTICE. EQUALITY.

IBEW Local 692 is an AFL-CIO affiliated labor organization which represents more than 600 construction electricians, servicing 19 counties in Northeast Michigan. Local 692 has been providing the area with the industry’s most skilled, highly trained professionals since 1937. Our members are responsible for installing and maintaining power systems and data networks in the residential, commercial, and industrial sectors. IBEW Local 692 is committed to improving the lives of its members and their families by:

- Negotiating fair wages and strong benefits in return for an honest day’s work
- Advocating for safer jobsites and better working conditions to benefit all working families
- Providing on-the-job and classroom training

## OUR MISSION

- To organize all workers in the entire electrical construction industry, including maintenance and telecommunications workers, in Northeastern Michigan.
- To elevate the moral, intellectual, and social conditions of our members, their families, and dependents, in the interest of a higher standard of citizenship.
- To settle all disputes between employers and employees by arbitration.
- To promote reasonable methods of work.
- To secure adequate pay and provide job security for all members.
