and to their successors power to establish and hold a Union of the Brotherhood to be located at Bay City in the State of Michigan to be known as Union No. 692 with jurisdiction over the following territory: Bay City and vicinity.

This charter grants to said Union all powers delegated by the Constitution. The conditions of this charter are such that it may be retained as long as fine members in good standing comply with all requirements of the constitution and general rules of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, unless reclaimed by the International Union.

In Witness Whereof, we have hereunto set our hands and the seal of this International Union this last day of April in the year of one thousand nine hundred and sixty-seven.

## 1958

Special Called Meeting. The negotiating committee represented by Brothers Al Horneber and Clark Nitschky presented to the local what was in the offering at present. The contractors offer 8¢ now and 10¢ next May 1st, one year hence. The Union is asking for a 28¢ package in the following spread, 8¢ now, 10¢ in January and 10¢ a year from May 1st., plus Steward and job or shop seniority. At every effort employed in negotiating the contractors throw in that Saginaw signed for 8¢ now and 10¢ next year and thats what the contractors are sticking to. They refused to even negotiate on steward or job senority.

After much discussion on the above Bro. Al Nuttall moved we refer our negotiations as proposed to council. Supported by Forrest Gray. Carried.

* * * *

Executive Board appoints Floyd Young as Business Manager/Financial Secretary of the Local.

* * * *

Talked about the Alpena Local merging with Bay City or Traverse City.

## 1972

Saginaw fitters have hired one man to go out and organize the non-union shops.

* * * *

B.A. reported that the financial condition of the local is such that we have to dip into our bank account to make ends meet.

* * * *

Business Agent Young met with Dow Chemical and was informed they are going to an open shop in regards to their construction.

* * * *

Virgil Bauer, Business Agent of Alpena Local #1232, addressed the floor telling the local floor of their problems and how the I.O. wants them to merge with the Traverse City Local. Bro. Bauer would like a vote of confidence from our local to the possibility of them merging with Bay City. Discussion followed as to membership size, wages, apprentices, etc. B.A. Floyd Young moved that Alpena merge with Bay City. Jerry Burley seconded the motion. With a show of hands the motion carried. With the vote of confidence the Alpena B.A. can go to the I.O.

* * * *

New Contractor in the area, that being Faulkner Electric.

* * * *

## 1973

Project Agreement covering the Karn addition as well as the Midland Nuclear and Quanicassee Nuclear was read to the floor. I.O. informed us we (692) will sign this or the I.O. will take over such projects. Ron Ryder moved for Local 692 to hold fast on signing the Agreement. Sam Ladouceur seconded. Motion carried.

* * * *

Roger Miller moved to send our Congressmen and Senators a telegram informing them of the "police brutality" taking place at the Dow picket line.

* * * *

B.A. gave his report as such: the Apprentice Instructor was called in regards to the dress code of apprentices, hair cuts were stressed.

## 1974

Bay City Times reported in the paper that the electricians walked off the Karn job, which was untrue. The B.A. will check into this.

* * * *

Dow Chemical Plant on strike. Contractors asking for the men to go into the plant and work. The I.O. is concerned that we are losing work by not being there. B.A. has neither refused nor pushed anyone to go to work at the Dow Plant.

## 1975

Letter to the I.O. asking permission to spend $10,000.00 on a Retirees Party was read.

* * * *

Negotiating Committee reports that we will be heading to Washington to Council to settle our differences. NECA has now included in their council papers a request to affiliate our apprenticeship program with Delta College.

* * * *

Word was received from Council, a 2 year agreement with an 80¢ increase each year on June 1st. School will remain the same.

* * * *

Letter from the I.O. urging all of us to write President Ford asking for his support of the Situs Picketing Bill.

## 1976

Letter from our Health & Welfare Fund, our hospitalization has been raised to $30,000.00 under major medical.

* * * *

President Ford vetoed the Situs Picketing Bill, everyone knows what that means.

* * * *

Apprenticeship attendance at union meetings leaving alot to be desired. First and second year people very good, third and fourth year boys are hard to find on Monday night. Apprentices to meet after this regular meeting.

* * * *

I.O. has called for a meeting of all the B.A.'s of the State on June 25th, to be held here in our hall. Organizing will be the topic.

* * * *

B.A. informed the Local that the Contractors were proposing a wage cut to $10.00 per hour plus fringes or one residential man to every journeyman to try to fight the non-union on the commercial jobs. (Scale is $11.75)

* * * *

B.A. told of a hand carved plaque of the I.B.E.W. insignia hanging in the B.A. office. This was carved by Bro. Mack McCully.

* * * *

Apprenticeship teacher was discussed. The floor instructed our committee to push for our man, Terry Franklin, to be selected for the job.

## 1978

Flowers being sent in time of grief to a funeral home for a deceased were discussed. Our $15.00 limit is inadequate, Vern Barber moved to raise the limit to $30.00 maximum. Dennis Voisine seconded. Motion carried.

* * * *

B.A. attended a pre-job conference with Babcock & Wilcox, who has the installing of the steam generator at the Midland Nuclear.

* * * *

Vote on by-laws change, submitted by Bro. Paul Clements. Collect $3.00 per member for death benefits and pay out $3.00 times the number of remaining members. Vote was 64 yes and 5 no. Carried.

* * * *

B.A. gave his report, reviewed the work picture and apologized for getting hot and upset at the last meeting with a few bad words.

* * * *

Letter read from Townsend & Bottum contractors on the Consumers Power Karn job thanking us for the fine job and cooperation.

* * * *

B.A. needs help and has hired Bob Rajewski as Assistant.

* * * *

One man contractors were discussed with the B.A. asking the floor what it wishes to do with this situation. After much discussion Ron Ryder moved for the B.A. to enforce the working agreement in regards to hiring a man from the union hall. Ron Voisine seconded. Motion carried.

* * * *

Bud Voisine for County Executor was discussed. Bob Voisine moved for the local to go on record endorsing Bud Voisine. Dennis Voisine seconded. Motion carried.

* * * *

Local pension plan was discussed. LU 692 is the lowest in the state, at 30¢ per hour, of 14 locals. Floyd Young moved to have a mail out notifying members for the first meeting in March of a possible pension increase.

* * * *

Larry Pittsley moved that we increase our pension contribution to the Michigan Plan by taking 20¢ of our upcoming raise and 20% of any raises for the next 5 years. Dennis Gandy seconded. Vote was 60 yes and 32 no. Carried.

* * * *

Assistant B.A. will be taking over as night shift Steward at Bechtel. Night Steward, Jim Peil, will be taking the Assistants job in the office.

* * * *

Local union membership standing at 306.

* * * *

Bechtel is looking into a Presidents Agreement for the start-up at the Midland job.

* * * *

Letter from the I.O. on white ticket people was discussed with Tom Ryder moving to forward a letter to the International Vice President that we will be taking in 5 people and that we are organizing our area and have in the past. Lloyd Goss seconded. Motion carried.

* * * *

Local purchased the house and property adjacent to present property. After discussion from the floor it was decided the LU should dispose of the house rather than up-grade for offices or apprentice classrooms.

## 1981

Alpena Local has asked to merge with us. It has been discussed at three E-Board meetings. E-Board is in favor.

* * * *

Alpena Local has voted in favor of merging with Bay City. Alpena B.A., Al Kerr, commented they were a part of our local until 1949. Few problems have to be worked out yet, wages, apprentice school, etc. Bob Macaulay moved to accept the Alpena Local under our jurisdiction. Gus Voisine seconded. Vote by secret ballot was 99 yes and 19 no. Carried.

* * * *

B.A. spent today in court over the picket line at Petro-Chem.

Apprenticeship school will be moved to Bay City in May. The Instructor will stay on, classes will be held in Woodside School.

Alpena will negotiate their own contract this year, after that there will be one contract for our whole jurisdiction.

Letter received from Retired Brother Everett McCully, who enjoys reading articles in the Journal from the Local. Keep up the good work!

B.A. reported on bomb scare at Bechtel and time lost.

B.A. met in Chicago with I.O. and Bechtel on securing reverse lay-off procedure for Nuclear Plant.

Show up time for bomb scare, B.A. reports, we will get 4 hours but only this time.

Letter from Bechtel Power Corporation, manpower peak should be around 690.

Building Trades went on record endorsing Doug Kienbaum for City Commissioner.

Letter from Michigan State AFL-CIO on Solidarity Day in Washington, D.C. on September 19th, to protest Reagan's policies. There was a discussion on possibly renting buses to take the members there.

B.A. informed the floor that we have a member who has not missed a local meeting in 18 years, that being Jack Oslund. It was moved and supported for the B.A. to buy a small plaque commemorating this.

## 1984

Assistant Agent Erv Bauer reports the B.A. is off due to a heart attack.

Robert Rajewski is hired to assist Erv Bauer.

Lloyd Canute reported for the Apprenticeship Committee. The School Board has given the Committee 3 weeks to move out of the present location. Will be moving to Webster School at Midland and Mackinaw Roads.

Nuclear Plant is shut down all men laid off.

Letter from Mr. Owens of the General Presidents Committee to Mr. Jack Joyce, Vice President of the National Building and Construction Trades Council, requesting him to look into Local Union 692's grievance against Bechtel for a work stoppage.

B.A. Young gave a report of the membership at 365 and where they are working, only 78 members are employed.

B.A. reported we had a bomb scare in our building. Spoke of a need to update our entrance doors as a result of the police recommendation, or better yet install an entrance set of doors for security and to save heat.

## 1985

Letter was read from our President, Michael Chaffin, informing the Local that he is resigning. Vice President Ron Ryder informed the Local body that per the IBEW Constitution the Executive Board appoints to fill out the term.

Tom Ryder is appointed President and is sworn in by the Vice President.

Letter from Faulkner Construction informing their employees not to touch any asbestos products, but to inform supervision and it will be taken care of in an approved manner.

Letter was read from Treasurer, Walter Pawlak, informing the Local he will be resigning effective July 1st.

Doug Anderson is appointed Treasurer.

Double booking was discussed. I.O. says no man working should be on a book. Apprentice Scholorship Loan Agreement was discussed. Job safety was discussed.

Letter from Vice President Conway. He will be coming to meet with Local 692 and Local 498 on jurisdictional boundaries on August 15th.

Building Trades Rep. is going to be speaking to Dow Chemical on a possible package for all trades to do Dow's work.

I.O. taking of merging Bay City, Traverse City and Sault Ste. Marie all into one local.

Letter read to the floor by former members of the Alpena Local addressed to V.P. Conway in regards to re-establishing the old local and opposed to being merged into Traverse City.

Fred Schmidt reported for the advertising committee. Possibly putting up a sign on the property, checking all legal aspects.

Zoning of the local was discussed, with the original Bay City area being Zone I and the original Alpena area being Zone II. Two different wage scales.

## 1986

Dow Chemical and what other trades are doing to secure work was discussed to some length.

* * * *

N.E.B.F. letter on raise in pension from $11.00 per month for each year credit to $13.00 per month for each year of credit.

* * * *

Letter from our Michigan pension fund raising benefits to 3.4% plus a 5% increase for retirees, these raises are retroactive to September 1, 1985.

* * * *

Regular order of business was suspended to hear from the committee set up to see what it would take to get the Dow work back. The committee has proposed setting up a "Pre Apprentice Pool" (P.A.P.). This pool would be paid $6.00 per hour with no benefits; would work on a 1-1 ratio with journeymen. After much discussion Erv Bauer moved we put the P.A.P. program to a vote. Bud Voisine seconded. With all the ballots counted the vote was 72 yes and 41 no. Motion carried.

* * * *

B.A. reported the apprenticeship school will be moving soon. School board wants the building back (Webster School) for school children.

* * * *

Mailing referrals to members living in Zone II area was discussed. Jack York asked for a show of hands from those who favor the mailing. Unanimous support was shown for the mailing.

* * * *

Letter from the Michigan State Building Trades urging all of us to write the Public Service Commission on support for converting the Midland plant to gas fire was read.

* * * *

John Bond, B.A. Local 948, spoke on Flint's new referral procedure and thanked our members for their help and quality of work performed. Local 948 may be going on strike and he would like the support of those working in their area.

* * * *

Apprenticeship classes are now being held in the union office building. The Apprenticeship Committee is looking at buying property east of the union property for the school.

* * * *

Fred Schmidt reported for the sign committee. The Committee is ready to ask the International Office for permission to spend $10,000.00 for the parking lot sign.
