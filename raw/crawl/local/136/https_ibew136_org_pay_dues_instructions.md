# Pay Dues Instructions

Dues increase went into effect on July 1st, 2025\. The monthly dues amounts are now:

Journeyman Wireman - $61.00 per month

Apprentice Wireman - $54.00 Per month

CE/CW -$54.00 Per Month

Death Benefits -$3.00 Each

If you owe 4 - 6 months of dues, you need to pay the total amount owed, along with a $30.00 reinstatement fee as per Article XXII Section 2 of The IBEW Constitution. If you are not sure of the total you owe, call Pick at the hall and he can let you know or email [pick@ibew136.org](mailto:pick@ibew136.org).
