# Educational Videos

On this page, you will find three separate videos made for IBEW 136 members. The first video is for those taking their first job referral with one of our contractors. It will guide you on what to expect during your first referral. The second video focuses on Benefits and Retirement, showing real examples of the retirement benefits and money you can expect to retire with as an IBEW 136 member. The third video is designed for New Journeymen Wiremen. If you have recently become a JW, this video is useful whether you are working locally or away.
