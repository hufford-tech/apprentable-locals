### Job Fair for Electricians on July 23

**Electricians: You're invited to a job fair!**

**Date**: July 23rd

**Time**: 4-7 p.m.

**Location**: Dort Financial Center, 3501 Lapeer Rd., Flint, MI

- $46 for journeyman level employees with an additional $29+ in benefits

- Paid family healthcare (not from your check)

- Retirement funds provided by employer

- Hiring for immediate openings: 5+ years experience, journeyman and foreman


Register here: [wepowernorthamerica.org/job-fairs-hiring-events](https://wepowernorthamerica.org/job-fairs-hiring-events/)
