### About IBEW Local 948

At the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 948, we power careers, build communities, and stand strong for the rights of electrical workers in Mid-Michigan. Since our founding in 1919, we’ve been dedicated to ensuring fair wages, top-tier training, and a safe working environment for our members.

​

We partner with leading contractors to deliver the highest quality electrical work—from commercial and industrial projects to cutting-edge renewable energy solutions. Our commitment to excellence is reflected in our state-of-the-art apprenticeship programs, ongoing skill development, and unwavering advocacy for workers' rights.

​

Whether you’re an experienced electrician looking to elevate your career, a contractor seeking the best-trained workforce, or someone ready to start an exciting career in the electrical trade, IBEW Local 948 is your connection to a brighter future.
