### Voice, Data, and Video Technicians

# Advance Your Career as a Voice, Data & Video Technician

Unlock Your Potential: Become a Voice, Data & Video Technician with IBEW Local 948. In today's booming tech landscape, skilled low-voltage technicians are in high demand. IBEW Local 948 provides the platform you need to launch a thriving career, offering unparalleled training, access to top industry contractors, and the security of competitive wages and comprehensive benefits. Step into a future filled with growth and stability.

## Why Join Local 948?

✅ Higher Pay & Benefits– Contractor-paid health insurance, pension and retirement plans, and strong contracts

✅ Job Security – Access to top-tier projects with trusted contractors

✅ Ongoing Training – Stay ahead with the latest industry advancements

✅ A Brotherhood That Cares – Support when you need it most

Are you ready for better opportunities, better benefits, and a stronger future? IBEW Local 948 is ready for you.
