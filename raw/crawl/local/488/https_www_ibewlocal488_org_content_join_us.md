# Join Us

**Are you a skilled electrical worker looking for fair pay, great benefits, and a secure future?**

We are actively building a highly skilled workforce to partner with our top-tier contractor base—and we want you on our team.

Your employers often join associations—like the ABC, CECA, SAA, or IEC—to protect their company’s best interests. Doesn’t it make sense to join an organization dedicated to protecting _yours_?

Think about it: Electrical contractors rely on signed contracts for every bid and supply order. They would never work without one. You deserve that same level of security.

A union contract is a legally binding agreement that guarantees your rights, wages, and safe working conditions. Because we operate democratically, you get a direct voice in negotiating and approving the terms of your future.

**Ready to take the next step?**

Curious if the Union is right for you?  Visit [www.CTGoingUnion.com](http://www.ctgoingunion.com/) for additional resources.
