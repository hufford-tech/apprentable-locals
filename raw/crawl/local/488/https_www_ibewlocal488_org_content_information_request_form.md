# Information Request Form

It is NOT an employers choice for his workforce to be unionized, it is YOU and YOUR coworkers choice.

You have the RIGHT to join and be represented by a Union.  IBEW Local 488 would like to be that Union. The IBEW represents over 750,000 workers across the country and you too can be a part of our brotherhood.

[CLICK HERE FOR ADDITIONAL INFORMATION ON ORGANIZING](https://ibewlocal488.org/content/organizing)

Curious about what you could be making under an IBEW Agreement?  [CLICK Here to check out our Wage Comparison Calculator!](https://c1acr186.caspio.com/dp/c9e2700053f951bc860f49f69278)

All information submitted will be kept confidential to protect YOUR rights.

Completion of this form does not guarantee membership in Local Union 488 and does not guarantee employment with any contractor party to an agreement with Local Union 488.
