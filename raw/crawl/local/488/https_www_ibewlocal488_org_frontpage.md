# [39th Memorial of the L'Ambiance Plaza Tragedy](https://www.ibewlocal488.org/content/39th-memorial-lambiance-plaza-tragedy)

On April 23, 1987, the L'Ambiance Plaza collapsed in a devastating tragedy, taking the lives of 28 hardworking construction workers. It was a dark day that forever changed Bridgeport and left an indelible mark on the families and friends left behind.

We must never forget them, nor the sacrifices they made.

Please join us Thursday April 23, 2026 at Bridgeport City Hall (side entrance) 45 Lyons Terrace, at 11:00am (service to start promptly at 11:30am), as we gather to honor their memory, support one another, and ensure their legacy is never forgotten.

[![](https://www.ibewlocal488.org/sites/ibewlocal488.org/files/styles/medium/public/2026%20L%27Ambiance%20Ad.png?itok=WZqnSS1J)](https://www.ibewlocal488.org/content/39th-memorial-lambiance-plaza-tragedy)
