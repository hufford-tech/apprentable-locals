| # Local 430 - Training Opportunities<br>IBEW Local Union 430 is proud to be a partner in training with the NECA chapter and the NJATC. We provide an avenue for all electrical workers to attain the best possible training for Journeymen and Apprentices.<br>Skill enhancement classes are available for all members interested in improving their skills as electricians to keep up with today's ever changing electrical codes and methods of wiring.<br>Information on Apprenticeship training opportunities and it's requirements are listed below.<br>* * *<br># **Racine Area Electrical Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee  Notice of Opportunity**<br>Applications for the Electrical (Construction) Apprenticeship Program are accepted year-round and interviews are three times a year.<br>All applications must be completed by due date be considered for the interview session. All listed information must be received by due date to qualify for an interview. ( _Please call our office for exact due dates._)<br>Applications can be filled out Monday 7:30-3:30. (Except for scheduled office closures and holidays) at:<br>> International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local Union 430<br>> <br>> 1840 Sycamore Avenue<br>> <br>> Racine, WI 53406<br>> <br>> (262) 633-2844<br>APPLICANTS MUST APPEAR IN PERSON TO COMPLETE THE APPLICATION FORM.<br>All applications will be received and considered without regard to race, color, religion, sex, or national origin.<br>* * *<br># **Basic Minimum Requirements to Quaify for an Interview**<br>> **AGE**: Must be at least (18) years of age before the Time of Assignment for an Apprenticeship and Furnish proof of age. ( _To Qualify: Copy of Birth Certificate or Valid Drivers' License Required._)<br>> <br>> **EDUCATION**: Must be a High School Graduate or have attained a General Equivalency Diploma before the Time of Assignment for an Apprenticeship. ( _To Qualify: Copy of Transcripts and Certificate Required._)<br>> <br>> **MINIMUM MATH**: Must have completed one (1) full year of High School Algebra with a passing grade of "C", or one (1) post High School Algebra course with a passing grade of "C". Successful completion of Interactive ModuMath Algebra, available at the WI Technical College, is also an equivalent to the minimum math requirement.<br>> <br>> **APTITUDE TEST**: Must attain established norms on the NJATC Apprentice Selection Test Battery. ( _Developed and validated by the American Institute for Research._) Arrangements for testing are made by the Committee.<br>> <br>> **PHYSICAL ABILITY**: Must present or sign a statement that he or she is physically able to perform electrical construction work. In the future, applicants selected for an apprenticeship may be required to provide results of a physical examination.<br>> <br>> **TRANSCRIPTS**: Must provide official High School transcript(s) and ( _if the minimum Math requirements listed elsewhere_) post High School transcript(s) showing courses and grades attained. Transcripts must indicate Algebra or list classes that are equivalent to one full year of High School Algebra. You must provide a letter from the school confirming the class is an equivalent to the minimum Math requirement.<br>> <br>> **LICENSE**: Must have a valid Wisconsin Driver's License and be insurable. | | |
