# ANNUITY FUND

The Trustees of Local 139 have in place a unique supplement for your pension-an annuity. This invested fund is part of your package and is similar to a 401-K in the sense that it is a finite resource, where your pension will continue to pay the rest of your life. Please review the Annuity Trust Document for the details on how your Plan operates.

**REMEMBER**-If you have any changes in address, marital status or beneficiary-you must contact the fund office and amend your profile card.

If you have any questions regarding your annuity, please call the Fund Office at [**(607) 732-1237**](tel:6077321237) or email Kris at [**fundmanager@ibew139.com**](mailto:fundmanager@ibew139.com).
