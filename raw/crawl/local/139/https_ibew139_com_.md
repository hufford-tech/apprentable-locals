#### BECOMING A MEMBER

Welcome to IBEW Local 139. We appreciate your interest in our Local. You may become a member by any of the following means: Applying for the apprenticeship program. Meet the minimum qualifications. Take and pass an aptitude test. Obtain an interview. Get placed on an eligibility list that the local chapter uses to pick its new apprentices.

[LEARN MORE](https://ibew139.com/becoming-a-member/)

![](https://elmiraibew.com/wp-content/uploads/2021/02/HANDSHAKE.jpg)

#### BECOME A PARTNER CONTRACTOR

Ours is an organization consisting of 220 members. IBEW Local 139 has been in existence since 1901, supplying skilled electricians to nearly 40 contractors in the Southern Tier of New York. The pride and workmanship of Local 139 is evident in just about every project.

# Who Are We?

Local 139 has been serving and servicing the Southern Tier of New York with skill, pride and dedication since 1901. We are proud to be a community partner and our members are proud of their accomplishments in the Southern Tier.

Our diversity in training puts us at the forefront from the simplest of electrical projects to wind farm and solar arrays. Learn More About IBEW Local 139
