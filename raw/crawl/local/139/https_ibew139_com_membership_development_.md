# MEMBERSHIP DEVELOPMENT

Welcome to Local 139’s Membership Development Page.

Our mission is to make it possible for all people that are working at the electrical trade to have the same opportunities that our members have. The opportunity to be paid what your skills are worth, to provide a decent life for his/her family and the peace of mind that there will be a pension to take care of their needs when it’s time to retire is what we all strive for.

We have several ways to become a member of this Local Union. Please contact us if you have questions. Keep in mind that you don’t have to leave your employer to become a member of Local 139. We welcome your employer to become a signatory contractor and that way all of your colleagues will enjoy the benefits of being a member of Local 139.

Bringing a Union to your workplace is your decision, not your employers. It’s your federally protected right to form a Union. Let us tell you how we can help.
