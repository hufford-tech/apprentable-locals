# PENSION FUND

The Trustees of Local 139 have in place a generous pension which, depending on how much you work, will allow our members live out their golden years with dignity and peace of mind. The pension, along with your annuity, NEBF, International Pension and Social Security can make your retirement years everything that you deserve.

REMEMBER – If you have any changes in address, marital status or beneficiary-you must contact the fund office and amend your profile card.

When the time comes to think about your retirement plans, whether its regular, early or disability, please contact the Fund Office to discuss your options. You can contact the Fund Office at [(607) 732-1237](tel:6077321237) or email Kris at [fundmanager@ibew139.com](mailto:fundmanager@ibew139.com).
