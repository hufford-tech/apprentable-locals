## Union Worx Referral Starts May 6, 2026

IBEW Local 812 will be moving to the Union Worx Platform for job referral, messaging, and optional online dues payment.

The rollout for referral and messaging is May 6, 2026.
The anticipated rollout for online dues payment will be June of 2026

Please register in advance at ibew812.unionworx.cloud or by downloading the Union Worx App and selecting IBEW Local 812 from the drop-down list.
