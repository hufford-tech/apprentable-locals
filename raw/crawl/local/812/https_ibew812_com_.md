# Welcome to the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Union Local 812.

![](https://ibew812.com/wp-content/uploads/821-badge.png)We are glad you’ve come to visit our site. We’ve provided a lot of information for our visitors and members. If you don’t find what you are looking for please feel free to contact us.

IBEW Local 812 is committed to the advancement of the wages, benefits, and working conditions of all electrical workers in our region. Our goals are to organize all contractors’ electricians, and technicians into the IBEW who want to advance the interests of workers within the electrical industry.

With a highly trained workforce, we are able to provide contractors with skilled workers when they need them. We are proud of the skills that our members bring to the jobsite day after day.
