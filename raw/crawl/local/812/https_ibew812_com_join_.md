### What Does Union Membership Mean for Me?

Nearly everyone is a member of something.  It may be a church, a club, a team, or a special interest group. Some belong to the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers…as a matter of fact, more than 700,000 men and women across the U.S. and Canada are already members of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers. Your employer may belong to an organization that represents business interests. Maybe you are considering joining a group that will represent yours.

The IBEW is an organization created to further the interests of workers in the electrical industry. Its primary goal is to raise the standard of living and improve the working conditions of its members by collectively bargaining with employers for terms and conditions of employment. The IBEW is not “anti-contractor”, but it is “pro-worker”.

Some of the potential benefits of becoming a member of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers are as follows:

- Premium Wages

- Family Healthcare Coverage

- Retirement Benefits

- Improved Working Conditions

- Education and Training Opportunities

All employers have their own views of union membership. You will find employers that will tell you that there’s no significant benefit to union membership. Conversely, you will find employers that realize union membership is a key component in their success. Whichever employer you encounter, you should know that your right to join a union is legally guaranteed.

The National Labor Relations Act gives you the right to form or join a union. All employers have their own views of union membership.
