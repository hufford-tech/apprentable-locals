# Privacy Policy

## Who we are

Our website address is: https://ibew812.com.

## What personal data may be collected

### Contact forms

If you use a contact form (for instance, if you re-sign online for a job through the Hall), your information will be kept on file by the business manager so that you can be notified of work in our area. If you are signing our book and are from a different Local, our business manager may call your Hall.

Personal information provided by website users will be used strictly for internal union member communications and union administration.

### Embedded content from other websites

Articles on this site may include embedded content (e.g. videos, images, articles, etc.). Embedded content from other websites behaves in the exact same way as if the visitor has visited the other website.

These websites may collect data about you, use cookies, embed additional third-party tracking, and monitor your interaction with that embedded content, including tracking your interaction with the embedded content if you have an account and are logged in to that website.
