## BUSINESS CENTER

IBEW Local 607 is located at 25 S. 5th Street in Shamokin, PA. Local 607 has been representing central Pennsylvania electricians for over 90 years.

The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers in partnership with the National Electrical Contractors Association provides training in safety and technical applications for every worker to insure the consistent ability to provide safe, skilled, and productive employees to meet your demand.

Affiliated contractors have incontestable, flexible access to highly skilled and productive electrical workers. Workers enjoy outstanding benefits and security!
