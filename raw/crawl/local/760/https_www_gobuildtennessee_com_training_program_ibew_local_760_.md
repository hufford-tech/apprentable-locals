## about the training

ETAK offers apprenticeship and other programs to start a career as an Electrician. ETAK programs are not just classroom time instruction. Most of your time will be spent on a job earning a paycheck while you learn electrical skills. On the job training coupled with related classroom training is required to become a true craft worker. ETAK offers two paths to Journeyman Electrician status. One is through the traditional Apprenticeship program and the second is through the CW/CE program. Both programs not only offer but require on the job training and related studies.

## application process

Contacting the program directly is the first step to starting your journey towards a successful career in the trades. By reaching out to the program, you can learn more about the program requirements, the application process, and the various trades that are available for apprenticeships. Many programs are happy to answer any questions you may have about the program, the industry, and what you can expect from your apprenticeship and any available scholarships. They can also provide you with guidance and support as you navigate the application process, ensuring that you are fully prepared to begin your training. Whether you’re a recent high school graduate, a career-changer, or someone looking to advance your existing career, contacting a skilled trades apprenticeship program is a crucial step towards achieving your career goals.
