**IBEW LOCAL 129**

**REFERRAL PROCEDURE**

All applicants must initially register the "OUT OF WORK LIST" in person during normal business hours of 7:30-Noon and 1:00-4:30 Monday through Friday.

Registrants must renew their applications between the **10th and the 16th** of each month by one of the following methods:

- **ONLINE** \- Log in using your member credentials. Navigate to the "Re-Sign" tab.
- **ON THE IBEW Local 129 APP** \- Download the IBEW Local 129 App in the Apple Store or Google Play Store. Login using your member credentials. Click on the "Re-Sign" tab.
- **IN PERSON**\- Between our normal business hours
- **FAX**\- (440) 233-4221
- **E-MAIL**\- Send to reregister@ibew129.org \*PUT ALL INFORMATION IN THE SUBJECT LINE\*
- **MAIL**\- 6100 S Broadway, Suite 201, Lorain, Ohio 44053

Registrants who fail to renew their applications between the **10th and 16th** of each month will be removed from the list and required to reregister in person.

**Emails must have NAME, HOME LOCAL, PAGE and LINE # in the subject line.**The Subject line of the email should look like this;_Joe Electrician, 130, 141-3_

_That information is; Name, Home Local, Page & Line #_

**\*Registrants who fail to put this information in the subject line will not be considered for re-sign.\***
