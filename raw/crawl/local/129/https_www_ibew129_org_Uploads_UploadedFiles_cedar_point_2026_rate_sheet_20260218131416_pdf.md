## Subject: Wage Increase- Effective February 9, 2026

Dear Michelle Geiman,

We are writing to notify you that, as of February 9, 2026, the Electricians will have as follows:

A $2.00 per hour wage increase, of which $0.20 is allocated to Health & Welfare, making the new Health & Welfare rate of $9.20, and the new hourly pay rate per hour $42.40.

FOREMAN will be paid $2.00 per hour above the regular pay rate.

Shift Pay:
2nd shift $0.50 per hour above the regular pay rate.
3rd shift $1.25 per hour above the regular pay rate.

Employee shall receive the shift rate of pay for every hour worked, including overtime pay.
