## IBEW 611 Celebrates New Journeymen Wiremen

On July 11th, the IBEW 611 and NMJATC celebrated the newest members of the union becoming Journeymen Wiremen. This is the culmination of many, many years of hard work, study, and sacrifice. The IBEW is proud of all its members, but especially those members who were able to breakout and earn their EE98-J.
