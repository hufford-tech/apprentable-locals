# IBEW Objectives

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/6387999f8058957c459c023d_businessman%20(2).svg)

To organize all workers in the entire electrical industry in the United States and Canada, including all those in public utilities and electrical manufacturing, into local unions

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/638790bb35f5594d6241d6d7_iteration%20(2).svg)

To promote reasonable methods of work

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/63879126b38e83569cfb0245_honesty%20(2).svg)

To cultivate feelings of friendship among those of our industry

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/63879274bce3ce6039aef2c2_intermediary%20(2).svg)

To settle all disputes between employers and employees by arbitration (if possible)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/638797750805a911a6f7b749_fever.svg)

To assist each other in sickness or distress

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/638797db80589588f49be817_suitcase.svg)

To secure employment

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/6387981f1c9a00c4936c365e_clock%20(1).svg)

To reduce the hours of daily labor

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/638798865e706c37355be682_cheque.svg)

To secure adequate pay for our work

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/638798ead0ba6e0169971c8e_house.svg)

To seek a higher and higher standard of living

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/6387999f8058957c459c023d_businessman%20(2).svg)

To seek security for the individual

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/635197aa2dfb2cdd0df3ca00/63879c604f6b1002dcb64fe4_citizenship.svg)

And, by legal and proper means, to elevate the moral, intellectual, and social conditions of our members, their families, and dependents, in the interest of a higher standard of citizenship
