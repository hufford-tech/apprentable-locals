# Journeyman Wireman

Electricians deserve to be represented by an organization that puts them first. The IBEW represents more than 750,000 electrical workers and provides them with the very best working conditions and benefits that come with the IBEW. Join our team at IBEW Local 611 and see why electrical workers from across North America choose the IBEW.

[Join Local 611](https://www.ibew611.org/join-as-journeyman-electrician) [Become an Apprentice](https://nmjatc.org/)

### Training

## Foreman's Development Series

Local #611 and the JATC are pleased to announce the Albuquerque Foreman Development Series .

[Learn More](https://nmjatc.org/journeymen)

There is no cost for these classes but advanced registration is required.

**For registration call the JATC at** [**(505) 341-4444**](tel:5053414444) **, a week in advance.**

To register you will need to provide your name, address, your NM EE98J Journeyman’s license number, your Union Card number and a telephone number where you can be reached.

Class size is limited to 25 people, on a first come / first serve basis; so register early and be sure to attend.

## Inside Wages and Benefits

### Journeyman Wireman 2026:

$42.20 per hour on your check

$6.20 per hour for Health Insurance \*\*

$6.15 per hour for Pension/401K \*

$1.27 per hour for NEBF (Retirement Plan) \*

**Total Package worth: $55.82 per hour**

\\* PAID BY CONTRACTORS ON ALL PROJECTS

\\*\\* FOR ENTIRE FAMILY

### 2027:

$44.20 per hour on your check

$6.20 per hour for Health Insurance \*\*

$6.50 per hour for Pension/401K \*

$1.33 per hour for NEBF (Retirement Plan) \*

**Total Package worth: $58.23 per hour**

\\* PAID BY CONTRACTORS ON ALL PROJECTS

\\*\\* FOR ENTIRE FAMILY
