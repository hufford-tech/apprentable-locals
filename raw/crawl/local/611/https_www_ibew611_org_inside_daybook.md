## Information    **The annual IBEW summer picnic is 8/22 from 11-5 at La Luz campground in the Jemez Mountains!**    **The Labor Day picnic is 8/29 from 10-2 at Mariposa Park in ABQ.** [**There is a golf tournament coming up. Honor Flight at Isleta on 9/11. If you’re interested, call the hall.**\ \ The carhartt store is giving 15% off with a current dues receipt.\ \ Cavenders is giving 10% off with a current dues receipt.](https://www.ibew611.org/inside-daybook\#)

Online bidding is now available! To bid online, you must be logged into your personal account through the either the website or the IBEW 611 app. You must be on the book for the job you are trying to bid on. You must click on the box for the job you are interested in and then hit the submit button. If you do not have the option of checking the box, you are not on that specific book. You will get an email to confirm that you have successfully bid on the job.

The New Mexico Regulation and Licensing Department has issued a **temporary directive on temporary journeyman certificates**. Please call the hall for more information.

If you want to work as a Journeyman Tech (traffic signalization, street lighting) or as a Substation Tech, you must sign those books and then check the Outside Daybook for those job calls.

Facebook only does orientation on Monday and Tuesday.

Intel does a background check. It takes about a week for the background check to clear before you can start work.

To obtain a New Mexico Journeyman certificate go to [public.psiexams.com](https://public.psiexams.com/)

In order to get on the books, in person signing is required; we have quarterly re-sign periods in Jan, April, July, and Oct, via fax, mail, in person or on our website. Yearly In-person is also required.
