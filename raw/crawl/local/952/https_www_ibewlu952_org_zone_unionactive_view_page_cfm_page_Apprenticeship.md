**For More Information:** **Please DO NOT CONTACT the IBEW Local 952 Union Hall for application process questions; they do not have any information regarding entrance into our program. For more information or to contact the Ventura County Electrical Apprenticeship Program**, [Click Here](http://www.electricalcareersvc.com/)

**How to Apply:**

To view details regarding the availability of applications, click on one of the links below to apply at a Training Center near you:

     Los Angeles County: [Click Here](http://www.laett.com/)

     Orange County: [Click Here](http://www.ocett.org/)

     Riverside / San Bernardino Counties: [Click Here](http://www.ieetc.org/)

     Santa Barbara County: [Click Here](http://ibew413.org/index.cfm?zone=/unionactive/view_page.cfm&page=SBJATC)

     San Diego / Imperial Counties: [Click Here](https://etiedusandiego.org/)

     San Luis Obispo County: [Click Here](http://www.ibew639.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_page.cfm&page=Apprenticeship)

**Ventura County:** [Click Here](http://www.electricalcareersvc.com/)

     Other locations and trades may be searched through the California Division of Apprenticeship Standards website: [Click Here](http://www.dir.ca.gov/databases/das/aigstart.asp)
