# **INITIAL BOOK SIGNING**

**EFFECTIVE FEBRUARY 15th, 2021:**

IBEW Local Union 426 will allow members to initially sign the out of work books in person, via email **( [office@ibew426.com](mailto:office@ibew426.com))** or via fax **_(605-335-7672)._**

![](https://cdn02.webit.com/api/v1/Image/5F0FD476944D90D6B6416467291FEEDB/5F0FD476944D90D6B6416467291FEEDB.JPG?size=1920x1440)

##### REQUIRED MONTHLY:   BEGINNING ON THE 10TH OF EACH MONTH AND ENDING ON THE 16TH OF EVERY MONTH.

_Failure to resign according to the rules below will mean the removal of your name from the "Out of Work List"_

##### WAYS TO RE-SIGN

Re-signs can be done for Local 426 in FIVE different ways (no phone calls please)

1. In person during business hours (M-F 8.00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m.)

3. Online at [www.ibew426jobcalls.com](https://www.ibew426jobcalls.com/). You must login and go to My Account and click on RE-SIGN. The system will only accept re-signs from 12:00 a.m. on the 10th of the month untill 11:59 p.m. on the 16th of the month.

5. Via the Union Worx App. You must login and go to My Account and click on RE-SIGN. The system will only accept re-signs from 12 a.m. on the 10th of every month untill 11:59 p.m. on the 16th of the month.

7. Via fax between 8:30 a.m. & 4:30 p.m. Monday-Friday. Fax number is 605-335-7672 _(must include original sign date.)_

9. Via mail: 3725 N 4th Ave Sioux Falls, SD 57104 _(must include original sign date.)_
