| Entry Level Positions |

|     |
| --- |
| |     |
| --- |
| Jun 10, 2026 | |
| **A worker may begin at one of several levels, depending on whether they have prior experience, and the extent of their experience:**<br>Do you have experience in electrical construction, and can you provide proof of prior electrical experience? (W-2s, pay stubs, letter from employer, etc.)<br>Or are you looking to get started, but have little or no prior experience?<br>Based on hours of experience, you could be placed in the Construction Wireman (CW) program. This is an hours-based advancement program.<br>These positions are not apprenticeships.<br>**No prior experience, or limited prior experience in Electrical Construction:**<br>**Inside Construction Wireman (CW):**<br>CW-1 starting wage is 40% of Inside Journeyman wage, or $20.68 per hour, plus benefits. <br>**Classifications, Hours, and Wages:**<br>CW-1: 0-2000 hrs, 40% of Journeyman wage<br>CW-2: 2001-4000 hrs, 45% of Journeyman wage<br>CW-3: 4001-6000 hrs, 55% of Journeyman wage<br>**No prior experience, or limited prior experience in Limited Energy (VDV) Electrical Construction:**<br>**Installer Trainee (IT):**<br>IT-1 starting wage is 60% of Limited Energy (VDV) Technician wage, or $21.91 per hour, plus benefits. <br>**Classifications, Hours, and Wages:**<br>IT-1: 0-900 hrs, 60% of Technician wage plus benefits<br>IT-2: 901-2000 hrs, 60% of Technician wage plus benefits<br>IT-3: 2001-4000 hrs, 65% of Technician wage plus benefits<br>IT-4: 4001-6000 hrs, 70% of Technician wage plus benefits<br>IT-5: 6001-8000 hrs, 80% of Technician wage plus benefits<br>IT-6: 8001+ hrs. 90% of Technician wage plus benefits<br>[**Click here**](https://ibew159.org/?zone=/unionactive/data_manager/form_page.cfm&usegroup=1320) to contact one of our Membership Development Representatives and to request a General Application. Please note that this is not an Apprenticeship Application. |
