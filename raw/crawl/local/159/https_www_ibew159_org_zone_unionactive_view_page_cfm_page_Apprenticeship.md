HOW TO APPLY:

- **New Applicants**: Create a WI NECA-IBEW JATC account here: [https://tinyurl.com/159NEWapplicant](https://tinyurl.com/159NEWapplicant "https://tinyurl.com/159NEWapplicant")
- **Reapplicants**: An account has already been created for you. You will need to set up a password to access a new application: [https://tinyurl.com/159REAPPLYapplicant](https://tinyurl.com/159REAPPLYapplicant "https://tinyurl.com/159REAPPLYapplicant")

All required documentation must be uploaded directly to your online application. Documents cannot be emailed, mailed, or dropped off.

**If you should have any questions regarding the application process or your application, please feel free to contact:**

- **WI NECA-IBEW Apprenticeship and Training Office: (608) 221-3321**

(Application period is closed on the 4th Friday of each month)

(There is a $20 processing fee.)

**Application Requirements:**

Applicant must be at least 18 years of age at the time of assignment for an apprenticeship, and provide proof of age.

Must be a high school graduate or have a GED/HSED. Provide copy of high school transcripts and GED/HSED certificate, if applicable.

Must have completed one full year of high school Algebra, or one post-high school Algebra course, with a passing average grade of C or better.

If your math course was listed generically, such as "Math 1" we have a process to verify the subject matter. If you do not meet the math requirement, there are resources to assist you.

**The Process**:

After the application deadline, applicants who submit complete applications will be contacted by US Mail to take an aptitude test which measures reading and math (algebra) skills.

Everyone who scores at least a 4 out of 9 on the test will be invited by US Mail to an interview with the Apprenticeship Committee.

Based on the responses to interview questions applicants will receive a score which will place them on a ranked list of applicants. Scores are not given out to applicants.

As contractors need apprentices, they will be pulled from the ranked list and offered an apprenticeship.

The application process takes a couple of months.

**Compensation:**

**Inside Apprentices** begin at 45% of JW wage (equals $23.27/hour, plus benefits). They advance through six pay periods (from 45% through 80% of JW scale)

**Limited Energy (VDV) Apprentices** begin at 55% of Limited Energy Technician wage (equals $20.08/hour, plus benefits) They advance through six pay periods (from 55% through 85% of Technician scale)
