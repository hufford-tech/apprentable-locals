## Active Member Information

This section provides quick access to the benefits and systems you use while on the job.

### Health & Welfare (NEBA)

For **Eligibility or Reported Hours Questions**, you can call NEBA and ask for the Bookkeeping Department, or email [nebajaxelg@secure.neba-fl.com](mailto:nebajaxelg@secure.neba-fl.com).

For **Enrollment or Beneficiary Questions,** you call NEBA and ask for Laura or Jeni, or email [nebajaxenroll@secure.neba-fl.com](mailto:nebajaxenroll@secure.neba-fl.com).

For **Claims and Benefits Questions**, you can call NEBA and ask for the Claims Department, or email [nebajaxclaims@secure.neba-fl.com](mailto:nebajaxclaims@secure.neba-fl.com).

For **Pension or Vanguard Questions,** you can call NEBA and ask for Jane, or email [nebajaxpen@secure.neba-fl.com](mailto:nebajaxpen@secure.neba-fl.com).

_Fortunately, we have a great administrative staff that handles all these important questions and life changing events. Please feel free to contact us at any time with questions or concerns you may have._

### ERTS (Electronic Reciprocal Transfer System)

If you are traveling please make sure that you check with the Local 508 Hall to make sure that you are signed up for ERTS ask for Denika. By signing up for this program, it ensures that your pension and health and welfare money are transferred back to your designated funds no matter where you are.

\*\*\*This is the responsibility of the traveling member to make sure you are enrolled in ERTS and let the receiving local know that you want your money to be reciprocated.

Phone: (912)964-5080

## Retired Member Information

Resources for those who have completed their service and are transitioning to or enjoying retirement.

Please make sure that when you are retiring you check with the hall to make sure all your information is current. We need this information so we can still reach you for our special occasions and the goodies you have earned. Please check the calendar for dates on our retiree breakfast.

##### IBEW PBF

This plan comes from paying your dues to Local 508 continuously, up until your retirement paperwork has been approved by International. (Plan amount times your years of service)

Phone: (202)833-7000

Fax: (202)728-6138

900 Seventh Street NW

Washington DC, 20001

##### National Electrical Benefit Fund

This plan comes from working for one of our signatory contractors that pay NEBF 3% of gross payroll on you per the CBA. (Plan amount times your years of service)
