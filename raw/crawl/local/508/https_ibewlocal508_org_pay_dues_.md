## PAY DUES

#### **ATTENTION**

Please make sure that when paying your dues online once you have saved changes to your demographics screen then click pay dues again. If you have not already signed up for the DEATH BENEFIT FUND PLEASE CALL THE HALL FIRSTbefore paying online to avoid any interruption in the process otherwise set death benefit to zero and just pay your dues.

Also for those of you that are currently paying the death benefit fund there will be no green receipt anymore it will be printed on the dues receipt as well. Please make sure that you are paying Dues and Death Benefit Fund at the same time so you do not have to pay twice to be current in both.

As always if you have any questions please call the hall and we will assist you!

**Pay your dues at [ibew508.workingsystems.com](https://ibew508.workingsystems.com/) by logging in with your info.**

#### Login Instructions

Members can log in with the following credentials:

- **Username:** IBEW Card Number
- **Password:** Last Name + Last 4 SSN (ex. SMITH1234)

Anyone logging in for the first time will be prompted to change their password.
