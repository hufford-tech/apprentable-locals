## Our Mission

To assure that every worker enjoys the full benefit of IBEW representation and membership for a better quality of life.

To achieve the power to set the terms and conditions of employment of every worker for a better standard of living.

To elevate the economic, political and social standing of every worker in our community and society.

To enable the IBEW to play a positive and stabilizing role for the benefit of the industry participants, customers and community at large.

To assume a leading role in the revitalizing of the Labor Movement.

## Objectives of the   International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers

To promote reasonable methods of work

To cultivate feelings of friendship among those in our industry

To settle all disputes between employers and employees by arbitration (if possible)

To assist each other in sickness or distress

To organize all workers in the entire electrical industry in the United States and Canada, including all those in public utilities and electrical manufacturing, into local unions

To secure employment

To reduce the hours of daily labor

To seek higher and higher standard of living

To seek security for the individual

And, by legal and proper means, to elevate the moral, intellectual, and social conditions of our members, their families, and dependents, in the interest of a higher standard of citizenship

## Organizing

###### _Harnessing the Power of Organization in the Electrical Labor Industry_

The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) stands out as a beacon of solidarity in the world of labor, especially within the electrical industry. With a strong ethos centered on organizing, the IBEW is not just about ensuring that the lights stay on; it’s about lighting up the lives of thousands of workers with the benefits of unity.

At the heart of the IBEW’s mission is the drive to organize. But what does organizing really mean in the context of electrical labor? It’s about threading the cables of common interests among electricians and related professionals to create a network that supports, protects, and energizes its members.

Contact our Local 508 Organizer to help organize yourself or your entire crew into the IBEW 508 Union.

**EMAIL**

[organizer@ibewlocal508.org](mailto:organizer@ibewlocal508.org)

Read More

Why organize, you might ask? In the volts and jolts of the electrical industry, workers face unique challenges – safety being electrifyingly important, as is fair compensation for the high-skill, high-risk job at hand. The IBEW operates on the simple principle that there is strength in numbers, and when electrical workers band together, their collective voice can demand the industry grounding they deserve – pun intended!

Organizing with the IBEW means advocating for fair wages, top-notch safety standards, continuing education, and the all-important work-life balance. It’s about ensuring that when one of their members is climbing up a pole or crawling through a tight space to wire up a building, they do so with the assurance that their rights and welfare are being championed.

Moreover, the IBEW understands that the electrical industry is not immune to the current of change. Technological advancements and green energy solutions are sparking up new avenues and approaches within the trade. By organizing, the IBEW ensures that their members are not left behind as the industry evolves but are instead equipped with the training and opportunities they need to shine bright in the ever-changing electrical landscape.

Organizing isn’t a one-and-done affair, either. It’s an ongoing process of building and sustaining relationships, not just between workers but with employers as well. It’s about creating an environment where everyone involved can reach a consensus on the best practices, ensuring the prosperity and safety of all parties involved.

The IBEW’s commitment to organizing within the electrical sector is a testament to the enduring power of collective action. When electricians and other laborers come together under the IBEW’s banner, they aren’t just joining a union – they’re becoming part of a family that stands shoulder to shoulder, making sure that while they might work with wires, they’re never alone in the fight for a fair deal.

## Apprenticeship Opportunities

###### _Savannah JATC Apprenticeship_

Train for a rewarding career in the electrical industry through a program backed by the Electrical Training Alliance—a partnership between the National Electrical Contractors Association and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers. At the Electrical Training Alliance of Savannah (SETA), apprentices gain hands-on experience, expert instruction, and ongoing support to build a successful future in the trade.

Contact us at (912)964-1989 or click the link below to learn more about our apprenticeships.
