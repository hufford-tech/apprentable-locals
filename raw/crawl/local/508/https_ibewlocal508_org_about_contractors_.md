## Contractors

Excellence in electrical work starts with the right partnership. IBEW Local 508 works hand-in-hand with the region’s leading electrical contractors to power our homes, businesses, and infrastructure. These organizations invest in the best trained talent in Southeast Georgia and South Carolina to deliver reliable results while supporting our local economy and workforce.
