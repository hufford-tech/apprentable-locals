## National Electrical Contractors Association, Inc. And International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Agreement on Employee Portability

This revised agreement, between the International Brotherhood of Electrical Worker ("IBEW") and 
the National Electrical Contractors Association ("NECA"), shall become effective May 15, 2024. This 
agreement shall apply throughout the United States, and, except as provided in paragraph 5, it 
shall supersede any inconsistent provisions of agreements between Local Unions of the IBEW 
and Chapters of NECA

The IBEW and NECA agree as follows:

1)  The purpose of this agreement is to allow a traveling contractor to bring into another 
jurisdiction a limited number of bargaining unit employees already on the payroll who are 
knowledgeable of the contractor's work practices and customers' requirements for startup 
and completion of work to be performed. Any bargaining unit employee being assigned into 
the jurisdiction of another Local Union under this agreement must have been employed by 
the Inside or Outside traveling contractor for a period of not less than two (2) weeks 
immediately prior to traveling to the job where the work is to be performed unless a lesser 
period is agreeable with the receiving Local Union.

2)  A contractor who is a member of NECA and who is bound by a collective bargaining 
agreement between one IBEW Local Union and a NECA Chapter may bring up to four (4) 
bargaining unit employees employed in that Local Union's jurisdiction ("bargaining unit 
employees") into the jurisdiction of another IBEW Local Union, provided that the contractor 
is bound by a collective bargaining agreement with that other Local Union covering the 
work to be performed. No more than four (4) bargaining unit employees may be employed 
at any one time under this paragraph in the jurisdiction of that other Local Union.

3)  A contractor who is a member of NECA and who is bound by a collective bargaining 
agreement between one IBEW Local Union and a NECA Chapter may bring up to two (2) 
bargaining unit employees per job from that Local Union's jurisdiction into the jurisdiction 
of another IBEW Local Union to perform specialty work or service and maintenance work. 
A contractor may also bring up to two (2) bargaining unit employees per job for electric 
vehicle charging station work for level one and level two projects and may bring up to four 
(4) bargaining unit employees per job for level three projects, provided that the contractor 
is bound by a collective bargaining agreement with that other Local Union covering the 
work to be performed.

---

4)  The portability provisions of paragraphs 2 and 3 shall also apply where a contractor with a 
shop in the jurisdiction of one IBEW Local Union opens a shop in the jurisdiction of another 
IBEW Local Union, but only for the first 180 consecutive calendar days that the new shop 
is open. Such a contractor may, consistent with the limits in paragraphs 2 and 3, bring 
employees from the jurisdiction in which it has maintained a shop into the jurisdiction in 
which it has opened or acquired a new shop for the first 180 consecutive calendar days 
that the new shop is open or acquired.

5)  Notwithstanding the provisions of paragraphs 2 through 4 of this agreement, a NECA 
Chapter and IBEW Local Union may agree that a contractor may bring more bargaining 
unit employees than permitted by those paragraphs into the Local Union's jurisdiction, 
provided that the contractor meets all of qualifications described in paragraphs 2 through 
4 of this agreement.

6)  A contractor bringing bargaining unit employees into a Local Unions jurisdiction pursuant 
to paragraphs 2 through 4 of this agreement will provide that Local Union and NECA 
Chapter, either before such employees begin working or on the first weekday on which 
such employees work, with the names and addresses of the employees and the location 
and identity of the job on which they will be or are working.

7)  A contractor bringing employees into a Local Union's jurisdiction pursuant to paragraphs 2 
through 4 shall comply with any terms of the collective bargaining agreement that concern 
terms and conditions for traveling employees in the agreement covering the jurisdiction 
from which the employees are brought. In all other respects, the contractor will comply with 
all of the terms of the collective bargaining agreement applicable to the work performed.

8)  In times of unemployment in the jurisdiction of a Local Union where the work is to be 
performed, the traveling contractor shall be allowed to bring in the first two (2) bargaining 
unit employees. The next two (2) bargaining unit employees shall come from the Local 
Union where the work is to be performed. The next bargaining unit employee will be from 
the traveling Local Union, followed by the next bargaining unit employee from the Local 
Union where the work is performed. This system may continue until the traveling contractor 
has a total of no more than four (4) bargaining unit employees in the Local Union 
jurisdiction.

9)  Times of unemployment shall be defined as periods where unemployment exceeds 10% 
of the bargaining unit employees for a period of three (3) weeks in the Local Union in whose 
area the work is being performed. Those persons who are on Book 1 and are not available 
for employment within 48 hours of a request for bargaining unit employees shall not be 
considered  as  unemployed. Any questions  or  interpretations of  what  constitutes 
unemployment shall be referred to the IBEW International Vice President and the NECA 
Regional Director.

10) This agreement is intended to apply only to contractors who are members of NECA, and 
nothing herein is intended to limit or otherwise affect the right of the IBEW or its affiliated 
Local Unions to bargain with any other person, firm, corporation, or entity with regard to 
subjects similar or identical to those herein.

---

11) This agreement will not apply to any work performed under the Joint National Agreement 
for Instrument Technicians, the Outside Utility Construction National Project Agreement, 
the Voice-Data-Video National Agreement, or any International Specialty Agreement.

12) This agreement will remain in effect from year to year. Either party may terminate this 
agreement by providing the other with written notice at least 180 days prior to the next 
anniversary date of this agreement.

Signed this 15th day of May, 2024.

1. Does the agreement mean only NECA contractors are allowed to bring in four (4) 
employees?

**For legal reasons, the specific agreement is between the IBEW and NECA. Item 10 of** 
**the agreement covers non-NECA union contractors that are bound by a collective** 
**bargaining agreement covering the work performed.**

2. Do the employees have to be Journeymen, Foremen, or General Foreman, or can the 
employer bring in apprentices?

**The agreement speaks for itself. The contractor may bring up to a maximum of four** 
**(4) bargaining unit employees for a job. This includes bargaining unit employees** 
**acting as Foreman and/or General Foremen. If the contractor had four (4) jobs in the** 
**jurisdiction of the receiving local union that were not specialty work, service and** 
**maintenance work, or electric vehicle charging station work, the contractor could** 
**have only one (1) bargaining unit employee from the sending local on each job.** 
**Apprentices are placed on jobs in their jurisdiction by their own JATC. They are not** 
**to be moved under this agreement.**

3. Can the incoming employees be shifted from job to job?

**Yes.**

4. Do the employees have to be employed prior to being sent into another jurisdiction and for 
how long?

**The incoming bargaining unit employees must be on the payroll of the inside or** 
**outside traveling contractor for a period of at least two (2) weeks immediately before** 
**moving into another jurisdiction unless a lesser period is agreeable with the** 
**receiving local union.**

5. Are the fringe benefits, working dues, etc. paid to the local union where the work is?

**The contractor shall comply with any terms of the collective bargaining agreement** 
**that concern terms and conditions for traveling employees in the agreement covering** 
**the jurisdiction from which the employees are brought. In all other respects, the** 
**contractor will comply with all of the terms of the collective bargaining agreement in** 
**the jurisdiction where the work is performed. If pension and health and welfare** 
**contributions are governed by the collective bargaining agreement where the work** 
**is performed, the reciprocity of pension and health and welfare contributions back** 
**to the local from which the employees traveled is governed by the Electrical Industry** 
**Pension Reciprocal Agreement and the Electrical Industry Health and Welfare** 
**Reciprocal Agreement.**

---

6. How does the local union where the work is get the necessary papers signed for reciprocity 
of health and welfare, pension, working dues, etc.?

**It is the responsibility of the incoming bargaining unit employee to complete the** 
**necessary forms required for the reciprocity of fringes, etc. However, it is not** 
**necessary for the incoming member to physically report to the local unions where** 
**the work is to be performed, since the necessary information has been provided by** 
**the employer.**

7. How does the local union where work is know that proper wages and fringes are paid to 
the employees?

**The local union would have knowledge of wage and fringes paid the same as they** 
**do for any contractor that is signatory to their collective bargaining agreement.**

8. Is the contractor required to hire any employees through the local union where the work 
is?

**The answer to this question is either "yes" or "no" depending on the total number** 
**of employees needed to perform the work.**

9. If a contractor violates the agreement, can they be restricted from using the agreement?

**Such a violation would be handled under the appropriate sections of the collective** 
**bargaining agreement dealing with disputes. A contractor found in violation or multi-** 
**violations of the agreement by the grievance procedure may be subject to** 
**appropriate remedies.**

10. Can a contractor based in the jurisdiction of Local A with a shop/office move employees 
under the Portability Agreement to a job in Local B where they also have a shop/office? 
The contractor is, of course, signatory to a collective bargaining agreement in both Local 
A and Local B.

**Yes, under paragraph 4 of the Portability Agreement, in the example above, the** 
**contractor can bring employees from Local A's jurisdiction to work in Local B's** 
**jurisdiction, consistent with the limits in paragraphs 2 and 3, but only for the first** 
**180 consecutive days that the shop/office in Local B's jurisdiction is open.**

---

11. In item 3 they refer to specialty work. What is specialty work? Are the two (2) employees 
part of the four (4) employees in item 2?

**Item 3 in the agreement covers specialty work, service and maintenance work, and** 
**EV Charging Station work where a contractor is party to your local collective** 
**bargaining agreement. The intent of the paragraph is to cover and maintain** 
**portability practice that has been in existence in most local unions for many years.** 
**In this example, the up to two (2) or four (4) employees on this type of work are not** 
**part of the four (4) employees in item 2.**

## 12. How does the provision concerning unemployment affect existing work?

**If an unemployment situation should come into existence after a traveling contractor** 
**comes into a jurisdiction, the terms of the full portability agreement continue to** 
**apply to the specific job in question.**
