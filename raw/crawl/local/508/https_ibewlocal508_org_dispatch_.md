## Book Procedures

Beginning January 2020

##### BOOK 1

###### IBEW Union 508 Members Only

Book signs are accepted from Local 508 members by one of the following methods below. If you have previously been working, please include your **termination papers** with the date of termination before we can add you to the Books. Thank You!

**Local 508 members may sign by one of these four (4) methods:**

MAIL -1526 Dean Forest Road Savannah, Georgia 31408

EMAIL – [dispatcher@ibewlocal508.org](mailto:dispatcher@ibewlocal508.org "mailto:dispatcher@ibewlocal508.org")

FAX – 912-964-2171

IN PERSON

_\*\*\*Please make sure you follow up with the dispatcher at 912-964-5080 after submission of your termination to make sure you are added on the Books._

##### BOOK 2

###### Traveling IBEW Union Members

Original/Re-Signs must be received every month during the week of the 10th-16th.

**All Book 2 signs must be submitted from your hall on local union letterhead and include the following information:**

Full Name

Address

Card number

Current Dues Receipt

Birth Date

Current contact phone number

**Traveling IBEW Union members may Original/Re-Sign by one of these four (4) methods:**

MAIL -1526 Dean Forest Road Savannah, Georgia 31408

EMAIL – [dispatcher@ibewlocal508.org](mailto:dispatcher@ibewlocal508.org "mailto:dispatcher@ibewlocal508.org")

FAX – 912-964-2171

IN PERSON
