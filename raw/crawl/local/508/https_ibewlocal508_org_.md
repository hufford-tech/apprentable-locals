## IBEW Local 508 Now in Your Pocket!

Stay connected with the new **Union Strong** mobile app, designed specifically for IBEW 508 members. Get instant notifications on job calls, check your portability status, and manage your dues on the go!

Available on iPhone and Android devices. Click the button below or scan the QR code to download now.
