# Members

IBEW Local 145 was chartered on April 27, 1925, and boasts a current membership of over 1,110. Our members are divided into several categories of electrical work, including: Teledata / Communications, Inside Construction, Residential Wiring, Outside Construction, Utility, Sign, and Stanley Magic Door. Local 145 negotiates and services approximately ten different contracts.

### Member Divisions

##### Teledata / Communications

A variety of services are covered including voice, data, video, sound, security, computer cabling, nurse call, fiber optics, Ethernet, coaxial cable, telephone, and several other low voltage applications.

##### Inside Construction

Inside construction includes commercial, industrial, high voltage, motor controls, instrumentation, lighting, power distribution, fire alarm, parking lot lighting, traffic signals, etc.

##### Residential Wiring

Members meet the wiring needs of private residences including apartments, condominiums, etc.

##### Outside Construction

High voltage power distribution, transmission lines, street lighting, substation construction, etc., are a few of the projects completed by outside construction members.

##### Utility

Companies such as Central Iowa Light & Power, Eastern Iowa Light & Power, and the Maquoketa Municipal Utility are just a few companies employing utility members.

##### Sign

Sign members assemble and install an assortment of signs including vinyl, electric, and neon signs.

##### Training

Local 145 offers extensive and thorough apprenticeship training programs in the following fields:

- Inside Journeyman Wireman
- Residential Journeyman Wireman
- Teledata Journeyman Technician

We also offer dozens of advanced training courses for our Journeyman members. For more information, [click here](https://www.ibewlocal145.com/continuing-education-classes/).
