# About IBEW Local 145

### Jurisdiction

The IBEW Local 145 jurisdiction in Iowa includes Cedar, Clinton, Jackson, Muscatine, and Scott counties and in Illinois includes all of Rock Island and parts of Carroll, Henry, Mercer, and Whiteside counties.

If you’re planning on building within our jurisdiction, [click here](https://www.ibewlocal145.com/neca-members-contractors/) for a list of our NECA and signatory contractors.
