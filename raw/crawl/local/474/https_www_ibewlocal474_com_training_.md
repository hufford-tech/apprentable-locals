**Temporary Arkansas JW License**

- Please go to [https://apps.lnpweb.com/authenticate/rZzOeu0iSPiIPAWjNJFLw](https://apps.lnpweb.com/authenticate/rZzOeu0iSPiIPAWjNJFLw) and create an account and complete the application there, being sure to attach all documentation.
- **Remember your Login information or you will have to contact the licensing office to recover your account!**
- **Click on State Portal to pull up a list of available applications and click on the Electrical Application Temporary Journeyman**
- Answer all questions in Step 2. If you have/had a license you must fill in all information about that license (license number, license date, testing date, score, testing facility, etc.)
- If you completed a DOL Apprenticeship, you must fill out the information pertaining to that. You will upload proof in Step 3 Document Upload.
- Submit experience (and education if apprenticeship was completed) in Step 3 Document Upload. A notarized letter from Glenn **(or your home local if you are a traveler)** should suffice
- Review your Information Summary in Step 4
- Click Save and Exit to save your information

**Regular Arkansas Journeyman License**

- **If you have an account, log in and click on State Portal. If not, create an account and click on State Portal.**
- When you are on the State Portal page, Click on Electricians Exam Clearance in the list of available applications
