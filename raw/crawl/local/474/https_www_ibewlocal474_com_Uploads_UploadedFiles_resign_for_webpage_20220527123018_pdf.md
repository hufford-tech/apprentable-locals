## All Journeyman Wireman and Linemen on Local 474 Books

## Out of Work List Re-Signing

## Re-Signing of the out of work list is required every month th th

**between the 10 and the 16 of the month.** If you are on the Out of Work list **YOU MUST RE-SIGN** to maintain your position on the Out of Work List. If you do not re-sign in one of the following ways each month you will be removed from the Book: The ways that can be used are; In Person at the Union Hall, by email to <u>resigns@ibewlocal474.com</u>, Faxed to 901-726-4077, or Mailed to the Union Hall. For a Re-sign to be considered for th the month it must be received between the 10th and the 16 of the month. When re-signing you must provide your Name and Card Number or Name and Last 4 numbers of your Social Security Number.
