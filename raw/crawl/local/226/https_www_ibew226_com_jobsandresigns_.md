**BOOK SIGN**\- Out of Work List

You can now sign the book in person or by email at booksign226@ibew226.com.

Local 226 members can send an email containing their name, card number, and request to be placed on the book.

All others wishing to sign must have their home local send proof of book status via email to booksign226@ibew226.com **AND** setup an account on 226 Union Worx.

An example of book status would be a labor power report and intro letter sent from their home local showing their status.

RE-SIGN \- 10th through 16th AFTER you are on the books

Remember, after your initial Book Sign, to RE-SIGN the Out-of-Work Book between the 10th-16th (Every Month)!

RE-SIGN EMAIL ADDRESS: resign@ibew226.com or on the UNION WORX app.
