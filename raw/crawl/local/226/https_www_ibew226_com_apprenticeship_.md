**APPRENTICESHIP**

**NATIONAL JOINT APPRENTICESHIP & TRAINING COMMITTEE (JATC)**

**Do you want to be an ELECTRICIAN?**

We have all heard the expression **_"To get a job, you need experience. The only way to get experience is to have a job."_**

The Inside Wireman Apprenticeship offered at the Electrical Joint Apprenticeship & Training Committee, will give you a good paying job with great benefits while you are getting free technical training at the same time.

The finest electrical workers receive their training through the National Joint Apprenticeship & Training Committee of NECA and the IBEW. It is a model education partnership that produces the best trained and knowledgeable electrical apprentices and journeymen in the country.

![](https://www.ibew226.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/688a3537097f95.03112741-AdobeStock_1267568179.jpeg)

WHAT ARE THE BENFITS OF ELECTRICAL APPRENTICESHIP AND TRAINING?

![](https://www.ibew226.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/688a64f8541271.04104333-Safety.jpg)

### SAFER WORKING CONDITIONS

Work with experts, with years of experience.

![](https://www.ibew226.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/688a68edd4d825.79964143-AdvancementOpportunities.jpg)

### ADVANCEMENT OPPORTUNITIES

As Apprentices advance through the 6 period levels of the program, the wage rate (based on percentage of the Journeyman wireman rate) increases with each period level.

![](https://www.ibew226.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/688a6c21b31f48.58418762-PensionBenefits.jpg)

### GUARANTEED PENSION BENEFITS

Benefits will start the first day of on-the-job training. These benefits are funded through the employer.

![](https://www.ibew226.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/689cb667dce218.70460113-earnwhileyoulearn.jpg)

### EARN WHILE YOU LEARN & COLLEGE CREDIT

Every other week they attend a day of school, while the other days they are working with there hands on the job. Starting pay, (no experience necessary) starts at $18.10 per hour plus benefits.

![](https://www.ibew226.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/688a6ef4f30ff4.56539671-paidhealthinsurance.jpg)

### PAID HEALTH INSURANCE

Medical Insurance is provided by the industry. This means you won't be seeing a deduction from your paycheck to pay for your insurance premiums. In addition to the medical coverage, there is prescription drug, dental and optical coverage as well.

![](https://www.ibew226.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/689cbe60120964.34088387-Careerfuture.jpg)

### CAREER WITH A FUTURE

Over eighty-five percent of our apprentices complete the apprenticeship program and have meaningful careers as highly paid as well as skilled journeymen electrical workers.

![](https://www.ibew226.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/689f4f4e8756c2.06011095-PRIDEANDDIGNITY.jpg)

### PRIDE & DIGNITY

A career in the electrical construction industry will not only build your character but will also give you pride in your work while offering you a bright future in the industry.
