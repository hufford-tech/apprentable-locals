NOW HIRING EXPERIENCED ELECTRICIANS

Union Benefits, Strong Future

We need skilled electricians, and opportunity is everywhere.  Ongoing and upcoming projects mean it’s the perfect time to join us.  If you’re ready for meaningful work and a career you can be proud of, we’ve got your back.

- **Great Pay & Benefits** Strong wages (journeyman wireman starting at $74.41/hr), excellent healthcare, and solid retirement plans – so you can focus on your future.

- **Continuing Education** Keep your skills sharp and your certifications current with training tailored to today’s jobsite demands.

- **Reliable Work** Our referral system keeps you connected to steady, well-paying jobs year-round.
