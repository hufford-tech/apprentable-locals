# Raising the Standard: the Flag Raises Over a New Chapter

- June 27, 2026
- [Rachel Johnson](https://ibewlocal743.org/author/racheljohnson/)
- [News](https://ibewlocal743.org/category/news/ "News")

This week after the successful move of the union administration offices of IBEW Local 743, the leaders of the IBEW 743 Veteran’s Committee joined together to raise a brand-new United States flag over our new headquarters at 1022 James Dr, Leesport, PA 19608.

![](https://ibewlocal743.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/20260625_172112-1024x768.jpg)

These veterans know first-hand the importance of this symbolic ceremony of our patriotism and continual belief in the freedom and equality of all in our country, our state, and our local community.

![](https://ibewlocal743.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/20260625_172135-1024x768.jpg)

May this flag fly high and proud as our local welcomes a new chapter in its over 100-year legacy.

![](https://ibewlocal743.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/20260625_172719-1024x768.jpg)
