# Continuing Education

![IBEW Local 743 Apprenticeship Training](https://ibewlocal743.org/wp-content/uploads/2020/01/IMG_1218-scaled.jpg)

To stay at the forefront of the electrical industry, the IBEW Local 743 training center offers education courses beyond those required during the five-year apprenticeship program.

Members can return to the training center for courses in fire-alarm training, equipment operation and other topics as the industry evolves. All of these courses make the IBEW 743 workforce more marketable and prepared to take on various jobs.

IBEW 743 members are also welcome to take advantage of safety courses such as the OSHA-30 hour certification. This investment in the IBEW Local 743 workforce is all to ensure that they are equipped to work proficiently and safely, limiting time off due to injuries and increased costs for workers compensation.

## [If you would like to learn more about continuing your education with IBEW Local 743, click here to visit our training website.](https://743electricaltraining.org/journeymen-upgrade/)
