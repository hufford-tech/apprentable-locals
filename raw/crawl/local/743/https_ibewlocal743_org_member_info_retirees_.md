# Retirees

## **IBEW Local 743 Retirees’ Club**

_Chartered January 13, 2004_

**To All Retirees:**

Starting in August 2026, changes have been made to where monthly meetings will be held. The meetings will still remain on the 1st Wednesday of each month, and yearly membership dues to the Retirees’ Club are still $5. All monthly meetings will be at the new union hall at 1022 James Drive, Leesport, PA 19533 starting at 10 a.m.

To keep our organization strong and active, we need new members.

Come join us!

**50/50 DRAWING WINNER**

_CONGRATULATIONS TO STEPHEN KRUPIAK, WINNER OF THE 50/50 DRAWING AT THE JULY 2026 GENERAL MEETING TO BENEFIT THE RETIREES’ CLUB. STEPHEN DONATED HIS PORTION OF THE WINNINGS BACK TO THE RETIREES’ CLUB. THANK YOU, STEPHEN._
