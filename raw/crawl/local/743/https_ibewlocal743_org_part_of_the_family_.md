# PART OF THE FAMILY

- April 27, 2026
- [Rachel Johnson](https://ibewlocal743.org/author/racheljohnson/)
- [News](https://ibewlocal743.org/category/news/ "News")

This is a special time in the history of IBEW Local 743 with electricians in our jurisdiction as well as throughout the country in high demand. To meet the surge of work with data centers, hospitals, renovations, and so many other types of projects during this time of historic job growth, IBEW Local 743 has been exhausting all efforts to reach out to all electricians in our area.

As these electricians hear of the benefits and community offered to them by our local, the decision is clear that being a union electrician is indeed the right choice!

Our local has been growing quickly to meet the needs of our contractors, their clients, and our new members.  This past General Meeting held on April 22, 2026 a record number of new members became a part of the family of IBEW Local 743 as inside wiremen.

![](https://ibewlocal743.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/04/IMG_20260424_101624-1024x768.jpg)

We welcome these new faces as we continue to grow and build upon the greatness that has been in place since May 1918. Come join us so that we can say to you as well, “Welcome to IBEW Local 743!”
