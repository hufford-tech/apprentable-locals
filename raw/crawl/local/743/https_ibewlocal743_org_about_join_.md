# Looking for a career in the electrical industry?

Are you a recent high school or college graduate? Did you just transition out of the armed service? Are you stuck in a career and looking for a change?

A career with IBEW Local 743 may be the answer to all of your questions. People who are trained in the skilled trades are in high demand as older generations are retiring and younger generations are increasingly opting to go to college. Fill this demand by joining IBEW 743.

Whether you have no prior experience in the electrical industry, or you are currently working for an unorganized shop, IBEW Local 743 wants you.

Once accepted, IBEW members earn while they learn, receiving training at little to no cost to the apprentice. The education does not stop after completing the five-year apprenticeship program. IBEW 743 journeymen can return to the training center to further their education through upgrade training programs and safety certifications.

For over 100 years, the electricians of IBEW Local 743 have led the way in eastern Pennsylvania in quality construction and training. Apply for our Registered Apprenticeship Program and get your rewarding career started today.

## Want to learn more about our apprenticeship program?

[Visit our JATC website for more information.](https://743electricaltraining.org/)
