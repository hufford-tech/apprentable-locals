## Cedar Rapids Electrical Training Center

Our Electrical training center is located at 2300 Johnson Ave NW, in Cedar Rapids Iowa.  We train and provide job assignments for apprentices.  We also provide Journeyman education.  For more information you can call 319-654-9238 or visit our training center website by clicking the link below.
