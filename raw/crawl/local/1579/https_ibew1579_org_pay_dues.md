# Pay Dues Online

[PAY MY DUES](https://members.ibew1579.workingsystems.com/)

IBEW Local 1579 now has a secure online payment process to accept your dues with American Express, MasterCard, Visa or Discover Card. All credit card payments are processed through a secured gateway provided by Authorize.Net and Local 1579 is unable to see your credit card number. The IBEW does not charge you a fee to pay online.

**How to Pay Online.**

**• Click the PAY MY DUES button above.** **•** On your first login, you will need to generate a Username and Password. Follow the TIPS provided on the login page to begin.

**•** When making your online payment, please make sure you have your **MEMBER NUMBER** available _(located on the right-hand side of your dues receipt)._ **•** If you are 6 months or more past due, **do not pay online**. You will need to call the hall at 706-722-6357 and speak to the Business Manager. The Local Union will process the dues payment manually and your receipt will be mailed to you at the address you have on record.

**If you have any questions about payment processing, please email [admin@ibew1579.org](mailto:admin@ibew1579.org) or call 706-722-6357**
