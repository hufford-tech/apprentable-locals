# Teledata Job Calls

## **Taking A Job Call**

#### If you would like to take a call, please stop by the Union Hall in person to sign the day sheet from 8:00 am- 9:00 am.  Referrals will start at 9:00 am.  For any questions regarding calls or TeleData, contact Bruce Ponce at (713)-869-8900 ext. 244.

#### Thank you!

## **\*\*EFFECTIVE IMMEDIATELY\*\***

##### **OSHA 10-Hour Certification Requirement**

##### All contractors require that all new hires complete an OSHA 10-Hour (or higher) course prior to taking any calls.

##### You can register for the course at the following link: [_OSHA 10-Hour Construction Card Shipped in 2 Days, Only $59_](https://www.careersafeonline.com/courses/osha-10-hour/construction) or  register any other accredited website for an OSHA 10-Hour Construction class.
