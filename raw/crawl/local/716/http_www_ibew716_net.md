# IBEW 716

The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) is the oldest and largest union dedicated to electrical workers in the U.S. IBEW Local Union 716 represents any and all electricians in the Houston Gulf Coast Area.

![Journeyman Electrician Services](https://i0.wp.com/ibew716.net/wp-content/uploads/services-journyman.webp?fit=660%2C441&quality=62&ssl=1)

Journeyman

Not all electricians are the same. IBEW Journeymen are the industry’s gold standard. Learn what it means to be an IBEW-certified Journeyman.

[Learn More](https://ibew716.net/journeyman/) [Job Calls](https://ibew716.net/journeyman-job-calls/)

![Teledata and Wiring Services](https://i0.wp.com/ibew716.net/wp-content/uploads/services-teledata-1.webp?fit=660%2C441&quality=62&ssl=1)

CW/CE

Start or advance your career by becoming an IBEW-certified construction wireman (CW) or construction electrician (CE) today.

[Learn More](https://ibew716.net/cw-ce/) [Job Calls](https://ibew716.net/cw-ce-job-calls/)

![Power Line Electrician Services](https://i0.wp.com/ibew716.net/wp-content/uploads/services-cwew.webp?fit=660%2C441&quality=62&ssl=1)

Teledata

Join, work, and train with IBEW 716 to become a certified Teledata technician servicing teledata communications systems everywhere.

## Contracting   with IBEW 716

For over a century strong, IBEW Local 716 has served the Houston community playing a major role in the construction of local landmarks such as the JPMorgan Chase building, the Astrodome, the Intercontinental Airport (IAH), and NASA’s Johnson Space Center.

Houston contractors know the value of working with Local 716. With our highly trained and skillful electricians, you’ll save time and while avoiding costly project delays and mishaps.

**Our Houston contractors benefit from IBEW 716’s:**

- [Manpower Capability](https://ibew716.net/)
- [Extensive Training](https://ibew716.net/)
- [Code of Excellence](https://ibew716.net/)
- [OSHA Compliance](https://ibew716.net/)
