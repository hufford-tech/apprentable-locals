# Veterans

# **Welcome to the IBEW Local 716 Veterans Page**.

## ![](https://i0.wp.com/ibew716.net/wp-content/uploads/ibew-veteran-committee.jpg?resize=293%2C300&quality=65&ssl=1)

## Vision Statement

**The Local Veterans Committee proudly serves all those who have served. We are committed to building a strong community and amplifying veteran voices within IBEW Local 716 and across the electrical industry.**

## Veteran Resources

**Veterans Crisis Line –**

**Dial 988 then press 1**

**Text 838255**

**Available 24/7 for confidential support. You do not need to be enrolled in VA benefits to connect.**

## Career & Training

**[Houston JATC](https://www.houstonjatc.com/)**

Training and education for electrical and telecommunications workers. Operated as a registered apprenticeship program with the U.S. Department of Labor.

**[Veterans Electrical Entry Program (VEEP)](https://in2veep.com/)**

Designed to help transitioning service members and recently separated veterans enter the electrical trade with strong career potential.

**[VIPER Transitions](https://www.vipertransitions.org/)**

Nonprofit supporting military families with employment readiness and internships.

**[Helmets to Hardhats](https://helmetstohardhats.org/)**

Connects veterans and reservists to skilled training and career opportunities in the construction industry.

_Serve your country. Secure your future. Build North America._

## State & Federal Services

**[U.S. Department of Veterans Affairs](https://www.va.gov/)**

Access healthcare, benefits, and services for veterans and families.

**[Texas Veterans Commission](https://www.tvc.texas.gov/)**

Advocating for and serving Texas veterans since 1927.

**[TexVet](https://texvet.org/)**

Trusted information and resources for veterans, families, and service providers in Texas.

**[Texas Workforce Commission – Just for Veterans](https://www.twc.texas.gov/jobseekers/just-veterans)**

Employment support and job search tools specifically for veterans.

**[USERRA – Employment Rights](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/vets/programs/userra)**

Resources for veterans and employers about reemployment rights and Veterans’ Preference.

## Advocacy

**[Union Veterans Council](https://unionveterans.org/)**

Uniting working-class veterans to advocate for good jobs, a strong VA, and policies that support veterans and their families.

_Engage. Educate. Mobilize._
