# CW/CE

## How to Become a CW/CE

##### If you have 2+ years of commercial/industrial electrical construction experience, please come by our office Monday thru Friday 8 a.m.-5 p.m. We will discuss what the IBEW has to offer and then set you up to do a hands-on evaluation to determine your rate of pay. Please come in jobsite attire.

##### If you cannot attend at those times, please call [713-869-8900](tel:713-869-8900) ext. 235 for accommodations.

##### Have more questions? Reach out to one our IBEW 716 Organizers so they can answer them for you.

**JOSE GONZALEZ (832) 931-9391**

**JORGE BALDERRAMA (832) 455-2284**

**MANNY HINOJOSA (832) 515-2256**
