# Solar Job Calls

##### ![](https://i0.wp.com/ibew716.net/wp-content/uploads/20210128_114716.jpg?resize=1380%2C653&quality=65&ssl=1)

## **Notice:**

### **Effective immediately,**

Business hours are Monday to Friday from 8:00 am to 5:00 pm.

Please contact our main phone line for assistance at 713-869-8900 and press \*3\* to reach an organizer.

If you are needing to sign the Out of Work Book, you may do so in person or please email us a copy of your dues receipt and your termination slip to [**resignbook@ibew716.net**](mailto:resignbook@ibew716.net)

We appreciate your patience, understanding and cooperation at this time. Stay safe Brothers & Sisters.

**Travelers –** If you need to sign the Out-of-Work Book, please bring a Travel Letter & Dues Receipt when you come into the hall or email your Travel Letter & Dues Receipt to **[resignbook@ibew716.net](mailto:resignbook@ibew716.net)**

Please Note: On your Travel Letter, please include D.O.B., Last Four of SSN, Phone Number, Email Address and wage rate (percentage) in order for us to add you to our system.

Fraternally,

Stephen Gonzales

_Business Manager/Financial Secretary_

### **SOLAR JOB CALLS**

##### **\*\*YOU MUST EMAIL\*\*:**

##### [solardaysheet@ibew716.net](mailto:solardaysheet@ibew716.net)

##### between the hours of 5:00 p.m. and 8:00 a.m.

##### **Include the following:**

- ##### _Full name_

- ##### _Card Number_

- ##### _Phone Number_

- ##### _Job Call desired (Location Wise)_


#### **\*\*Emails sent _before_ 5:00 pm and after 8:00 am WILL NOT be eligible to sign the Day Sheet\*\*  With an effort to make the daysheet more efficient, SUBMIT ONLY ONE EMAIL.**

#### To check if there are any left over calls, please call after 10:30 am,  There is not an in person daysheet for SOLAR Job Calls only via email at the designated time frame.

### **PARA TOMAR UNA  LLAMADA**

##### Envie un correo electrónico a [solardaysheet@ibew716.net](mailto:solardaysheet@ibew716.net), entre las horas de 5:00pm a 8:00am. **(SOLO UNO)**

##### Incluya los siguentes datos:

- ##### nombre completo

- ##### número de tarjeta amarilla

- ##### número de teléfeno

- ##### llamada de trabajo deseada.


#### **_\*\*Correos electr_ _ó_ _nicos ANTES de las 5:00 pm y DESPUES_ _de las 8:00am NO SERAN ACEPTADOS\*\* Con el fin de hacer el proceso más eficiente, SOLAMENTE MANDE 1 CORREO ELECTRÓNICO._**

#### Para verificar si quedan llamadas disponibles despues de los llamados de trabajo, llame después de las 10:30 a.m. No hay una hoja de del dia en persona para las llamadas de trabajo SOLAR solo por correo electrónico en los tiempos indicados.
