For Journeymen who are already IBEW 332 members, there are many opportunities to deepen your skills and further your trade knowledge.

​

[Go to the ETA-SV’s website](https://www.etasv.org/journeyman-courses) to learn about IBEW Journeyman Series Classes.

​

IBEW Local 332 welcomes motivated individuals to join our team of highly skilled electrical workers in Santa Clara County, California. We represent thousands of members, mainly in construction, but also electricians working for the City of San Jose, County of Santa Clara, and the San Jose Unified School District. We have several opportunities for you to join our team and continue on with a rewarding career in construction including: commercial, residential, and sound & communications.

​
