# Political Action

Unity=Power, and as a united union, we have political clout. Local 332's Political Action Committee (PAC) is made of members. We work to elect leaders and pass policies that advance the trades, pass and protect PLAs, and support all working families in our communities. We partner with our local and state building trades; our local and state labor councils; and our local, state, and federal IBEW organizations to recognize and understand political issues, endorse candidates and legislation, educate our members, and actively participate in the political process.

​

Local 332 actively participates in the non-partisan COPE (Committee On Political Education) process through the Building Trades of Santa Clara and San Benito Counties and the South Bay Labor Council.

​

The committee welcomes participation from all members of Local 332 who want to learn about and/or get involved with the political process. Join us!
