# Statements from 332’s New Officers

- Jul 23
- 6 min read

New 332 officers were sworn in at the July 7, 2026 membership meeting. They were voted in by 870 members who cast their ballots in the June 5th internal election. The following are statements from our new officers who will lead our union for the next three years.

## Newly Elected Business Manager Pete Seaberg

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/c75ec2_f2dcd58464e544ecadce523d016c7187~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_350,h_350,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/c75ec2_f2dcd58464e544ecadce523d016c7187~mv2.jpg)

**Greetings Brothers & Sisters,**

I want to extend my deepest gratitude to each of you. Being elected as your Business Manager is the honor of a lifetime. Thank you for placing your trust in me to lead our great local.

The future of Local 332 is a very serious matter, and I do not take this responsibility lightly. I am fully committed to working hard every day to protect our jurisdiction, improve our benefits and pensions, and strengthen our brother- and sisterhood.

Summer is finally here! I hope everyone is taking some well-deserved time to relax, recharge, and enjoy the warm weather with your families. Our work can be demanding, but our loved ones are the reason we do what we do. Please make time for those barbecues, camping trips, and family gatherings. Also make sure to attend 332’s summer events, including the annual family picnic on July 11 and the big softball tournament on July 25.

As we look at our current local economy, we are experiencing a period of tremendous growth. Work is booming across our jurisdiction. There are massive projects underway and a steady pipeline of upcoming jobs. Our skilled labor remains in high demand, proving that the quality of Local 332 hands is unmatched here in Silicon Valley.

The boom means robust opportunities and strong work hours for our members. To meet demands, we are taking in 140 new inside apprentices and employing over 1,200 traveling brothers and sisters from across the country. I encourage you to take hours while work is good. If the pandemic taught us anything, it’s to expect the unexpected.

Please look out for one another on the jobsites. Summer heat introduces extra safety hazards. Stay hydrated, take your breaks, and keep safety as your top priority. We want every single member to return home safely to your families at the end of every shift.

Finally, if you see something that needs attention, please call the hall and we’ll get a rep out ASAP. Thank you again for your support and your dedication to our local. Let’s keep moving Local 332 forward, together!

**Always 332,**

**Peter G. Seaberg** Business Manager

## Newly Elected President Peter Huber

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/c75ec2_1b5fc9cea53347598ea257e19dcc7fcc~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_350,h_350,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/c75ec2_1b5fc9cea53347598ea257e19dcc7fcc~mv2.jpg)

“I believe the strength of Local 332 is tied to our unity and how we support one another. As your president, I will continue to create opportunities for members to connect with each other and our families. That means regular social events and outings, volunteering in the community, and bolstering our clubs/committees. I encourage all members to get involved. It’s not only fun and increases our community impact, but it strenghtens our union.

“In my role, I will also continue to fight for exceptional contracts, benefits, and working conditions for all members, whether you are inside, resi, S&C, or a materials handler. I will ensure members’ funds and pension trusts are handled responsibly. And finally, I will bring 332 member voices to the IBEW International Convention to ensure we are represented.

“It is an honor to serve you, and I hope to see you at union meetings, social outings, and volunteer events! Get involved!”

## The Newly Elected Examining Board Tests Potential New Members

The Examining Board’s role is to test potential new members being organized into the union. The Board verifies basic skill sets, as well as people’s experience relative to their classification. This ensures new members are up to IBEW 332’s standards of excellence. The newly elected board includes: Daniel Grimaldo, Mark “Yogi” Martinez, Osbaldo Navarette, Andrew Olague, and Javier Preciado.

## The Newly Elected Convention Delegates Will Attend the IBEW International Convention
