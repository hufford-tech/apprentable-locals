If you’re a contractor debating the benefits of having a union shop, we want to talk to you!

​

By joining 332, you’ll gain an industry partner invested in your success. The more successful you are, the more our members work. You will have access to the largest pool of highly-trained journeymen, apprentices, and foremen in the Bay Area, allowing you to bid on more work and have the resources to hire quickly to perform larger projects.

​

Some of the additional benefits of being an IBEW contractor that we can discuss:

- More than 70% of electrical work in Silicon Valley is done by IBEW contractors. Talk with us about how your company can gain access to more general contractors and customers.

- Manpower is an extremely critical resource for a company’s success. Learn how you can quickly and easily increase or decrease your workforce with the best electricians in Silicon Valley to help grow your company.

- Your time is very important when you’re trying to run a business. Let us take care of your employees’ benefits, raises, etc….so you can focus on running your business.

- We work with more than 100 IBEW signatory contractors here in Silicon Valley. They range from small, family-run shops to some of the largest electrical contractors in the country. Our contractors build everything from airports, BART stations, stadiums, Google campuses, data centers, shopping malls, retail stores, schools to high-rise buildings, apartment complexes, and new housing developments. There is no electrical project in Silicon Valley too small or too large for our contractors and members.


To learn more about becoming a signatory IBEW contractor and the opportunities that await you, reach out to us. We’re ready to welcome you to the IBEW 332 family!
