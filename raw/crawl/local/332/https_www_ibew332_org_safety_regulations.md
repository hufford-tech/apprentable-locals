# Safety

Local 332 Safety Award

The IBEW Local 332 Safety Award is an annual recognition of an IBEW Local 332 member or members who demonstrates a conscious effort towards the safety of others on the job. It is the purpose of this award to recognize an individual, or group of individuals, for their efforts to promote, advocate, improve, educate, or show sustained commitment to the safety of others on the job. It may also be a recognition of an act of courage or perseverance for the safety of one or more people on the job or in the community.

​

OSHA Top 10 Most Frequently Cited Standards

IBEW Local 332 is proud of our record on safety. Our trained workforce prioritizes safety in everything we do, and our worksites have very few OSHA violations. The results are as follows. OSHA 29 CFR 1910 regulations detail general industry safety and apply to most worksites. Alternately, the OSHA 29 CFR 1926 standards focus on the construction industry.
