# Join Us

Whether you have few to no skills and want to be an apprentice, you’re an experienced journeyman looking to go union, or you’re a contractor who has decided to become a union shop, IBEW Local 332 has a place for you! We are always looking for motivated individuals to join our team of highly-skilled electrical workers powering Silicon Valley’s high-profile sites.

​

Learn more about getting started in a life-changing career:

​

- [For Apprentices](https://www.ibew332.org/apprenticeships)


- [For Journeymen](https://www.ibew332.org/for-electricians)


- [For Contractors](https://www.ibew332.org/for-contractors)


- [Union Facts](https://www.ibew332.org/union-facts)


By going union, you will be able to work on many of the largest, most complex and important projects in Silicon Valley. IBEW 332 electricians have built all of the following projects, plus many more!

​

- Google: Googleplex HQ, Bay View and Charleston campuses

- Stadiums: 49ers, Sharks, Earthquakes & Stanford

- Tech: Apple, Facebook, HP, Yahoo, Nvidia, Intel & more

- Datacenters: DuPont, Equinox, Vantage, Digital Realty & Raging Wire

- Public buildings and infrastructure: San Jose Airport, hospitals, universities, San Jose City Hall, water treatment plants, and more!
