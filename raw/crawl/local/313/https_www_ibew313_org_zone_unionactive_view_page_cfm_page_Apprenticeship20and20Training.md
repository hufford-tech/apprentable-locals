_**Members and Apprentices:**_



Please monitor “ibew313.org” under the “Apprenticeship and Training” tabs for training courses and additional JATC information and updates.



Classes will be scheduled and posted on the website under the  "Local 313 JATC Courses" tab.

**_Interested Applicants:_**
