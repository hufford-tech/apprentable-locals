### **Why Join IBEW Local 5?**

Joining **IBEW Local 5** (International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local Union 5) as a non-union electrician offers several significant potential advantages, generally centered around improving compensation, benefits, and career security.

Here is a list of key reasons a non-union electrician might want to join:

- **Better Wages and Compensation:** Union contracts often secure **higher hourly wages** for members compared to many non-union positions, which directly increases take-home pay.
- **Comprehensive Benefits Package:** Union membership typically includes **excellent full-family health insurance**, dental, and optical benefits, often starting on the first day of work.
- **Retirement Security:** IBEW members benefit from strong retirement plans, which usually include **pension contributions** and **deferred compensation (401k-type) contributions**.
- **Job Security and Steady Employment:** IBEW local unions have a **vast network** of signatory contractors that work on large, high-profile projects, which can lead to more consistent work opportunities and **steady employment**. The union often acts as an employment agency, matching members to available jobs.
- **Superior Training and Skill Upgrades:** The IBEW provides extensive, world-class training through the Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee ( **JATC**), including paid apprenticeships and **ongoing upgrade training** for journeymen to stay current with new technologies and industry standards.
- **Safety and Working Conditions:** Safety is a top priority, and the union advocates for better **workplace safety procedures** and policies, ensuring electricians have the right to return home safely at the end of every shift.
- **A Voice at Work:** Becoming a union member provides a collective **voice at work** to bargain over wages, benefits, and working rights, ensuring fair treatment and respect as a professional.
