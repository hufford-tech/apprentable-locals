**IBEW 5 Newsletters – IBEW Local Union #5 – Pittsburgh, PA**

**DEDICATION AND ACKNOWLEDGEMENT**

To all of our brothers and sisters, past, present, and future, who have made this local prosper and grow and will make it continue to expand new horizons in the future.

![](https://ibewlocal5.pairsite.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/john-mcintyre.jpg)

Acknowledgement is given to **Former Business Manager John F. McIntyre** for initiating the newsletter in 1979 as an informational and historical network of the Local Union. His time and dedication are greatly appreciated.

![](https://ibewlocal5.pairsite.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/tom-mcintyre-sr.jpg)

Acknowledgement is also given to **Brother Thomas McIntyre, Sr.**, for his diligent work and assistance on the newsletter since its inception.

![](https://ibewlocal5.pairsite.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/12/mike-duneavy_646faec538935-e1765502355153.jpg)

Acknowledgement is also given to **Former Business Manager, Michael R. Dunleavy**, for his foresight in making the newsletters available online, the first issue being published in April 2007.
