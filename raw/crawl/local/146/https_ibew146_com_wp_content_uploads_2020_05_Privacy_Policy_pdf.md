## Privacy Policy

We provide paid products and/or services on our Website. In that case, we use third-party services for payment processing.

We will not share or collect your payment card details. That information is provided directly to our third-party payment processors whose use of your personal information is governed by their Privacy Policy. These payment processors adhere to the standards set by PCI-DSS as managed by the PCI Security Standards Council.
