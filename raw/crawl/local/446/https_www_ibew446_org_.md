# Welcome to Local 446

Thank you for visiting the website of NE Lousiana's International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local Union 446. We have the best electricians in the area.

Imagine a job in the electrical industry where you can earn while you learn. Electricians' jobs are some of the most technical jobs in America, and we provide the highest quality, technical training to its members. Here at Local 446, we believe in standing up for our members rights and always have their best interest in mind. Our staff of Business Representatives is here to help you when you need them.

# What We Offer

##### We help local power professionals secure a better life and future through the power of

##### ﻿collective bargaining.

### Education

We offer Apprenticeship programs, professional training and many other educational resources for power pros.

[wp-money_thin](https://www.ibew446.org/what-we-do)

### Higher Wages

We harness the power of collective bargaining to get power pros better pay. IBEW members make a higher hourly wage than non union workers.

[wp-checked_icon](https://www.ibew446.org/what-we-do)

### Benefits

We offer members and their families insurance benefits that are well above the industry standard.
