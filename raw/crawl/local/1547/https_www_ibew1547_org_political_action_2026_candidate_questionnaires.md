# 2026 Candidate Questionnaires

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/663d0b717d1a4a3a623e3a68/669692813c14d16c90120c85_download%20(2).svg)

# IBEW Local 1547 endorses candidates for elected office who best align with the mission and values of our union and who support the policies and issues that best benefit the members of our union. IBEW 1547 also takes electoral viability into account in order to best serve our membership.

For questions about our endorsement process, please contact Alex Baker, Political Director: [abaker@ibew1547.org](mailto:abaker@ibew1547.org)
