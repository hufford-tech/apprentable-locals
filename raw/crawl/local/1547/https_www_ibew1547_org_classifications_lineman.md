# Lineman

Linemen are professionals who install, repair and maintain electrical power and telecommunications systems.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/663d0b717d1a4a3a623e3a68/669692813c14d16c90120c85_download%20(2).svg)

# Local 1547 Lineman Rep:

Garrette Francis: [907-777-7241](tel:+19077777241)

JOURNEYMAN LINEMAN WAGE as of 4/1/26: $76.59 (plus full benefit package)

Linemen are professionals who install, repair and maintain electrical power and telecommunications systems. Generally, linemen maintain electrical and telecommunications equipment in power stations and on telephone poles on the street.

Other technicians maintain equipment in homes and businesses. Becoming a journeyman lineman requires years of training and apprenticeships. Some states require certification for journeymen linemen.
