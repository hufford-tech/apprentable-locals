# Become a Member

With close to 5000 members, IBEW Local 1547 is one of the strongest unions in the state of Alaska.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/663d0b717d1a4a3a623e3a68/669692813c14d16c90120c85_download%20(2).svg)

# How To Join IBEW Local 1547:

There are several ways to become a member of IBEW Local 1547.  You can become a member by being hired by a company that is represented by IBEW 1547.  You could also visit our Dispatch Office and sign the referral list which will give you an opportunity to apply for an IBEW-represented position when one is available; you would become a member after you are officially hired.  Or, if you are interested in organizing your workplace, you can contact one of our organizers.

_If you are not a member of the union, but would like to learn more, please contact either:_

Kyle Kaiser at [907-777-7270](tel:+19077777270) or [kkaiser@ibew1547.org](mailto:kkaiser@ibew1547.org)

Paul Robarge at [907-777-7240](https://www.ibew1547.org/join/become-a-member#) or [probarge@ibew1547.org](mailto:probarge@ibew1547.org)

## Required Documents to Join IBEW:

1. Alaska Certificate of Fitness (See [Electrician/Wireman](https://www.ibew1547.org/classifications/wireman) or [Lineman](https://www.ibew1547.org/classifications/lineman) for more info)

2. Proof of Graduation from an Apprenticeship Program

3. Proof of Alaska Residency (e.g. PFD Eligibility Letter)


# Construction

Do you have questions or concerns about Prevailed Wage rules, commonly referred to as “Bacon Work”?

Click here to learn more: [https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/government-contracts/construction](https://www.dol.gov/agencies/whd/government-contracts/construction)

If you work and live in Alaska, you have probably heard of Title 36 or “Little Bacon”. Want to know more about you are entitled to?

Click here to learn more: [https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/forms/Pam400.pdf](https://labor.alaska.gov/lss/forms/Pam400.pdf)

# Non-Construction

Do you know your rights? Have you ever considered organizing your workplace?

Go to [https://apps.nlrb.gov/wwwm/index.htm](https://apps.nlrb.gov/wwwm/index.htm) for more information.

You can also visit [http://www.ibew.org/Join-the-IBEW](http://www.ibew.org/Join-the-IBEW) to learn more about joining the IBEW.
