## 2026 Governor Primary Election - August 18, 2026

### Dual Endorsement: Tom Begich and Click Bishop

While multiple candidates in the crowded field have a history of supporting organized labor, both Tom Begich and Click Bishop are steadfast champions who will do right by IBEW 1547 membership. Tom Begich is a proud 35-year member of IBEW Local 1547. Click Bishop is a proud 51-year member of IUOE Local 302.

In the August 18 pick-1 primary, members are encouraged to vote for which of the two candidates best fits their values and vision for Alaska.

Both longtime union members have a plan to build our state, champion pro-labor policies, and make our state a better place for working men and women.

Learn more about Tom Begich: [https://www.tombegichforalaska.com](https://www.tombegichforalaska.com/)

Learn more about Click Bishop: [https://www.clickbishopforgovernor.com](https://www.clickbishopforgovernor.com/)
