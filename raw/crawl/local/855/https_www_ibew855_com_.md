least 18 years old with a high school diploma or GED and a passing algebra grade. The

### Ibew 855 Journeyman Wireman

![Journeyman Wireman](https://www.ibew855.com/images/wireman.png)

An IBEW Journeyman Wireman is a skilled electrician who has
completed an apprenticeship and can work without supervision, installing and maintaining
electrical systems in commercial and industrial buildings. They are members of the
International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) and have a Code of Excellence that
emphasizes safety, professionalism, and quality workmanship. This role includes installing
equipment like lighting and motors, running conduit, and performing maintenance and repairs
on existing systems.
