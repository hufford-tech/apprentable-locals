**APPRENTICESHIP**

The Muncie Electrical JATC was established as a partnership between the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) and International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW). Its purpose is to train the next generation of electrical workers. We pride ourselves on producing the best-trained and most skilled electricians in the workforce. We utilize a 5-year apprenticeship model, which ensures a quality combination of both on the job and classroom training. Muncie Electrical JATC represents Eastern Indiana counties of Blackford, Delaware, Fayette, Franklin, Henry, Jay, Randolph, Wayne and Union.
