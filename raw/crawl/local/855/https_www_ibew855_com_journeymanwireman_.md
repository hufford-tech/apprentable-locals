**JOURNEYMAN WIREMAN**

An IBEW Journeyman Wireman is a skilled electrician who has completed an apprenticeship and can work without supervision, installing and maintaining electrical systems in commercial and industrial buildings. They are members of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) and have a Code of Excellence that emphasizes safety, professionalism, and quality workmanship. This role includes installing equipment like lighting and motors, running conduit, and performing maintenance and repairs on existing systems.
