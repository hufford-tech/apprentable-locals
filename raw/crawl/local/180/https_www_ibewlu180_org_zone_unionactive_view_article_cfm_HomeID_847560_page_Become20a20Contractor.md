| Posted On: Mar 28, 2021 | |
| As stated in our mission statement, we look to remove some of the financial impact to becoming a Local 180 signatory electrical contractor by reimbursing some of the costs required to secure a new contractors license, such as:<br>  - Application fee of $250.<br>  - Test fee of $150.<br>  - Course materials up to $450.<br>  - License bond up to $150.<br>  - Fringe benefit bond up to $200.<br>## The Fine Print<br>Maximum reimbursement shall not exceed $1,200 to any newly licensed and signatory electrical contractor. Reimbursement for re-activating an existing license is limited to $350 for the above mentioned bonds.<br>No other financial incentives and services are offered or implied. |
