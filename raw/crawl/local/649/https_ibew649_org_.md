# Objectives of the IBEW?

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/US-CANADA.jpg)

## To organize all workers in the entire electrical industry in the United States and Canada, including all those in public utilities and electrical manufacturing, into local unions.

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Powerline_worker.jpg)

## To promote safe, efficient, and standardized methods of electrical work that ensure quality, protect workers, and advance the interests of the industry as a whole.

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/workers.jpg)

## To cultivate feelings of friendship among those of our industry.

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/Worker_CoOp.jpg)

## To settle all disputes between employers and employees by arbitration. (if possible)

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/doctor.jpg)

## To assist each other in sickness or distress.

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/secrurity.jpg)

## To secure employment.

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/time_clock-1024x1024.jpg)

## To reduce the hours of daily labor.

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/paycheck-1024x1024.jpg)

## To secure adequate pay for our work.

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/family-1024x1024.jpg)

## To seek a higher and higher standard of living.

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/worker-1024x1024.jpg)

## To seek security for the individual.

![](https://ibew649.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/11/elevate-1024x1024.jpg)

## By legal and proper means, to elevate the moral, intellectual, and social conditions of our members, their families, and dependents, in the interest of a higher standard of citizenship.
