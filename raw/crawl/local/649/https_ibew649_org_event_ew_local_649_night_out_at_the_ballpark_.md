## IBEW Local 649: Night Out at the Ballpark

### July 24 @ 6:30 pm \- 9:00 pm

**IBEW Local 649 Night at the Ballpark**

Take Me Out to the Ballgame with IBEW Local 649!

Join us at Gordon Moore Park’s Lloyd Hopkins Field to cheer on the Alton River Dragons.

Bring your ballpark spirit and enjoy a fun evening with fellow IBEW Local 649 members as we watch the Alton River Dragons, proud members of the Prospect League, from the Party Deck.

**Game Time:** 6:35 PM

**Gates Open:** 45 minutes prior to first pitch

### What’s Included for Members

All IBEW Local 649 members in good standing will receive one complimentary ticket. Your ticket includes unlimited beer, water, soda, hot dogs, hamburgers, peanuts, and popcorn through the 7½ inning.

### Additional Ticket Pricing

- Family and friends: $41.00 each
- Children ages 6–14: $25.00 each
- Children 5 and under: Free

### RSVP by July 1

Reserve your spot by calling **618-462-1627** or emailing **[info@ibew649.org](mailto:info@ibew649.org)**.
