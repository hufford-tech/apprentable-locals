August 19, 2026

-

August 20, 2026

**IBEW Local 280 2-Night Blitz – McMinnville**

Join IBEW Local 280 for a two-night volunteer blitz to connect with skilled electricians, strengthen our union, and make a difference in our community.

**Event Details:**

- **Dates:** August 19 & 20
- **Time:** 4:30 PM – 8:00 PM each night
- **Location:** McMinnville, OR (exact location provided after registration)

**What to Expect:**

- Connect with fellow electricians
- Help grow IBEW Local 280
- Build union power
- Make a real impact

Snacks will be provided before the event, and dinner and drinks will be served afterward.

**Register:** Scan the QR code on the flyer or complete the online registration form.

To register, please click the link below and complete the registration form:
