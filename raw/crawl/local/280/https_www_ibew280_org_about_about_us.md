# ibew 280

## About Us

## IBEW Local 280 represents more than 1,900 members

covering Yamhill, Polk, Marion, Lane, Linn, Benton, Crook, Jefferson, and Deschutes counties.

[Our Mission and Vision Statement](https://www.ibew280.org/about/about-us#vision-mission)

## Who we are

One of the IBEW's primary missions is to **organize all workers in the electrical industry**, AND THAT MEANS YOU!

We have been organizing electricians since 1891 and continue to build on the foundation our early leaders laid more than 130 years ago.

Local 280 represents inside, residential, and limited energy (sound & communication) technicians. We are always looking for people with the skills to carry our trade forward.

## What's in it for you?

Our members see better wages, pension, insurance, and training, along with a staff that represents your rights pertaining to safety, licensing laws and negotiates your wages and benefits.

[Why join our union?](https://www.ibew280.org/join/why-be-union)

## Our Vision

IBEW/NECA shall be the leaders in the electrical industry,

exceeding the expectations of our customers and community with quick responses to their ever-changing needs utilizing our highly trained and experienced workforce.

Our mission

Together we will provide quality electrical services

with highly trained, hardworking members of the IBEW/NECA team. We will earn the respect of our customers and community by being innovative, responsive, productive, and cost-effective.
