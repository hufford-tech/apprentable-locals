## Organizing electricians since 1891

IBEW Local 280 represents more than 1,900 members covering Yamhill, Polk, Marion, Lane, Linn, Benton, Crook, Jefferson, and Deschutes counties. One of the IBEW's primary missions is to organize all workers in the electrical industry, AND THAT MEANS YOU!

[Learn more about us](https://www.ibew280.org/about/about-us)

Candidates must be 18+, U.S. citizens or legal residents, able to lift 50 lbs., live within 74 miles of the stadium, and be in good physical condition. A $20.00 Working Dues fee applies.
