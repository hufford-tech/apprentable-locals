### Central Oregon Meetings

Central Oregon Meetings are at 5:30 on the 2nd Thursday of each month. Presently holding meetings UA Local 290 Plumbers & Steamfitters Training Center 2161 SE 1st Redmond, OR. Food Provided.

**Second Thursday of each month at 5:30 pm** at the Central Oregon Hall, 2450 SW 6th Street, Suite 101, Redmond.

Food is provided.

# Valley Meetings

Valley Meetings are at 5:30 the 3rd Thursday of each month. Held at the training center in Tangent. Food provided.

**Third Thursday of each month at 5:30 pm** at the Local 280 Training Center, 33309 Highway 99E, Tangent.

Food is provided.
