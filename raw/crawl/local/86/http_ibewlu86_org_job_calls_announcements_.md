# \\*\\* Job Calls & Announcements \*\*

### **Journeyman Wiremen Job Calls:**

To log-in for personal info, job history, registration info, or TO BID ON JOBS. [CLICK HERE](https://ibew86.workingsystems.com/)

- Journeyman Scale:  $47.70
- Health & Welfare: $12.15 + $1.00
- Pension: $10.36
- Annuity:  $6.85
- Vacation: $1.45 per hour worked (deducted from net wages) this is an optional deduction
- Working Assessments: 5.5% of gross wages for JW (deducted from net wages)

Referral calls are made between 9 AM to 3 PM

Sign-in window hours are 8 AM to 5 PM

### Announcements:

**MEMBERS TRAVELING –**

Any member interested in heading anywhere else will need to be signed up with our funds office for the IBEW **E** lectronic **R** eciprocal **T** ransfer **S** ystem (“ERTS”).

Anyone working out of town in other jurisdictions must notify the referral office with proof.  To be able to freeze dings on the out-of-work list, verification of working out of town is necessary with proof of referral slip or pay stub **each month** when re-signing.

**RE-SIGN –**

To Re-sign click on the link below.  Then for the “Username” enter your **_“Card Number”_** and for the “Password” enter your _**“Last Name and the last 4 digits of your Social Security #”**_.

Web Re-Sign [CLICK HERE](https://ibew86.workingsystems.com/) or download the printable [Re-Sign Form](http://ibewlu86.org/wp-content/uploads/2017/07/re-sign_form.pdf)

**RE-SIGN POLICY –**

**Effective June 1, 2012 all re-signs will be once a month, from the 10th through the 16th of each calendar month. Re-sign can be done by email, fax, and mail or in person.  If the re-sign is not received in the Referral Office by the 16th of each month, the applicant on any book(s) will “roll off” the list(s).  The applicant will have to sign in person and re-register on the out of work book(s).**
