# Women’s Committee

_What We Do_

We are dedicated to empowering women in construction, advocating for equal opportunities, fostering mentorship, and creating a supportive network for professional and personal growth for all members.

_Our Values_

**Solidarity, Respect, Integrity, Inclusion**

**Solidarity** – We are always stronger together than we are apart. We will always stand together with our fellow workers.

**Respect**– Every person is deserving of respect and decency. We do not tolerate harassment or bullying of any kind.

**Integrity** – We represent the IBEW and strive to uphold its good name in everything we do.

**Inclusion** – We are all siblings under the IBEW. Everyone is welcome here, regardless of race, gender, or sexual identity.

_Contact Us_

If you have questions, would like to join, or would like to request our assistance with anything, please contact Kathryn Mucica at [local86womenscommittee@ibewlu86.com](mailto:local86womenscommittee@ibewlu86.com)
