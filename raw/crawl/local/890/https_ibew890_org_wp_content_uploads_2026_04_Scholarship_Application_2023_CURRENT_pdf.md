# IBEW LOCAL UNION 890

# SCHOLARSHIP PROGRAM GUIDELINES

The recipient of the scholarship shall be a member in good standing or an 
immediate family member of Local 890, I.B.E.W.

The scholarship will be available for any applicant for enrollment as a fulltime student in any accredited college, university, or vocational/technical 
school.  Scholastic honors of the applicant will be considered along with 
other achievements, activities, and abilities shown by the applicant during 
his or her scholastic career.  Applicants do not have to be honor students, but 
must show a desire and the ability to do  schoolwork on a college or 
vocation/technical level.

Applications should be received no later than <u>April 15th.</u>

The recipients of the scholarship will be announced in June.  Scholarship 
payment will be made at the beginning of the second semester after 
satisfactorily completing the first semester and showing proof of registration 
and payment for the second semester.  The maximum scholarship awarded is 
$500.00.

The decision of the Scholarship Committee regarding selection of the 
recipient and implementations of the scholarship shall in all cases be final.
