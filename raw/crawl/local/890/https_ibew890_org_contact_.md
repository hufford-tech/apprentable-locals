# About & Contact​

## IBEW Local 890 Mission Statement

International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local 890 was chartered on February 6, 1919 as a labor organization under the terms of the Constitution of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, and has jurisdiction in most of southern Wisconsin.

### Our Objectives Are

- To promote reasonable methods of work
- To cultivate feelings of friendship among those of our industry
- To settle all disputes between employers and employees by arbitration (if possible)
- To assist each other in sickness or distress
- To secure employment
- To reduce the hours of daily labor
- To secure adequate pay for our work
- To seek a higher standard of living
- To seek security for the individual

**And by legal and proper means to elevate the moral, intellectual and social conditions of our members, their families and dependents, in the interest of a higher standard of citizenship.**

On February 6, 1919, IBEW Local Union 890 was chartered by the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers. IBEW Local 890 represents over 700 electricians and retirees in southern Wisconsin. Geographically, Local 890 covers the following counties; Green, Jefferson, Lafayette, Rock and Walworth. Benjamin Myers is the current Business Manager.
