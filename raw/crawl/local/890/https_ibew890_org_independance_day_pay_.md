# Independence Day Pay

We have received a few questions about Independence Day. The holiday will be observed on Friday, July 3rd and is double time if worked. Saturday is a normal weekend at 1.5 time, and Sunday is always double time per the CBA.

On February 6, 1919, IBEW Local Union 890 was chartered by the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers. IBEW Local 890 represents over 700 electricians and retirees in southern Wisconsin. Geographically, Local 890 covers the following counties; Green, Jefferson, Lafayette, Rock and Walworth. Benjamin Myers is the current Business Manager.
