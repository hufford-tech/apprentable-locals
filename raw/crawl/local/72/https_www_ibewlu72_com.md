**FOR TUESDAY, AUGUST 11TH, WE HAVE: 12 CALLS.**



**1 CALL FOR A JOURNEYMAN WIREMAN** FOR HICKS LIGHTNING



PROTECTION. WORK IS IN TEMPLE, TX AT THE TTX DATA CENTER.



WORKING 40 HRS AT $37.70 WITH POSSIBLE OVERTIME. WORK



WILL CONSIST OF GROUNDING AND BONDING. MUST HAVE DL,



SS CARD, OSHA 10 MINIMUM, VALID TDLR, PASS DRUG TEST,



BACKGROUND CHECK AND HAVE SAFETY TOE BOOTS.



\\*\\*\\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*



**4 CALLS FOR A FOREMAN/GENERAL FOREMAN** FOR MILLER



ELECTRIC. WORK WILL BE AT THE HUBBARD DATA CENTER.



INTERVIEWS WILL BE SET UP BY THE CONTRACTOR TO



VERIFY SKILLS AND REQUIREMENTS.



SEND RESUMES TO:SGilliland@IBEWLU72.com



\\*\\*\\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*



**1 CALL FOR A FOREMAN** FOR BIG STATE ELECTRIC. WORK IS IN



TEMPLE, TX AT THE ROWAN DATA CENTER. PAY IS $46.70/HOUR.



WORKING 5-8'S WITH POSSIBLE OVERTIME.  MEDIUM VOLTAGE



EXPERIENCE IS A PLUS. MUST HAVE TWO FORMS OF ID: MUST BE



ORIGINALS, NO COPIES. (DL AND/OR SS CARD, STATE ID CARD,



BIRTH CERTIFICATE, PASSPORT, EMPLOYMENT AUTHORIZATION



CARD OR PERMANENT RESIDENT CARD). MUST HAVE VALID TDLR,



OSHA-30, MUST PASS A DRUG TEST AND BACKGROUND CHECK,



AND HAVE ALL DIRECT DEPOSIT INFORMATION.



NO WEAPONS OF ANY KIND ALLOWED ON PREMISES.



\\*\\*\\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*



**5 CALLS FOR JOURNEYMAN WIREMAN**FOR SCHMIDT



WORK IS IN TEMPLE, TX. WORKING 4-10'S AND 2-8'S.



MUST HAVE DL,SS CARD,VALID TDLR,OSHA-10 MINIMUM



AND DIRECT DEPOSIT INFORMATION FOR ORIENTATION.



MUST PASS DRUG TEST.



\\*\\*\\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*\*

WORK IS AT SPACE-X WORKING 5-10'S AND POSSIBLE WEEKENDS.



PAYING $3 OVER SCALE. MUST HAVE VALID ID,SS CARD, AND PASS



DRUG TEST AND BACKGROUND CHECK.
