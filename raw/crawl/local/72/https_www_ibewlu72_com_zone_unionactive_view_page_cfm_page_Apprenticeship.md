THIS APPRENTICESHIP SPONSOR IS COMMITTED TO EQUAL OPPORTUNITY FOR ALL APPLICANTS.

THE RECRUITMENT, SELECTION, EMPLOYMENT AND TRAINING OF APPRENTICES DURING THEIR

APPRENTICESHIP, SHALL BE WITHOUT DISCRIMINATION BECAUSE OF RACE, COLOR, RELIGION,

NATIONAL ORIGIN, GENDER OR AGE — EXCEPT THAT THE APPLICANT MUST MEET THE MINIMUM

AGE REQUIREMENT. THE JATC DOES NOT, AND WILL NOT, DISCRIMINATE AGAINST A QUALIFIED

INDIVIDUAL WITH A DISABILITY BECAUSE OF THE DISABILITY OF SUCH INDIVIDUAL.
