## Dispatch

[  (303) 744-7171](tel:(303)744-7171)

## Business Agents Directory

**‍** If you are a Local 111 member with an on-the-job problem that needs attention, please contact your Business Representative by telephone or e-mail.

If you need help identifying your union Business Representative, call Local 111 at [(303) 744-7171](tel:(303)744-7171) and ask the union operator for assistance.

[Business Agents Directory](https://www.ibew111.org/about/officers-staff#business-agents)

## Membership Development Department

If you are an employee at a non-union company and believe there may be interest among your fellow employees in being organized by IBEW Local 111, please contact our Membership Development Department, or visit our Organize page.

[  (303) 398-7350](tel:(303)398-7350)

[Organize your workplace](https://www.ibew111.org/organize/become-a-business-partner)

## Outside Construction Department

If you are a contractor with an interest in becoming signatory to a Local 111 labor agreement, please call our Outside Construction Department.

[  (303) 398-7369](tel:(303)398-7346)

## Letters to the Editor

If you are a Local 111 member and wish to submit a letter to the editor for possible publication on our website or in our quarterly newsletter, please e-mail your letter (along with your name and telephone number) to Letters to the Editor.

[  mail@ibew111.org](mailto:mail@ibew111.org?subject=Newsletter%20Webpage%20Suggestion)
