# IBEW Local Union 701

We are the right choice. We strive to provide you with the safe, professional, drug free workforce needed to complete your projects on time and on budget.

![IBEW 701 building in Warrenville, IL](https://www.ibew701.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/05/IBEW-701-warrrenville-illinois.jpg)

In today’s ever changing electrical & telecommunication fields the members of Local 701 receive the finest continuing education & safety training ensuring they have the knowledge and skills needed for the future.

Scholarship Info

The 2026 PowerForward DuPage Scholarship Application is available and this year, (5) $1,500 scholarships will be awarded. PowerForward DuPage is proud to provide college tuition assistance to the children and grandchildren of IBEW Local 701 members and signatory contractors. IBEW Local 701 members are also eligible to apply.

**To view the program details on the PowerForward DuPage website and/or print the 2026 application, please [CLICK HERE](https://powerforwarddupage.com/news/2025-powerforward-dupage-scholarship-program/).**

The deadline to submit the application with official transcripts is Friday, July 3, 2026. If you have any questions regarding this program, please contact the PowerForward DuPage Director, Lauren Mesich at (630) 393-1701 Extension 5

Meade Electric has launched their 2026 Scholarship Program to provide college tuition assistance to the members of the IBEW Local 701 and their children. This program is being offered to high school seniors or currently enrolled college or trade/technical students planning to attend a university, college, community college, or trade/technical school. You do not have to be an employee of Meade Electric to apply.

The scholarship amount is yet to be determined but will be awarded in a single payment for one year and may be applied for again in each of the four years while attending school. Scholarship awards are to be used for tuition and/or fees, books, room and board expenses.

To view and/or print the current application, please click on the following link: **[2026 Meade Electric Scholarship Application.](https://powerforwarddupage.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/02/2026-Meade-Electric-Scholarship-Application.pdf)**

**Please note all** **applications with supporting documents MUST be postmarked by March 15, 2026 to be eligible for consideration** **.** Please refer to the application form for judging criteria and eligibility requirements. Winners will be notified by direct mail.

If you have any questions regarding this program, please contact Meade Electric directly at (708) 588-2500.
