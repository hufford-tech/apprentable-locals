# Celebrating 85 years

June 6, 2025
\|
by
IBEW 1186

![](https://ibew1186.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/85years-1024x1024.png)

Brothers and sisters,

Aloha! Today marks a very momentous day for all of us at IBEW Local 1186 as it marks our 85th anniversary, and we are beyond grateful for all of our members for what you have done to make our local not only a huge, contributing part of Hawaii’s infrastructure, unique dynamic and communities but also a major part of building Hawaii’s history and putting us on the map. We would like YOU to contribute to our 85th celebration by letting us know what it means to you to be a member of IBEW Local 1186 by sharing a few short thoughts and a photo of you at the link below.\*

**[MEMBER SUBMISSION HERE](https://docs.google.com/forms/d/e/1FAIpQLScFKX9df92ZHlccYLbQfin81PQOVu2T2cOdNacK6cAaG17zXQ/viewform?usp=sharing&ouid=112746207142742729778)**

\*Submitting this google form gives IBEW Local 1186 permission to use member’s name/photo/quotes freely on social media, advertising and any and all 85th anniversary multimedia material. Member owns rights to photo(s) submitted and IBEW Local 1186 will not be responsible for any unauthorized use of photos/consent for photos.

Aloha brothers and sisters,

Please watch the video below from our Business Manager/Financial Secretary Damien Kim, below in it’s entirety for your invitation to our anniversary dinner(s).
