**How To Join**

**Journeymen**

The Process is Simple

If you have a valid Texas Journeyman License or 12,000 hours of verifiable electrical experience and looking either to switch jobs or gain employment with our wages and benefits, then all you would need to do is come into the office with the right paperwork and credentials and you could be eligible to take a call that same day.

**CE/CW**

If you would like to enroll in our CE/CW program and you have 2 or more years of commercial/industrial electrical experience, please call Lance Tuck to set up a hands-on evaluation to determine your rate of pay and discuss the benefits the IBEW can provide for you.

**Apprenticeship**
