# Galveston Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee

# Training

## _Work while you learn_

The Galveston JATC accepts applications year round for individuals seeking to start a career in the electrical industry.  Apprentices accepted into the program are placed with an electrical contractor for full employment and attend school two nights a week during the fall and spring semesters.  An apprentice is required to work 8,000 hours of on the job training and 900 hours of classroom instruction in order to graduate the program and receive certification as a journeyman  wireman with the U.S. Department of Labor.

# Journeyman Training

## _Keep up with latest technology_

Classes are available on an as needed  basis to meet the demands of the ever changing industry.  Classes range from continuing education classes for state issued license, to motor controls, instrumentation and  other specialty skills required by our contractors and customers.
