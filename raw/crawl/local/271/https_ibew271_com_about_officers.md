## Our Officers

Meet our officers and board members at the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local Union 271 in Wichita, Kansas. They are focused on organizing all workers in the electrical industry and raising the standard of living for all. Our officers work to promote reasonable methods of work, secure employment and elevate the conditions of our members, their families and dependents.
