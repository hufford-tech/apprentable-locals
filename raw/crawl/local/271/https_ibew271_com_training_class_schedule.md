## Class Schedule

**Please note:** Enrollment must be done in advance by phone or email for all courses. You must attend all scheduled days of the course to receive CEU’s, and partial credit will not be issued. All classes will be held at the WEJATC unless otherwise noted.

**Attention:** You must have 12 hours of Continuing Education Units from approved courses, with six hours consisting of training on the National Electrical Code (NEC), every two years for license renewal.

\*These courses meet an NEC requirement.

Classes are subject to change. Please check for class updates [here](http://www.wejatc.org/journeymen/jwclass.html).
