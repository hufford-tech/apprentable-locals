Why Join IBEW?

[![Tradition](https://ibew271.com/assets/img/home/tradition-icon.svg)](https://ibew271.com/why-join)

Tradition

The IBEW Local Union #271 has **over 107 years experience** representing electrical workers.


[![Strength](https://ibew271.com/assets/img/home/strength-icon.svg)](https://ibew271.com/why-join)

Strength

By standing together as a group, the IBEW is able to negotiate **better wages, benefits, and training.**

[![Value](https://ibew271.com/assets/img/home/value-icon.svg)](https://ibew271.com/why-join)

Value

Union Workers make an average of **28% more per year, in wages and benefits** than non-union workers.
