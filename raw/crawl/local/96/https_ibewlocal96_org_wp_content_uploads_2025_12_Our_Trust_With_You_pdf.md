# Our Trust With You, Is Built In

## Skilled Workforce

### While there are many advantages to becoming signatory with Local Union 96,

**the most important advantage is having access to the region’s most highly skilled and highly trained electrical workforce.**

### From the time one of our members becomes an apprentice, they are placed into

**an intensive registered apprenticeship program, where they build their skills while learning on-the-job from experienced journeymen. To supplement this on-**

### the-job training, apprentices also learn from accredited instructors in the classroom.

**Local 96 signatory contractors are able to adjust their manpower based on the** **size or complexity of their job. If they need to increase manpower because they** **are working on a larger job or need to decrease manpower because work has** **slowed, they can do so. If a contractor needs someone who has a certain** **certification, then they can request that Local 96 send them tradesmen or tradeswomen with that certification.**

**No matter the size or scope of the work, IBEW Local Union 96 will be able to man** **the job – even if it requires bringing in travelers from other IBEW Locals.**

## Increased Market Share

**Every business wants to increase their market share foot print, and electrical contractors are no different.**

**By becoming signatory with IBEW Local Union 96 an electrical contractor** **expands their ability to bid on projects, which may have been too large for them** **in the past. The ability to staff projects with IBEW Local Union 96 members** **opens up opportunities to bid more competitively.**

## NECA Membership

### Another advantage of becoming signatory with

**IBEW Local Union 96 is the membership to the National Electrical** **Contractors Association (NECA).** **IBEW Local Union 96 signatory contractors are members of the Central** **Massachusetts, NECA chapter. With this membership, they gain access to** **training programs, advocacy for the use of NECA contractors and much more.**

## Quality Training

### In addition to the above benefits, when an electrical contractor becomes

**signatory to Local 96, their existing employees gain access to the best training in the industry moving forward.**

**If a journeyman wants to further their learning, continued education is** **offered through Local 96. This can include upgrade safety courses, CPR First** **AID training, Code Updates and much more to keep experienced electricians updated on the latest industry developments.**

### Whether it is completing the apprenticeship program to earn journeyman

**status or taking an upgrade safety course, your existing workforce will have all training provided to them.**

**The advantages of becoming an IBEW Local 96 signatory contractor are** **plentiful. From valuable training for your existing workforce, to the increased** **opportunity to bid on large projects, IBEW membership provides plenty of advantages.**
