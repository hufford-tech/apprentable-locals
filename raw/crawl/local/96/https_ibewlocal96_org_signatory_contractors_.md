## Signatory Contractors

IBEW Local 96 signatory contractors adeptly perform specialized construction work related to the design, installation, and maintenance of electrical systems for wide-ranging projects throughout Central Massachusetts. They are at the forefront of meeting electrical construction requirements for high-voltage projects, shell and core electrical construction, tenant fit-outs, public works projects, energy-efficient lighting retrofits, public works and infrastructure projects, building automation control systems, renewable energy projects including solar (PV) and wind energy, and more. Our signatory contractors are dedicated to the safest work environments, employing safety procedures that meet the highest industry standards.
