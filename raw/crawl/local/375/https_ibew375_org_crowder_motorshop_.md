# Crowder Motorshop

CROWDER JR COMPANY

2184 Industrial Dr.

Bethlehem, PA 18017 \| [Get Directions](https://goo.gl/maps/gVeFHMfvZW22)

![](https://maps.googleapis.com/maps/api/js/StaticMapService.GetMapImage?1m2&1i4873489&2i6311754&2e1&3u16&4m2&1u400&2u300&5m6&1e0&5sen-US&6sus&10b1&12b1&14i47083502&8e1&key=AIzaSyCmL18misQw9KdwqGaw3zHkitj8vG6QF2Y&token=20203)

IBEW Local Union #375 represents 18 employees at Crowder Motorshop (Crowder Jr. Company) .  This unit signed their first Collective Bargaining Agreement on April 24, 2001.  The current Collective Bargaining Agreement is binding from June 01, 2016 through May 31, 2019.

**Unit Chair** Alan Sherman

Visit our website: [http://www.crowderjr.com/](http://www.crowderjr.com/)

## The Union Difference

All training is

FREE and provided with membership via the JATC.

Our members are CPR and First Aid Certified. The cost for certification is included with your union benefits.
