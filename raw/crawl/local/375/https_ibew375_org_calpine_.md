# Calpine

CALPINE MID-ATLANTIC OPERATIONS, LLC

2254 Applebutter Road

Bethlehem, PA  18015 \| [Get Directions](https://goo.gl/maps/LJFTZJ5dU6D2)

![](https://maps.googleapis.com/maps/api/js/StaticMapService.GetMapImage?1m2&1i4878500&2i6313573&2e1&3u16&4m2&1u400&2u300&5m6&1e0&5sen-US&6sus&10b1&12b1&14i47083502&8e1&key=AIzaSyCmL18misQw9KdwqGaw3zHkitj8vG6QF2Y&token=9380)

IBEW Local Union #375 represents approximately 20 employees at the Bethlehem Energy Plant (Calpine Mid-Atlantic Operations, Inc.).  These members operate and maintain the eight electrical generators of the facility which is capable of producing 1,130 megawatts of power.  This unit was organized in 2007 and signed their first Collective Bargaining Agreement on November 1, 2008.  The current Collective Bargaining Agreement is binding from November 01, 2018 through October 31, 2021.

Monthly meetings are held at IBEW Local Union #375’s union hall on the first Tuesday of the month at 3:30 P.M.

**Unit Chair** Gregory J. Sinsky

**Unit Scribe** Gregory P. Kulanko

Visit our website: [http://calpine.com/](http://calpine.com/)

## The Union Difference

Training is included with membership and provided in our own state-of-the-art labs.

Members are NFPA 70E Certified.

The cost for certification is included with your union benefits.
