# Community Service

IBEW Local 375 has been a pillar in the community for over 100 years. Along with our members, we have donated thousands of hours, as well as materials, to various well-deserving causes and organizations throughout the community.

As part of the JATC program apprentices perform community services each year, many volunteer to donate more hours than required due to the culture we foster at local 375.

Apprentices also join our Journeymen and their families to engage in our community service endeavors.

Together, as an organization, we have been involved in the following projects.

- Adopt-A-Highway
- Miller Keystone Blood Bank
- Habitat For Humanity
- Bowl For Kid’s Sake
- Wreaths Across America
- Turning Point
- American Red Cross Smoke Detector Installs
- Lehigh County ASPCA
- Haffsville Sanctuary
- Bradbury Sullivan Center
- Camp Minsi
- Multiple Church/ Religious Activities

If you are looking for our assistance or sponsorship of your cause or organization, please contact Paul Anthony at [panthony@ibew375.org.](mailto:panthony@ibew375.org)
