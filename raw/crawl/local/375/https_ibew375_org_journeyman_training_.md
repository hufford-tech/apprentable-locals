# Journeyman Training

The JATC offers Journeyman up-to-date training on regular basis. This training allows Journeyman the ability to earn CEU’s towards their master license, as well as courses to increase their knowledge base. This training is included with membership to the IBEW Local 375. If you are a member, please see our [**calendar**](https://ibew375.org/events/) for upcoming training opportunities.

If you are not yet member, and wish to take advantage of this amazing opportunity, consider joining Local 375 today. [**Get Started**](https://ibew375.org/registration-page/)

## The Union Difference

Members are NFPA 70E Certified.

The cost for certification is included with your union benefits.

Keep your knowledge up-to-date using our Industry sponsored state-of-the-art training labs.
