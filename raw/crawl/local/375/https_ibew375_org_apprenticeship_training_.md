## Let’s Get Started. Select an option below.

[**How to Apply**](https://ibew375.org/how-to-apply/)

To get started, you must meet the minimum requirements for consideration. Please read the requirements in detail and then submit your application when you’re ready.

[Read More](https://ibew375.org/how-to-apply/)

[**Get Started**](https://ibew375.org/how-to-apply/)

All Applications must be submitted in person at the main union hall located at 101 South 7th Street on Tuesdays from 8:00-4:30.

[Read More](https://ibew375.org/how-to-apply/)

[**Training Center**](https://ibew375.org/jatc-training-center/)

Check out our digs. The JATC training center consists of six technology enabled classrooms, along with six, state-of-the-art, industry supported training labs.

[Read More](https://ibew375.org/jatc-training-center/)

[**Classes/Training**](https://ibew375.org/events/)

We offer apprenticeship classes and ongoing training for Journeyman and Master electricians. Check out what we have to offer!

[Read More](https://ibew375.org/events/)

## The Union Difference

Training provided in industry supported state-of-the-art training labs.
