# Officers of IBEW Local 375

**Meet The Officers** A team of veteran electricians who understand the challenges and concerns faced by the nearly 1000 members of this local represent IBEW, Local Union 375. They stand ready to serve the membership in every feasible capacity and invite all questions, requests, and inquiries. All are veteran Journeymen and a product of our apprenticeship program, with up to 35 years of service.

## The Union Difference

Members are NFPA 70E Certified.

The cost for certification is included with your union benefits.

Our workers make **$10.62** more an hour on average then nonunion counterparts.
