# Business Managers Message

Dear Brothers and Sisters:

Local 375 had a strong 2022, and the outlook for 2023 is equally encouraging. Please continue to attend safety classes and upgrade training in order for our local to maintain its position as the best trained, safest, and most productive in the industry. Your dedication and commitment to our craft are recognized throughout the Lehigh Valley and beyond. Let’s have a good year and stay safe.

Fraternally,

Paul Anthony

## The Union Difference

Training provided in industry supported state-of-the-art training labs.

Union workers make **$10.62 more an hour** on average than their nonunion counterparts.
