# Service Electric

SERVICE ELECTRIC CABLE TV, INC.

2260 Avenue A, LVIP #1

Bethlehem, PA  18017 \| [Get Directions](https://goo.gl/maps/DymH9E5pDxy)

![](https://maps.googleapis.com/maps/api/js/StaticMapService.GetMapImage?1m2&1i4873327&2i6311587&2e1&3u16&4m2&1u400&2u300&5m6&1e0&5sen-US&6sus&10b1&12b1&14i47083502&8e1&key=AIzaSyCmL18misQw9KdwqGaw3zHkitj8vG6QF2Y&token=92538)

IBEW Local Union #375 represents approximately 164 employees of the customer service, maintenance, construction, and installation departments of Service Electric Cable TV, Inc.  The company was founded in 1948 and provided the first cable TV service in America.  This unit was originally organized in the 1970’s by IBEW Local Union #1319.  The agreement was split in 1995, at which time the Lehigh Valley/Hunterdon Systems voted to join IBEW Local Union #375.  Their first Collective Bargaining Agreement with Local Union #375 was signed in January 1, 1995.  The current Collective Bargaining Agreement is binding from January 1, 2015 through December 31, 2019.

Monthly meetings are held at IBEW Local Union #375’s union hall on the second Thursday of the month at 6:30 P.M.
