# North Whitehall Township

North Whitehall Township

3256 Levans Road

Coplay, PA  18037 \| [Get Directions](https://goo.gl/maps/YSJioXeJayT2)

IBEW Local Union #375 represents approximately 11 employees in the Parks and Roads Departments of North Whitehall Township.  This unit was organized in 2007 and they signed their first Collective Bargaining Agreement on November 19, 2008.  The current Collective Bargaining Agreement is binding from January 1, 2018 through December 31, 2020.

The monthly meetings are held at the Township Building on the 2nd Tuesday of the month.  Meeting time varies because of the summer/winter shift schedule.

- Memorial Day to Labor Day meetings begin at 3:00 PM
- Labor Day to Memorial Day meetings begin at 3:30 PM

**Unit Chair**: Timothy J. Trunk

Visit our website: [http://northwhitehall.org/](http://northwhitehall.org/)

## The Union Difference

Our workers make **$10.62** **more an hour** on average then nonunion counterparts.

Our members are CPR and First Aid Certified using our own Safety Lab.
