# JATC Training Center

Our training center consists of six technology enabled classrooms, along with six state-of-the-art, industry supported, training labs. These high-tech classrooms and labs allow the JATC to provide real-world experience and training to all levels of electrician.

## Training Labs

- Motor & PLC Control Lab
- Conduit Lab
- Safety Lab
- Fire Alarm and Lighting Lab
- Residential Building Lab
- Telecommunications Lab

## Classrooms

- Accommodate between 12 to 30 students
- WIFI enabled for online curriculum

Apprentices have the ability to experiment with, and learn motor control and PLC systems, learn the best practices of, and build real conduit structures, engage in the best safety training the industry can offer, troubleshoot and build real fire alarm and lighting systems, engage in wiring in an environment exactly like a residential location, and, build-out and wire a telecommunications facility, all in one building.

Our Journeyman take advantage of our facility on a regular basis, moving their skill to the next level as well as training our apprentices on the latest technologies.

Our labs and classrooms are supported and equipped by the leaders in our industry, both locally and nationally. The JATC and IBEW 375 work hard to ensure great relationships with our industry, which allows for this level of facility. Access to our training facility is included with your membership in IBEW Local 375.  If you’re not a member, get started today and **[APPLY ONLINE NOW!](https://ibew375.org/registration-page/)**
