Fees

- There is no monthly fee (on active accounts) or minimum balance requirement.
- $5.00 per month dormant Account fee can be charged when there is no activity for 1 year.

Accessing Funds

Use ATM card to access account on a daily basis. Withdrawals are limited to $300.00 within a 24 hour period (Monday thru Friday, with Saturday and Sunday counting as one day). The card is an ATM only; it will not work as a debit card or point of sale.

To Request Withdrawal by Check

- Visit the Warrenville or the Chicago Downtown location.
- Request funds by mail: by sending request to the Warrenville address. Request must include account holders name and local number, account number and specify dollar amount.
- A bank check will be mailed upon request with a request by Fax: 312-541-6045 or US mail (use Warrenville address above).
- The bank will contact the customer to verify information on account before mailing the check to the address on the account.
