National Council of Senior Citizens

Article I, Section 2 \| The purpose of this club shall be to carry out the spirit of the Brotherhood; to aid and assist each other as necessary; and to promote the Social, Economic, Health and Political Welfare of its members.

Article II, Section 2 \| Associate members: Any IBEW member who is 55 years of age or older and spouse.

ALL 150 Retirees Invited

**We would like to extend an invitation to all IBEW Local Union 150 retirees** and their spouses to join us at our meetings and functions. We carry out the spirit of the Brotherhood, share and assist each other and have fun along the way.

In addition to meeting the second Tuesday of the month which has an occasional speaker from the area, we also have:

**Quarterly luncheons at local restaurants**

- Take several trips a year
- Attend the annual Union golf outing
- Ride the float in the Labor Day Parade hosted by Jubilee
- Host the Children’s Christmas Party at Midlane Country Club located at 4555 W Yourkhouse Road in Wadsworth, IL.
