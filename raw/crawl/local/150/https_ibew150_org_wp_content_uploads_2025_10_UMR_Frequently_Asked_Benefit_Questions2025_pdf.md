# <u>Frequently Asked Benefit Questions</u>

## How can I see my benefits online?

There are now 2 websites for viewing benefits.

1. At <u>www.UMR.com</u> you can view your Health Insurance benefits including filing a claim, viewing EOB’s, checking claims history, and accessing your WRA account. The included flyer provides instructions for registering and accessing your new UMR Health Insurance account.
2. The UMR Member Portal can be accessed from Local 150 website Benefits page at <u>[https://ibew150.org/benefits/](https://ibew150.org/benefits/)</u> and clicking on the UMR Benefits Portal button.
A Portal Guide can be found directly below the button to assist you in accessing your account.

On the Benefits Portal, you can access and track your Work History and view hours worked, gross wages earned, and contributions to Pension, Annuity, Vacation, HW, and WRA.

*COMING SOON* You can also utilize the Pension Balance Tool to be able to view pension checks, view your pension balance, and estimate your future pension benefit.

## What number should I call for questions on my benefits?

There are 2 phone numbers for benefits questions.

1. For Health Insurance Claims Administration contact 1-800-826-9781. This contact number is for all services including details about medical, dental, and vision coverage – checking the status of a claim or resolving issues with a denied or delayed claim – confirm payment of deductibles, copays, or coinsurance – or assistance with navigating the plans website or online benefits portal
2. For Fund Administration contact 1-888-999-7741. This contact number is for all services and questions regarding Pension, Supplemental Pension, Vacation, and Eligibility and Enrollment for benefits – updating personal information (address, phone number, dependent & beneficiary information, etc..) – verifying employer contributions – and self-pay questions.

## Who do I contact for prescription drug questions?

For all questions regarding prescription drug coverage, you need to contact our Pharmacy Benefit Manager, Sav-Rx, at 1-866-233-4239. You can also access your prescription drug history online by registering at <u>www.savrx.com</u>.

***Who should I call with questions as a Medicare eligible retiree on the Local 150 MAPD Plan?*** For Medicare retirees on the Local 150 Medicare Advantage w/ Prescription Drug (MAPD) PPO Plan through Humana, all calls should made to the dedicated team at Retiree First at 847-348-0222.
