# IBEW Local Union 150

We have been proudly serving Lake County for over 100 years.

Since 1914 we have grown to 1,200 members which include:

- Inside Wiremen
- Residential Wiremen
- Telecommunication (VDV)

[Referral](https://jobs.ibew150.org:4443/scripts/jobref.dll/)

- Signmen
- Lightning Rod Installers
