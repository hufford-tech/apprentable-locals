### Start with TEAM!

Please take care of yourself and watch out for others. If you or someone you know needs support, there are resources available. IBEW Local 292 has partnered with [TEAM](https://www.startwithteam.com/), an Employee Assistance Program, as our EAP Provider.  This can be your first step for support.

### _TEAM for our Employee Assistance Program_

**Need to reach TEAM? You can speak to a** [**TEAM**](https://www.startwithteam.com/) **counselor by calling TEAM’s toll-free number at** [**1-800-634-7710**](tel:18006347710) **or one of their local numbers at** [**651-642-0182**](tel:6516420182) **&** [**218-727-8589**](tel:2187278589) **day or night.**

If you have further questions about TEAM or any other Health Care Benefits. Contact the IBEW 292 Benefits office.  [https://www.ibew292benefits.org/](https://www.ibew292benefits.org/)  763-493-8830

# **Electrical license reciprocity**

## [Contact the Minnesota Department of Labor and Industry to get the latest information and most accurate.](https://www.dli.mn.gov/workers/electrician-or-electrical-installer/electrical-license-reciprocity)

### Class A journeyworker electrician license

[MN reciprocity information](https://www.dli.mn.gov/workers/electrician-or-electrical-installer/electrical-license-reciprocity)

Applicants may obtain a Minnesota journeyworker electrician license without examination if they obtained their journeyworker electrician license by examination in any of the following states:

- Alaska
- Arkansas
- Colorado
- Iowa
- Montana
- Nebraska
- North Dakota
- South Dakota
- Wyoming

* * *

# **ATTN: TRAVELERS:**

If you are traveling from another state and desire to perform electric work, but don’t have a MN Class JW License.

you will need to register with the MN Department of Labor as a; [Registered Unlicensed Individual](https://www.dli.mn.gov/workers/electrician-or-electrical-installer/electrical-licensing-forms)

If a call requires a MN Class A JW License and you do not have one, you are not eligble to take the call.
