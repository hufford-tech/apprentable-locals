# Labor First NOW RetireeFirst

Dear Medicare Eligible Retiree and/or Dependent,We have exciting news to share! Labor First is transitioning our name to RetireeFirst! This will only be a name change. The benefits and service to which you are accustomed will STAY THE SAME!

Same Leadership

Same Dedicated Advocacy Team

Same Outstanding Level of Service

IMPORTANT: You may notice RetireeFirst on future communication materials and on our introductory message you hear when you call into us.This has been several years in the making. Our new name, RetireeFirst, demonstrates our mission and commitment to putting YOU, the retiree, first. If you have any questions, please reach out to your dedicated Advocate team at: (612) 800-0972 (TTY 711)(844) 722-6401 (TTY 711) toll-free.

# **NEBF/NEAP Online Benefits Portal**

NEBF Benefit Recipients are able to **print a pension income verification letter** and NEAP participants will be able to **access their current NEAP account balance** in just minutes.

Our **Online Benefits** portal is accessible to participants through both the NEBF website [www.nebf.com](https://onlinebenefits.nebf.com/) and the NEAP website [www.neap.org](https://onlinebenefits.nebf.com/) by clicking on “ **Online Benefits**.”

[NEBF-OnlineBenefitsUpdate](https://www.ibew292.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/NEBF-OnlineBenefitsUpdate.pdf)

[NEAP-OnlineBenefitsUpdate](https://www.ibew292.org/wp-content/uploads/2018/07/NEAP-OnlineBenefitsUpdate.pdf)

#### ESTIMATE YOUR BENEFITS

Thinking about retirement? Easily generate your NEBF lifetime benefit estimate, including years of service. go to [www.nebf.com/nebf](http://www.nebf.com/nebf) and click on “Online Benefits” to register. It’s that easy. No calling, no waiting.

#### CHANGE YOUR ADDRESS

Moved? You don’t have to write in with your change of address! Simply enter your new adress after securely logging into your account. You’ll receive an email letting you know the change has been made and will take effect within 24-48 hours.

#### NEBF PENSION

NEBF has notified Local 292 that as of January 1, 2025, IBEW retirees collecting the NEBF Pension may work for a maximum of 600 hours during calendar year 2025 without a monthly hour restriction.  Questions may be directed to Local 292 business office (612) 379-1292.
