### **PURPOSE**

The Brotherhood Committee exists to assist the members in need or distress.

### FOOD SHELF

Local Union 292 members contribute to a Local Union 292 “Members Only” food shelf.  This fund can be accessed on a limited basis by a written or verbal request to the Brotherhood Committee.

Thank you for your continued prayers and support for Sister Melissa Bray, Brother Nick Bray, and their family during this incredibly difficult time. It is with deep sadness that we share that Jackie passed away on August 1, 2026.

**Honor Walk Planned for Tuesday, August 4 at Noon**

Jackie will leave a lasting legacy through the precious gift of organ donation, helping to save the lives of other children. An Honor Walk will take place on Tuesday, August 4, at 12:00 p.m. at Children’s Minnesota Hospital in Minneapolis. Family, friends, and members of our IBEW community are welcome to attend and show their support for the Bray family as they honor Jackie’s remarkable gift.

Please continue to keep Melissa, Nick, and their family in your thoughts and prayers.
