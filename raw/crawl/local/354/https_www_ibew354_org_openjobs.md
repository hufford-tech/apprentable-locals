### Available Jobs

These are the jobs local 354 currently have available. If you are a member of the Local 354 you can bid on jobs online from 7:00 PM-8:30 AM. Here are the instructions:

1. **Login to the website using your Last Name and Member Card Number**
2. **Navigate to the "Bid on Jobs" tab**
3. **Click on the job**
4. **If there is more than one job please list your prioritization.**
5. **Provide your contact number.**
6. **Submit**

You may also call the dispatcher between 8:00 AM and  8:30 AM at (801) 972-9354. Leave a detailed message, including your name, classification, card number, phone number, and the job you are interested in. The Referral Agent will begin making call backs after 8:45 AM

\*\*All call-backs will be made in the order of which you appear on the Out of Work Books.\*\*

You must be on the Out of Work Books in order to receive a call back. If you are not on the Out of Work Books, you will not receive a call back!

**For Friday August 7th, there were 5 regular calls filled that went into book 1 at 99 thru 111, 0 Communication calls, 0 C E, 0 C W and 0 short calls filled. There are currently 110 on Book 1, 6 on Book 2 and 11 on Communication book.**

Contractors will require the following at orientation:

- Utah State License
- Two forms of identification
- Bank routing information for payroll

**The available jobs list is updated daily at 5:30pm.**
