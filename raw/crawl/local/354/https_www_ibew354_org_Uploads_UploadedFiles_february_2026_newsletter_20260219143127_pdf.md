## IBEW LOCAL 354

## February 2026 Newsletter

## Business Managers Message

## Hello Brothers and Sisters,

As we move through the final months of winter, our work outlook remains strong and putting our members back to work continues to be our top priority.

We are seeing consistent weekly calls on the QTS Data Center project, with Cache Valley Electric, Cupertino Electric, and Wasatch Electric each projecting manpower in the hundreds. Additional large-scale projects are moving forward near Holden and Delta, along with expansion at Meta, a geothermal plant near Milford, hospital and data center work in the Salt Lake Valley, and a nuclear manufacturing and generation facility in Brigham City. On the Communications side, we have over 100 unfilled calls for NTI on a major Salt Lake Valley project.

On the legislative front, our Construction Wage Standard Act (HB 245) has passed committee and is now headed to the House floor — the first wage bill to advance this far in 30 years. This is a direct result of members standing together and making their voices heard. Now we need to finish the job. Please contact your Representative and urge them to support HB 245, carried by Tyler Clancy. If you need assistance or talking points, reach out to Codey Lindsay at the Hall.

In Solidarity,

Steve Woodman
