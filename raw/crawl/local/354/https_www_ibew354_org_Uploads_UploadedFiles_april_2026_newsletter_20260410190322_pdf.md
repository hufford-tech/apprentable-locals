## IBEW LOCAL 354

## April 2026 Newsletter

## Business Managers Message

Hello Brothers and Sisters,

It has been a busy month, with calls increasing significantly as contractors request manpower daily across the Wasatch Front. Dispatch remains in a walk-through situation, with many calls going unfilled each day.

Major projects continue with Wasatch, Cache Valley, Cupertino, and Skyline, while the QTS project is preparing to begin additional buildings, with long-term plans for extensive expansion. Other upcoming work includes projects at the National Security Agency, Dugway, and the Intermountain Power Project near Delta, where MJ Electric anticipates needing up to 150 wiremen at peak.

Looking ahead, the next phase of the Facebook project is expected to begin in early 2027. Additional work in the Salt Lake Valley includes the Rocky Mountain headquarters building, water treatment facilities, and other developments. Future bids include a nuclear facility in Brigham City, medical facilities, downtown renovations, and sports developments.

Membership currently stands at 3,193 and continues to grow. The Local is actively organizing to help meet demand and fill calls. Thank you to all members for your continued efforts in reaching out to non-union workers and promoting the benefits of our collective bargaining agreement. Please continue these efforts responsibly and with good judgment.

In Solidarity,
Steve Woodman
