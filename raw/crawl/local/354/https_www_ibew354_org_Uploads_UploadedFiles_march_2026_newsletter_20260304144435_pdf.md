# IBEW LOCAL 354

##### March 2026 Newsletter

#### Business Managers Message

**Hello Brothers and Sisters,** **I hope you’ve had a chance to recover from the busy holiday season. Our work picture** **continues to improve, although the QTS project has been slow to ramp up due to** **construction delays. Several contractors are currently on site at the QTS Data Center,** **including Cupertino, Cache Valley, Wasatch, Skyline, and others. We’re also seeing** **strong bidding activity across the jurisdiction, with several contractors landing sizable projects in the Salt Lake area.**

**As we near the end of the 2026 legislative session, it has been encouraging to see** **fewer direct attacks on organized labor. For the first time in many years, we were able** **to move a pro-labor bill (HB 245) out of committee and onto the House floor. While** **the bill did not ultimately pass, it received bipartisan support, including ten** **Republicans and all Democrats voting in favor. Thank you to everyone who contacted their legislators in support of this effort.**

**In Solidarity,** **Steve Woodman**
