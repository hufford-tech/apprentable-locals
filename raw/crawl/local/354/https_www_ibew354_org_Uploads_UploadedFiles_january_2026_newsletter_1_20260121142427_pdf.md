# IBEW LOCAL 354

### January 2026 Newsletter

## Business Managers Message

Hello Brothers and Sisters,

Happy New Year. I hope everyone had a great holiday season. As we begin the new year, our overall work outlook continues to improve, with several projects getting underway. However, inside calls have been slower than we would like, and many members are still out of work. I understand the frustration this causes. While job delays are beyond our control, I want to assure you that work is coming, and I appreciate your continued patience and trust.

Significant projects are moving forward across our jurisdiction. The QTS data center site includes multiple buildings and contractors and is expected to put several hundred members to work. Additional data center projects are planned or underway throughout the valley, including NOOVA, eBay, Oracle, and a new phase at Facebook. Other upcoming work includes a new cancer treatment center and trauma hospital in Salt Lake County, a modular nuclear power generation and manufacturing facility near Brigham City, new data centers near Fillmore and Delta, and multiple solar and geothermal power generation projects. These represent just some of the many projects planned in the coming years.

On January 14th, we held a specially called 8th District Trust meeting to discuss a potential merger with the FMCP national health plan. The trustees postponed the vote to review additional information. The membership will be informed as updates become available.

On the political front, the 2026 legislative session begins this week. We are taking an offensive approach in support of the Construction Wage Standard Act, which would restore state prevailing wage (Little Davis-Bacon) on major construction projects funded by the State of Utah. Representative Tyler Clancy is the House sponsor, and the bill is gaining bipartisan support. I encourage every member to contact their State Representative and Senator to urge support for this important legislation that benefits all working families. If you have questions or need assistance contacting your legislators, please call the Hall.

In Solidarity,

Steve Woodman Business Manager

# We Need You on the VOTE Committee

Brothers and Sisters,

##### Our VOTE Committee needs better participation — and we need you.

We are nonpartisan. We don’t ask you to join a political party, switch parties, or set aside your personal beliefs. What we are asking is simple: Protect worker rights Support pro-labor legislation Help safeguard the future of our union Your voice matters. What we win at the bargaining table can be taken away at the ballot box.

### Here’s what we need from you:

Register to vote — Your vote is your power. Download the IBEW Government Affairs app — See exactly what the IBEW is working on and why it matters. Help elect politicians who actually support labor — We don’t care about party labels; we care about who stands with working families. Join our VOTE Committee, whether in person or online — every member strengthens our voice.

##### Why this matters right now

This year, we’re pushing critical prevailing wage legislation. Utah lost its state prevailing wage in 1981 — and we’ve felt that impact ever since. It’s a big reason we don’t compete as well on state projects today. This legislation is fair, practical, and beneficial to contractors, workers, and communities. It keeps jobs local and levels the playing field for honest contractors. It should be supported on both sides of the aisle.

##### Let’s talk about it

We need more labor conversations on the job — at break, at lunch, wherever we gather. The more our members understand what’s at stake, the stronger we are.

### We are IBEW Local 354, and we fight for working families — past, present, and future.

If you have questions or want to get involved, please reach out to me anytime.

-Codey Lindsay Assistant Business Manager

# Attention Veterans and Current Reserve Members of Local 354

Local 354’s Veterans Committee (VETCOM) meets on the first Wednesday prior to each regular meeting, both in person and via Zoom. The Zoom link is available on the website and in the app.

The International Office has launched an initiative to recognize our service and transition into brotherhood within the IBEW. Veterans can receive a unique IBEW Veteran coin by using the provided QR code—please scroll past the “Join the Union” button.

Per an International Office letter dated November 19, 2025, veterans are also asked to report their status so it can be tracked in LPX (our dispatch database). This information supports planning for an IBEW Veterans Conference.

As VETCOM Chair, I ask that all veterans and reservists contact Dispatcher Mike Roberts to report your status.

In brotherhood and solidarity, -D. Scott Mackowiak
