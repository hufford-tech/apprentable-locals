## Business Managers Message

Hello Brothers and Sisters,

I hope everyone is enjoying the summer season and celebrating our nation's 250th birthday. We recently celebrated our members' years of service to the IBEW with our annual Local 354 Pin Party. The turnout was great, and it was wonderful to honor our members' dedication and commitment to our great union. I really enjoyed seeing and visiting with so many longtime friends. Congratulations to everyone who received a service pin this year, and thank you for your dedication to the IBEW. I also want to thank everyone who helped make the event a success.

Our work picture continues to be strong, and we still have unfilled calls every day. Upcoming projects include three battery storage additions to existing solar fields near Cedar City and Milford, Utah, for Rosendin Electric. This work is now projected to begin in September. We also have an exciting project in Delta, building the new converter building at the Intermountain Power Project for MJ Electric. This project is expected to begin ramping up this fall and could require as many as 150 wiremen at its peak. The next phase at Facebook is also moving forward and is expected to require additional manpower later this year and into next year. In addition, Cupertino Electric is bringing a new modular division to Salt Lake City. This project is expected to require approximately 80 to 100 wiremen and should begin ramping up in August. The new facility, located near the Salt Lake City International Airport, will consist of building modular power skids inside a climate-controlled building. Please help us properly staff these important projects.

Our membership continues to grow and now exceeds 3,700 active members. Organizing remains our number one objective. We continue to focus our efforts on organizing unrepresented journeymen and apprentices who are currently licensed in the State of Utah and have experience working in the trade under that license.

On another note, I am happy to report that the Eighth District Pension is now on track to reach 100% funded status, possibly as early as 2027, due to increased work hours throughout the Eighth District and strong investment returns, barring any major downturn in the industry or economy. Pension plans are now allowed to be overfunded, which means benefit improvements may be considered, provided those improvements do not cause the plan to become underfunded. The Eighth District Pension Trustees will be carefully evaluating different strategies in the coming months to develop a plan for future benefit improvements when they become allowable.

In Solidarity,

Steve Woodman
