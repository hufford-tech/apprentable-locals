# JAB FAIR DRAWS RECARD ATTENDANCE!

**OUR MAY 28 JOB FAIR AT THE UTAH ELECTRICAL TRAINING ALLIANCE WAS A**
**TREMENDOUS SUCCESS, ATTRACTING OVER 275 ATTENDEES—MORE THAN**
**DOUBLE THE HIGHEST ATTENDANCE AT ANY PREVIOUS JOB FAIR!**

**THE EVENT HIGHLIGHTED THE POWER OF PERSONAL CONNECTIONS, WITH 198**
**ATTENDEES REPORTING THEY HEARD ABOUT THE EVENT THROUGH FRIENDS**
**AND FAMILY. ANOTHER 62 PARTICIPANTS LEARNED ABOUT THE JOB FAIR**
**THROUGH SOCIAL MEDIA. EIGHT SIGNATORY CONTRACTORS ATTENDED THE**
**EVENT, MEETING WITH JOB SEEKERS AND DISCUSSING CAREER**
**OPPORTUNITIES WITHIN OUR INDUSTRY.**

**THIS SUCCESSFUL EVENT WOULD NOT HAVE BEEN POSSIBLE WITHOUT THE**
**HARD WORK AND DEDICATION OF OUR ORGANIZERS: TONY SORRO, WILLIAM**
**ROYCE, REX JOHNSON, AND GEORGE HALLIDAY. WE ALSO EXTEND OUR**
**SINCERE APPRECIATION TO OUR VOLUNTEER ORGANIZING COMMITTEE**
**MEMBERS: ALYSSA DAHL, ANNA VERMOUTH, ROY COLLINS, TROY KOSTELECKY,**
**TYSON SYME, AND JOE DEHERRERA. THEIR EFFORTS WERE INSTRUMENTAL IN**
**MAKING THIS OUR MOST SUCCESSFUL JOB FAIR TO DATE.**

**THANK YOU TO EVERYONE WHO PARTICIPATED AND HELPED MAKE THIS EVENT**
**A RECORD-BREAKING SUCCESS.**
