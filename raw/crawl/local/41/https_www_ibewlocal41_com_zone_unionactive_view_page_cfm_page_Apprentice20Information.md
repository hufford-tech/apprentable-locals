| Apprentice Information

Application Information

|     |
| --- |
| Applications are able to be picked up every Wednesday (except holidays) from 1pm-5pm.

**QUALIFICATIONS**

• Must be at least 18 years old

• Have a high school diploma or G.E.D.

• Successful completion of one full year of high school or post high school algebra

• Live within the jurisdiction of the Local Union (all of Erie County, parts of Genesee, Wyoming and Cattaraugus counties)

• Successfully pass NJATC Aptitude Test

• Upon selection for apprenticeship, a pre-employment drug test is required.

**WORKING CONDITIONS**

The electrical construction trade requires work inside and outside in a variety of conditions, that is in all weather conditions.  Also, in a variety of locations:  manholes, roofs, inside buildings and in trenches, to name a few.

**APPRENTICESHIP TRAINING PERIOD**

Inside wireman apprenticeship is a 5-year apprenticeship as follows:  Classroom related training at a minimum of 208 hours for 5 years and a total of 8,200 hours of on-the-job training (OJT).

**RELATED INSTRUCTION AND METHOD**

One to two nights per week from 5:30 p.m. to 9:30 p.m. with additional nights or weekends as required for OSHA and other training.  Classes commence in September and continue until the end of May

**PROBATIONARY PERIOD**

2,000 Hours

**TOOLS**

Hand tools, such as side cutters, diagonal cutters, tape measure, tool pouch, belt, etc.  Total cost is approximately $249.00 and is the apprentice's responsibility.

**CURRENT JOURNEYWORKER RATE**

$47.50 per hour

**APPRENTICE WAGE PROGRESSION**

• 1st Period - 1,000 hrs. OJT + school                      50% of Journeyworker Rate -  $23.75/hr.

• 2nd Period - 1,000 - 2,000 hrs. OJT   + school       60% of Journeyworker Rate -  $28.50/hr.

• 3rd Period - 2,000 - 3,500 hrs. OJT   + school       65% of Journeyworker Rate -  $30.90/hr.

• 4th Period - 3,500 - 5,000 hrs. OJT   + school       70% of Journeyworker Rate -  $33.25/hr.

• 5th Period - 5,000 - 6,500 hrs. OJT   + school       75% of Journeyworker Rate -  $35.65/hr.

• 6th Period - 6,500 - 8,200 hrs. OJT   + school       85% of Journeyworker Rate -  $40.40/hr.

**ESTIMATED JOURNEYWORKER ANNUAL EARNINGS**

$80,100\*

\*Estimates are based on 1,800 hours worked per year, which may vary from year to year.

**FRINGE BENEFITS - EMPLOYER PAID**

Annuity, Health, Pension, E. I. E. F. and N. E. B. F.

**UNION DUES**

• Initiation fee - $262.00

• Quarterly Dues $153.60
