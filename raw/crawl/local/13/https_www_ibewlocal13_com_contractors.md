#### We provide highly trained electricians for signatory contractors.

Hire from our list of capable members in the Burlington and Ottumwa Area

Electrical work is a highly-skilled job that takes an industry professional to complete properly and efficiently.  If you need to find a qualified electrician to handle your next project, contact the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 13 today. You will have the opportunity to hire from our trusted list of members who are experienced in the industry.

Receive top quality work from our signatory electrical contractors

When you hire one of our signatory electrical contractors, you are guaranteed to get a quality electrician.  We pride ourselves on having members who are experienced and knowledgeable in the electrical industry and have the skills to complete a wide range of electrical jobs.  You can trust our high standards the next time you need to hire an industry professional in Burlington, Ottumwa and the surrounding area.  Call us today to find a contractor near you, or choose one from our list of quality contractors.
