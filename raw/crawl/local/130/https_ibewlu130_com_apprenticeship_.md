Apprenticeship 504-835-9899

## APPLICATIONS FOR THE 2026-2027 SCHOOL YEAR ARE NOW CLOSED.  Please contact the office in January 2027 for information   regarding the 2027-2028 school year.

Start your electrical career with the most respected training program in Southeast Louisiana. The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers and National Electrical Contractors Association proudly sponsor the largest, most comprehensive electrical Journeyman & Apprentice training programs available in this country. Through the National Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee for the Electrical Industry, these Industry Partners have properly trained hundreds of thousands of men and women to become competent Journeymen. With world-class instruction, hands-on training, excellent benefits, and a clear path to becoming a Journeyman, this program gives you the skills, support, and future you need to build a successful career in the electrical industry.

Contact the Apprenticeship Office at [504-835-9899](tel:5048359899)

If you are interested in signing up for our monthly Safety Classes, please click the sign up the button below.

[ACTIVE MEMBERS ONLY!!! Sign up for safety classes](https://ibewlu130.com/safety-classes/)

## Benefits of Apprenticeship

- **Excellent wages while you learn**
- **Paid health insurance and comprehensive benefits**
- **State-of-the-art training**
- **Safer working conditions**
- **Real career advancement opportunities**
- **Pension and retirement benefits**

## Application Requirements

- **Valid Driver’s License (State ID or other forms of ID not accepted)**
- **Social Security Card**
- **Birth Certificate (must be in English or certified English translation)**
- **High School Diploma, GED, or higher (Associates degree accepted; must be in English or certified translation)**
- **DD-214 form (for military veterans)**
- **$25 Money Order for application fee (cash not accepted)**

## Application Process

Once you’ve submitted your complete application with all required documents, you’ll receive a letter within a few weeks with your Aptitude Test date. The test is offered every 5–6 weeks, and a passing score of 3 or higher moves you one step closer to joining the IBEW Local 130 Apprenticeship Program.

Stay on top of your application. If you don’t receive your letter within 3–4 weeks, contact the Apprenticeship Office at [(504) 835-9899](tel:(504) 835-9899).

Want to get ahead of the competition? Electric Prep has partnered with the NJATC to offer online courses designed specifically to help apprenticeship applicants succeed. These courses provide a better understanding of the electrical construction industry and the requirements, sharpen your math and reading skills, and prepare you for the interview. Each course costs $49.95 and is a smart way to stand out and start your career strong.

## Start Your Electrical Career

Don’t wait to build a future you can be proud of. By joining the IBEW Local 130 Apprenticeship Program, you’ll gain hands-on training, excellent wages, full benefits, and a clear path to becoming a Journeyman electrician. This a career with opportunity, security, and growth.

Take the first step now: complete your application, prepare with Electric Prep, and get ready to join the ranks of skilled, respected electricians shaping the future of New Orleans.

Contact the Apprenticeship Office today at [(504) 835-9899](tel:(504) 835-9899) or email David Crumb at [drc@noejatc.org](mailto:drc@noejatc.org)

Your future starts here.
