Retiree Club

## Welcome to the Local 130 Retirees’ Club

Are you retired, or getting ready to retire? Stay connected with your fellow electricians by joining the IBEW Local 130 Retirees’ Club! Our club is the perfect way to keep in touch with colleagues, enjoy fun gatherings, and relax in a welcoming atmosphere. We host quarterly meetings at the Union Hall with food, drinks, and special events like our seasonal crawfish boil. Annual dues are $35 for you and one guest.

To join, mail your dues to Retirees’ Club Local 130, P.O. Box 7553, Metairie, LA 70010-7553, or pay in person at a meeting.

To join, contact Sandy Theriot at [504-339-6855](tel:504-339-6855).
