# Retiree’s Summer Party

July 28, 2026

Dear Brothers, Sisters, Retirees, and Associates,

As summer winds down and fall approaches, we warmly invite you to join us for our annual End-of-Summer Bash. Please mark your calendar and enjoy an afternoon of fellowship, refreshments, and good company. We look forward to spending this special time together and hope to see you there.

**WHEN**

Thursday, August 13, 2026

**WHERE:**

Alexander Room, 3rd Floor

3200 Ridgelake Drive

Metairie, LA 70002

**TIME:**

11:00 a.m. to 3:00 p.m.

**Refreshments will be provided.**

Cordially,

Sandy Theriot

President, IBEW Local 130 Retiree’s Club
