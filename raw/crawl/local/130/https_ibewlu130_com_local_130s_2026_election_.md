## Local 130 2026 Election

Local 130 will be holding their 2026 election on Saturday, June 6, 2026 from the hours of 10:00 am to 6:00 pm in the Alexander Room located at 3200 Ridgelake Dr., Metairie, LA 70002. If a run-off election is required, it will be held on Saturday, June 27, 2026 from the hours of 10:00 am to 6:00 pm in the Alexander Room located at 3200 Ridgelake Dr., Metairie, LA 70002.

If you are unable to vote in person, please mail an absentee ballot request with your name and address to the following PO Box:

**LOCAL UNION 130 ELECTION JUDGE**, **PO BOX 6166, METAIRIE, LA 70009**
