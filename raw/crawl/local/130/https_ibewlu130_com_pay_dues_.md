### Instructions to Pay Dues

You can now pay your dues online through our website or the APP store!!! Not only can you pay your dues, but you can track your working assessments paid through date, update your contact information and so much more. To sign-up for online dues payments, please follow the instructions below.

If you have any questions, please contact the Dues Department, [504-831-1372](tel:5048311372).

1\. [Follow this link to pay your dues](https://ibew130.workingsystems.com/)

2\. On this login page you will Set up your username and password with the instructions below:

- Your username is your Card Number.
- Your initial password is your last name and the last four digits of your

SSN. Your initial password is not case sensitive.
- You will be required to change your password on your first login.

![](https://ibewlu130.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/payduesinstructions3-651x1024-1.webp)

3.  Once you have logged in with your new password, you will be brought to

“current information.” On this page, you will see all of your contact information,

phone number, address, email address, and your member information.

![](https://ibewlu130.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/payduesinstructions5-541x1024-1.webp)

4\. To access the main menu, click on the three lines at the top left of your screen.

![](https://ibewlu130.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/payduesinstructions6-768x566-1.webp)

5\. From here, you will be able to pay your dues, see your transactions, update your contact information, and more.

### You will need to set up a username and password using steps 1 & 2 BEFORE you download the app

Once you have your username and password set up download the app and login to have access to all of your member information at your fingertips!!

[![](https://ibewlu130.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/androidapp-300x300.png)\\
\\
Download the app for Android](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.workingsystems.apps.lpmobile&pcam) [![](https://ibewlu130.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/apple-logo-icon-14895-2048x2048-1-300x300.png)\\
\\
Download the app for Apple IOS](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/laborpower/id1612891005?ign-itscg=30200&ign-itsct=apps_box_badge)

Once you have downloaded the app, you will see the following:

[![](https://ibewlu130.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/payduesappinstructins.webp)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.workingsystems.apps.lpmobile&pcam)

Click on get started.

You will then be asked to “Enable permissions”. Be sure to click “Allow LaborPower to send you notifications” so you don’t miss out on important information and updates the local will be sent out via text messages.

[![](https://ibewlu130.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/payduesappinstructins2-1024x1004.webp)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.workingsystems.apps.lpmobile&pcam)

You will then select your union as IBEW, then in the search bar type out 130.

[![](https://ibewlu130.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/payduesappinstructins3-913x1024.webp)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.workingsystems.apps.lpmobile&pcam)

From there you will be taken to the “Login Page” where you will sign in with the username and password you previously set up.

[![](https://ibewlu130.com/wp-content/uploads/2026/01/payduesappinstructins4-469x1024.webp)](https://play.google.com/store/apps/details?id=com.workingsystems.apps.lpmobile&pcam)
