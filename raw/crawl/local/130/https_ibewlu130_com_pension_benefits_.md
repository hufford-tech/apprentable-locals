Pension Benefits

### As a IBEW LOCAL 130 member, you will receive four (4) Pensions upon retirement

\*Federal law mandates five years for vesting

## IBEW (International) Pension Benefit Fund

### Paid for by your monthly union dues

At retirement, you will receive $5.50 per year of service per month.

For example, if you pay your monthly dues every month for 40 years, you would receive $220.00 per month.

$5.50 x 40 years = $220.00 per month

_\*Please contact Stacey at [504-831-1372](tel:5048311372) for more information regarding the IBEW (International) Pension Benefit Fund_

## NEBF (National Electrical Benefit Fund)

### Paid for by the contractor

At retirement you will receive $33.00 per year of service per month. For example, if you are an active member for 40 years, you would receive $1,320.00 per month.

$33.00 x 40 years = $1,320.00 per month

Create an account [here](https://onlinebenefits2.nebf.com/Home/Login)to view the following:

- Generate your NEBF benefit estimate
- Change your address
- Access your current NEAP account balance
- Print an NEBF Income verification letter

- View plan documents
- Opt in to receive plan communications electronically
- Download an NEBF Pension Benefit application

[Create NEBF Account](https://onlinebenefits2.nebf.com/Home/Login)

_\*Please contact Stacey at [504-831-1372](tel:5048311372) for more information regarding the NEBF (National Electrical Benefit Fund)_

## IBEW Local 130 Pension

### Paid for by the contractor

At retirement you will receive the following:

1,000 man hours earned/work hours = $45.00 per year of service per month

1,600 man hours earned/work hours = $72.00 per year of service per month

**The differential of hours between 1,000 and 1,600 is pro-rated according to the actual hours worked.**

For example; if you earned 1,600 man hours worked/work hours over 40 years of service you would receive $2,880.00 per month.

$72.00 x 40 years of service = $2,880.00 per month

_\*Please contact Kelley at [504-837-9812](tel:5048379812) for more information regarding the IBEW Local Pension_

## IBEW Local 130 Annuity (Investment Account)

### Paid for by the contractor

At retirement you will receive the full amount in your account. The current Annuity contributions are $6.50 per hour.

For example, if you are a full time employee working 2,080 hours per year x $6.50 per hour you would have $13,520.00.

$13,520.00 x 6% interest each year for 40 years = $2,230,206.59
