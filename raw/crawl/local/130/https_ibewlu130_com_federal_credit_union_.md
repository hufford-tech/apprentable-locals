## TOP 5 REASONS MEMBERS LOVE SAVING WITH US

**WE PAY YOUR DUES, EVERY MONTH, ON TIME**

_One less thing to remember!_

**SAVINGS GROW QUICKER**

_We don’t issue ATM cards nor is there online access.  Members appreciate that their savings are just a bit harder to get to, so they set up their payroll deduction or automatic bank transfer and watch their savings grow._

**BIG SAVINGS ON AUTO LOANS**

_As a benefit and courtesy to our members, we work to make sure we offer some of the lowest rates for new/used auto loans or refinances!_

**PERSONAL RELATIONSHIPS**

_Unlike traditional banks, we pride ourselves on establishing and preserving a relationship with all our members.  We’re genuinely interested and invested in helping you navigate your finances so that you make better decisions and are prepared for the unexpected._

**COMMITMENT TO THE BROTHERHOOD**

_Members would rather deposit into their credit union, as a commitment to the brotherhood, rather than benefit a large institution, so that we can help each other whenever there is a need.  We are a non-profit institution, unlike traditional banks.  We make decisions with the membership in mind, not the bottom line!_
