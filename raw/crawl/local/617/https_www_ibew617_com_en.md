# IBEW Local 617

![singlePostImage](https://www.ibew617.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmedia.linkedunion.com%2Fapps%2Fibew617%2Fnews%2F10-Secondary-Logo-450x4501.png&w=3840&q=75)

## Since its founding over 100 years ago, IBEW Local 617 has proudly served San Mateo County with highly skilled journeyman electricians dedicated to excellence, safety, and community. Our members install and maintain the electrical systems that power homes, businesses, and critical infrastructure—from lighting and power distribution to traffic signals and underground installations. Through rigorous apprenticeship training and an unwavering commitment to safety, our members deliver dependable, high-quality work on every project.

## Rooted in the values of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local 617 continues a legacy of craftsmanship, integrity, and solidarity. We're committed to building a brighter future for San Mateo County through quality workmanship, fair labor practices, and ongoing training that keeps our workforce at the forefront of the electrical industry.

# About Apprenticeship

### **EARN WHILE YOU LEARN. BUILD A CAREER THAT LASTS.**

## **Are you ready to power your future? IBEW Local 617's apprenticeship program offers a clear path to a rewarding career as a journeyman electrician. Our apprentices work hands-on with experienced professionals, installing and maintaining electrical systems in residential, commercial, and industrial settings throughout San Mateo County—from building power distribution and lighting to traffic signals and underground installations. This is skilled trade work that combines technical expertise with problem-solving, and no two days are the same.**

## **Our five-year program combines 8,000 hours of paid on-the-job training with 900 hours of classroom instruction. You'll earn while you learn, starting at 35% of journeyman's wage with regular raises as you progress. As an employee and union member, you'll receive excellent medical and dental coverage, along with savings and pension plan benefits. We're investing in your success—building the next generation of highly skilled electricians who will power San Mateo County for decades to come.**
