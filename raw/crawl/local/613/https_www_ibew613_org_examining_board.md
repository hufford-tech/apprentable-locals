# IBEW Local 613 Examining Board

#### Helping To Select New Members

The job of the IBEW Local 613 Examining Board is to consider the application of all eligible applicants, who want to join Local 613, with the exception of those selected as apprentices under the terms of a collective bargaining agreement.

Examining Board members review all applications and decide, as a collective, if the applicant is a good fit for Local 613.

The Examining Board meets at least quarterly when there are applicants to be examined. The results of the examination are reported to the Executive Board and the Local Union.
