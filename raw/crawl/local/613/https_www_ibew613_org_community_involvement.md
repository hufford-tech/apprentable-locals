# Local 613 In The Community

#### Community Minded

IBEW Local 613 and its members are proud to be part of the communities in which we work.

Our membership has always and will continue to make a concerted effort to engage with and volunteer in the community.

We are delighted to promote that Local 613 and the dedicated members of our Local who live and work in our communities have given back so much. Below is a list of those we have volunteered with and donated to in the past year:
