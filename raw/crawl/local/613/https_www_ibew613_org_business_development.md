# See your market share expand with IBEW Local 613

#### Grow Your Business

Increase the amount of business your company conducts and gain access to the area’s top electrical workforce by becoming an International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 613 signatory contractor!

IBEW 613 wants to increase the market share of our signatory contractors - no matter the size and capability of their company - with access to a highly trained, highly skilled, on-demand workforce.

In addition to access to the region’s premier electrical workforce, becoming an IBEW Local 613 signatory contractor gives their existing workforce access to upgrade and safety training courses through the Electrical Training Center.

Our signatory contractors also gain membership to the Atlanta chapter of the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA), which comes with benefits such as conference invitations, workshop opportunities, marketing opportunities and other incentives.
[Learn more about the Atlanta Chapter of the National Electrical Contractors Association](https://atlantaelectrical.org/)

The benefits of becoming an IBEW Local 613 signatory contractor are plentiful.

Small businesses are given the tools needed to grow through the IBEW SEED Program, which helps them provide excellent fringe benefits and additional training for employees and business owners.

Medium and large contractors are afforded immediate access to the Local 613 workforce, who are expertly trained to complete projects on-time and on-budget. Their existing employees can attend training courses at the Electrical Training Center to improve upon their skillset and gain industry-leading OSHA safety training certifications.

IBEW Local 613 signatory contractors, who work within our 59 county jurisdiction in Georgia and Alabama, have greater market share and expanded opportunities because of access to the Local 613 workforce and training.
