Guiding Our Union and Protecting Our Members

## IBEW Local 401 Bylaws

The bylaws of IBEW Local 401 outline the rules, procedures, and standards that guide our operations and protect the rights of all members. They ensure transparency, accountability, and fairness within our union while upholding the values that have shaped us for generations.

We encourage all members to review the bylaws to better understand your rights and responsibilities within Local 401.

## Ready to Join Us?

Experienced Electricians: Contact our office to learn how you can become a union member. We’ll guide you every step of the way.

Apprenticeship Program: No college debt. Earn while you learn. Our apprenticeship program trains tomorrow’s journeypersons through hands-on experience and expert instruction. Visit our Training Center to learn more.
