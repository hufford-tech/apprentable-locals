## Committees at IBEW Local 401

Committees are a vital part of member engagement at IBEW Local 401. They bring members together around shared interests and goals, offering ways to get involved, give back, and strengthen the brotherhood and sisterhood of our union. Whether you’re looking to advocate for fellow members, support community outreach, or connect over shared activities, there’s a place for you here.

![](https://ibew401.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/IBEW-Stock-9.jpg)

### WOMEN’S Committee

The Women’s Committee fosters leadership, support, and community for women in the electrical trades. Open to all who want to build a more inclusive union, the committee creates space for mentorship, advocacy, and connection.

### RENEW Committee

RENEW (Reach out and Engage Next-gen Electrical Workers) encourages younger members of the IBEW to become more active in their union. The committee focuses on leadership development, union education, and promoting solidarity across generations.
