## Help Support Mark Ashbaugh

[News](https://ibew401.com/category/news/)\|

Mark unexpectedly experienced a heart attack while at work; he was transported from the mine site to the hospital in Winnemucca via ambulance and then later flown to Reno where he was made aware of the need for bi-pass heart surgery. He has been a JW for 30 years and before that served his country in the Army. He is a generous man who is accustomed to meeting other’s needs. Any help is greatly appreciated!
