## About IBEW Local 401

IBEW Local 401 is more than just a union — we are a community of skilled electrical workers united by our commitment to excellence, fairness, and solidarity. Since our founding, we’ve stood at the forefront of the electrical industry in Northern Nevada, advocating for better wages, benefits, working conditions, and a secure future for our members and their families.

We believe every electrician deserves dignity and respect on the job, along with opportunities to grow their skills, advance their careers, and retire with confidence. That’s why we fight for high standards across the board — not just for ourselves, but for future generations of workers.

At the heart of IBEW Local 401 is our commitment to:

- [**Training & Education**](https://ibew401.com/about-ibew/#tab-31e6f1dd26b91d28094)
- [**Safety**](https://ibew401.com/about-ibew/#tab-e01dd4a078b898cf307)
- [**Workplace Fairness**](https://ibew401.com/about-ibew/#tab-eff11b2ab8d1f630886)
- [**Innovation**](https://ibew401.com/about-ibew/#tab-bc7c4d06c9452ca9525)

- [**Training & Education**](https://ibew401.com/about-ibew/#tab-31e6f1dd26b91d28094)

We invest in comprehensive apprenticeship and journeyman training programs to ensure our members are the best in the trade.

- [**Safety**](https://ibew401.com/about-ibew/#tab-e01dd4a078b898cf307)

We advocate for and uphold safe working environments that protect our members and allow them to do their jobs with confidence.

- [**Workplace Fairness**](https://ibew401.com/about-ibew/#tab-eff11b2ab8d1f630886)

We stand for equality, fair treatment, and transparency in every workplace.

- [**Innovation**](https://ibew401.com/about-ibew/#tab-bc7c4d06c9452ca9525)

As technology evolves, we continue to lead by adapting and advancing our skills in new and emerging areas of electrical work.

Whether you’re an experienced electrician looking for better opportunities or someone just starting out, IBEW Local 401 is your gateway to a meaningful and rewarding career.
