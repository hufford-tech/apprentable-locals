Member Benefits

## Investing in Your Health, Future, and Peace of Mind

IBEW Local 401 is committed to supporting our members not just on the job—but throughout every stage of life. From health coverage to retirement savings, your union-negotiated benefits are designed to provide security and peace of mind for you and your family.

![](https://ibew401.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/health-benefits.png)

### Health & Welfare Benefits

Through our partnership with the Electrical Workers Health & Welfare Trust, members and their families have access to comprehensive:

- Medical and prescription drug coverage

- Vision and dental benefits

- Life insurance

- Accidental death & dismemberment protection


[Learn More](https://ewtrusts.com/)

### Pension & Retirement

Your hard work today helps build a stronger tomorrow. IBEW 401 members benefit from pension programs designed to support a stable and secure retirement.

[Learn More](https://ewtrusts.com/)

![](https://ibew401.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/pension.png)
