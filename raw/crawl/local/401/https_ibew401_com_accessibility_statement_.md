## Accessibility Statement

IBEW Local 401 is committed to ensuring that our website is accessible to all individuals, including those with disabilities. We believe that equal access to information is essential and are dedicated to making our digital presence as inclusive as possible. Our goal is to create an online experience that allows all users to access information easily, regardless of ability or technology.

We continuously evaluate and improve our website’s accessibility to align with best practices and industry standards, including the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG). Our team actively works to identify and remove barriers to access, ensuring that all visitors can navigate our site effectively.
