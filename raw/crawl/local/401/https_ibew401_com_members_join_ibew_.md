## Join IBEW Local 401

Joining IBEW Local 401 means more than just starting a job—it’s about building a stable, rewarding career with a brotherhood that has your back. We represent skilled electricians across Northern Nevada, offering union support, industry-leading wages, quality benefits, and ongoing training to help you grow in your trade.
