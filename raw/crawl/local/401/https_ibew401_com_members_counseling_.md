## Your are not alone. help is here.

For assistance during a mental health crisis 24 hours a day, 7 days a week, **call or text the Suicide & Crisis Lifeline at 988**

The statewide Nevada Warmline is a stigma-free, non-crisis phone line.

**Call or text [775-241-4212](tel:775-241-4212) for direct, one-on-one peer support.**

The Nevada Teen Peer Support Text Line is a non-crisis text line for Nevadans aged 24 and younger.

**Text 775-296-8336 for peer support.**

## 10 Common warning signs of a mental health condition

- Feeling very sad or withdrawn for more than two weeks.

- Extreme mood changes, including uncontrollable “highs” or feelings of euphoria.

- Intense worries or fears that get in the way of daily activities.

- Severe, out-of-control, risk-taking behavior that causes harm to self or to others.

- Sudden overwhelming fear for no reason, sometimes with a racing heart, physical discomfort, or difficulty breathing.


- Throwing up, using laxatives, or not eating to lose weight, having an intense fear of weight gain.

- Seeing, hearing, or believing things that are not real.

- Excessive use of alcohol or drugs.

- Extreme difficulty concentrating or staying still, leading to physical danger or failing at school.

- Drastic changes in mood, behavior, personality, or sleeping habits.


## NAMI Family Support Group

NAMI Family Support Group is a peer-led support group for any adult with a loved one who has experienced symptoms of a mental health condition. Gain insight from the challenges and successes of others facing similar experiences.
