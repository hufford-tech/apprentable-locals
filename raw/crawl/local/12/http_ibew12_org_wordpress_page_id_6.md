# Apprenticeship

|     |     |     |
| --- | --- | --- |
| ### Application procedures for:<br>### Inside Construction Electrician Apprenticeship<br>#### To apply call Dan Kraus: (719) 561-8000 ext. 18<br>**or go to [www.pueblojatc.org](http://www.pueblojatc.org/)**<br>P.E.J.A.T.C. Training Facility<br>2924 Graneros, Pueblo, Colorado 81005 |
| #### Application Date | January 1st through December 31st |  |
| #### Sponsor | Pueblo Joint Committee for Electrical Apprenticeship and Training |  |
| #### Where to Apply | Dan Kraus<br>2924 Graneros<br>Pueblo,CO 81005<br>(719) 561-8000 ext. 18 |  |
| #### Application Times | By appointment by calling<br>(719) 561-8000 ext 18 |  |
| #### Geographic Area | By Counties: Alamosa, Archuleta, Baca, Bent, Chaffee, Conejos, Costilla, Crowley, Custer, Fremont, Huerfano, Kiowa, Las Animas, Mineral, Otero, Prowers, Pueblo, Rio Grande, Saguache. |  |
| #### Age Requirements | Provide reliable documentation for proof of age<br>(minimum of 18 years of age) |  |
| #### Education | Provide documentation of being a high school graduate,<br>or having a GED, or having a two-year Associates Degree<br>or higher and show evidence of successful completion of<br>one full credit of high school algebra, or more advanced<br>math with a passing grade, or one post high school algebra<br>course (e.g. Adult Education, Continuing Education,<br>Community College, etc.) or more advanced math, with a<br>passing grade, or provide evidence of having successfully<br>completed the NJATC Math Course. |  |
| #### Aptitude | To qualify for an interview you must score “4”<br>or higher on the electrical trade’s aptitude test developed<br>and validated by the American Institutes<br>for Research. |  |
| #### License | Must possess a valid Colorado Driver’s License at<br>the time of interview. |  |
| #### Other Requirements | Applicants are required to submit:<br>(Copies of all but transcripts)A. Copy of High school Diploma or GED Equivalency<br>Certificate and all records.<br>B. Copy of birth certificate or other acceptable proof<br>of age.<br>C. Official transcript of high school and post high school<br>education and training (unopened).<br>D. Veteran’s discharge records (DD-214), if applicable<br>and you wish to receive consideration for training.<br>E. Copy of Valid Colorado State Drivers License.<br>F. Copy of Social Security Card.<br>G. Must pass a drug screen test at applicant’s expense,<br>if selected. |  |
| All applicants will be selected without regard to race, color, religion, sex or national origin.<br>All of the above information has been disseminated by the Pueblo Joint Committee for<br>Electrical Apprenticeship and Training. |

## About:

International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local #12 In Pueblo, Colorado has Labor Roots as far back as 1882 and was Chartered on February 24, 1900 to Further the plight of Average American Labor to find equal footing with the corporate capital world. Tradesman and Labor Workers have built this community and contributed to success of Southern Colorado and will strive to make this community better in the future.
