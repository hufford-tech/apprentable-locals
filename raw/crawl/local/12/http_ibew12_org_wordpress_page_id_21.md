# Dispatch

### **Re-Sign Procedures**

### First time sign-in is in person. (Dues receipt & Travel letter) If you roll off of the books, you will need to sign in again in person.

#### In January, April, July, and October – Either in person, by mail, on line, or by fax!

If you mail it – it must be postmarked between the 1st and the 16th.

If you fax it : (719)561-8000 – it must be received between the 1st and the 16th.

#### If you email it ![🙁](https://s.w.org/images/core/emoji/16.0.1/svg/1f641.svg)[local12@ibew12.org](mailto:local12@ibew12.org) ) – it must be received between the 1st and the 16th.

Your re-sign should include your printed name, last four numbers of your social security number, or your card number, and your signature.

To remove your name from the books, it must be in writing, and you can either fax it or email it to us!

**If the job line mail box is full, you may leave a message at ext. 13 or ext. 10**

**or you can fax your acceptance in.**

Sign-in is Monday – Friday (excluding holidays) 8:00 a.m. to 12:00 p.m and 1:00 p.m. to 5:00 p.m Dues Receipt and Travel Letter are expected, you may have your Local Union fax a travel letter to us

Dispatch is Monday – Friday (excluding holidays) at 8:30 – 11:30 am. & 1:30 – 4:30 p.m. only!

CDL – Commercial Drivers License Class A

COE – Code of Excellence

DL – Valid Drivers License

DT – Pre-hire Drug Test

SL – Colorado State Electrical License

SS – Social Security Card

JW – Journeyman Wireman High Scale $33.55

JL – Journeyman Lineman $58.53 Colo SW Agrmt

## About:

International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local #12 In Pueblo, Colorado has Labor Roots as far back as 1882 and was Chartered on February 24, 1900 to Further the plight of Average American Labor to find equal footing with the corporate capital world. Tradesman and Labor Workers have built this community and contributed to success of Southern Colorado and will strive to make this community better in the future.
