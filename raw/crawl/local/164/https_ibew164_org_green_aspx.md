## IBEW Local Union 164’s Solar Installations

Since 2009, the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local 164 has reduced its carbon ![](https://ibew164.org/ULWSiteResources/ibew164/Resources/image/Solar/164-Solar-1.jpg)footprint with the installation of state of the art solar panels on its two Paramus, N.J. commercial buildings.  The Local has installed 648 photovoltaic panels, one of the largest systems of its kind, on the rooftops of 205 Robin Rd., 61,000 – square foot, Class-A office building and 65 West Century Rd., a 40,000 – square foot educational facility.   Local 164’s commitment to renewable energy has been nationally recognized, providing a cutting edge training facility.

The skilled men and women of IBEW Local 164 can provide this same service to your office or home.  Contact us today to schedule a consultation with one of our contractors that's right for your project!

Founded in 1901, Local 164 remains dedicated
to the IBEW's original objective and programs
to make life better for its members and those
around them. Originally established with exclusive
jurisdiction in Hudson County, Local 164 was
expanded to include Bergen County in 1928 and
Essex County in June 2000.
