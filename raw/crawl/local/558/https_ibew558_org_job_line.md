# Job Line

### NOTE: On mobile devices, turn lengthwise to view.

We have a bid System……. Make job bids on the Unionworx APP/ Job Line (256)381-3718.

ALL CALLS REQUIRE OSHA 10, DRIVERS LICENSE, & SOCIAL SECURITY CARD. YOU WILL BE REQUIRED TO SHOW THESE AT DISPATCH. NO EXCEPTIONS!!

Brothers and Sisters,

Monday, August 10 2026

7 JWs for Day & Zimmerman at Browns Ferry Nuclear Plant in Athens, AL working in MODs for the upcoming Fall outage. Must pass TVA Back Ground and willing to work either shift. PHQ date is August 16 and CIP date is August 26. (CIP is in Hollywood AL)

\\*\\*\\* SHORT CALL\*\*\* 1 JW for DZ with the Southwest Hydro group on a 4-10 schedule for up to 2 weeks. Must be willing to travel and must pass TVA back ground. Pick up the referral after dispatch and hire in at the PSS on Thursday, August 13

\\*\\*\\*\\* All bids are to be made through the UNIONWORX APP only. Any questions about this APP or setting up accounts please contact Dispatch @ 256-383-2279 ext 180.

\\*\\*\\*\\* YOU WILL BE HIT WITH A DING IF YOU DON’T BID. WHETHER YOU BELIEVE YOU HAVE A NOT FOR REHIRE WITH A CONTRACTOR OR UNABLE TO WORK AT TVA YOU MUST BID. IF YOU ARE IN THE TOP 30 YOU WILL BE HIT WITH A DING AND AFTER YOUR 3RD DING YOU WILL ROLL TO THE BACK OF THE BOOK. READ MORE ON THE UNIONWORX APP.

– All Book 2 classifications needs to have their Home Local send a travel letter and report along with creating an account on UNIONWORX

\*\*\*\*You can now access the Union Worx site through our 558 Website.

It is the Responsibility of Each Member to have your Name added to Book-1 after Layoff/ Discharge from a Contractor! The day you log into Unionworx and make an Out of Work List Request you’ll be put on the book
