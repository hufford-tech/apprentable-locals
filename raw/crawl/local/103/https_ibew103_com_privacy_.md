## IBEW 103 Officers & Staff

**Privacy Policy**

**International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local Union 103**

**Effective Date:** January 2026

* * *

### Introduction

International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local Union 103, referred to as “IBEW Local 103,” “we,” “us,” or “our,” respects your privacy and is committed to protecting the personal information you share with us through our website, **ibew103.com**.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights regarding that information.

* * *

### Information We Collect

We may collect the following types of information:

**Personal Information You Provide**

- Name

- Email address

- Phone number

- Any information you submit through contact forms or sign-up forms


**Automatically Collected Information**

- IP address

- Browser type and version

- Pages visited and time spent on the site

- Device and operating system information


This information is collected through standard website technologies such as cookies and analytics tools.

* * *

### How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

- Respond to inquiries and communications

- Provide union-related information and updates

- Improve website performance and user experience

- Monitor website usage and security

- Comply with legal or regulatory requirements


We do not sell or rent your personal information.

* * *

### Cookies and Analytics

Our website may use cookies and similar technologies to:

- Understand how visitors use our site

- Improve content and navigation

- Measure traffic and performance


You may control or disable cookies through your browser settings. Disabling cookies may affect certain website features.

* * *

### Sharing of Information

We may share information only in the following limited circumstances:

- With service providers who help operate the website, such as hosting or analytics providers

- When required by law or legal process

- To protect the rights, safety, or property of IBEW Local 103 or others


We do not share personal information for marketing purposes.

* * *

### Data Security

We take reasonable administrative, technical, and physical measures to protect your personal information. While no system is completely secure, we work to safeguard your data against unauthorized access or disclosure.

* * *

### Your Rights and Choices

Depending on your location, you may have the right to:

- Request access to the personal information we hold about you

- Request correction or deletion of your information

- Opt out of certain data collection practices


To make a request, please contact us using the information below.

* * *

### Children’s Privacy

Our website is not intended for children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

* * *

### External Links

Our website may contain links to third-party websites. We are not responsible for the privacy practices or content of those sites.

* * *

### Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. Any changes will be posted on this page with an updated effective date.

* * *

### Contact Information

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or how your information is handled, please contact:
