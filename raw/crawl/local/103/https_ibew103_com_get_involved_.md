## Get Involved

## A Space for every Member

IBEW103 has a variety of clubs, teams & committees that are ran by membership. If you are interested in joining or participating, reach out to the emails below


#### Renew

RENEW connects and empowers young IBEW members through leadership, education, and community involvement, driving the next generation of union strength and solidarity.

[Learn More](mailto:RENEW@ibew103.com)

#### Motorcycle Club

Our Motorcycle Club brings members together on two wheels for charity rides, community events, and brotherhood on and off the road.

[Learn More](mailto:info@ibew103.com)

#### EWMC

The Electrical Workers Minority Caucus promotes leadership, unity, and diversity within our workforce and the labor movement.

[Learn More](mailto:ewmc@ibew103.com)

#### Women's Committee

Our Women’s Committee creates opportunities for mentorship, networking, and advocacy, supporting women across all areas of the trade.

[Learn More](mailto:womenscommittee@ibew103.com)

#### Veterans Committee

The Veterans Committee honors those who served by helping fellow veterans transition to careers in the electrical industry and beyond.

[Learn More](mailto:103veterans@ibew103.com)

#### Safety Committee

Dedicated to promoting jobsite safety and awareness, the Safety Committee ensures every member goes home safe every day.

[Learn More](mailto:info@ibew103.com)

#### Softball Team

Our Softball Team brings members together for friendly competition, teamwork, and camaraderie on the diamond each season.

[Learn More](mailto:info@ibew103.com)

#### Hockey Team

Union pride hits the ice with the IBEW 103 Hockey Team

[Learn More](mailto:info@ibew103.com)

#### Basketball Team

The IBEW 103 Basketball Team brings energy, teamwork, and brotherhood to the court

[Learn More](mailto:info@ibew103.com)
