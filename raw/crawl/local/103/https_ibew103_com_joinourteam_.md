# Join IBEW Local 103

Whether you’re an aspiring apprentice, an experienced journeyman, or a contractor ready to partner with the best—we make joining us simple.


[Get Started](https://ibew103.com/joinourteam/#chooser)

## How would you like to join?

Select the path that fits you best.


### I’m a Worker

Apprentice or Journeyman looking to be part of Local 103.

### I’m a Contractor

Become a signatory contractor and access a world-class workforce.

## Join as a Worker

Choose the path that matches your experience level.

### Apprenticeship Path

**Check Eligibility**

High school diploma/GED, valid driver’s license, and ability to perform physical requirements.

**Submit Application**

Applications open once a year. Complete the online application and upload required documents.

**Aptitude Test**

Demonstrate readiness through sptitude test

**Selection & Enrollment**

If selected, you’ll begin paid on-the-job training plus classroom instruction.

[Apply for Apprenticeship](https://bostonjatc.com/apply-for-apprenticeship/)

### Journeyman / Experienced Electrician

**Gather Documentation**

State license(s), proof of hours, and recent work history.

**Request Organizing Meeting**

Meet with our organizers to discuss wages, benefits, and process.

**Skill Assessment**

We verify skill level to place you correctly on the wage scale.

**Become A Union Member**

Get placed with a union contractor or remain with your newly organized contractor.

[Talk to an Organizer](https://ibew103.com/organize/)

### Frequently Asked Questions

When do you open applications for the apprenticeship?

We typically open applications once per year. Check BostonJATC.com periodically.

Do I get paid during the apprenticeship?

Yes. Apprentices earn a percentage of journeyman wages and receive regular raises as they progress. Apprentices attend school once per week while working remaining days.

Can I transfer from another IBEW local?

Yes—contact the Business Office for portability and reciprocity information.

## Become a Signatory Contractor

Partnering with IBEW Local 103 gives you access to the region’s most qualified electrical workforce, comprehensive training programs, and the support to scale your business.


#### Skilled Workforce

Highly trained electricians with ongoing safety & code education.

#### Predictable Labor Costs

Transparent wage & benefit packages, no surprises.

#### Project Flexibility

Manpower when you need it to scale up or down fast.

#### Competitive Advantage

Win bids that require union labor and demonstrate quality standards.

#### Training & Safety

Best-in-class apprenticeship & continuing education at no cost to you.

#### Benefits Administration

Full benefits handled through our funds meaning less overhead for your team.

### How to Become Signatory

**Initial Conversation**

Meet with our Organizer to review your company’s needs.

**Review Agreement**

We’ll walk through the Collective Bargaining Agreement and benefit contributions.

**Submit Required Documents**

Corporate info, insurance certificates, and any licensing documents.

**Sign & Launch**

Execute the agreement, get access to manpower, and start bidding with confidence.

### Contractor FAQ

Is there a cost to become signatory?

No upfront fee. You’ll agree to wage/benefit terms, and contributions are paid as you employ our manpower.

Can I keep my existing employees?

Yes. Existing qualified electricians can join the union and keep working for you.

What if I only need a few electricians occasionally?

That’s fine, you can use the hall as needed. We're here to provide you manpower for any job.
