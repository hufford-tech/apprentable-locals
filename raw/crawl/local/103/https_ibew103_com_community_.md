# COMMUNITY INVOLVEMENT

## Bullpen Buddies

Bullpen Buddies brings local community groups to a Boston Red Sox game for an unforgettable day at Fenway. Guests arrive early to watch batting practice from the field, meet players, snap photos, and soak up the big-league atmosphere, creating memories that last long after the final out.

## Kids Corner

Through our Kids Corner program, we bring local youth groups to Boston Celtics games for a night they’ll never forget. From the excitement of the crowd to their moment on the Jumbotron, it’s an opportunity to inspire young fans, build community pride, and share in the energy of Celtics basketball.

## Community Power Play

Through our Community Power Play program, we bring local organizations and community groups to Boston Bruins games for an unforgettable night of hockey. It’s our way of saying thank you to those who strengthen our neighborhoods by giving them a chance to experience the energy and pride of Boston’s home team together.
