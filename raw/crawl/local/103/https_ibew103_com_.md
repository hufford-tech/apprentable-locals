## Message From Business Manager Lou Antonellis

For 125 years, IBEW Local 103 has been at the heart of Boston’s progress, lighting up the skyline and powering the city’s future. Since 1900, our members have built the infrastructure that keeps Boston running — its hospitals, universities, transportation, and innovation hubs.

Beyond our worksites, we are committed to strengthening our communities, giving back through education, outreach, and service. Our legacy is one of hard work, solidarity, and resilience. As we celebrate this milestone, we honor the generations who came before us and the impact they’ve made on our city.

Now, let’s get to work!

## Frequently Asked Questions

If your question is not answered here, feel free to contact IBEW 103 at 617-436-3710 or reach out via form on contact page.

How do I join IBEW103?

Apprenticeship applications are taken once a year in January.

Current Journeyman can reach out to IBEW103 organizers to have conversations about becoming a member

How do I contact Health & Welfare?

You can visit www.trustfunds103.com or call the Health & Welfare office at (617) 288-5999.

How do I pay my dues?

Your quarterly dues can be paid in person at Dorchester & Wilmington locations or online at [https://ibew103.workingsystems.com/](https://ibew103.workingsystems.com/)

I'm a contractor interested in becoming signatory

Thank you for your interest in become a Union signatory contractor! Please reach out to our organizing department on the [Join Our Team](https://ibew103.com/joinourteam/) page. They will be able to provide answers to all the questions you will have and walk you through the entire process.
