### Apprenticeship programs for Electricians in the Puget Sound

Interested in becoming an Electrician? Get started by joining PSEJATC's (Puget Sound Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee) Apprenticeship program. IBEW Local 46 cosponsors PSEJATC to provide training for Apprentices and Journey Level electricians in the organized electrical industry within the IBEW Local 46 jurisdiction which includes King, Kitsap, Jefferson, and Clallam counties.

**NOTE:** Effective July 1, 2023, all 01 Trainees (Inside Wire Journey Level electricians) will be required to be enrolled in a state approved apprenticeship program.

Be ahead of the crowd and enroll today!
