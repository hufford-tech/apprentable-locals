# Dispatch

IBEW Local 1316 Dispatch

Book 1 Process

- Initial signing can be in-person, through text, or email
- Resign period is 10th through 16th every month
- Resign through text, email, or in-person
- Removal from the Book will occur after a failure to resign during the resign window or after 3rd ding(not taking a callout that a member was eligible to take)
- Names will remain on the Book for 2 weeks after taking a referral
- Jobs are posted on the JOBLINE after 6PM EST
