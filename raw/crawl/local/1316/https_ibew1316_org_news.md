Please see important QR Code below. This is a 2-minute way to write a letter to your state senator to oppose the two senate bills that will basically kill our future data center work, which employs hundreds of electricians. Capture the QR code with the camera app on your cell phone, enter your name, home address, and email address. It will auto generate a letter to each constituent's state senator (you don't have to know who their senator is beforehand) on the next page. Then click 'send letter'.
