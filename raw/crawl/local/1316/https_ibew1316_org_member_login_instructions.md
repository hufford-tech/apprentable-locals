# Member Login Instructions

We have a new password-protected member site to access Pay Dues, Calendar, Job Board, Health Insurance Forms, Governing Documents, In Memory, and other information pertinent to IBEW 1316 members.

**After May 2025, you will only have access to this member information on the password-protected site.**

Click on the MEMBER LOGIN button to go to the password-protected site. Your **Username** is your **Member Number**.

The default temporary password is **ibew1316**. It will prompt you to change your password before you can enter the site.

If you have questions, please call 478-743-7017.
