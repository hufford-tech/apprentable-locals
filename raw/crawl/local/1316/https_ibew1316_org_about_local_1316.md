# About

IBEW Local 1316 is a branch of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW), a trade union that represents electrical workers across the United States and Canada. Local 1316, based in Macon, Georgia, supports electricians and electrical professionals in the region by advocating for fair wages, safe working conditions, and quality benefits. We provide training programs and apprenticeships to help workers develop skills, stay updated on industry standards, and advance in their careers. Additionally, Local 1316 works with local businesses and contractors, ensuring projects are completed to high standards by skilled, licensed professionals.

> If you are willing to give an honest day's work, continually strive to be a better craftsperson, lift up your Brothers and Sisters around you, and live and work with integrity, I guarantee a comfortable middle class life for you and your family where you can have pride in your work, build generational wealth, and be part of a Brotherhood that improves the lives of all working people in this nation.
>
> **Stacey Mixson** _Business Manager & Financial Secretary_

## Our Mission

**_To organize all workers of the electrical industry in the United States and Canada, including all those in public utilities and electrical manufacturing, into local unions,_**

**_To promote reasonable methods of work,_**

**_To cultivate feelings of friendship among those in our industry,_**

**_To settle all disputes between employers and employees by arbitration (if possible),_**

**_To assist each other in sickness or distress,_**

**_To secure employment,_**

**_To reduce the hours of daily labor,_**

**_To secure adequate pay for our work,_**

**_To seek a higher and higher standard of living,_**

**_To seek security for the individual,_**

**_And by legal and proper means to elevate the moral, intellectual and social conditions of our members, their families and dependents, in the interest of a higher standard of citizenship._**
