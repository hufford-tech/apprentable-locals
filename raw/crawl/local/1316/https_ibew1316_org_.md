## Welcome to IBEW Local 1316

IBEW Local 1316 sets the standard of excellence for electricians and electrical workers in Middle Georgia. Every Local 1316 journeyman and apprentice takes an oath to be the very best in our craft, and we pride ourselves on our rigorous training and commitment to safety and quality of work. Many of the hospitals, data centers, schools, office buildings, and manufacturing facilities that drive the Macon and Middle Georgia Economy were brought to life by the skilled hands of IBEW Local 1316 electricians. If you're ready to be the best in the industry or you are searching to hire top-tier electricians, you've come to the right place.
