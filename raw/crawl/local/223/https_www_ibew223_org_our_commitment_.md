# Our Commitment

IBEW Local 223 was chartered in 1902. Throughout its history, Local 223 has maintained its reputation nationwide as one of the leaders in the electrical industry. Through partnerships with contractors regionally and internationally, Local 223 has taken great pride in its craftsmanship, training, and work ethic to ensure job  site success.

IBEW Local 223 values its members and the communities we represent. Partnerships with organizations like [Helmets to Hardhats](https://helmetstohardhats.org/), [Building Pathways South](https://www.buildingpathwayssouth.com/), [Brockton & S.E. Mass Building Trades Council](https://massbuildingtrades.org/local-building-trades-councils/), [Rhode Island & S.E. Mass. Chapter NECA](https://www.necanet.org/find-a-local-chapter/chapter-detail/rhode-island-southeast-massachusetts-chptr-neca), and [Modern Assistance Programs](https://modernassistance.com/) are Local 223’s way to say thank you and to try to serve those who mean the most.

IBEW Local 223 is the leader in electrical training in Southeastern Massachusetts. Our 30,000-square-foot training facility and fully accredited training program offer opportunities for the most thorough and inclusive education for apprentices and journeymen ensuring our workforce is trained in the newest technologies of our rapidly changing industry.

IBEW Local 223 partners with over 150 electrical contractors nationwide who work in all electrical field aspects. Local 223 provides these contractors with the workforce they need to get their project, large or small, completed on time and under budget with a safety record and value that cannot be matched.

## Interested in becoming a member? Learn more about joining IBEW Local 223

### Think about why you would like to affiliate with the IBEW

- Do my co-workers and I want more money or better benefits?
- Do my co-workers and I want better work rules or safer conditions?
- Do my co-workers and I want to  be treated fairly and with  greater dignity?

### Talk with your co-workers

The National Labor Relations Act (NLRA) provides legal protection for workers who join together to address workplace problems or improve wages, benefits, and conditions of employment in  the workplace.

### About Local 223

Representing a robust membership of skilled professionals, IBEW Local 223 proudly serves Southeastern Massachusetts, boasting a century-long legacy in the electrical industry. With affiliations with over 150 contractors nationwide, our expertise spans a diverse range of projects, ensuring timely and budget-friendly completion while maintaining an unparalleled commitment to safety and excellence.
