# Licensing Renewal

Ensure your licenses stay current by renewing them conveniently through the Commonwealth of Massachusetts [ePLACE Portal](https://elicensing21.mass.gov/CitizenAccess/Default.aspx). Renewing your licenses online is quick and easy, saving you time and hassle. Simply log in to the ePlace portal and follow the prompts to complete your renewal process efficiently.

**Don’t wait until the last minute – renew today and maintain your active status!**

[Click here to access the ePLACE Portal](https://elicensing21.mass.gov/CitizenAccess/Default.aspx)

### About Local 223

Representing a robust membership of skilled professionals, IBEW Local 223 proudly serves Southeastern Massachusetts, boasting a century-long legacy in the electrical industry. With affiliations with over 150 contractors nationwide, our expertise spans a diverse range of projects, ensuring timely and budget-friendly completion while maintaining an unparalleled commitment to safety and excellence.
