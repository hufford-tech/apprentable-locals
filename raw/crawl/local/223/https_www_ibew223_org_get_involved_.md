# Get Involved

Active engagement in our community’s political landscape is paramount for every member of IBEW Local 223. As the collective voice of our community, we reside, work, raise families, and exercise our voting rights within it.

In this pivotal election year, we seek assistance in electing candidates who align with the values and objectives of Local 223’s members.

Let’s not forget: **If we all do a little, no one will have to do a lot!**

### About Local 223

Representing a robust membership of skilled professionals, IBEW Local 223 proudly serves Southeastern Massachusetts, boasting a century-long legacy in the electrical industry. With affiliations with over 150 contractors nationwide, our expertise spans a diverse range of projects, ensuring timely and budget-friendly completion while maintaining an unparalleled commitment to safety and excellence.
