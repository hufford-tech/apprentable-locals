# Updates

## 2023 Brockton & S.E. Mass Legislative Breakfast

- July 18, 2024

![](http://www.ibew223.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/354220010_10223686205291458_3075652755049177789_n-1024x768.jpg)![](http://www.ibew223.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/354582035_987843869177165_6144862076584090280_n-1-1024x536.jpg)![](http://www.ibew223.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/355108227_987842012510684_4171682462799299078_n-1024x552.jpg)![](http://www.ibew223.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/355120732_987846685843550_4567280107624338580_n-1024x536.jpg)![](http://www.ibew223.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/355129088_987840795844139_6724902942889603287_n-1024x768.jpg)![](http://www.ibew223.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/355151803_987842599177292_513568402642018906_n-1-1024x537.jpg)

We were honored to host the 2023 Brockton & S.E. Mass Building Trades Legislative Breakfast at our Local 223 Union Hall. Thank you to all of our State Senators and Representatives who attended and toured our facility as well as discussing important issues to Organized Labor in our area. Special thanks also to Massachusetts Building Trades Unions for putting the event together in partnership with Brockton and Vicinity Building Trades and the Southeastern Mass Building Trades Councils. It was great to see so much support for the Southeastern Massachusetts area represented.

### About Local 223

Representing a robust membership of skilled professionals, IBEW Local 223 proudly serves Southeastern Massachusetts, boasting a century-long legacy in the electrical industry. With affiliations with over 150 contractors nationwide, our expertise spans a diverse range of projects, ensuring timely and budget-friendly completion while maintaining an unparalleled commitment to safety and excellence.
