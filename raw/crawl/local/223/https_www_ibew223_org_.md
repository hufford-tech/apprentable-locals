# Welcome to   IBEW Local 223

## Brothers and Sisters,

Welcome to the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local 223.

We’re here to strengthen and improve the electrical profession through best-in-class training and family-sustaining wages and benefits, and to deliver the highest quality of work across the electrical industry.

If you work in the electrical field and aren’t a Local 223 member yet, we want to hear from you. Or if you’re a contractor who wants to work with the best, you’ve come to the right place.

Please call me at 508-880-2690 with any questions on how to become a member or how to work together on a project. Let’s keep powering Southeastern Massachusetts, Cape Cod, and the Islands with excellence!

In solidarity,

Steven M. Barry

**Steven M. Barry**

## Job Opportunities

Looking for a rewarding career with the best benefits? Look no further. IBEW Local 223 is on the lookout for talented individuals to join our dynamic team.

Whether you’re an experienced electrician or just starting out in the industry, we’ve got opportunities for you to build a rewarding career as an electrical professional. Join us in powering up communities, shaping the future of the electrical industry, and making a real difference in people’s lives.

### About Local 223

Representing a robust membership of skilled professionals, IBEW Local 223 proudly serves Southeastern Massachusetts, boasting a century-long legacy in the electrical industry. With affiliations with over 150 contractors nationwide, our expertise spans a diverse range of projects, ensuring timely and budget-friendly completion while maintaining an unparalleled commitment to safety and excellence.
