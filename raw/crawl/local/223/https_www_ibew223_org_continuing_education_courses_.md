# Continuing Education Courses

Stay ahead of the industry curve with continuing education courses, designed to empower members with the latest skills, knowledge, and certifications essential for thriving in today’s ever-evolving electrical trade.

For more information or to sign up for classes, please reach out to Bob Revil at (508)-880-2693 or [brevil@ibew223.com](mailto:brevil@ibew223.com).

Please check back at a later date for class schedules. All nights must be attended.

- A laptop is required each night.

### About Local 223

Representing a robust membership of skilled professionals, IBEW Local 223 proudly serves Southeastern Massachusetts, boasting a century-long legacy in the electrical industry. With affiliations with over 150 contractors nationwide, our expertise spans a diverse range of projects, ensuring timely and budget-friendly completion while maintaining an unparalleled commitment to safety and excellence.
