## **UNION PROUD. UNION STRONG.**

### WELCOME

Welcome to the website of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 903. Our highly skilled electricians can be seen on many of the high-profile jobs you drive by every day. We are happy to partner with the area’s top companies to ensure safe and efficient jobs.

Throughout our website, you’ll be able to learn more about 903 – from our history and training programs to upcoming activities and and future professionals.

### JOIN US

Looking for a skilled career in the electrical industry? Or do you want to improve conditions in your current job? Looking for fair pay, decent benefits, an opportunity to grow? On-the-job respect?

A union of 750,000 members, the IBEW is the largest organization of electrical workers in North America. Our members work in nearly every part of the economy: construction, utilities, telecommunications, broadcasting, manufacturing, railroad and government.

## CODE OF EXCELLENCE

The Code of Excellence is a program designed to bring about the best in our construction members and demonstrate to our customers that IBEW members:

Perform the highest quality and quantity of work.
Utilize their skills and abilities to the maximum.
Exercise safe and productive work practices.
