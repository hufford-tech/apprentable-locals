# About 903

![electrician-729240_1280](http://lu903.com/wp-content/uploads/2015/07/electrician-729240_1280.jpg)IBEW Local 903 provides a highly-skilled Electrical Workforce to all of South Mississippi. This means you receive the highest quality craftsmanship with on-time and on-budget projects. Local 903’s Apprenticeship Training Program also ensures that the next generation of Electrical Workers will be as highly skilled and qualified as the last generation.

There are ten specialty areas where you will find the members of IBEW Local 903, each best described by the type of work done:

**Inside Wiremen** are electrical workers who install the power, lighting, controls, as well as security control systems and other electrical equipment in commercial and industrial buildings.

**Outside Linemen** are the electrical workers who install the distribution and transmission lines, which move power from power plant to a factory, a business or your home.

**Construction Wiremen** are electrical workers who specialize in installing all of the electrical systems in single-family, multi-family houses or dwellings, and small commercial buildings.

**Telecommunications Technicians** are workers who install equipment for telephones, computer networks, video distribution systems, and other low voltage systems.

**Sign Wiremen** and Fabricators are electrical workers who fabricate and install electric signs such as neon, matrix, and all other types of electrical based signage.

**Lightning Rod Installers** are workers who are specially trained in the protection of property and equipment from lightning strikes.

**Line Clearance** are workers who perform the task of keeping the energized utility lines cleared from coming in contact with branches causing electrical outages.

**Maintenance Workers** maintain the various electrical systems in commercial facilities.

**Crane Operators** work with utility companies in the installation and maintenance of transmission lines and switchyards.

**Administrative Technician** work in both public and private offices covering a broad range of job classifications within the companies they serve.

## Political Action

Local 903 understands the importance of being politically active. Our union’s involvement in legislative issues and our member IBEW Local Union Political Action Committees are vital if we intend to succeed in our constant battle for the good paying jobs and safe working conditions that our members deserve.

Elected officials know that when we care about an issue or a cause, we commit our full support wherever and whenever necessary. Remember, all politics are local!

## CODE OF EXCELLENCE

The Code of Excellence is a program designed to bring about the best in our construction members and demonstrate to our customers that IBEW members:

Perform the highest quality and quantity of work.
Utilize their skills and abilities to the maximum.
Exercise safe and productive work practices.
