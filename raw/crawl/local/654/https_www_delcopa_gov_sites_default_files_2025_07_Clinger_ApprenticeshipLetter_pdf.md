Fire Alarm Replacement

Wm. H. Clinger Corp is a signatory contractor with IBEW LU 654 and utilizes apprentices that are part of the nationally recognized apprenticeship program.

The Chester Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (CJATC) for the Electrical Industry offers a five (5) year apprenticeship to become a Journeyman Electrician. This committee is partnered through the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) and The National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA). The apprenticeship program consists of 8,000 hours of on-the-jo training, as well as 900 hours of classroom and lab training.
