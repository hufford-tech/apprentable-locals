# Welcome to IBEW Local 175

Welcome to IBEW Local 175's new website. We wanted a website that provides real-time information that our members can use, while reflecting the kind of craftsmanship and professional look that our members deserve. IBEW 175 provides the finest electricians, wiremen, and linemen in our jurisdiction of Chattanooga, Southeastern Tennessee, Northwest Georgia and Northeast Alabama. We're proud to work on the most important projects in this area of the country, and our members are the reason why Chattanooga and the surrounding area is one of the best places to live and work, and one of the hottest places to do business in the country.

**In Solidarity,** **Sterling "Toby" Shelton** , _Business Manager, IBEW Local 175_
