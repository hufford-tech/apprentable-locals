**Positions of IBEW Local 60 Concerning Labor and the Advancement of our Cause**

1) IBEW Local 60 supports workers drinking an adequate amount of water when working in the heat during the summer.

2) IBEW Local 60 expresses Solidarity with San Antonio AFT and San Antonio Alliance in their pursuit of adequate funding for public schools.

IBEW 60 is a part of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers. The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) represents approximately 725,000 members who work in a wide variety of fields, including utilities, construction, telecommunications, broadcasting, manufacturing, railroads and government.
