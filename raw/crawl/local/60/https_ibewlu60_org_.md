## Welcome To IBEW 60

International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers

Serving San Antonio since 1893, we have over 130 years experience in the electrical trade. We are over 3,100 members strong employed by more than 25 Signatory Contractors.

If you're looking for the best electricians in San Antonio, look no further.  Contact us today and we'll help pair you with one of our licensed, bonded and insured contractors.

**"ASK NOT WHAT YOUR LOCAL CAN DO FOR YOU, ASK WHAT YOU CAN DO FOR YOUR LOCAL"**

## Death Benefits

The Local 60 Death fund is a benefit; not to our members, but their families. We pay our dues, which funds the ability of the local union to pay out death benefits with the  expectation that when we pass away, our families will be taken care of in the same way.

Check your dues receipt and if you are behind, get caught up!  For more information on this benefit as well as the National Electrical Benefit Fund, **[CLICK HERE](https://ibewlu60.org/benefits.aspx?zone=benefits&pID=9245)**.
