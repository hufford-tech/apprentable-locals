## Wages and Benefits

- **Journeyman pay: $37.45/hr +fringes= $49.64/hr Total Package**
- **OT After 8 hours**
- **Time-and-a-half on Saturdays**
- **Double Time on Sundays**
- **6 PAID Holidays**
- **Contractor funded healthcare coverage for you & your family**
- **3 Pensions**
- **Contractor funded 6% on 401K**
- **2 weeks paid vacation**
- **Continuing education classes offered at "Member Only" discounted rate**
- **Union meetings every 2nd Wednesday of every month.**
