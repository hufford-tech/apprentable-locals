#### IBEW Local 429

# **JOIN THE IBEW LOCAL 429**

[**Request More Information →**](https://www.ibew429.org/membershipApplication)

Are you looking for a great paying career in the electrical industry? Do you want a career that offers some of the most comprehensive insurance in the industry? What about retirement benefits that will enable you to enjoy life after your career? And not to mention the ability to travel and work anywhere in the country, if you so desire, all while earning some of the very highest wages in the electrical industry. The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers is looking for individuals that want to start a career, or who are already working, in the Electrical Industry!

**Qualifications:**

• High school graduate or GED

• At least 18 years old

• Desire to learn a valuable skilled trade

• No experience required to get started

• Those with experience in the electrical industry are HIGHLY encouraged to talk with us about career opportunities in the booming electrical trades industry. Call (615) 889-4429 today.

**Benefits:**

**•** Apprenticeship is an "Earn as you Learn" program

• Medical/Dental/Vision Insurance after 90 days

• Multiple retirement benefits include two different pensions, as well as an employer funded 401(a) account

• Structured pay raises as you progress through the apprenticeship program

• The best wage & benefit packages in the electrical industry
