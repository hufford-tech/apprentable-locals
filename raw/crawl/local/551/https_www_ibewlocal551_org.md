#### **Traveling and the Electronic Reciprocal Transfer System (ERTS)**







**ARE YOU TRAVELING OUT OF YOUR HOME LOCAL?** **If you are traveling and want your benefits to stay with your current home Trust Fund, then you must have an ERTS form on file. Otherwise, your benefits will get dispersed to the current local you are working within. Contact your Local Union Office for assistance with the Electronic Reciprocal Transfer System (ERTS). We would be happy to assist you** | | |
