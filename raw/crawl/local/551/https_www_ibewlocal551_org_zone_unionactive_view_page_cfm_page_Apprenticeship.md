| --- | --- |

|  |

We currently have two different Apprenticeship Programs:

1.  5-year Inside Wireman Apprenticeship. Visit [rejatc.org](http://www.rejatc.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_page.cfm&page=Apprenticeship20Program) for information on how to apply.
2.  3-year Sound and Communications Installer Apprenticeship. Visit [norcal-jatc.com](https://www.norcal-jatc.com/) for information on how to apply.

Relevant experience in the trade is not required.
