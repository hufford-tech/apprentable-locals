**Monthly Open House**

**Last Thursday of the Month\* at 3:00 PM - 5:00 PM**

**2525 Cleveland Avenue, Santa Rosa, CA 95403**

**In our meeting room, upstairs.**

Anyone who wants to join IBEW Local 551 is invited to join us at our monthly Open House. Stop in and speak with a representative face to face. Open to all, especially Foremen, Journeymen, Apprenties, Trainees, anyone with experience working in electrical construction, or anyone with questions about our Apprenticeship Program.

If you have any questions or can't make one of these meetings, email Jared Mumm at jaredm@ibewlocal551.org.

**\\*** **November's** Open House will be on Thursday, **November 19th.**

**\\* December's** Open House will be on Thursday, **December 17th.**
