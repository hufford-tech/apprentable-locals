Basic requirements of Inside Wireman Apprenticeship

Earn money while you learn a trade.

Electrical construction is a dynamic and diverse industry with specialized skills. The electrician must be able to layout, assemble, install, repair, and test entire electrical and electronic systems. Along with their electrical training, journeyman electricians must have a complete working knowledge of blueprint reading and know the National Electric Code and local codes. Electricians must be mechanically inclined, physically fit and enjoy working with mathematical formulas. Along with classwork you will have on-the-job training.

- 18 years of age
- Successfully completed 1 year of algebra
- Valid Drivers License
- Birth Certificate
- High School Diploma or GED
- High School Transcripts
- Vocational and/or College Transcripts
- Vocational and/or College Diplomas
- Photo of Yourself
- $25.00 Application Fee
- Military DD214 _if applicable_
