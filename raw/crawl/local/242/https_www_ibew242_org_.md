IBEW 242 is located in Duluth, MN representing over 600 electrical workers covering 15 counties in Northeast/East Central MN and Northwestern WI. We also cover the Brainerd lakes area, Long Praire, Wadena, Park Rapids, Walker areas.Our members are the most highly trained, highly skilled and most capable of handling any project in the area.

If you would like to start your career with IBEW 242 please visit this [**site**](https://www.ibew242-neca.org/).
