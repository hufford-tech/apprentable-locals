## Continuing education

All IBEW Local 163 Construction Wireman/Construction Electrician (CW/CE) should check the training schedule for updates to current listings of classes offered.

Since the demand for upgrade training constantly changes, the most up-to-date schedule of classes (including OSHA 30, Basic Life Support and NFPA 70E) can all be found through Trade School. You can also register for the classes as well on Trade School.
