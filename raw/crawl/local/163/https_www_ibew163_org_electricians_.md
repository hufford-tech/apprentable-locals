# IBEW Local 163 offers opportunities for experienced electricians

![](https://ibew163.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/05/electricians_main.jpg)

## Join us today

IBEW Local 163 encourages experienced electricians in northeastern Pennsylvania to seek membership with Local 163 in order to receive the total compensation package and added job security they deserve.

Experienced non-union electricians who become members of IBEW Local 163 gain access to fully-paid family healthcare. Membership also provides them with retirement benefits including a pension and annuity. It is these fringe benefits — health insurance and retirement — that separate the union from the non-union sector. Most non-union electrical contractors cannot provide benefits near the level of those earned by members of Local 163.

Men and women with industry experience can also take advantage of journeyman upgrade training. This instruction not only helps our electricians work more safely, but more efficiently. These courses help keep you up-to-date on the latest industry technology and allow you to upgrade your skillset and improve your employability. All of this is offered at our state-of-the-art training facility, which is equipped to be a constructive learning environment for all members.

Make the best choice for yourself and your family by becoming a member of IBEW Local 163.
