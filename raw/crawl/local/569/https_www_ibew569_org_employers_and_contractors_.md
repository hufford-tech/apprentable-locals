## Now employers and contractors can hire the best electrical workers from one place

### Choose from a large highly- qualified labor pool

Our apprenticeship program is the 3rd largest in California. Every Journeymen Wireman and Journeyman Soundman are required to do ongoing education throughout their career.

### Predictive labor costs

No more dealing with secret wage negotiations. No more concerns about negotiating individual wage increases. Predictive labor costs helps your business stay profitable.

### HR benefits administration, done for you

We take the complicated distraction of benefits administration (Health Insurance, Retirement, Etc.) off your plate. Streamline your workflow. Focus on getting the job done right.

[HIRE IBEW Local 569](tel:8585698900)

#### (858) 569-8900

## Key benefits to becoming a signatory contractor

### Gain the competitive edge

With the time you save from not having to worry about hiring, recruiting, or benefits administration – you can focus on growing your business.

### Get new business opportunities

IBEW Local 569 broadly expands your network with General Contractors, universities, industry leaders. We also advocate with elected officials at the local, state, and federal level on behalf of our industry.

### Hire the best and brightest

You hire our professionals who train in regularly upgraded curriculums, including the latest cutting-edge technologies. Leading emerging market sectors. Driving industry innovations.

### Protect your brand's reputation

Your success is our success. We help you complete projects on budget, on schedule. With highlytrained electrical workers who do it right the first time.
