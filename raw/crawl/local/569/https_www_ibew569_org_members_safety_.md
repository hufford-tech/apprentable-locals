# Safety

![](https://www.ibew569.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/safetypic_0.jpeg)

Our highest priority while on the job is keeping ourselves and each other safe. Please check this page frequently for updates from our Safety Committee and other IBEW 569 members, or feel free to send alerts of your own with any of our [Business Agents](https://www.ibew569.org/meet-our-staff) for posting.

### Member Assistance Program (MAP)

Everyone needs a little help sometimes, even if we don’t want anyone else to know about it. We can help!

Aetna Resources For Living is a San Diego Electrical Health and Welfare Trust sponsored program, available at no cost to you and every member of your household. Even your dependent children up to age 26 who may no longer live at home are covered.

Services are completely confidential and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

To access services: [1-800-342-8111](tel:1-800-342-8111) \| [www.resourcesforliving.com](http://www.resourcesforliving.com/)(link is external)

Username: san diego electrical trust
