# NECA/IBEW SAN DIEGO COLLECTION SITE LOCATIONS

![](https://www.ibew569.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/09/necaibew48-logo.png)

UPDATE: Drug testing is no longer suspended due to COVID-19. Members must take the drug test. If you missed your annual birthday/birth-month test due to pandemic closure, you need to contact our IBEW 569 [Electrical Health and Welfare Trust Office](https://569trusts.org/) for your new drug testing deadline: (858) 569-6322.

[Click here](https://www.ibew569.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/08/8.10.26-Drug-Testing-Collection-Sites.pdf) to download a current copy of NECA/IBEW 569 Collection Site Locations.

If you have any questions, please call (760) 723-3056.

### Member Assistance Program (MAP)

Everyone needs a little help sometimes, even if we don’t want anyone else to know about it. We can help!

Aetna Resources For Living is a San Diego Electrical Health and Welfare Trust sponsored program, available at no cost to you and every member of your household. Even your dependent children up to age 26 who may no longer live at home are covered.

Services are completely confidential and available 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.

To access services: [1-800-342-8111](tel:1-800-342-8111) \| [www.resourcesforliving.com](http://www.resourcesforliving.com/)(link is external)
