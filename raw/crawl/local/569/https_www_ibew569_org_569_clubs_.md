# 569 Committees

Our union is strong because of you, our hard-working members, and our IBEW 569 Clubs provide opportunities for both new and experienced members to come together and get engaged with our local union.

Stay informed, get involved, and keep our union strong by joining our IBEW 569 Clubs. Check out our calendar for upcoming club meetings and events.

![](https://www.ibew569.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/409034524-ewmc-group-shot-1.jpg)

![](https://www.ibew569.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/409034526-ewmc-logo-2.png)

##### EWMC

The San Diego and Imperial County Electrical Workers Minority Caucus (EWMC) is committed to promoting a vibrant, diverse Local that embraces creative ideas, social and economic justice, and equal opportunities for minorities and those from traditionally underrepresented populations at all levels of our organization. These principals are critical to the success of our Local and the strength of our community.

[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/EWMC569) \| [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/ewmcsandiego/) \| [Website](https://ewmcsandiego.com/) \| Contact us at [info@ewmc569.org](mailto:info@ewmc569.org) (attn: Marcel McMillan)

##### RENEW

The mission of the Reach out and Engage Next-gen Electrical Workers (RENEW) initiative is to inspire the next generation of IBEW workers to become active in their local union by focusing on issues important to younger workers, providing education about the IBEW and the labor movement, and fostering relationships with members and local union leadership.

[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/RENEWSanDiego)\| [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/renew569/) \| Contact us at [RENEWIBEW569@gmail.com](mailto:RENEWIBEW569@gmail.com)

##### United Sparkies

The mission of United Sparkies is to unite, empower, and enrich the lives of our members and to give back to our Union and our community; to generate solidarity amongst women and their allies in the electrical industry; to recruit qualified women into the electrical industry; to promote opportunities to our members; and to assist our members into leadership roles.

[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/UnitedSparkies(link%2520is%2520external)) \| [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/unitedsparkies/) \| [Twitter](https://twitter.com/UnitedSparkies) \| Contact us at [unitedsparkies569@gmail.com](mailto:unitedsparkies569@gmail.com)

##### Alianza

Alianza 569 is a subcommittee of the Electrical Workers Minority Caucus (EWMC) that seeks to further the purpose of the EWMC and IBEW by engaging with Latino communities throughout San Diego and Imperial Counties. Alianza assists in creating meaningful community connections that will foster business opportunities and diplomatic ties with local elected officials.

[Facebook](https://www.facebook.com/Alianza569) \| [Instagram](https://www.instagram.com/alianza569_sdiv/) \| Contact us at [info@ewmc569.org](mailto:info@ewmc569.org) (attn. Alianza).

##### Retirees

Ready to get involved in your local community, promote your union, and meet new people who share your values? Why not join our IBEW 569 Retirees Club! These clubs are popular with IBEW retirees all over the nation. Our Retiree Club is involved in a range of activities from political volunteering to picket work to just grabbing a cup of coffee and chatting about the good ol’ days.

[Website](https://ibew569.org/retirees) \| Contact us at [ibew569retirees@gmail.com](mailto:ibew569retirees@gmail.com)

##### Legislative Committee

The IBEW 569 Legislative Committee helps set the political agenda of our Union. Members participating in the Legislative Committee strategize on elections; interview and endorse candidates; and determine how funds are spent through the Political Action Committee, which is voluntarily funded by our members. The Legislative Committee is where we shape the future of work through our collective political action.

Contact Cori Schumacher at [cschumacher@ibew569.org](mailto:cschumacher@ibew569.org)
