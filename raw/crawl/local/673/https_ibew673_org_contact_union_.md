**IBEW Local 673** provides highly skilled and highly trained journeymen and apprentice electrical workers to our signatory contractors throughout Lake County, much of Ashtabula County, and parts of Geauga countiy, Ohio. We are committed to helping our members prosper by providing them with opportunities to work for our signatory contractors, who want to employ the region's most efficient, experienced, and safest workforce.
