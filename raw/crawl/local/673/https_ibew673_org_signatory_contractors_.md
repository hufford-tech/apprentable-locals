## Are you a contractor wanting to become a signatory?

Local 673 signatory contractors know they will always have the labor force that is going to produce a high-quality product in a safe environment.

The most successful companies are union signatories. Partnership with IBEW Local 673 will help you to remain competitive and to gain the marketplace advantage. We bring positive factors to the relationship – training, reliable workforce, safety, quality and a stronger community presence.

We can help you grow and strengthen your business!
