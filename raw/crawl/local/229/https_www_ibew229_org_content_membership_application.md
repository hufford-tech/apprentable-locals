# Membership Application

### **_⚡ Now Accepting Applications – Construction Wireman / Construction Electrician Program_**

If you have experience in the electrical trade or are looking to start a career as an electrician, we encourage you to apply to our Construction Wireman (CW) / Construction Electrician (CE) program. This program is a **pathway into IBEW membership** and offers real jobsite experience, competitive wages, and opportunities for advancement.

**Qualified individuals may be offered IBEW membership after 90 days of employment**, based on performance, reliability, and workforce needs.

**Journeyman classification** may be available for experienced electricians but requires verification of qualifications, certifications, and work history.

**Please allow 3–5 business days** for your application to be reviewed.

Resumes and certifications can be sent to: **Jobs@ibew229.org**

**Requirements:**

- Must be at least 18 years old

- Must have a valid driver’s license

- Must be legally authorized to work in the U.S.


**Please Note:**

Submitting an application does not guarantee placement or employment. All applications are subject to review and approval based on experience, qualifications, and workforce needs.

**Equal Opportunity:**

IBEW Local 229 is an equal opportunity organization. We welcome applicants from all backgrounds and experiences.

**Questions?**

Call or text _**Edson Morales**_ at **717-900-7077** for assistance or updates.

**SMS Consent (Optional)**

By submitting this form, you understand that **you may receive SMS text messages from IBEW Local 229** with updates about job opportunities, meetings, and union-related news.

**You are not required to agree to receive text messages to complete this form or participate in any programs.**

If you would like to receive text messages, please ensure your mobile number is accurate. You may opt out at any time by replying “STOP” to any message. Standard message and data rates may apply.
