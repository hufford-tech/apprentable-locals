| Telecom/Low-Voltage Apprenticeship |

|     |
| --- |
| |     |
| --- |
| May 26, 2026 | |
| **Telecom / Low Voltage / Voice, Data, Video**<br>**TITLE:** Wisconsin TeleCommunications (Voice, Data, Video) Installer-Technician Apprenticeship Program<br>**TERM:** Three (3) Years<br>**TRAINING:** On-The-Job Training – Minimum 4800 Hours<br>Paid Related Instruction – Minimum 432 hours attending Paid Related Instruction. Classes are attended one full day (eight hours) every two weeks (2) during the school year.<br>Unpaid Related Instruction – Apprentices are required to attend class instruction as designated by the Committee, on their own time. This is usually accomplished in a night school setting.WAGES:<br>**WAGES:** The TeleCommunications (Voice, Data, Video) Installer-Technician Apprenticeship Program is divided into Six (6) Pay Periods. The wage at each level is a designated percentage of the Installer-Technician’s Base Wage Rate. Advancement to each subsequent level is determined by successful completion of hours and training acquired.<br>![](https://www.ibew158.com/05-31-26%20to%2005-29-27%20VDV%20Blitz%20wage%20sheets.jpg)<br>**COMPLETION:** Upon completion of the TeleCommunications (Voice, Data, Video) Apprenticeship Program, apprentices are advanced to Installer-Technicians status.<br>**COMMITTEE JURISDICTION:** Brown, Door, Kewaunee, and Oconto Counties; all of Manitowoc County except the township of Schleswig; all of Marinette County except the Townships of Pembine, Beecher, Dunbar and Goodman; Menominee County that portion of a line six miles west of the west boundary of Oconto County; all of Shawano County except the Townships of Aniwa and Hutchins; and the Townships of Menominee, Melon, and Ingallston in Menominee County in the State of Michigan.<br>Applicants are required to notify the Application Site of any changes in Address & Phone |
