# _**Members - Login to view and bid on posted jobs**_

Local 197's job line is (309) 820-0608.  In order to eliminate busy phone lines the calls will be listed on the website as well as the phone system.  To bid on a job you **may leave a message on the jobline or bid online.**

Members who login between 5 pm and 7 am are able to see and bid on job calls
