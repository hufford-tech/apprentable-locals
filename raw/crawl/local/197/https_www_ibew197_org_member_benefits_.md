**Benefits**

In addition to learning a valuable trade and skill, being a member of IBEW Local 197 allows you to participate in family health insurance which includes vision and dental as well as several pension funds.

For more info from the Decatur Health & Welfare Fund Office--- [www.neca-ibew.org](http://www.neca-ibew.org/ "decatur fund")

For more info about the St. Louis Plan-- [ibewnecaservicecenter.com](http://www.ibewnecaservicecenter.com/ "St. Louis Plan")

For more info on NEBF-- [https://www.nebf.com](https://www.nebf.com/)
