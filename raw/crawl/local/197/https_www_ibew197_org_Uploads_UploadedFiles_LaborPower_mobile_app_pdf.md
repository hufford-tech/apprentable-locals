Brothers and Sisters,

We are pleased to announce that our Local uses the LaborPower system for member services, including dues payments and access to important membership information. As part of this effort, the LaborPower Mobile application is now available for your use. This app will allow you to update your contact information, receive announcements, bid on job calls, and pay your quarterly dues from your smartphone.

Please follow the instructions below to get started:

Go to your device’s app store (Apple App Store or Google Play Store) and download “LaborPower Mobile”.

1. Open the app and select “Add Account.”
# 2. When prompted, choose “International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers” as your union.
3. Select Local 197 as your local union.

---

4. Choose “Membership Services” as the application type.

To activate your account for the first time, your IBEW card number will be your username. Your initial password format will be your last name followed by the last 4 digits of your Social Security Number (example: Smith1234). After you log in the first time, you will be prompted to change your password to something secure that only you know.

Once logged in, you will be able to view and update your contact information, review your work and dues history, and pay your membership dues online. Keeping your information up to date helps us serve you better and ensures you receive timely notices about jobs, meetings, and important Local 197 news.

If you have any questions or run into any issues with setup, please contact the hall at (309) 827-4868 or email info@ibew197.org.
