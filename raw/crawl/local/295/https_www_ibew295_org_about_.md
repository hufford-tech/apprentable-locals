## LOCAL UNION 295 IN LITTLE ROCK, ARKANSAS

Chartered June 15, 1916 Affiliated with AR AFL-CIO -- AR State Electrical Workers Assn.

\-\- Central AR Building & Construction Trades Council & Central AR Labor Council

If you are an electrician in central Arkansas take time to find out the facts for yourself. You could be missing out on thousands of dollars in wages and benefits. Please feel free to give us a call at 501-562-2244 There is no obligation and all calls are strictly confidential.
