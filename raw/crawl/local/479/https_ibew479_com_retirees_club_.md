# Retirees Club

## You’re Invited

We would like to invite all Local 479 retired members to attend our meetings and maybe join our club.

We meet the first Tuesday of each month at 12p.m. (noon) and serve lunch at each meeting. If any retiree is interested we welcome you to our meetings and to enjoy the company of those retirees and their wives or significant others that are currently members of the club.

### RENEW

Reach out and Engage the Next generation of Electrical Workers (RENEW) is a group that was formed for our young members to become activists not only within our Local Union but in the community as well. The Young Members Group is moving forward within our Local as Justin Cooper and Committee directs the younger generation towards understanding this is as much their Union as it is the rest of us.
