## Beaumont, Texas

[Training Programs](http://electricaltrainingsetx.org/) [Job Line](https://ibew479.com/job-line/)

### POWERING SOUTHEAST TEXAS

#### Recognize

To recognize the importance of upholding the highest level of qualified skilled worker

#### Pursue

To pursue, establish, and maintain an adequate wage and proper benefits for our labor

#### Advance

To advance our interest, our knowledge, and our skills throughout the industry

#### Encourage

To aid and encourage the honorable efforts for safer and better labor conditions

#### Uphold

To uphold the IBEW Code of Excellence within our selves, our membership, and our work

#### Make Better

To make this trade better than it was when we found it, for us and for those who follow

![](https://ibew479.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/03/LU479-04.jpg)

### Training Programs

Our electrical training programs are set up to provide the very best curriculum the trade has to offer. We provide full time on the job work experience along side of part time classroom instruction.

You heard right – _earn while you learn_, with the safety of us providing you with job site placement and the peace of mind of never filling out another job application again.

![](https://ibew479.com/wp-content/uploads/2019/01/ibew_logo.png)

### What the IBEW Offers…

#### Training

We offer training that will teach you the skills of the trade.

#### Insurance

We provide insurance to keep you and your family healthy.

#### Retirement

We offer retirement so you can enjoy the later years of life.

#### Living Wage

We offer a living wage so you can support yourself and family.

#### Career

We offer a career for you to live a better and more fulfilling life

#### Family

We offer you the guidance, support, and kinship of a family.

### RENEW

Reach out and Engage the Next generation of Electrical Workers (RENEW) is a group that was formed for our young members to become activists not only within our Local Union but in the community as well. The Young Members Group is moving forward within our Local as Justin Cooper and Committee directs the younger generation towards understanding this is as much their Union as it is the rest of us.
