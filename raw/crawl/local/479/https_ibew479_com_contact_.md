### RENEW

Reach out and Engage the Next generation of Electrical Workers (RENEW) is a group that was formed for our young members to become activists not only within our Local Union but in the community as well. The Young Members Group is moving forward within our Local as Justin Cooper and Committee directs the younger generation towards understanding this is as much their Union as it is the rest of us. See contact info below for more information.
