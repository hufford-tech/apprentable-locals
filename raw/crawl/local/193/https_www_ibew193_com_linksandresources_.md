#### **[Training & Education](https://www.ibew193.com/linksandresources/\#collapse-1698335957899)**

![](https://www.ibew193.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/electricaql_training_alliance.jpg)

The electrical training ALLIANCE (previously called NJATC) was created over 70 years ago as a joint training program between the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) aimed at training the best electrical workers the industry has to offer. Today, the electrical training ALLIANCE has developed into the largest apprenticeship and training program of its kind, having trained over 350,000 apprentices to journeyman status through local affiliate programs.

#### **[Other Resources](https://www.ibew193.com/linksandresources/\#collapse-1698336209372)**

![](https://www.ibew193.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/nlmcc.jpg)

The NLMCC is a unique entity working to ensure our industry partners—NECA electrical contractors and IBEW tradesman—have the capability to reach and serve their customers in the most effective way possible. We assist them by:

- providing national advertising and editorial exposure,
- making public relations resources available at the local level,
- offering conferences and workshops related to labor-management cooperation,
- conveying information about the latest proven technologies and techniques to member organizations,
- supporting efforts to recruit and train outstanding apprentices and journeymen in order to provide a skilled and reliable workforce,
- and more.


![](https://www.ibew193.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/nebf.jpg)

Provides educational information and overview on the National Electrical Benefit Fund (NEBF) concerning benefits, rights and responsibilities under the Plan.

![](https://www.ibew193.com/Uploads/UploadedFiles/NECA_IBEW.jpg)

The IBEW-NECA Benefits Administration Association presently administers the NECA-IBEW Welfare Trust Fund ( [Welfare Trust Fund](http://www.neca-ibew.org/About-Us/%5CWelfare-Trust-Fund)) and the NECA-IBEW Pension Trust Fund ( [Pension Trust Fund](http://www.neca-ibew.org/About-Us/%5CPension-Trust-Fund)) jointly with the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) for many locals located in Alabama, Indiana, Illinois, Florida, Georgia, Kentucky, and Wisconsin.
