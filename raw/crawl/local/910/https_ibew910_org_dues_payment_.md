# **Online Dues Payment**



2026 Dues Information



Apprentice: **$144.60 quarterly** Journeyman: **$150.60 quarterly**

If you **owe** more than 1 quarter then there will be a **$30.00 reinstatement fee** that will need to be added.

If you need to verify what you owe please call the office at [315-782-5630](tel:3157825630)

On the payment page in Contact Name please enter Local 910’s member’s name.
