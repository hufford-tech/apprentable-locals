WHO WE ARE

About Local 910

Many changes have occurred over the years in Local 910, but there remains a firm resolve to provide our contractors with the highest quality of skilled and trained electricians, apprentices and technicians in the industry.

Skill development, training, education, safety programs, pension investments and other important services can be tapped easily within _our Local._

The Local offers all of its electricians ongoing, continuous training, and we pride ourselves on our ability to keep our _Apprentices_ and _Journeyman_ _Wireman_ up to date and knowledgeable on the latest technologies and work processes.

Our Members

We have the **best trained construction electricians** in the electrical industry, offering a world class apprenticeship training program with a focus on skill, training, and being the best in the world. The Electrical Training Alliance is a nationally recognized program that produces highly qualified electricians. We’re proud our members are a part of the training program, and you can join too. Ask us how the IBEW apprenticeship program can help you begin a career in the electrical trade.

Further, ongoing classes throughout their careers ensure that our electricians know all of the latest technologies and can apply their skillset and knowledge in every job; **residential, commercial, industrial or agricultural.**

The heart of our training is our Inside Apprenticeship Program. It is a regimented training program that assures competency in the standardized skills needed to be an electrician. Upon the completion of the program, our apprentices go to work armed with the skills that have, by that time, become second nature to them.

About NNY NECA

**NECA** stands for **The National Electrical Contractors Association**. This organization was founded in 1901 and is the leading representative of a segment of the construction market which is comprised of over 70,000 electrical contracting firms. The industry employs over 650,000 electrical workers and produces an annual volume of over $95 billion. NECA includes 120 U.S. chapters in addition to others in countries around the world.

NECA is dedicated to enhancing the industry through continuing **education, labor relations, current information and promotional activities**. It is the voice of the electrical contracting industry, working to promote _higher standards, quality workmanship and training for a skilled workforce._
