# BUSINESS MANAGER

Report at February Regular Meeting

U=Union/NU=Non Union

## Business Manager's Report:

Currently we have 65 Journeymen on Book I,0 on Book II,5 Apprentices,5 CE/CW's and 2 Tele-data technicians available for work.

Jobs bid in the last two weeks include: Ogdensburg Wastewater Pump Station-NYTRIC $216,900 North Elba Shelter-Rozell, Altona Town Hall-ACE (NU) $254,000,

Jobs bid in the last two weeks include (from Executive Board meeting): Rouses Point WTP Improvements-Triangle $1,204,000, ADK Fish Hatchery Santa Clara- Watson $48,500, Altona Correctional Facility-Matco.

Upcoming work includes: Wheeler Sack Airfield Lighting, Canton Boces Phase 2, Lewis County Public Safety Building Generator, St. Lawrence DOT Building, SUNY Canton Meter Upgrades Village of Canton Wastewater Treatment.

Upcoming work includes (from Executive Board meeting): Ft. Drum Gas Alley ACP Modernization, SRMT Addiction Treatment Facility, Oval Wood Dish Factory Housing Project Tupper Lake, SUNY ESF Newcombe Generator.

I was asked to speak in favor of the Sugar Maple Solar Project AES is proposing. Sugar Maple is a 125 MW solar with 20 MW of Battery Storage located near Carthage Area Hospital. We are talking with AES about having a memorandum of understanding signed that states AES will use Union Labor for this project. The earliest this project would start is in the Spring of 2027.

The Chaumont Solar Project that Swan Contracting has been working on is getting shut down for the Winter. They plan to start back up in April. There will be quite a bit of work to finish once they start this one back up.

The work outlook remains about the same we are busy in Clinton and Essex County while the rest of the jurisdiction remains slow. Local 41,43 and 237 are still very busy and are good places to sign.

I met with Watertown City manager Eric Wagenar about the upcoming $68 million Water Treatment Facility Upgrades Project. Talked about a PLA to help keep the workforce local.I had three General Contractors that said they would bid the project under a PLA.The meeting was positive they talked about their fund balance and how they would not want to do anything to increase the project cost.I followed up with an email with some more information and answers to their questions.

I want to thank all the members that were able to attend the Holiday Party. It was a nice event and all who attended seemed to have a good time.
