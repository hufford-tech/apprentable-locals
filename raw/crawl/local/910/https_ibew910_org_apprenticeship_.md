# Apprenticeship

## Apprentice

If you are interested in a rewarding career in the electrical construction industry, we can help. The Electrical Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee, [EJATC of Watertown](http://www.ibew910.org/ejatc/) provides Electrical Contractors in the Northern New York Region with skilled and knowledgeable Electricians (Inside Wireman ). EJATC of Watertown is co-sponsored by the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers ( [IBEW](http://www.ibew.org/)) Local 910 [(IBEW 910](https://ibew910.org/)) and the National Electrical Contractors Association ( [NECA](https://www.necanet.org/)) Northern New York Chapter ( [NNY NECA](http://www.nnyneca.com/)). The National Joint Apprenticeship Committee ( [NJATC](http://electricaltrainingalliance.org/)) has developed uniform standards that are adopted and used nationwide by local programs to select and train thousands of men and women each year. EJATC of Watertown provides apprentices with [tuition free classroom training](https://ibew910.org/classroom-training/) and training assignments – [Paid on the job training (earn while you learn).](https://ibew910.org/apprenticeship-training/)

As a union electrician, you will earn top wages, have access to a multi-faceted pension and retirement plan, enjoy excellent job security, and receive free ongoing training in your field.
