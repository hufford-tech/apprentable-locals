The Watertown City Courthouse Project was bid in October but rejected by the last City Council. It has been recently approved by the new City Council. Watson is the lowest bidder at $477,000.The project is now scheduled to start in February or March.

Local 41 and 237 are still very busy and good places to sign.

I have been working on our proposals for contract negotiations with Northern Credit Union. We plan to start in February.

I have been appointed to the Development Authority of The North Country (DANC). It has been quite a process, but I received my appointment from Governor Hochul and was sworn in last week. I am going to the Dulles State Office Building next Wednesday for an orientation. I am looking forward to being part of the process. There is a checklist of tasks to complete (Orientation, Ethics Training, Govt. Board Member Training). Next week I will start completing them. I will be on two committees for DANC, Project Development, and Telecommunications.

I will be attending a NYS Business Managers meeting this week in Binghamton. We will be discussing information from the State of The State Report, Lobbyist, Tri Trade Solar agreements, etc.
