### Available Jobs

These are the jobs local 577 currently have availalbe. If you are a member of the Local 577 you can bid on jobs online. Here are the instructions:

1. **Login to the website using your Last Name and Member Card Number**
2. **Navigate to the "Bid on Jobs" tab**
3. **Click on the job**
4. **If there is more than one job please list your prioritization.**
5. **Provide your contact number.**
6. **Submit**

You may also call the office at (920) 739-9408 ext. 1. Leave a detailed message, including your name, classification, card number, phone number, and the job you are interested in. The Referral Agent will begin making call backs after 9:00 am.

Local 577 members are able to log in, and place bids by selecting which jobs you would like to go to. You will get a confirmation email afterwards. Or you may call the number listed above and leave a message.

\*\*All call-backs will be made in the order of which you appear on the Out of Work Books.\*\*

You must be on the Out of Work Books in order to receive a call back.If you are not on the Out of Work Books, you will not receive a call back! Please [click here](https://www.ibewlu577.com/ReSignandProcedures/) for Re-Sign & Referral Procedures.

**The available jobs list is updated daily at 4:30pm.**
