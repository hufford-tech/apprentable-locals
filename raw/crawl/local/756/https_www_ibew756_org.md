| [**Travel Letter Requirements**](https://www.ibew756.org/?zone=/unionactive/view_article.cfm&HomeID=993244) |



|     |
| --- |
|  |





As Per our Local Union By-Laws, Article X, Sec. 4(F) Basic Dues are Payable quarterly in advance. Therefore, any member requesting a travel letter must have their dues paid at least 3 months in advance , i know we have been allowing members to pay monthly but, due to recent events, we are going to require them to be paid up 3 months in advance. We regret to have to enforce this Article. | |
