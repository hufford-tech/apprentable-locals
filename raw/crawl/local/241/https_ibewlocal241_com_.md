Apprentice

Apprenticeship begins with the Ithaca JATC., an apprenticeship program that combines the tradition of apprenticeship in a high-demand trade with a formal training program, producing the best trained electricans in the world.

[Visit Page](http://www.ithacajatc.com/)

Journeyman

The Journeyman is the foundation of the apprenticeship program. After completing their own apprenticeships, Journeyman Wiremen go on to mentor incoming apprentices, continuing the cycle of success.

Training

Apprentices learn both in the classroom and on-the-job, preparing them for a great career in the electrical construction industry. Apprentices are paired up with a Journeyman mentor, helping them become as successful as possible.
