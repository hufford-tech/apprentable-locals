## IBEW Local 567 represents the electricians of Maine.

We are proud of the tradesmen and tradeswomen, who have put the man hours into our communities to make our jurisdiction what it is today. From our highly accredited registered apprenticeship program to continued training for our Local’s journeymen, our members stay on top of their training and set the bar high for the rest of the electrical construction industry. **[Read More »](https://www.ibew567.com/about)**
