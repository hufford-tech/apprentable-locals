# Join IBEW Local 567

Are you interested in a career as a member of International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 567?

If so, it could be one of the best decisions you make in your lifetime. From great wages and generous benefits to a consistent flow of work, IBEW Local 567 membership opens up a world of opportunities.

Throughout Maine, men and women join our ranks for a myriad of reasons. Some realize the registered apprenticeship program model is the best education they can receive, while also providing the best on-the-job training. Others see great wages and good supplemental benefits, including health insurance and retirement benefits, as a way to provide for themselves and their family.

Contractors are also welcome to join our Local! We are always encouraging local electrical contractors to become signatory with Local 567 in order to increase their work opportunities and grow their company.

No matter your reason for joining, IBEW Local 567 will always work to secure a better future for you and your family!

## **Apprentices**

Learning a trade through a registered apprenticeship program is a great way to earn while you learn. Apprentices learn on-the-job from experienced journeymen, as they earn a livable wage with scheduled pay raises matching their progress through our four-year program. In addition to on-the-job training, apprentices learn in the classroom from accredited instructors at the IBEW Local 567 Portland Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee.

[Click to Learn More](https://www.ibew567.com/join-us/apprentices/)

## **Experienced Electricians**

If you are an experienced electrician, you may be comfortable in your current situation. IBEW Local 567 wants to make your life and career better. From full family health insurance, to free upgrade training opportunities, retirement benefits and more, IBEW Local 567 wants to help improve your situation!

[Click to Learn More](https://www.ibew567.com/join-us/electricians/)

## **Contractors**

As an electrical contractor, becoming signatory with IBEW Local 567 can create plenty of opportunities for your shop. Along with increased opportunities to bid on jobs, our signatory contractors also gain access to a highly trained and highly skilled workforce. Local 567 signatory contractors have greater chances of winning work and increasing their market share.

[Click to Learn More](https://www.ibew567.com/join-us/contractors/)
