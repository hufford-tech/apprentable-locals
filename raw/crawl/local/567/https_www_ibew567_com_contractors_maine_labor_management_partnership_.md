## High Standards for Productivity

A strong commitment to labor-management cooperation is at the heart of the Maine Electrical Industry and the affiliated Portland and Lewiston Electrical Contractors. We are united to bring the highest level of quality and productivity to Maine’s electrical and energy projects, which translates into efficient projects and zero work-stoppages. As you scale your business and projects, we will provide an expandable workforce, drawing on our network of skilled trades people from across the country.

### How Can We Help?

As a general contractor, architect or business owner, you need to build your next industrial, commercial, institutional or residential project on time and under budget. Our contractors keep their sophisticated shops up-to-date with state-of-the-art equipment. We bring innovative and creativity to the design, manufacture and installation of the most complex building and energy systems across America. This training and education can only be provided by the IBEW, and ensures the highest quality work for diverse construction requirements throughout the region. For all assignments – large or small – we assure you that all of our building projects are completed safely and efficiently.

### Unparalleled Productivity

IBEW affiliated contractors and the electrician craftsmen they employ meet or surpass all contract requirements on time, to specifications and with an outstanding degree of safety.

### The Standards We Keep

The affiliated electricians of Portland and Lewiston, Maine will demonstrate quality work on each assignment. We ensure your building projects are completed in the most efficient and safe manner, every time. Please contact us with your questions and project specifics!
