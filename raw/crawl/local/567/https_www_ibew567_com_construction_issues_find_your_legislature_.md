## Who are my elected officials?

It is crucial for IBEW Local 567 members to know the names, records and stances of the elected officials, who represent them. Your legislators make decisions on construction issues such as Prevailing Wage, So-Called “Right to Work” and more. They also decide what projects receive funding. Electing worker-minded legislators is important to the survival of our Union, the quality of work our members perform and the benefits our members enjoy.
