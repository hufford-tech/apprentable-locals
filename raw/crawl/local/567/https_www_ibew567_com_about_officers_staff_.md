# **IBEW Local 567 Officers and Staff**

The leadership of our union is what keeps IBEW Local 567 strong. As union members, we bargain collectively with our employers over issues that are important; wages, benefits and rights.

Our officers and staff are here to serve our hard-working members who make up IBEW Local 567.
