# IBEW Local 567 Needs Experienced Electricians

The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 567 is seeking experienced electricians to join our membership. Men and women, who have Commercial/Industry experience and become members of IBEW Local 567, will gain access to competitive wages, great family health insurance, excellent retirement benefits such as a pension and 401K, continued education and quality safety training.

## Why Join A Union?

- On average, union workers earn higher wages and enjoy better health and retirement benefits than non-workers, who do not have a voice on the job.
- We all know people in our industry, who worked under a union contract and left to work a non-union job. Now, they cannot believe how much more they must pay for medical benefits.
- Union membership secures your retirement. Part of the IBEW Local 567 benefits package includes a full pension negotiated into our total package.
- Workers formed unions so they could have some say over wages, hours, working conditions and the many other problems, which arise in the relationship between a worker and employer.
- Unions give workers a voice with their employers and provide a means to gain a measure of security and dignity on the job.
- Unions exist to put power in the hands of workers, so they can act in their own interests and restore their footing in the middle class.
- Union workers continually set the highest standards in job performance, quality and safety.
