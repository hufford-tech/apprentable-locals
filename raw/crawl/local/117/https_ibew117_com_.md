## Serving Members Since 1901

Local #117 is an Inside local and was chartered by the IBEW in 1901.  Local #117’s jurisdiction includes all of McHenry County and the northern 9 townships of Kane County, all in the State of Illinois.

Our classifications include Journeyman Inside Wireman, Residential Wireman and VDV Installer/Technician and Manufacturing.

**All Book signings are in person.**

**The Local’s general meeting is on the first Wednesday of each month at 5:00 pm at the Union Hall.**
