# requirements for joining Inside/outside

Please reach us at [Ibewlu676@yahoo.com](mailto:Ibewlu676@yahoo.com) if you cannot find an answer to your question.

INSIDE-

**\*Minimum inside hours 12,000 without certificate (Apprenticeship)verified through login.gov or ID.me (THESE TWO SITES ARE THE ONLY SITES THAT PROOF OF HOURS WILL BE ACCEPTED/ NO EXCEPTIONS. LETTERHEADS WILL NOT BE ACCEPTED)**

**\\* 8-10,000 hours with certificate, depending on which apprenticeship attended.**

**\\* Physical Driver's License with address in our jurisdiction, as well as 2 forms of mail with matching address. (Unless you are working in our jurisdiction, and can provide REAL paystubs & employment verification)**

**\*Certified Apprenticeship Certificates MUST have proper signatures & contact number for apprenticeship you attended.**

* * *

OUTSIDE-

**\*12,000 Hours verified through login.gov or ID.me**

**(THESE TWO SITES ARE THE ONLY SITES THAT PROOF OF HOURS WILL BE ACCEPTED/ NO EXCEPTIONS. LETTERHEADS WILL NOT BE ACCEPTED)**

**\\* Physical Driver's License with address in our jurisdiction, as well as 2 forms of mail with matching address. (Unless you are working in our jurisdiction, and can provide REAL paystubs & employment verification)**

**\*Certified Apprenticeship Certificates MUST have proper signatures & contact number for apprenticeship you attended.**

* * *
