## IBEW L.U. 640 Journeyman Wireman Exam

This exam will consist of 30 written questions worth 1 point each and 7 hands on tests worth 10 points each.

30 Field questions

You must pass both Conduit bending and a total of at least 4 other boards for the Hands on portion.

Questions not completed will be counted as incorrect.

Cell phones are not permitted during testing. You must turn them in prior to beginning the test. Failure to do so may result in being asked to leave the examination and return at a later date.

There is to be no talking during the test. If you need to clarify a question, raise your hand and a proctor will assist you.

Once completed, the results of the written exam will be combined with your scores on the hands on portion and reviewed by the examiner.

You must obtain a minimum passing combined score of 75 points including:

• 30 general Questions
• Conduit Bending Board 1 (10 Points) - MUST PASS
• Conduit Bending Board 2 (10 Points) - MUST PASS
• Transformers (10 Points)
• 3 Way / 4 Way switch connection (10 Points)
• GFCI / Isolated Grounding (10 Points)
• Motor Leads Hookup (10 Points)
• Start / Stop Stations (10 Points)

You will be officially notified the day of the exam if you passed or failed

If you fail to pass the examination you will be notified via e-mail and mail with assigned classwork and/or labs that you will be required to pass prior to receiving your JW ticket.
