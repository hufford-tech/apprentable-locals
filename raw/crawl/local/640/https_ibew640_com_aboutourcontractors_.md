##### **About Our Contractors**

##### **"Our partnership with our electrical contractors and our joint commitment to quality, professionalism, and productivity provides our customers with the best overall value in the electrical industry!"**

##### _**James Crutchfield--IBEW Local 640 Business Manager**_

##### When you are looking for a reliable, competent electrical contractor for your next project, ask them where they get their electricians, how they are trained, and if they are provided with a good living wage and benefits for their families. Our signatory contractors are licensed, bonded, and insured, and use only the best skilled electricians who are trained and qualified through our privately funded apprenticeship, or who have passed our comprehensive qualification examination. Our members give a fair day's work for a fair day's pay, and that means higher productivity and efficiency, and a better end product on your job. With more spendable income, good healthcare, and secure retirement benefits, IBEW electricians don't cost our communities and our state, they contribute. To find out more we welcome you to continue to explore our website.
