**Overtime:** Time and one-half Monday through Saturday.  All work on Sundays and Holidays is at double time.

**Work Performed at Heights:** 50' to 100' - 110% of base wage, 100' to 200' - 150% of base  wage, 200' and over -  200% of base wage

*1 -* ***Second Shift is 110% of the base wage, and Third Shift is 115% of the base wage.***

*2 - First foreman on job - not less than 110% of the base wage.  When two (2) or more Foremen - not less than 113%  of the base wage.*

*3 -* ***NEBF*** *is 3% of gross monthly payroll and increases with overtime.*

*4 - The* ***NECA Serivce Charge*** *is 0.3% gross labor payroll.*  ***NECA Contractors Only***

*5 -* ***COPE*** *and* ***Assessment*** *are* ***only withheld when written authorization*** *is given by a dues paying member.*
