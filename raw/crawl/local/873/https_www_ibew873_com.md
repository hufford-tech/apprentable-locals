      (Updated 8/10/2026)



      **Referral Hours are Monday-Friday from 7am-10am**



      **IBEW 873 Job Line (765) 457-5371**



      **JW Wage- $44.20/hr**



      **H&W- $10.11/hr**



      **DC Pension (Pyramids)-** **$13.00/hr**



      **\*BENEFITS MAY TAKE UP TO 90 DAYS THROUGH ERTS TO DEPOSIT IN YOUR HOME FUNDS\***



      **For benefits question please call:**



      **NECA-IBEW (Health & Welfare): 1-800-765-4239 (Option 6)**



      **IBEW 873 Retirement Plan: 317-715-7433 (John Dill or Scott Hancock)**



      **\*Travel Letter required to Sign Book\***







      * * *



      **NEW CALLS****(Filled by bid/book position. MUST call Jobline and bid on call no later than 9am the day after the call is listed to be considered. Referral pickup will be scheduled)**



      **\*NO NEW CALLS\***



      * * *



      **OPEN CALLS(Filled in person between 7a-10a Mon-Fri on 1st come,1st serve basis)**



      **\*NO OPEN CALLS\***







      * * *



      **Referral Requirements**



      **\*2 pcs of i.d that meet I-9 requirements\***



      **\\* Bank Info (If direct deposit is preferred)\***



      **\*5 panel drug test within 1 year (Unless customer requirements differ)\***



      **\*Starplus calls require 10 panel drug test within 30 days\***







      **StarPlus Energy Battery Plant -**This is a $7.6 billion joint venture between Stellantis and Samsung in Kokomo. Lines 3/4 of Phase 1 (Clean room area- details below) are currently in progress. No cameras allowed on site, sticker will be placed over phone camera. Phase 2 (new facility) is expected to start late 2026.



    - Huston Electric - **ALL OT is 2X**. Clean room suit/mask. Clean room boots required (Leather, above the ankle, electrical hazard rated) Boots will be provided or reimbursed at $150. Boots must be left on site.
    - Samyoung - Clean room shoes required (provided by Samyoung). Must be left on site.
    - Stelko - **ALL OT is 2X**. Clean room boots required. Boots must be left on site.

**Haynes International-** This is a $170 million expansion that includes the addition of a vacuum induction melting system and a new radial forging system. Construction is expected to start 3Q 2026.

**Howard County Jail-** This is a $130 million new construction project. The project will cover 46 acres near Dixon/Markland Ave in Kokomo. The new jail is designed to have a capacity of 1,056 beds. Construction is expected to start 3Q 2026. | |
