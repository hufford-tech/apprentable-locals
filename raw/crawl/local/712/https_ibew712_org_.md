MEMBERSHIP WITH IBEW LOCAL UNION 712

By providing the services that Local 712 members need and deserve, we hope to help each of our members secure the life and career they desire. That is why we work together to address and promote the issues that affect our industry and our union – issues such as organizing, political action, training, safety, wages and benefits.
