**Why YOU Should Be Politically Aware**

As an IBEW Local 712 member, it is important to stay politically aware. Being an active participant in our country effects you now and in the future both politically and socially.

- Your participation counts!
  - Being involved impacts your future and younger generations
- You do have political power!
- You will understand why people support certain parties.
  - Communicating and sharing ideas comes from understanding different views.
- Being locally, nationally, and globally aware will help in making appropriate decisions.
