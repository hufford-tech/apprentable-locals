Local Union 712 Is The Right Choice When It Comes To Electrical Projects

Every electrical job is unique, and each has its distinct challenges. Our highly qualified electricians have exstensive experience which allows them to handle any challenge that could possibly occur on the job site. IBEW Local Union 712 is helping to power Western Pennsylvania everyday!

![](https://ibew712.org/mt-content/uploads/2018/11/r_2018-11-07-19-09-40.jpg)

**Hickory Run Power Station**

In Edinburg, PA, Bruce and Merrilees Electric Company is working on the Hickory Run Power Station.

MEMBERSHIP

WHY SHOULD YOU JOIN A UNION?

* * *

![](https://ibew712.org/mt-content/uploads/2018/03/img_1138_2017-11-16-17-29-51_2018-03-07-19-19-53.jpg)

EMPLOYMENT CONTRACTS

Local 712 forms employment contracts with employers on behalf of our members. Any changes must be negotiated. Having employment agreements generally enables job security.

[MORE INFO](https://ibew712.org/organizing/)

![](https://ibew712.org/mt-content/uploads/2018/03/benefits_2018-03-07-19-20-47.jpg)

ADVANTAGES OF BECOMING A MEMBER

Working for a union will allow you to work beside some of the best electricians in the country. You will have access to employment with multiple contractors. Qualifications that you earn are accepted throughout the United States giving you an increase in opportunities and so much more.

[MORE INFO](https://ibew712.org/membership/)
