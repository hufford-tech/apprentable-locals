[**CONTRACTOR KEY BENEFITS**](https://ibew712.org/contractors/) ​​​​​​​

Manpower

Qualified electricians are just a phone call away. When you have a large quantity of work, you simply call the union hall and request the number of skilled and trained employees needed to get the job done. When work is slow, your company can easily send your excess employees back to the union hall.

Training

Our apprentice electricians serve in a Pennsylvania State approved five-year program. They receive on-the-job-training, as well as 185 hours of hands-on-training in a classroom environment per year. They are exposed to all parts of the electrical trade in order to maximize their knowledge when becoming a journeyman. The program also provides training to all classifications of electricians. Courses are regularly updated to include new codes and technologies. The outcome is an employee who is well trained in all aspects of the electrical construction industry.

Stability

A collective bargaining agreement ensures you will have the peace of mind in knowing precisely what your labor costs will be from year to year for a specified period of time. Collective bargaining agreements are usually three years in length, and are negotiated between union and employer representatives. When working with us, you are guaranteed a partnership for success!

Safety

Our members are trained in OSHA 10, NFPA 70E, ICRA and several other electrical safety related programs. This training is provided at no cost to you, that way our members are ready to work as soon as they are called out to the job-site.

Workforce Quality

Our members are participants in the “Code of Excellence Program”. This advanced new program guarantees that you are 100% satisfied with the union electricians on your job-sites. We also provide a drug-free workforce program run by an independent third party. Click to view the [Code of Excellence Program](https://ibew712.org/#code).
