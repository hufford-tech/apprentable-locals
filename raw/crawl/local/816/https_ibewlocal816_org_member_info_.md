## Announcements

### New requirements to renew your State Electrical License

First thing, if have not received your HBC ID#, you will need to either email [eleclicensing@ky.gov](mailto:eleclicensing@ky.gov) or call the electrical licensing office [502-782-7800](tel:5027827800) to receive this number.

Step by Step Instructions:

1\. Access the Portal:

The official website for the online licensing portal is [https://dhbc.ky.gov/HBCOnline/login](https://dhbc.ky.gov/HBCOnline/login). This portal is used for license renewals, document uploads, and payment of fees.

2. Creating a New Account:

Once you are at the portal site [https://dhbc.ky.gov/HBCOnline/login](https://dhbc.ky.gov/HBCOnline/login) you will click create a new account and then you will be guided through several steps to setup your account.

If you would like a detailed PDF guide explaining the steps on how to create a portal account and renew a license on-line, please contact:

- Phone:
  - JATC Office at [270-575-9646](tel:2705759646)
- Email:
  - Jarrod Shadowen, JATC Training Director at [jarrod.shadowen@padjatc.com](mailto:jarrod.shadowen@padjatc.com)
  - Beau Barrett, JATC Asst. Training Director at [beau.barrett@padjatc.com](mailto:beau.barrett@padjatc.com)

### 2025 International Dues Increase

Accordingly, effective July 1, 2025 there will be an increase of one dollar ($1.00) for the I.B.E.W. per capita, which applies to **“A” membership ONLY** **!** **This will increase July – December 2025 dues for “A” membership to $52.00 monthly.**

The following rates will apply to the International Office and Local #816 portions of all dues payments for July 2025 through December 2025.

**“A” Members —$52.00 per month**

Breakdown

Per Capita –  $24.00/month

Pension Fund – $23.00/month

Local Dues – $5.00/month

**“BA” Members —$30.00 per month**

Breakdown

Per Capita $24.00/month

Pension Fund – N/A

Local Dues – $ 6.00/month

_In accordance with Article IX of the IBEW Constitution, $24.00 per month from each “A” member will be deposited in the IBEW General Fund, and the additional amount paid by “A” members ($23.00 per month) will be deposited into the IBEW Pension Benefit Fund._
