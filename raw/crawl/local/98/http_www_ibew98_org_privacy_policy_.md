# Privacy Policy

This policy establishes how IBEW Local 98 will use information that IBEW Local 98 collects and receives through our web sites.

IBEW Local 98 Receives two types of information when you visit the web site. First IBEW Local 98 automatically receives and records information on our server logs from each visitor’s web browser including the IP address and the page(s) requested. This information, while useful, does not provide any Personal Information to IBEW Local 98 and is not within the scope of this policy. Secondly IBEW Local 98 receives personal information you choose to provide. Personal information is information about you that is personally identifiable like your name, address, email address, social security number, union card number, phone number or any other information that is not otherwise publicly available.

Once you register with IBEW Local 98 and sign in to our services you are no longer anonymous to us. IBEW Local 98 DOES collect Personal Information when you sign the IBEW Local 98 guest book, join our members-only section, purchase products from our on-line store or access your credit union account. IBEW Local 98 also collects information about your ongoing transactions that occur through our site once you have registered to one of the above services.

IBEW Local 98 uses information internally for the following general purposes:

Fulfilling your requests for services

Improving our services

Contacting you for Local 98 business

Conducting internal research

Providing internal reports

IBEW Local 98 takes the privacy of your personal information very seriously and will use your information only in accordance with the terms of this Privacy Policy. We restrict access to your personally identifying information to direct employees of IBEW Local 98 who need to know that information in order to operate, develop or improve our services. We take appropriate security measures in having physical, electronic, and procedural safeguards to protect against unauthorized alteration, disclosure or destruction of data.

IBEW Local 98 WILL NOT rent, sell or share any information about you with any other person or organization unless:

We are responding to subpoenas, court order, warrant, levy, attachment, legal process or to establish or exercise our legal rights or defend against legal claims;

We are advised it is necessary to share information in order to investigate, prevent, or take action regarding illegal activities, suspected fraud, situations involving potential threats to the safety of any person, violations of IBEW Local 98’s terms of use or as otherwise required by law.

Please note this Privacy Policy may change from time to time. We expect most such changes to be minor, but there may be changes that are more significant. Regardless, we will post changes on our web site and if the changes are significant, we will also provide a more prominent notice.
