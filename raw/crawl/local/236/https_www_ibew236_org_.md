## IBEW Benefit Funds

## Health & Benefits

The IBEW Local 236 **Health and Benefit Fund** is a **Health Reimbursement Account (HRA**), which is a modified personal account plan. For each hour you work in covered employment, employer contributions will be made to the non-taxable, medical expenses only **HRA** Plan.

[Learn More](http://www.ibew236funds.org/health-benefits/)

## Pension Fund

The NECA-IBEW Pension Trust Fund (Pension Trust Fund) was established on June 1, 1971 and became effective on March 14, 1972. A plan year begins on July 1 and ends on June 30 each year. The Plan Year is the period on which the Plan keeps its annual records.

[Learn More](http://www.ibew236funds.org/pension/)

## Annuity Fund

The Annuity Fund acts as a buffer in times of need when you are working – through supplemental benefits when out of work or disabled, educational reimbursements for you & your dependents.

[Learn More](http://www.ibew236funds.org/annuity/)
