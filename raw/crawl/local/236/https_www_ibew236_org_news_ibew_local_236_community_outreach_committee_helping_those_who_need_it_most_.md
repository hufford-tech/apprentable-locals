## IBEW Local 236 Community Outreach Committee – Helping those who need it most.

Thu Mar 12

Our members aren’t just great electricians – they’re great people in the community. Frequently our Community Outreach Committee performs volunteer work to benefit those who need it most; from serving food at soup kitchens to the homeless and hungry, to sponsoring struggling families and bringing them a great holiday season, to working with organizations like Big Brothers Big Sisters. We want to show the world why being a part of our organization is the right choice for not only the best quality electrical work, but also the people who care and give back.
