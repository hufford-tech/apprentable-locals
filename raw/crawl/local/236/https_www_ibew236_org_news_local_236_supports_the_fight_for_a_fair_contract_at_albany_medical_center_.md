## Local 236 supports the fight for a fair contract at Albany Medical Center

Wed Oct 9

![](https://www.ibew236.org/wp-content/uploads/IMG_20190925_074011.jpg)

On September 25, 2019 Local 236 rallied with the nurses at Albany Medical Center to fight for a fair contract.  It has been a long road and a hard fight for them since their decision to organize in early April 2018.  The overwhelming support provided by the building trade Unions and the public invigorated them and demonstrated to the hospital administration that Albany really is **UNION STRONG!** Thank you to all of those who supported this movement!
