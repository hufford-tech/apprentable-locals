# About Us

![singlePostImage](https://www.ibew445.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmedia.linkedunion.com%2Fapps%2Fibew445%2Fnews%2FFF1%2520copy.png&w=3840&q=75)

At IBEW Local 445, we pride ourselves on fostering a strong sense of solidarity among our members, promoting excellence in craftsmanship, and ensuring the highest standards of safety in the electrical industry. Our union is built on the principles of fairness, equality, and mutual support.

Through collective bargaining, training programs, and ongoing advocacy efforts, we strive to provide our members with the tools, resources, and opportunities they need to thrive in their careers. We believe in empowering our members to achieve their full potential and uphold the values of professionalism, integrity, and solidarity.

Whether you're an experienced electrician, an apprentice just starting out, or someone interested in joining our union, we welcome you to IBEW Local 445. Join us as we continue to build a stronger, more prosperous future for electrical workers in our community.
