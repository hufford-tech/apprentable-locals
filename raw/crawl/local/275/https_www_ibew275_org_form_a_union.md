## Bring the IBEW to your Contractor

### Representation

The IBEW represents over 800,000 working folks across all sectors of the electrical industry in the US and Canada.  800K workers with a Union that fights for their livelihoods. 800K workers with a Union that has their back!

### Contract

The only difference between a non union electrical worker and an an IBEW electrical worker is a contract.  IBEW members have their wages, benefits, schedules, working conditions, etc. spelled out in black and white!  A contract that's negotiated by and for the members!  Any changes are negotiated!

### Dignity

Do you want to retire in dignity?  With a Union, you would have the power to negotiate for real retirement savings, not just a lousy 401K.  IBEW electricians get hourly contributions to  defined benefit pensions and an annuity.  All collectively bargained for and the contributions are guaranteed in a contract!

### Power

When we stand together in solidarity, we have more power. We have bargaining power. We have economic power. We have power in security.  We create leverage.  We are our Brothers and Sisters keeper.  We have each other's backs!  United we bargain, divided we beg!!
