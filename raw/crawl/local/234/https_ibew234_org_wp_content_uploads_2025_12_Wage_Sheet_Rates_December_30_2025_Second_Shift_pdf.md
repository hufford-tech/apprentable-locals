NOTE:  Section 3.16 SHIFT WORK.  The second shift (swing shift) shall be worked between the hours of 4:30 P.M. and 12:30 A.M. Workmen on the "swing shift" 
shall receive eight (8) hours of pay at the regular hourly rate plus 10% for seven and one half (7 1/2) hours of work.

A Lunch period of thirty (30) minutes shall be allowed on each shift.

NOTE:  Health & Welfare, Education and Training, LMCC and Pension are paid on hours PAID, not hours worked.  (Must reflect the overtime hours)

NOTE:  Working Dues 6%
