# MEMBER FORMS

### **Travelers:**

**To get on the out-of-work books please fill out forms A, B, C, & D.**

_Form D you will need to upload current dues receipt, travel letter, photo ID, and your certifications._

### **Local 234 Members:**

**To get on the out-of-work books please fill out form B.**

### **Incoming on Portability:**

**Please fill out forms A, C, & D.**

_Upload current dues receipt, driver’s license and any certifications._

### **Book Removal:**

**Please fill out form E.**

_It is the responsibility of applicants to remove themselves from the Out-of-Work list when they have taken another job and/or are unable to take a call._

## Request A meetinG:

**Please fill out form F.**

_Members who want to schedule a meeting with Jose Munoz, Trudi Teller, John Barber, or Natasha Marsiguerra._

## [A: Information Card Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/information-card)

[![](https://ibew234.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Information-card-2023-1.jpg)](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/information-card)

The Information Card Form is general information on the individual in order to create a profile within the IBEW 234 database.

[Fill Out Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/information-card "")

## [B: Sign In Form/Re-sign](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/sign-in-form)

[![](https://ibew234.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/08/sign-in-Re-sign-1.jpg)](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/sign-in-form)

The Sign-In Form is what will put the individual onto the out of work books. If you are out of work and need to be added to the books, please fill out this form.

[Fill Out Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/sign-in-form "")

## [C: Dues Authorization Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/dues-check-off-authorization)

[![](https://ibew234.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Dues-Checkoff-2023-1.jpg)](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/dues-check-off-authorization)

The Dues Authorization Form acknowledges that the individual understands working dues will be deducted from their paycheck. Please fill out the form, and if you have questions, reach out to the hall.

[Fill Out Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/dues-check-off-authorization "")

## [D: Members Upload Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/member-upload-form)

[![](https://ibew234.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/member-upload-2023-1.jpg)](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/member-upload-form)

For the Member Uploads form, please attach any uploads that you need to include from other forms that are placed on this page.

[Fill Out Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/member-upload-form "")

## [E: Book Removal Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/removal-form)

[![](https://ibew234.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/Book-removal-2023-1.jpg)](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/removal-form)

Applicants shall be responsible for removing themselves from the Out-of-Work List.

Please submit this form to have your name removed.

[Fill Out Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/removal-form "")

## [F: Request A Meeting Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/request-a-meeting)

[![](https://ibew234.org/wp-content/uploads/2023/12/meeting-request-2023.jpg)](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/request-a-meeting)

Members who want to schedule a meeting with Jose Munoz, Trudi Teller, John Barber, or Natasha Marsiguerra.

Please submit this form if you want to schedule a meeting.

[Fill Out Form](https://form.jotform.com/ibew234/request-a-meeting "")
