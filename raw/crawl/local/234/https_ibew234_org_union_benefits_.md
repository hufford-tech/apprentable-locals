# Union Benefits

## Health and Welfare

Medical, vision and dental insurance are provided for members based on hourly contributions. Each member and his/her immediate family are covered according to requirements.

[**More Info…**](https://ibew234.org/health-and-welfare-plan/)

## Retirement

Local 234 members participate in a Defined Contribution Plan through the Ninth District Pension Plan and the National Electrical Benefit Fund.

[**More Info…**](https://ibew234.org/retirement/)

## Workers’ Compensation

Learn more about the Workers’ Compensation benefits provided through the National Electrical Contractors Association/International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Workers’ Compensation Trust Fund.

[**More Info…**](https://ibew234.org/workers-compension/)

## National Electrical 401(K) Plan (NEFP)

The 401(k) plan is an optional resource available to you in addition to the other benefits you receive as an IBEW member. Local 234 levels of participation are $2.00 per hour, $5.00 per hour, or $8.00 per hour. Selection can be made at dispatch. This voluntary plan is a way to save for your future while taking advantage of a variety of perks. For directions on how to enroll [click here](https://ibew234.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/05/NEFP-Enrollment-Instructions.pdf)

## Education and Training (JATC)

Our training center is located at 10300 Merritt Street in Castroville. Apprentices attend classes two nights a week. Time and frequency of journeyman upgrade and continuing education classes vary. Contact the [JATC](https://www.tricountyjatc.org/) at (831) 741-9069 for more details.

## Labor-Management Cooperation Committee (LMCC)

The LMCC participates in a Labor-Management Cooperation Fund under authority of Section 6(b) of the Labor Management Cooperation Act of 1978, 29 U.S.C. §175(a) and Section 302(c)(9) of the Labor Management Relations Act, 29 U.S.C. §186(c)(9). The LMCC is comprised of (three) management representatives and (three) labor representatives.
