# Health and Welfare Plan

Local 234 members can check their Health and Welfare benefits and eligibility at [www.ibew234benefits.org](http://www.ibew234benefits.org/). A personal login and password is required.

Contact Benesys, our third party administrator, for assistance. On this portal you can view benefits, work history, current eligibility and more.

Other Health and Welfare information includes website links, addresses and phone numbers for all Local 234 trades.
