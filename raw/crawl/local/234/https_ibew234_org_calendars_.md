| **Floating Holidays**<br>- Friday, May 22nd, 2026- Memorial Day<br>- Friday September 4th, 2026- Labor Day |

Two additional days each year shall be selected by mutual agreement as designated “Off Days.” Designated Off Days shall be paid at time and one-half the straight time rate. Selection of Off Days will be coordinated with other Holidays to make four-day weekends or align with Holidays with other Building Trades.

Any of the above designated Holidays occurring on Sunday shall be observed on the following Monday. Any of the above designated Holidays occurring on Saturday shall be observed the previous Friday.
