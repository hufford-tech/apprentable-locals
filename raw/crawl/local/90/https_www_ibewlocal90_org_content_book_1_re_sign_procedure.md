# Book 1 Re-sign procedure

Applicants, who have registered on the Out of Work List, must resign **between the 10th and the 16th of every month.**

**PROCEDURES TO RE-SIGN**

1) Call John Bellemare at **(203) 265-9533 ext. 5**leave your name and request to re-sign Book 1

**OR**

2) In person at 2 N Plains Industrial Rd, Wallingford, CT 06492

**OR**

3)  Fax **(203) 265-9422** with your name and request to sign

**OR**

4) Email **[info@ibewlocal90.org](mailto:info@ibewlocal90.org)**

(You will need to be [logged in](https://ibewlocal90.org/user))
