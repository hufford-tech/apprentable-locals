# IBEW 102 125TH CELEBRATION

* * *

May 17th was truly unforgettable as Local 102 came together to celebrate 125 years of unity and achievement! At the Palace at Somerset Park, generations of members—young and seasoned—gathered with family and guests to honor our proud legacy and look ahead with excitement.

The evening was alive with joy and camaraderie as familiar faces reconnected. The Nerds and Sweet Tea Trio energized the crowd with live music, while Harrison Kramer amazed guests with captivating street magic. Clear skies set the stage for a dazzling fireworks display, and delicious food and drinks kept spirits high.

Events like this remind us why our bond is so strong. We've captured the highlights in a special memory collection—featuring our 13-minute history video, sponsor slideshow, commercial, event photos, and more. We hope you enjoy reliving this special night!
