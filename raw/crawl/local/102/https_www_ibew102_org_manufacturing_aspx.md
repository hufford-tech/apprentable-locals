## Manufacturing

Manufacturing has been front and center as "Made in America" has seen a much needed rebirth in our country. At Local 102 we are proud to partner with companies who choose to call our area home and make quality products right in our back yard. Mike Scotto heads up our manufacturing division and his experience in this sector runs deep. Mike served as Business Manager of Local 2066 before his Local Union found a home under the roof of IBEW Local 102. Mike has forged many relationships over the years and we will continue to build on that foundation.

If you have any questions about our manufacturing division please do not hesitate to contact Mike Scotto at our Parsippany office, 973-887-1718, or you may reach him by e-mail at mscotto@ibew102.org.

##### About us

For 125 years, the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers' Local 102 has been powering progress and
lighting the way for communities throughout New Jersey and Pennsylvania. Founded on the principles of unity, craftsmanship, and
innovation, Local 102 has built a legacy of excellence.
