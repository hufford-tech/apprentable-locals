# book II re-sign

Members requesting to re-sign Book II must review the official re-sign procedures prior to submitting a request. Re-signs are only accepted during the designated monthly window and must comply with Local 400’s established guidelines.

Please review the rules carefully before completing the form below. Incomplete or ineligible submissions will not be processed.

[View Re-Sign Rules](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6883df1dd5b7323fb1fc6a65/6998b56d36ca84f683283aac_Resign%20Rules.pdf)
