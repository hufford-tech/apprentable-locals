# Classifications & Jurisdiction

The following is our territorial jurisdiction by work type and geography.

  Inside Wireman

Monmouth & Ocean Counties

  Outside Lineman

Monmouth & Ocean Counties (excluding utility provider work)

  CE / CW

State of New Jersey

  VDV Installers teledata

State of New Jersey

  Sign Wireman

Sussex, Passaic, Bergen, Essex, Morris, Warren, Hudson, Hunterdon, Somerset, Union, Middlesex, Mercer, and Monmouth Counties, also the northern part of Ocean County to Toms River
