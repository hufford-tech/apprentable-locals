## Training for Tomorrow’s Electricians

Our apprenticeship combines **paid, full-time on-the-job training with weekly classroom and lab instruction.** Apprentices learn real-world skills—from conduit bending and high-voltage splicing to fire alarm installations—that prepare them for lifelong careers in the electrical industry.

  Crafted Here

## Modern Facilities

Our JATC offers a college-level environment with **advanced classrooms, high-end tools, and specialized labs** for hands-on training. Outdoors, apprentices practice on solar rooftops, manholes, and a utility pole yard for live high-voltage work.

**Training continues beyond apprenticeship** with ongoing journeyman education, ensuring members stay sharp and ready for an evolving industry.

## Programs Offered

Our training programs are designed to prepare the next generation of electricians with the **skills, safety practices, and knowledge needed to succeed.** Whether you’re starting your career or advancing your expertise, our programs provide **hands-on experience, classroom instruction, and mentorship** from industry professionals.

## Why Training Matters

**Our training is about more than learning a trade.** It’s about building a lifelong career. At IBEW Local 400 JATC, we combine hands-on experience with classroom instruction to prepare electricians who are leaders in safety, skill, and innovation.

**Every graduate leaves with the knowledge and confidence to excel in today’s electrical industry.**
