# meetings & holidays

## Meetings

All meetings will be held at [the Local 400 Union Hall.](https://maps.app.goo.gl/uNBMCZKggQ1Fh2nf7)

 **Local 400 General Meetin** g

4th Wednesday of the month at 6 p.m.

 **Executive Board Meeting**

2nd Wednesday of the month at 4:30 p.m.

 **Joint Apprenticeship & Training Committee (JATC)**

3rd Monday of the month at 4 p.m.

 **Local 400 Retirees Committee Meeting**

1st Tuesday of every month at 12 p.m.
