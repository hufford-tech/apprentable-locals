# Who We Are

The IBEW Local 400 JATC is committed to shaping the next generation of electricians through hands-on training, classroom education, and safety programs.

![Two men wearing safety glasses working on electrical wiring with metal electrical boxes on a workbench in a workshop.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6883df1dd5b7323fb1fc6a65/6965618307a1474e9dfa58af_139-YMP08024.avif)

##  **About the IBEW Local 400 JATC**

## Powering Careers

**For over 100 years, IBEW Local 400 stands by its long-standing promise of training and safety.** Our Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (JATC) is the cornerstone of that commitment—delivering **cutting-edge education to thousands of apprentices and journeymen** throughout Monmouth and Ocean Counties. We also proudly partner with N.E.A.T. to offer lineman training.
