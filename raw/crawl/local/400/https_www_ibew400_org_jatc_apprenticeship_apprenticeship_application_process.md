## Basic Requirements (2027-2028 Apprenticeship Program)

**Application Period:** January 1, 2026 through December 31, 2026

Applicants must meet all of the following requirements at the time of application:

**Eligibility Requirements:**

- **Age:** Must be at least 18 years old at time of application
- **License:** Must possess a valid **New Jersey Driver’s License**
- **Residency:** Must reside in **Monmouth or Ocean County**


  - Address on driver’s license must **match mailing address**. P.O. Boxes are not accepted.

**Education:**

Applicants must be a high school graduate or possess a **GED, Associate Degree, or higher.** Applicants must have successfully **completed one full year of high school Algebra or an equivalent post–high school Algebra course** with a **minimum grade of 75%.** CLEP testing will not be accepted.

Applicants who do not meet the Algebra requirement may satisfy this requirement by successfully completing the **TECH Math course** through [www.skillsprep.org](http://www.skillsprep.org/). Applicants choosing this option must upload a copy of the TECH Math completion certificate with their application at the time of submission.

‍ **Aptitude Test**

**Applicants must receive a qualifying score on the aptitude test.** The aptitude test is not administered on the same day the application is completed. Once an application has been processed, applicants will be notified by email of the date, time, and location of the test. Applicants who do not receive a qualifying score and wish to retake the aptitude test must reapply during the next application period.

The aptitude test is administered at **IBEW Local Union 400**, located at 3301 State Highway 138, Wall, NJ 07719.

**Physical Condition:**

Applicants must be physically capable of performing the **rigorous work** of the electrical trade. If selected, they will be required to complete a **substance-abuse screening, a physical examination, and a background check.**

**Items Required at Time of Application** **‍**

**‍** You must bring all of the following when applying in person:

(All uploaded documents must be clear and legible.)

1. **Headshot-style photograph** of the applicant
2. Copy of **birth certificate**
3. **DD-214** (required for military veterans only)
4. Copy of current **New Jersey Driver’s License**
5. **$100.00** non-refundable application fee

**Official Transcripts**

**‍** Applicants must have **official high school** and **college transcripts** (if applicable), showing courses completed and grades earned, **sent before the application deadline.** Transcripts are considered official only when sent directly from the issuing school and will not be accepted if submitted by the applicant.

Official transcripts or other official educational documentation must be sent to one of the following:

Local 400 JATC

P.O. Box 1256

Wall, NJ 07719

Email: [local400jatc@ibew400.org](https://www.ibew400.org/jatc/apprenticeship/apprenticeship-application-process#)

Applicants are encouraged to request transcripts from their high school and college at least one month before the application deadline.

**Important Notice**

Applicants must meet all eligibility requirements and submit all required information and documentation **no later than** **30 days from the start of the application** to proceed with the application process. Transcripts must be received by the JATC no later than 30 days after the application submission date. **Failure to comply will result in disqualification from the application process.**

**Equal Opportunity Statement**

IBEW Local 400 will not discriminate against apprenticeship applicants or apprentices based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex (including pregnancy and gender identity), sexual orientation, genetic information or because they are an individual with a disability or a person 40 year old or older.

## **Application Process**

Applications are completed online only on the **first Thursday of March and November**. The application link will be available between **9:00 a.m. and 7:00 p.m.** New applicants will be required to create an account. If you are reapplying use your current account.
