# F.A.Q.’s About being a Union Contractor

Q) IF I WORK WITH THE IBEW WILL I BE 
GIVING UP MY RIGHTS TO RUN MY COMPANY? 
**A)NO.**LOCAL 456 DOES NOT RUN YOUR 
BUSINESS. WE SIMPLY SUPPLY YOU WITH 
MANPOWER WHEN YOU REQUIRE IT.

Q) WHO DETERMINES THE INDIVIDUALS THAT 
WILL OVERSEE MY PROJECTS AS FOREMAN?

A) YOU CHOOSE WHO YOUR FOREMAN WILL 
BE AND WHERE THEY WORK.

Q) HOW CAN LOCAL 456 HELP MY COMPANY 
WITH RISING HEALTH CARE AND INSURANCE 
PREMIUMS?

A) ALL CONTRACTORS WORKING WITH LOCAL 
456 CONTRIBUTE TO THE SAME HEALTH AND 
WELFARE FUND, THIS ALLOWS FOR A BETTER 
“GROUP RATE.” ALSO THE PREMIUM IS BASED ON 
[Image: Im8]

“HOURS WORKED” SO YOU ONLY PAY FOR YOUR 
EMPLOYEES AND ONLY FOR THE HOURS THEY 
WORK. THOUGH OUR PATRNERSHIP WITH OSHA 
AND WITH THE SAFETY TRAINING THAT ALL OF 
OUR MEMBERS RECEIVE, UNION ELECTRICAL 
CONTRACTORS PAY LESS INSURANCE PREMIUMS 
AND HAVE LESS LOST TIME INJURIES. THIS IS 
MONEY IN YOUR POCKET. 
[Image: Im6]
