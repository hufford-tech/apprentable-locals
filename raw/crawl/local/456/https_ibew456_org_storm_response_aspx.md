## Storm Response Contractors

IBEW Utility contractors that are either dispatched or placed on stand-by for PSE&G, JCP&L (GPU), or Orange Rockland Utilities in New Jersey, must submit rosters to Local 456 by the next business day. Rosters must include, Name of Contractor, Power Company, Members Name, Local, Classification, IBEW Card number, and Social Security number (may be just last four digits).

Benefits are to paid to Local 456 at the rates listed in the storm language and the wage sheets available below. Benefits will be reciprocated back to the members home funds though our local administrator. Benefits are to be sent to:

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| IBEW Local 456 Distribution Fund |
|  | c/o I.E. Shaffer |
|  | PO Box 1028 |
|  | Trenton, NJ08628 |

Any questions can be be directed to the Union Hall at **732-246-2122** during normal business hours.

For any request for manpower you may contact Wayne Martiak at **908-415-8594.**
