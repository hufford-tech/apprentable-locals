## Going Green

1. [Home](https://ibew456.org/index.aspx)
2. Going Green

**IBEW 456 - A Leader in the Green Movement** IBEW Local 456 is leading the way in the Green Energy movement throughout New Jersey.  By providing the training for energy saving lighting, LEED Certified Installations, Solar and Wind Power Installations our installers are on the cutting edge of this movement.

Our installers undergo rigorous upgrade training on a regular basis so that we're always ahead of the curve to help save our environment and to save our owners and end users energy and money!
