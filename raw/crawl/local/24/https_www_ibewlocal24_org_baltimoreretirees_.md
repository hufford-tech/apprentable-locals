**![](https://www.ibewlocal24.org/Uploads/UploadedFiles/Retirees.jpg)**

## **Welcome to IBEW Retired Members Club 24!**

Organized on July 21, 1981, the Retired Members Club meets at **11:00 a.m. on the third Tuesday of each month** at the Union Hall, 2701 W. Patapsco Ave., Baltimore, MD. Refreshments are served after the meeting.

We would like to invite you to join us at these monthly meetings. Gather with many of your retired friends who enjoy meeting regularly to discuss old times.

Also, you’ll appreciate:

- Occasional speakers
- Bus trips throughout the year
- 50/50 drawings
- Updates on benefits
- Friendship luncheons
- Anniversary dinner in July
- Christmas luncheon in December and more!



Please feel free to bring your spouse or friends – all are welcome!
