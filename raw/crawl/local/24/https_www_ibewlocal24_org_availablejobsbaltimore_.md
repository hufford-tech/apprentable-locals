### Available Jobs

#### **BALTIMORE**

Please [click here](https://www.ibewlocal24.org/hiringhallrules/) for all of our Hiring Hall Rules. Call 410-247-5511 with any questions.

**Website Updates Daily at 6:00pm - Jobs are not updated throughout the day!**

Personnel calls for the following day will be posted on the Job Hotline and available for review after 6:00 P.M.

The day book is open between 8-8:45am when calls are posted from the previous night. Signing the Baltimore Daybook will make you eligible for job calls.

**Original registration for the out-of-work list must be done in person at the union hall.**

[Sign Baltimore Daybook](https://www.ibewlocal24.org/baltimoredaybook/)

**Please do not forget to re-sign!**
