| ## ::ATTIRE::<br>## As an CW/CE or apprentice, you are required to be dressed appropriately for work on construction jobs, with special consideration towards safety.<br>Therefore, please be aware that:<br>- boots<br>- long pants &<br>-  a proper fitting shirt<br>are required to be worn at all times.<br>Work attire must be:<br>- free from tears or holes<br>- clean <br>- must not bear any writing or image that may be offensive to the public<br>It is important that our appearance shows our customers that we are electrical professionals. Our customers are our livelihood, and it is our responsibility that we provide them with a positive impression of our union, our contractors and ourselves.<br>::SIDE NOTES:: <br>## IBEW LU 728 SHIRTS are for sale in the hall(should you need an appropriate shirt quickly.)<br>## For an example of appropriate work boots, click the link ==> [https://www.redwingshoes.com/work/mens/King-Toe-02240.html?cgid=mens-work](https://www.redwingshoes.com/work/mens/King-Toe-02240.html?cgid=mens-work)<br>## ::APPRENTICE/CW/CE Tool List::<br>- 9" Side-cutting pliers<br>- 2 Pair of Channel Locks<br>- Pencil<br>- Straight Screwdriver - LARGE<br>- Straight Screw Driver - TRIM<br>- Phillips Screwdriver <br>- Hacksaw Frame<br>- Wire Stripper 10-18 AWG solid Wire<br>- Electricians straight claw hammer (18 oz)<br>- Torpedo Level <br>- Locking Pocket Knife<br>- 15' or longer tape measure<br>- 3/4" cold chisel<br>- No Contact Voltage indicator <br>- Tool Pouch and Belt | |
