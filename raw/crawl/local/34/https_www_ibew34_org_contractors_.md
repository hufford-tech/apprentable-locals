**Contractors**

Use our simple Find A Contractor tool at [**www.wcipowerpros.com**](http://www.wcipowerpros.com/) for best results.

Electrician's Local 34 encompasses all of the Peoria, Galesburg, and Quincy areas. Our apprentice trained technicians wire much of central and west central Illinois. We partner with the National Electrical Contractors Association to not only train the best electricians, but to also insure that our contractors are qualified and capable of completing your project on time and on budget. Get it done right the first time: use IBEW Electricians and our NECA Contractors.
