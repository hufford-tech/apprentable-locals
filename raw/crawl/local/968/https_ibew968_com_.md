### **Jurisdiction**

Wood, Wirt, Tyler, Ritchie, Jackson, and Pleasants County West Virginia

### **Type of Work**

Inside, Teledata, Ouside commercial Constuction, Radio-Television Service and Sound and Public Address work

### **Object of Local**

To promote by all proper means the material and intellectual welfare of it members.

### **Electrical Training - Earn While You Learn**

The National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) jointly sponsor apprenticeship training programs that offer you the opportunity to earn wages and benefits while you learn the skills needed for a trade that can be both challenging and rewarding.
