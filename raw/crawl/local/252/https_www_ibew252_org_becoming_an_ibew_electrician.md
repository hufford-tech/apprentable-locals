# IBEW LOCAL 252    Become an IBEW electrician

## Here's why you should become an IBEW Electrician:

![](https://www.ibew252.org/_assets/img/ibew-local-252-dormatory-blimpe-burger.png)

## Are you interested in:

- A challenging professional trade?
- Earning competitive wages?
- Qualifying for a comprehensive employer-paid benefits package?
- Receiving a competitive pension and annuity plan?
- Helping to build new homes and buildings to enhance your community?

## If your answer is yes, there are additional benefits to becoming a union electrician with IBEW Local 252:

- Paid Apprenticeship Training Center education
- Referral hall for hiring
- Political representation for health, safety and economic issues
- Paid life insurance
- Comprehensive medical coverage, including prescriptions
- Sickness and accident benefits
- Long-term disability benefits
