# IBEW LOCAL 252    How can I support my union?

A

There are a number of things members can do to make the IBEW Union even stronger than it is today. Participating in your local union and educating
potential members and the public about our union are simple, everyday ways we can support the IBEW. Here are a few others:

- **Attend local union meetings regularly**.
- **Stay informed**. Keep up with current events and what's happening in your workplace. Visit IBEW.org and this web site to keep up with news that affects you and your union.
- **Participate in union elections**. Whether it's elections for officers or local union business, your opinion counts and can
make a difference.
- **Get to know your local officers** and trustees.
- **Attend union rallies** and events when you can. You may not be able to make them all, but standing together shows strength
in numbers.
- **Become an e-activist**. If you are online regularly, be sure to follow [IBEW Local 252’s Facebook page](https://www.facebook.com/IBEW252/) to be notified about key issues and ways you can take action online.
- **Spread the word**. If you hear about an issue that concerns you, tell your friends, relatives and coworkers and show them how
to get involved. Educate others. Talk about what being a IBEW member means to you. Tell your friends, relatives and other workers you bump
into about benefits of being an IBEW member.
