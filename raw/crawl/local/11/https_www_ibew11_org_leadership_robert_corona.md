Robert Corona was appointed Business Manager of IBEW 11 on Dec. 14, 2023. A member of the union since 1991, Corona served as the Local’s Senior Assistant Business Manager for five years and Organizing Director for 11 years.

A union electrician for the past 32 years, Corona served four years in the United States Air Force and was a shop steward at the Chevron refinery before joining the electrical union.

He has served many roles in IBEW 11 including: Apprentice, Apprentice Council member, Journeyman Wireman, District 2 Chairman and Welfare Committee Member, District 3 Chairman and Welfare Committee Member, Instructor, Foreman, General Foreman, Organizer, Temporary I.O. Political assignment, Business Representative, Senior Assistant Business Manager, Director of Organizing, President of the LA Electrical Workers Credit Union, Health and Pension Trustee, and Joint Apprenticeship Committee Trustee.

In addition to his long activism in Local 11, Robert is a recognized figure in Los Angeles County and serves as a Delegate to the LA/OC Building Trades Council and Delegate to the Los Angeles County Federation of Labor, State Building Trades Executive Board Member, Trustees Masters Program with the International Foundation of Employee Benefit Plans, and is a Delegate to the California State Association of Electrical Workers.
