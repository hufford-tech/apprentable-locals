## 11,000+ dedicated Workers

Working across industries to keep L.A. connected

[](https://www.ibew11.org/#) [](https://www.ibew11.org/#)

### Electricians

Powering L.A. from construction sites to city services

### **Sound and Communications Installers**

Keeping the city connected with reliable networks and systems.

### **Intelligent Transportation Systems**

Maintaining and improving the infrastructure that keeps LA moving.

### Civil Service & EAA Members

Representing thousands of city employees across nearly every department and bureau.

### Apprentices

Training the next generation of skilled trade professionals.

### Community & Partnerships

Working with business, labor, community, and organizations to serve Los Angeles.

[](https://www.ibew11.org/#) [](https://www.ibew11.org/#)

### Excellent Wages & Benefits

Get regular raises, top-notch benefits, and union representation on the job. Enjoy discounts and scholarships for you and your family.

### Ongoing Training & Apprenticeships

From skills building to leadership training, earn while you learn and advance your career with our Electrical Training Institute.

### Top-Notch Safety & jobsite Support

Work on the safest jobsites with guidance and support from the Union every step of the way.
