Powering Growth

Are you a licensed electrical contractor looking to grow your workforce, stay competitive, and simplify your operations?

**At IBEW Local 11, we partner with C-10 and C-7 contractors who want access to a skilled, reliable workforce without the administrative burden of managing benefits, retirement, and healthcare on their own.**

![Construction workers wearing safety vests and helmets standing in front of a building under construction with scaffolding.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/6904ae07d69667f9f48b75bd/6904f4c0ba7d7abebbc95273_ibew-local-11-site-visit-to-the-jefferson-high-school_54032602717_o.webp)

## We provide a workforce across all classifications, including:



**Inside Wiremen - Journeymen & Apprentices**



**Low Voltage / Sound & Communications - Journeymen Sound Installers and Apprentices**



**Residential Wiremen**



**Intelligent Transportation Systems - Journeymen Transportation and Apprentices**



**Construction Wiremen**



**Construction Electricians**

Whether your projects are prevailing wage, private work, residential, or low voltage, you can strengthen your workforce or scale down as your workload demands. Our hiring hall system allows you to adjust your workforce efficiently giving you the flexibility to grow when work increases and scale responsibly when it slows.

**Becoming a signatory contractor is more straightforward than you may think.**

built on trust

## Relationship-Driven Approach

**IBEW takes a relationship-driven approach.** We want to understand your business, your workforce needs, your current projects, and your long-term goals. Our partnership model is built around helping contractors become successful. The more successful our contractors are, the more IBEW members are on the job site. This isn't a sales pitch, it's a conversation. Let's explore what makes sense for your company.
