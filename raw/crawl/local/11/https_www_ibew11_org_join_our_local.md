## the benefits of joining ibew local 11

Organizing with IBEW Local 11 empowers workers with a united voice, better wages, safer conditions, and stronger job security. Through collective bargaining, members secure fairness, transparency, and meaningful input in how their workplace operates.

**Better Pay & Benefits**

Union members often earn more in wages and benefits than non-union workers through collective bargaining power.

**Safer Workplaces**

Union members have a unified voice to help create Improved working conditions: Union members have a say in how their jobs are done.

**Transparency**

Union agreements with employers include pay levels and scales for different jobs, which helps ensure fairness in pay.

**Grievance & Arbitration Process**

Union agreements include a process for resolving issues, which can save time and money compared to going to court.

**Sense of Purpose**

Organizing a union can be a rewarding process that helps workers grow closer to their coworkers.

**Building Working-Class Power**

Unions can help build economic power for the working class, especially for women and minority workers.

**Freedom to Join**

Employees have the right to form, join, or assist a union.
