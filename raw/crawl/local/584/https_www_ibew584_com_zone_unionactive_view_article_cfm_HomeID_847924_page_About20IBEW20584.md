| By becoming a skilled electrician, you deserve to be making the best pay and benefit package possible. And the IBEW – the largest professional association of electricians in the country – can help you achieve that.<br>Right now, our IBEW Journeymen are earning excellent wages and benefits for them and their families. And that’s just for starters.<br>**Health Care, 401(k), Pension, & More**<br>Under our health plans, our members’ families enjoy affordable health care premiums and low deductibles. Members also receive dental and vision coverage, plus $5.90 per hour employer-paid supplemental 401(k) and pension contributions.<br>**More Than ‘Just A Job’**<br>As an IBEW member, you’ll enjoy more than just good pay and solid benefits. You’ll be part of our Brotherhood, a proud tradition stretching back more than 100 years in the industry.<br>From volunteer projects to union gatherings, hunting, fishing, and other organized events, IBEW membership isn’t just a career – it’s a supportive and empowering way of life.<br>We work hard, we play hard, and we take care of one another. It’s what our country, and the IBEW, was founded on.<br>**So, what’s it like to work union?**<br>Simple: better pay, stronger benefits, a jobsite culture that promotes excellence, and support for you and your family to help keep you working.<br>Call us today at [(918) 592-2989](tel:9185922989). Let’s talk about improving your future. |
