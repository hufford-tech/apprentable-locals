From Your Business Manager

President Elect Joe Biden, nominated a 2nd generation union member, a

brother who carried his tools and who was an elected officer of his union, Marty Walsh,

current Mayor of Boston, to be the next Secretary of Labor of the United States of

America. If confirmed by the Senate, Walsh will be the first union member to serve as

Labor Secretary in 50 years.

Walsh will succeed Donald Trump’s Secretary of Labor, Eugene Scalia, a

vicious union buster. Walsh joined Laborers Local 223 in Boston at the age of 21 and

served as President until 2014, while simultaneously serving in the Massachusetts

House of Representatives and as Boston Metropolitan District Building & Construction

Trades Council. Walsh was elected Mayor of Boston in 2014 and re-elected in 2018. He

had led a successful effort to raise the city’s minimum wage to $15.00 per hour, while at

the same time creating 135,000 new jobs. This appointment by Joe Biden is just one of

the thousands that will be forthcoming over the next few months.

As some of you have probably heard, we have been working on a new website

which we will roll out on January 15, 2021. While the website will be functional, we will

still be tweaking it until we are satisfied with the overall content. The 688 Wire will be

uploaded there from now on instead of email. The Members only portion is not quite

ready, but we hope to be able to have it up and running smoothly by the months end.

In Solidarity,

Carl Neutzling

Work has continued to be steady around the Local, again no big jobs, but little

ones here and there that our keeping our guys busy.

Organizers Report

My report this month is jobsite activities that I visited & non-union electricians I have

spoken with.

I referred Area Energy & Electric foreman Chris Butsko to Owens and have had

continued positive dialogue with another of their foremen’s Don Perry. I have also been in

contact with guys from Carter, Knapp Electric, BP Electric, Peak Power, Cogburn Electric, and

WireNut Electric.

On December 11, I was involved in accident, I was northbound on Diamond street, and

someone ran a stop sign on 6th St. and I was t-boned, right in front of Alpine’s shop. I am OK,

still seeking treatment for soft tissue damage, as well as a possible fracture of the mid back, still

trying to work with BWC on treatment. The car has been repaired, I was stuck in the office for

the holidays, but I was able to continue organizing efforts from here, I sent emails & made

phone calls.

If anyone is friends with an unrepresented worker, please let me know. Again, if you

haven’t already, be sure to like our Facebook page. The page is for organizing purposes, feel

free to share pics of your work, I would love to flood our page showcasing our craftsmanship!

News from Gary Osborne (District Organizer)

In the Fourth District, our construction membership continues to grow. From August

through November our District grew by 603 members or 1.6%. We were fortunate during 2020

that the majority of our projects remained on track, with very few projects cancelled.

We have the Regional Agreements which allows our contractors the tool to

competitively compete for the work, our organizers on the Inside and Outside have the ability

and the tools to recruit workers from their chosen targets. Growing our membership, growing our

market share, and increasing the number of signatory employers equals better wages, more

secured benefits, and greater employment opportunities for our members. Let’s make 2021 our

year!

Calling all Volunteers!

Calling all interested in volunteering for a little project, would like to get 5-6 guys for

Saturday, January 30, 2021. Choice Material’s provides used items to the needy, including our

own members. We would like to help them out by donating our time and skill to install a

seperatly derived system and some lights and receptacles in their warehouse in Bellville. If this

is something that you would like to help out with, please contact Dave Carnes at (419) 689-2421

or call the hall.

International Convention:

The IBEW International Convention is scheduled for September of this year in

Chicago, Illinois. With the recent release of the COVID-19 vaccines, we are hopeful

that the convention can take place in person. However, International President

Stephenson and International Secretary-Treasurer Cooper are preparing for both a

virtual and in-person convention should it be unsafe to hold the event in-person. I will

keep you posted as more information is available.

The Fourth District Needs Your Creativity!

With the 40th International Convention upon us in late August/early September

2021, it has been a long-standing tradition that each district design a unique district pin

that can be worn proudly by each of our delegates, in addition to trading these pins

with other delegates from other districts. It is also a tradition to redesign the logo every

5 years. With that said, we are asking for design ideas for our Fourth District Delegate

Pin from the local union’s membership. In these creative designs, please keep in mind

the district’s motto of “The Fighting Fourth.” Selection of the design will be done by

presenting the designs to each business manager for a vote.

Please have designs submitted by email or mail by March 1, 2021.
