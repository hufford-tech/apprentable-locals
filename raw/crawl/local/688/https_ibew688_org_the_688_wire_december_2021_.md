688 The Wire: IBEW Local 688 Newsletter www.ibew688.org P.2

The People Helping People of North Central Ohio Inc. Thanksgiving was another

success! Special thanks to Brother Mike Mrozik for starting this annual tradition several

years back. “Our 100th anniversary was coming up so I tried to think of a way to give

back to the community. They used to serve the food on the square so that’s where we

started the year of our 100th.” Mike went on to say “It’s a true blessing to serve those

who have less and what better day to do it then Thanksgiving morning!”

Pictured from left to right starting in the back are Jeff Phillips, Jerry Conrad, Mike Mrozik, Brooklyn

Spears, Sean K. Spears, Brian McPeek, Johnathan Reyna, Abby Mullins, Jenna Kiser, Michelle Smith,

and these Brothers and Sisters children gave their Thanksgiving morning to help others in need.

It truly is a wonderful gesture to just give a little bit of your time to give those

who are less fortunate a brighter Holiday Season! Thanks to all those who

donated their Thanksgiving morning away from family and loved ones to

give their time to total strangers! Thanks Mike Mrozik, Sean K Spears,

Brian McPeek, Jerry Conrad, Michelle Smith, Jeff Phillips, John Reyna, and

Jenna Kiser (wife of former 688 member and current Local 82 member

Jeremy Kiser). They’ve given their time every year. This small act of

humanity exemplifies the Union comradery, not just within our Brotherhood,

but within the communities that we serve. I am proud to have brothers and

sisters who are willing to give back to the communities that we reside in

from east to west, north to south, we are Union Strong, we are IBEW

The 2021 Retiree’s Christmas Luncheon

The luncheon had a great turnout! There was a lot of catching up done, as well as

stories told, but all and all it was a great time! To me it was great hearing the stories of

other generations that were not only entertaining, but things to be learned from as well.

This is a great group of electricians that have a lot of valuable experience because they

were the ones doing the work when our area was still heavily in manufacturing, where

as now most factories are being sent over seas. I could sit and talk with each of them

for hours and just try and absorb as much as possible!

Pictured above from left to right are starting in the back row: Fred Claus, Gene Haliburton, Guy Lannan,

Jim Tackett, Jack Pifher, Dennis Spangler, Jim Hughes, John Kline, Dave Mock, and Jim Herrick. Front

row beginning on left, Charlie Burch, Dave Yetzer, Paul Delong, Forest Foust, and Clarence “Rocky”

Stipp.
