## Page 2 of 5

Work has been steady around the Local, no big jobs, but little ones here and

there that our keeping our guys busy. There was a fire at AK Steel, that created an

emergency job call, that we were able to fill. Currently there are 4 schools being built in

our jurisdiction, Hillsdale Local Schools PK-12 building, Lexington Schools PK-12,

Marion Pleasant Schools, Shelby City Schools is building a PK-8 building. BCU has the

Hillsdale sitework, Vaugn Industries was low bidder on Marion Pleasant, Speelman

Electric out of Local 306 is trying to get into Lexington, I have reached out to Adena to

find out who the electrical contractor is on the Shelby School project, but so far have

not heard back, I have been by the jobsite several times and so far only temp service to

the job trailer has been done.

Retired Brother Clarence (Bob) Fulton had a circuit not working, I went over and

troubleshot the situation, I found a bad connection in the attic, and repaired the issue.

Organizers Report

I have had some continued positive dialogue one of Carters top guys. I had

referred Dustin Tate to Owens, to start December 14, but he called me on the 1st and

informed me that John Carter made him an offer he could not refuse. While I was

unable to strip Dustin from Carter, I feel that I did level the field and made John pay his

guy a fair wage. I have also been in discussion with Chris Butsko and Don Perry, both

are foreman’s for Area Energy and Electric out of Marion.

Kiess Electric is working on a new Brew Pub in Bucyrus, this project has been

put on hold due to financial issues, but now seems to be back on track. Kahl’s is

installing power for 19 door openers at Bucyrus City Hall. Knapp Electric is involved in

the renovation of Bucyrus Manor Apartments. Strong Electric is still on the Ohio Health

Ashland Project. Buckingham is still active in Ariel Corporation and busy around Mt.

Vernon, so far, I have not had much of a vibe from any of the guys, though they have

been cordial.

If you haven’t already, be sure to like our Facebook page. The page is for

organizing purposes, feel free to share pics of your work, I would love to flood our page

showcasing our craftsmanship!Be sure to check out our Facebook page IBEW Local

688, and don’t forget to Like it!

Merry Christmas and a Happy New Year
