I would like to apologize to the body for the conduct of some of our members at the

Tuesday March 9th Union Meeting, especially Mark McDanel, who moved his seat to retreat

from the loud behavior. I have spoken to President McPeek about keeping the meeting under

control. After some thought, I have decided to make changes to the Referral Policy, which I

have full supervision and direction over (Bylaws Article 15 Section 10). Typically, I allow input

from membership for decisions made for the referral policy, but I do feel the need to go a

direction where those that are willing and want to work rather than those “cherry picking” the

next best job. Highlights of the changes are as follows effective April 1st, 2021:

1\. If applicant is rejected or turned down by the contractor, they will receive a turndown.

2\. You will be allowed 3 turndowns without penalty and you will roll completely off the

book for the 4th turndown. You must call job line or office to be placed at the bottom of

the list within 48 hrs.

3\. Turndowns will be given for calls deemed 14 days or less and calls for AK Steel.

4\. Turndowns will be given for calls contractors deem 3 weeks or less to those IBEW

688 members working out of town.

The New Referral Policy can be seen here https://ibew688.org/

Additionally, after the Tuesday meeting, I was threatened with bodily harm, therefore the

union hall will be on lockdown with limited access to the lobby/window area unless access

is approved by myself or staff. The safety and wellbeing of our staff is important. I regret

any inconvenience this may cause.

Negotiations with employers begin soon, hopefully the negotiating team will have

something to bring to you late April for ratification. Please make yourself available to

participate in the process once the date has been set.

In Solidarity,

Carl Neutzling

The Health of the Union

Organizers Report

I would like to apologize for not being in attendance at the General Meeting

Tuesday, as I was out of state for a family medical emergency. I have had continued positive

communications with a Foreman from Area Energy and Electric. I have also had continued

conversations building a good relationship with electricians from Cogburn Electric, Carter

Electric, Buckingham Electric, Clouse Electric, Vaughn Industries, and Area Wide Electric out of

Dayton. I made some new contacts with a couple of guys from Peak Power, who are working on

a project that has had been boarded up with construction fence for months, where the activity

has just begun in the past few weeks.

Carl and I sat with other locals around the 4th District, as well as International Reps, and

District and State Organizers via Zoom. The meeting was focused on training us in the Call Evo

program, a program that would allow us to mass text all the unrepresented workers that we

have contact information in our database. This program is funded by the 4th District and some of

the other locals who have used this program have reported positive feedback. They are setting

our local up now, and we should hopefully be online this week.

As always, if you know any unrepresented electricians, please let me know or pass on

my contact information to them.

In Solidarity,

Dave Carnes

Saturday, March 13, 2021. Choice Material’s provides used items to the needy, including our

own members. We are installing a sub panel in a separate building with an overhead tri-plex

drop and installing some conduit for some lights and receptacles. The weather is getting nice
