# Dues

## UPDATE - 3/11/21

Our dues payment system is now active. You will receive an email address as accounts are created.

##### _**If you current email address is not on file, please contact the office so we may prepare your account.**_

## Access Your Account

1. Hover over _Members_ and click _My Account._
2. Enter username and password, then click _Log In._

## Enrollment Instructions

1\. Access account page (see above).

![](https://ibew688.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/click-subscriptions-300x257.webp)

2\. If you have not already enrolled, you should see “You have no active subscriptions”. Click _Browse Products_.

![no-available-subs](https://ibew688.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/no-available-subs-1.webp)

3\. Click _Select Options_ under the appropriate dues rate.

![select-dues](https://ibew688.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/select-dues.webp)

4\. Choose a renewal length, then click _Enroll Now_.

![add-to-cart](https://ibew688.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/add-to-cart.webp)

5\. Review the selected item in the Cart, then click _Proceed to Checkout_.

![cart](https://ibew688.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/cart.webp)

6\. Enter your billing details (address must match the one used for your credit or debit card) and click _Enroll Now_.

![checkout](https://ibew688.org/wp-content/uploads/2021/03/checkout-e1615781253265.webp)

7\. You will receive a confirmation email which shows receipt for payment.
