The Health of the Union

Organizers Report

I ran into Dustin Tate, whom I previously referred to Owens, but John Carter

made him an offer he could not refuse. Dustin said he is content for the time being, he is driving

a brand-new Cargo van. Discovered that Buckingham Electric is building a new shop across

from Ariel Park in Mt. Vernon. Stopped by the old GM plant in Ontario and spoke with a couple

guys from BIS, who is signatory with Local 306, I let Carl know and he made the arrangements

to ensure we are getting the proper assessments. They said they were only doing the service

and the building shell.

With the rise of Covid cases, I am following the same habits of other locals I have been

in contact with, I am trying to reduce my face-to-face interactions, and I am leaving my card on

vehicles and calling and emailing contacts that I have already made. I am still patrolling the

jurisdiction, but unless they are outside, I am not speaking with anybody. I am not walking

through jobsites; I don’t want to be “that union guy who is on my jobsite spreading Covid.”

If any one has a non-represented friend in the industry, please, talk to them, give them

my number, or give me theirs. The best way to bring them on board, is to help spread the

information, because most of them have been mis-informed by their employers.

Again, if you haven’t already, be sure to like our Facebook page. The page is for

organizing purposes, feel free to share pics of your work, I would love to flood our page

showcasing our craftsmanship!

Calling all Volunteers!

Calling all interested in volunteering for to help wrap up our Choice Material’s project,

Saturday, February 20, 2021. Choice Material’s provides used items to the needy, including our

own members. We are installing a sub panel in a separate building with an overhead tri-plex

drop and installing some conduit for some lights and receptacles. I know its not the idea time of

the year, but any and all help would be greatly appreciated. Click here for map

If this is something that you would like to help out with, please contact Dave Carnes at (419)

689-2421 or call the hall.
