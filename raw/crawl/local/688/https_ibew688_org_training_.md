### Apprenticeship Opportunities

Application to apply [**here!**](https://secure2.tradeschoolinc.com/v5/ibew688-org/login/index.php)

There is a $25 **non-refundable** fee to complete the application above that must be paid online with a credit or debit card. Within 30 days of submitting application, you will need to provide the following:

·Official high school transcript showing that you graduated and completed Algebra I with a “C” or better

·Copy of your High School Diploma or Official GED. Official Ohio GED transcripts are available on line [here](https://ged.com/life_after_ged/)

·Copy of your Birth Certificate

**Incomplete documentation and applications will be closed after 30 days, and you will need to start over, so have all of your documentation ready before paying the $25.**

All documents submitted during the application process become property of the Mansfield JATC and will not be returned to the applicant.

# Aptitude Test

Once Application has been completed and all required documents have been uploaded, you will then be scheduled via email for our Aptitude Test. The Aptitude test is two (2) parts, Algebra Functions & Reading Comprehension.

Follow the link below for sample test questions

https://electricaltrainingalliance.org/SamplePage/PreparingfortheTest

Upon successful completion of the Aptitude test, you will then be scheduled for an interview.

# The Interview

The Interview will consist of you interviewing with up to six (6) Training Committee members going around the  room asking preformatted questions, you will be given adequate time to answer each question. After the Interview, you will then be given a score based on your interview. This score is then uploaded to a ranking list that will rank you based on that score. Applicants are then offered acceptance into the program based on the  order of the ranking list. You will be on the ranking list for two (2) years from date the of your Interview. After two (2) years, you must reapply and go through the process all over again to be considered for the apprenticeship again.
