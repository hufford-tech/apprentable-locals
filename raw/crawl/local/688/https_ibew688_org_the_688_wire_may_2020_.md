The Executive Board met on Monday May 4th, 2020 in a special meeting. Under

consideration was the viability of holding a May meeting. With the Governors decision to

extend the Covid-19 Stay at Home Order to May 29th, 2020 and the requirement of no

gathering greater than 10 people, Executive Board canceled the May meeting. An

Executive Board meeting will be held Monday May 11th 2020 via Zoom to take care of any

local union business.

April Executive Board Meeting Highlights:

1\. Postponed election process one month ( see P.2)

2\. Approved a participating withdrawl card for Eric Clady.

Trustee Report:

Pension and Annuity plans have taken a hit with the stock market free fall in the

latter part of March, however has recovered somewhat and along with gains at the end

of 2019, should show very modest earnings for the plan year ending May 31st

, 2020.

Thoughts From the Business Manager

Employers work has been steady for the most, Alpine’s work that was on the

books has been pushed back because of the Coronavirus. Very few calls coming in-
really new work has been very slow starting. Ohio Department of Jobs & Family

Services has issued a letter to employers asking them to report furloughed employees

who refuse to return to work for the employer.

In Solidarity

Carl Neutzling, Business Manager

Officers Take Office:

Newly Elected Officers will take

office at their regularly

scheduled meetings in August.

No Member may be a

candidate for more than one

office. Anyone nominated to

more than one office must

choose the office for which they

will be a candidate.

Nominations will proceed in the

order of offices as they are

listed in the IBEW Constitution

whose term of office shall be

three (3) years.

In order to be nominated, a

member must have a minimum

of two (2) years of continuous

good standing in Local Union

No. 688. Good standing means

that the member is current in

the payment of dues and has

been for the two (2) years

preceding the nominations.

According to the Bylaws of

Local Union No. 688, Article III,

Section 8e, no apprentice shall

be eligible to hold office in the

Local Union. Apprentices may

seek office provided they

become a journeyman by

Tuesday, July 14, 2020.

An election judge and tellers

will be appointed at the close of

nominations. None of these

may be a candidate for any

office.
