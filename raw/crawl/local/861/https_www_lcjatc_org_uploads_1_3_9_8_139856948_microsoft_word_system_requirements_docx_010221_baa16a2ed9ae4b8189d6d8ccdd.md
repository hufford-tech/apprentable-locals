## What Kind of Device Will Run The LMS?

The Blended Learning system has specific device requirements that ensure compatibility with its various modules and technologies. Generally, students must have access to a broadband Internet connection and a personal computer manufactured within the last three years. Specific requirements are below.

## Chromebook:

- Many components with the exception of the Simulators will run on a Chromebook. If you would like to use one of the Simulators then you will need to purchase a Windows PC or Laptop meeting the requirements above.

## Android and IOS Tablets/Smartphones:

- At this time these items will not run all components of the LMS. Among other things, Flash is not supported by these devices many quiz items use Flash and will not display.

- We do not recommend that you use Android or IOS devices to access the LMS.
