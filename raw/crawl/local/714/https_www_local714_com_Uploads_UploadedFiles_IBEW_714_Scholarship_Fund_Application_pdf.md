# IBEW LOCAL 714 SCHOLARSHIP FUND APPLICATION

## GUIDELINES:

1. Scholarships are available to members, spouse, children or grandchildren of members of IBEW, Local 714.

2. Applicants must be enrolled in the second semester of their freshman year or higher in any post-secondary Institution recognized by and certified by the appropriate state agency where the Institution is located.

3. Applicants must have a college GPA of 2.0 minimum.

4. Awards will be paid directly to the Institution where the winner is registered. Awards may be applied to tuition, books or other fees as directed by the winner.

5. Winners will be selected by random drawing.

6. Winners may not apply for future IBEW, Local 714 scholarship awards.

7. Applications must be received at P. O. Box 1906, Minot, ND 58702, bearing a postmark of not later than June 30th of the year of the application.

8. Winners will be announced during the month of August.

9. Number and size of awards will depend on fund size and earnings and will be determined by the Scholarship Committee.
