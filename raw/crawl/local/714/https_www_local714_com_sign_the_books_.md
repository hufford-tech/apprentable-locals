# **Sign The Book**

Due to Covid-19 the original sign-in may be by telephone, or **fax**. In order to sign our out-of-work book you will need to send the following to us:

- Travel Letter
- Copy of your current dues receipt
- Copy of your last separation notice
- Copy of your current ND Journeyman's license **if you have one**
- Current OSHA card (within the last 3 years) \*if applicable and needed BEFORE you can take a call for a job requiring a current OSHA
- Phone number
- Email Address
- Last four digits of your SSN

These items can be **Faxed** or emailed to the hall.  For OSHA information and online classes [click here.](https://www.local714.com/osha/)

Bismarck       email [bismarckoffice@local714.com](mailto:bismarckoffice@local714.com)
