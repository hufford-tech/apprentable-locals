**Education & Training: A Commitment to Your Future!**

**Education and Training are provided through and by the Dakotas JATC, affiliated with the electrical training _ALLIANCE_. The _ALLIANCE_ was developed in cooperation by the IBEW and NECA to help both organizations meet the needs of providing a skilled, highly trained, professional workforce for the electrical contracting Industry.**

**Your Pathway to Higher Wages, a Safer Workplace, a Better Life.**

**The IBEW and Local 714 have a strong tradition in education, and providing you with the tools, resources, and training you need to be successful in a changing work environment is among our highest priorities.  Classes are offered regularly in Bismarck, Minot, and Williston and include training in the following areas and many more.**

- **Telecommunications Wiring**
- **Fiber Optics**
- **Fire Alarms**
- **Programmable Logic Control**
- **Power Quality**
- **Safe Job Practices**
- **The National Electrical Code**
- **Instrumentation**

**A well-trained worker is indispensable, and the IBEW and Local 714 will give you the opportunity to become as valuable as you want to be.**

# **Classes take place at the respective Union Halls in [Bismarck](https://www.local714.com/bismarckoffice/),  [Minot](https://www.local714.com/minotoffice/), and [Williston](https://www.local714.com/willistonlocation/). The upcoming class schedules are available at the [Dakotas JATC](http://www.dakotasjatc.org/) website.**
