1 - Journeyman I&E technician based out of Minot area for work at the Velva ADM plant.

**Requirements**

- North Dakota Journeyman Electrician License (required)
- Valid driver's license with a clean driving record
- Ability to pass a drug screening with negative results
- Current OSHA 10 certification (issued within the last 3 years)
- Minimum of 2 years of experience troubleshooting, installing, and programming:
  - Instrumentation and control devices
  - Variable Frequency Drives (VFDs)
  - Automated equipment
  - Programmable Logic Controllers (PLCs)

**Compensation & Schedule**

- **40 hours per week**
- **$56.35 per hour**
- On-call rotation approximately **once per month**

**Key Responsibilities**

- Troubleshoot and repair electrical and instrumentation systems
- Install and commission instrumentation and control equipment
- Program and maintain PLCs and VFDs
