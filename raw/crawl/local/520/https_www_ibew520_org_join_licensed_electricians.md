# Licensed Electricians

IBEW Local 520 is dedicated to ensuring that licensed electricians in Central Texas receive the recognition and compensation they deserve.

By becoming a member, you'll gain access to a comprehensive benefits package, including fully-paid family healthcare, retirement plans, and opportunities for ongoing professional development.

## IBEW Local 520 offers better opportunities for licensed electricians

**Currently, thousands of electricians work in Central Texas, but are not covered under a Collective Bargaining Agreement.**

Men and women who become members of IBEW Local 520 gain access to fully-paid family healthcare. Membership also provides retirement benefits such as a pension and 401k.

It is these fringe benefits — health insurance and retirement — that separate the union and non-union sector. Most non-union electrical contractors cannot provide benefits anywhere near the level received by those of Local 520 members.

### Stay ahead of the curve with IBEW Local 520's continuing education opportunites

Electricians who seek IBEW 520 membership can also take advantage of journeyman upgrade training.

This instruction not only helps our electricians to work more safely, but more efficiently. They are kept up-to-date on the latest industry technology and advances.

Whether you join Local 520 and need to complete your training, or want to participate in further training, the state-of-the-art IBEW Local 520 [Austin Electrical Training Alliance (AustinETA)](https://www.austineta.org/) is equipped to be a constructive learning environment for all members.

**Make the best choice for yourself and your family by becoming a member of IBEW Local 520.**
