# About IBEW 520

Empowering electrical workers and illuminating Central Texas since 1906.

## Over a Century of Service

Founded in 1906, the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local 520 has been serving Central Texas for over 100 years.

We are proud to represent over 1,800 skilled electricians, apprentices, and electrical workers in the greater Austin area and surrounding regions.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/662a846e3ce988a4e2780c16/6685bf2cf68079fca29ac8bf_52112217371_b2e4650a72_k.avif)

## Our Mission

At IBEW Local 520, our mission is to empower electrical workers, elevate industry standards, and contribute to the growth and prosperity of our communities. We strive to provide our members with unparalleled opportunities for career advancement, fair wages, comprehensive benefits, and a safe working environment.

### Who we are

We are a diverse community of dedicated professionals committed to excellence in the electrical industry. Our members range from apprentices just starting their careers to seasoned journeymen with decades of experience.

We take pride in our craft and our commitment to delivering high-quality electrical work across various sectors, including commercial, industrial, and residential projects.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/662a846e3ce988a4e2780c16/6685bebe6fcc6f67720e7c7c_51284871831_2b9d4bfe98_k.avif)

### What we offer



### Comprehensive apprenticeship program

Our state-of-the-art training facility in Austin provides hands-on learning experiences and classroom instruction, ensuring our apprentices receive the best possible education in the electrical trade



### Continuing education

We offer ongoing training and certification programs to keep our members at the forefront of industry advancements and safety standards.



### Competitive wages and benefits

Our collective bargaining agreements ensure that our members receive fair compensation and a comprehensive benefits package, including health insurance, retirement plans, and more.



### Job Placement Assistance

We work closely with our signatory contractors to provide steady employment opportunities for our members throughout Central Texas.

### Our Impact

As a leading force in the electrical industry, IBEW Local 520 has played a significant role in shaping the landscape of Central Texas.

Our members have contributed to numerous major projects in the region, helping to build and maintain the infrastructure that powers our communities.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/662a846e3ce988a4e2780c16/6685bfabba31296892f1a444_50344585228_e9f40f9a8c_c.avif)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/662a846e3ce988a4e2780c16/6685bf830248416dfb3a7b39_50345436792_c337dcdb8a_c.avif)

### Join Us

Whether you're an aspiring electrician looking to start your career, a licensed professional seeking better opportunities, or a contractor in need of skilled electrical workers, IBEW Local 520 welcomes you.

Together, we can build a brighter future for the electrical industry and our community.
