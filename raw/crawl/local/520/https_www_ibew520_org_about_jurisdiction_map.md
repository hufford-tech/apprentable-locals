# Jurisdiction map

Explore IBEW Local 520's service area across Central Texas and surrounding regions.

## IBEW Local 520 jurisdictional map

The IBEW Local 520 jurisdiction covers central Texas. Use this map to see if your location falls within IBEW 520’s jurisdiction.

Please [check with the Local](https://www.ibew520.org/contact-us) before making any final determinations.

Outside IBEW 520's jurisdiction? [Find the local union in your area](https://www.ibew.org/Tools/Local-Union-Directory).

![520 Map](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/662a846e3ce988a4e2780c16/6658bec1bc92a47dc3727688_IBEW-520-TX_Inside_Map-Only.png)

### What is a jurisdiction?

IBEW local unions are just that. We represent, help employ, and support local working-class people within the electrical industry.

There are several IBEW local unions in the great state of Texas, IBEW Local Union number 520 being one of them.

If you are interested in joining the IBEW take a look at our jurisdiction map above to find out which Local Union represents electrical workers in your area.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/662a846e3ce988a4e2780c16/668356882cbb12aa0a748329_50344585608_b89148a947_c.avif)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/662a846e3ce988a4e2780c16/668356bc50cd57c12808121b_50345277871_56dfd05dc5_c.avif)

### **These are the counties within 520’s jurisdiction.**

Travis, Bastrop, Hays, Blanco, Burnet, Williamson, Lee, Llano, San Saba, Burleson, Caldwell, Fayette, Runnels, Coleman, Brown, Tom Green, Concho, McCulloch, Menard, Mason, Kimble and parts of Coryell and Bell Counties to include that part of Fort Hood in Coryell County south of Cowhouse Creek, and not to extend more than two (2) miles into Bell County from the Southeast boundary line of Coryell County, Gray Field, and the City of Killeen, and parts of Lampasas, Bell and Milam Counties, which are nearer to Austin than Waco, in the State of Texas.
