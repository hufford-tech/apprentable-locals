**IMPORTANT HEALTH COVERAGE DOCUMENTS: HOW TO REQUEST IRS FORMS 1095-B**

As permitted by federal law, the Local Union No. 704 I.B.E.W. Health and Welfare Fund is no longer automatically mailing federal Forms 1095-B. If you wish to receive a copy of your Form 1095-B, you may request one by contacting the Benefits Office as follows:

\\* By mail: RJLee & Associates, LLP, 1700 52nd Avenue, Suite B, Moline, IL 61265

\*By Telephone: 309-757-7550

\*By Facsimile: 309-764-3438

\*By E-mail: [shanethomas@rjlee.com](mailto:shanethomas@rjlee.com) (due to privacy concerns, the Benefits Office will respond to e-mails via encrypted e-mail only.)

Your request must include your first and last name, address, your contact number, and e-mailaddress (if you want an electronic copy of the Form 1095-B). You should expect to receive yourForm 1095-B within thirty (30) days of your request. If you have any questions about the Form 1095-B:

\*Talk with your tax consultant or other professional advisor

\*Visit irs.gov

\*Call the Benefits Office at 309-757-7550

      Richardson Motors, Chick-Fil-A, Five Guys Burgers and Perkins used a non signatory contractor to complete the electrical on their new buildings.  We urge all of our members to patronize the places in town that use our contractors to do their work.

      Kunkel and Associates is servicing the local health insurance plan.  For more information check out their website [www.kunkel-inc.com](http://www.kunkel-inc.com/) or contact the Union Hall.

    We now have the ability for you to make payments to the Union Hall with a credit card.  If you have any questions contact Shelly.

    Just a reminder, if your status (married/single/kids), your address, or your phone number changes, don't forget to notify Local 704 in a timely manner.  This information helps us ensure that problems with your insurance do not go without notice.  Thanks.

    Anyone who has information on Electrical Contactors, (other than Contractors Signatory with Local 704), working in our jurisdiction please inform the hall.

**Brothers and Sister, this is an important announcement from the Health and Welfare Trustees of Local 704**

**Cyberattack on Change Healthcare, Inc.**   Health Choices, the Claims Administrator for the Local Union No. 704 I.B.E.W. Health and Welfare Fund (“Welfare Fund”), has reported that a significant data breach was recently identified by Change Healthcare, Inc. (CHC), a company that provides a variety of services to Health Choices.  This breach was the result of a February 21, 2024, cyberattack against CHC.  Although the breach includes personal health information (PHI) of members of plans administered by Health Choices, **at this time the Trustees have not been notified that any member or dependent covered by the Welfare Fund was included in the breach**.  CHC is presently working to identify affected individuals and plans to mail written letters to affected individuals for whom CHC has a sufficient address.   To learn more about the breach click [here](https://linkprotect.cudasvc.com/url?a=https%3a%2f%2fwww.changehealthcare.com%2fhipaa-substitute-notice%3futm_source%3deloqua%26utm_medium%3demail%26utm_campaign%3d2406_ALL_41.11_na_EM_PR_cyberattack_MKTG_Group1%26utm_content%3dChange%2520Healthcare%2520Cyber%2520Customer%2520Notification%26utm_term%3dnone&c=E,1,f_P76d8dnRoPaCij8kd2vciHSpC7fUbsO-I0lHSptqffSEkwbSuBFx2Nj7y_qsJUJSd8JJb9IDD0Fl303HXuOyjZwinImheP4wmKVgFqjx7zhoTVUYM,&typo=1).   CHC is offering two (2) years of free credit monitoring and identity theft services to any individual who believes their information may have been impacted by the breach.  The Trustees are keenly aware of how important your PHI is to you and encourage you to take advantage of these services by visiting [changecybersupport.com](https://www.unitedhealthgroup.com/ns/health-data-breach.html?utm_source=eloqua&utm_medium=email&utm_campaign=2406_ALL_41.11_na_EM_PR_cyberattack_MKTG_Group1&utm_content=Change%20Healthcare%20Cyber%20Customer%20Notification&utm_term=none).    The Trustees regret any inconvenience or concern that this situation has caused and want to reiterate that the issue here was not a result of any error by the Trustees of the Welfare Fund.  The Trustees are committed to providing quality care, including protecting your PHI, and want to assure you that they expect vendors working with the Welfare Fund to exercise the highest level of caution.  If you have questions or need help, you should call CHC at 1-866-262-5342 or Health Choices at 1-800-747-8900 or visit the Health Choices website at [www.healthchoicesplans.com](http://www.healthchoicesplans.com/).
