# Lighted Parade sponsored by IBEW

November 24, 2025 @ 6:15 pm \- 7:00 pm

Free

- [« Downtown Sioux City Christmas tree decorating](https://www.ibew231.com/event/downtown-sioux-city-christmas-tree-decorating/)
- [Union Hall Closed for Thanksgiving »](https://www.ibew231.com/event/union-hall-closed-for-thanksgiving/)

![](https://www.ibew231.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/09/IBEW-231-moving-in-the-parade-2024-300x225.jpg)

The Downtown for the Holidays IBEW Lighted Parade is scheduled for Monday, November 24, 2025, at 6:15 p.m.in Sioux City, Iowa. The parade, sponsored by the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local 231, will begin at the corner of Iowa and 4th Streets, travel along 4th Street, and conclude at Nebraska Street. At the end of the route, Santa will light the downtown Christmas tree in front of the [Sioux City Public Museum](https://www.google.com/search?cs=0&sca_esv=5bcfae1adc0171b1&sxsrf=AE3TifPRHWQk8N4wWLh-57lYW4S5v1NZRg%3A1758292408073&q=Sioux+City+Public+Museum&sa=X&ved=2ahUKEwjjwvPSheWPAxWL1fACHc_hMjAQxccNegQIBRAB&mstk=AUtExfChUDambdGfyBxSwvbRNmnApTbTjnOvtL-R0vgdJPFtWVb4gOBcqPyeagJp6U-tJMvQguAQ1Eu6v_r-A7QPtyRJ6mnrNRiC0d5ny5KW8e6LHWB2WN905OYuMQZFtj0jXCh3TgSoO2QWcc8rdu9UqkDcNwd20xv7-aIX3_QY7XmXebk&csui=3).

# Book Signing Procedures

Our Books are open Monday thru Friday, 8:00AM to 4:30 PM. You must sign in person. To be referred to a job you must also have an OSHA card. Re-sign is the 10th through the 16th of the month.
