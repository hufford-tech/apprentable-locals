Electrical Apprenticeship

Electrical Apprenticeship

The Des Moines Joint Electrical Apprenticeship (J.A.T.C.) Training Trust is a nonprofit organization jointly sponsored by the Des Moines chapter of NECA and Local 347 to provide training to the local’s apprentices and journeymen through the Des Moines Electrical Apprenticeship. The J.A.T.C. consists of an eight-member committee – four appointed by Local 347 and four appointed by NECA.

Currently the Des Moines J.A.T.C. trains between 475 and 500 apprentices annually in the three disciplines of the program while providing continuing education courses for more than 2,000 electrical workers.

![](https://media.canva.com/v2/image-resize/format:JPG/height:150/quality:75/uri:ifs%3A%2F%2FM%2F5f4fb346-85d1-4e3d-8238-2a837a4b3ba2/watermark:F/width:200?csig=AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAGhvnalwcqho43PpVVzJ2cLT2dyoOKfptpaaopCOvqU9&exp=1786401224&osig=AAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAAADKYRB8HRufT88IW7Ha9-C0TGygqwohEq92CN82yPGjt&signer=media-rpc&x-canva-quality=thumbnail)

Journeyman Training

Journeyman Training

Best in the Region in Quality, Education & Technical Expertise

IBEW Local 347’s members are highly trained professionals who receive ongoing industry training throughout their career.

Our curriculum is constantly upgraded to include the latest technological developments and our instructors are the most qualified in the industry. Each one has several years of field experience and on-going education. Each apprentice who graduates to “Journeyman Wireman” status has logged a minimum of 8,720 hours of combined classroom and field experience. While Journeyman Technicians, have a minimum of 6,480 hours and Journeyman Residential Wiremen have logged around 5,280 hours of combined classroom and field experience.

All apprentices are held to the strictest of standards and are overseen by an Apprenticeship Committee. Journeymen are required to complete 18 training hours, every 3 years, of continuing education at one of our Training Centers to stay ahead of emerging technologies in the marketplace.

Training expenses for each apprentice and Journeyman including tuition, instructor fees, curriculum development, facility fees, and equipment and overhead costs are covered by a scholarship through our Training Trust which is funded through our collective bargaining agreement with the IOWA NECA.
