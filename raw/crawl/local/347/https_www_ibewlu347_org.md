| |     |
| --- |
| **What We Do**<br>Our members are skilled electrical professionals working in the construction, maintenance, broadcasting and manufacturing fields.<br>**Who We Are**<br>IBEW Local 347 is based out of Des Moines, Iowa, with our office newly located in Ankeny. We represent over 2200 members working throughout 28 counties in Central Iowa.<br>**Who We Represent**<br>The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers is the largest electrical union in the world. The IBEW represents workers' rights in all areas of the electrical industry. | |
