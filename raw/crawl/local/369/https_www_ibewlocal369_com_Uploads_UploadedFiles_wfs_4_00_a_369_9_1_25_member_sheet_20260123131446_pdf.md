5/10 of 1% of Gross Labor Payroll on first 75,000 work hours        
2/10 of 1% of Gross Labor Payroll on second 75,000 work hours
No payment on Gross Labor Payroll over 150,000 work Hours

An amount equal to 25% of the Employee’s Gross Wages
