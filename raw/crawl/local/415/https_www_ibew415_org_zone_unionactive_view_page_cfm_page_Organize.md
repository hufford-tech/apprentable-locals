**Benefits of Membership:**

- _Fringe Benefits (Paid on top of wages)_
  - Receive 3 pensions, an annuity and health insurance at no additional cost to you.
  - Your health insurance and retirement benefits will follow you from one contractor to the next.
    - ​ **_Insurance covers you and your family with no extra fees!!!_**
  - Have access to Journeyman CEUs at a very low cost or free.
- ​Stable working conditions with a negotiated agreement between labor and management.
- Have the opportunity to pursue work in over 850 IBEW Local Unions across the country.

For more information on becoming a member of the IBEW, please call 307-421-1642.
