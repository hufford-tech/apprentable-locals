# Who We Are

**Founded in 1905, IBEW Local 124 has been the home of Kansas City’s inside wiremen for more than a century. Built on the legacy of the early electrical workers who first organized in 1892, Local 124 has grown through perseverance, solidarity, and a commitment to raising standards for both workers and the industry. From major milestones like the 1915 Union Station strike to decades of steady progress, Local 124 has remained focused on protecting its members, advancing the trade, and delivering safe, high‑quality electrical work across the region.**

**Today, Local 124 proudly serves a 20‑county jurisdiction spanning Eastern Kansas and Central and Western Missouri. Our members power the commercial, industrial, and institutional infrastructure that keeps our communities moving.**

[LEARN MORE](https://ibew124.web.linkedunion.com/news-details/webview/our-history/single/78057)

# Apprenticeship Program

**The Kansas City JATC offers two pathways into the electrical trades: the five-year Inside Wireman program and the four-year Telecommunications program. Both combine on-the-job training with classroom instruction, 8,000 field hours and 900 hours of coursework, to build career-ready tradespeople.**

**There's no tuition. Apprentices earn a paycheck while they learn and are only responsible for books and tools. Inside Wireman graduates can also earn up to 42 college credit hours toward an Associate's Degree through Metropolitan Community Colleges.**

**Ready to build a skilled, in-demand career?**
