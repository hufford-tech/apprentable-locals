The Wiremen’s Golf Association started back in 1958 and is great time spent with fellow IBEW members from various Locals. You do not have to be a great golfer to play, it’s about having a great time and playing a little golf.  If you are interested in playing or would like more information, please contact Greg Palmer at **[wga697@msn.com](mailto:wga697@msn.com)**



The 2023 Wiremen's Golf Association Annual Golf Tournament will be hosted by Local 86, Rochester, NY.  The Tournament will be August 16th - 18th with a 3-man Scramble on August 15th.  Tournament fee is TBD and the Scramble fee is TBD.  Please contact Greg Palmer at [wga697@msn.com](mailto:wga697@msn.com "mailto:wga697@msn.com") if interested in playing.  Deadline for entries is May 1st, 2023. Names and information are needed as soon as possible so Hotel information can be sent out and new players can get registered prior to the deadline.  The Host Hotel fills up pretty fast.







Thank you,



Greg Palmer
