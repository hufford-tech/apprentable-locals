# The Knowledgebase

Empowering IBEW Local 212 Members with Comprehensive Resources:

Benefits, Collective Bargaining Agreements and Membership Engagement

## Inside Collective Bargaining Agreement

The Inside collective bargaining agreement is also called the CBA or ‘The Agreement’ deals primarily with the Inside Journeyman Wireman Classification and the Inside Apprentices Classifications.

Start here if you have questions about Journeymen or Apprentice classifications.

[Inside Agreement](https://local212.com/inside/)

## TeleData Collective Bargaining Agreement

The TeleData collective bargaining agreement is also called the CBA or ‘The Agreement’ deals primarily with Data Technician classifications and the Data Technician Apprentice classifications.

Start here if you have questions about Data Technicians or Apprentice Technician classifications.

[TeleData Agreement](https://local212.com/teledata/)

## Southwest Recovery Agreement

The Southwest Recovery agreement is also called the CBA or ‘The Agreement’ deals primarily with the Construction Electrician Classification and the Construction Wireman Classifications.

Start here if you have questions about Construction Electrician or Construction Wireman classifications.

## A TRADITION OF EXCELLENCE

IBEW Local 212 has a long-standing tradition of excellence in the electrical industry. We are committed to providing our members with the highest quality training and support. Our focus is on maintaining high standards of professionalism and skill, ensuring our members are among the best in the field.

[About Us](https://local212.com/about/)

![](https://local212.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/logo-dark.webp)

IBEW Local 212 is dedicated to supporting and advancing the careers of skilled electricians. We offer top-tier training programs, excellent benefits, and a strong community committed to excellence in the electrical industry. Whether you're an experienced electrician or just starting out, we provide the resources and support you need to succeed.
