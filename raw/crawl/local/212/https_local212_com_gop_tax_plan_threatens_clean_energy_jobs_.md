# GOP Tax Plan Threatens Clean Energy Jobs

![](https://local212.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/06/action-1.png)

- [June 23, 2025](https://local212.com/2025/06/23/)
- [Chad](https://local212.com/author/wpadminuser/)

## Clean Energy Jobs

## 🔊 A Message from IBEW Leadership

On May 14, 2025, **IBEW International President Kenneth W. Cooper** released a powerful statement condemning the Republican-led tax proposal now advancing through Congress. His message was clear: this bill is a direct attack on **IBEW jobs, energy independence, and the American middle class.**

> “This legislation takes a sledgehammer to the progress the country has made in clean-energy technology and manufacturing.”
>
> — IBEW International President Kenneth W. Cooper
>
> [ACTION NETWORK – Take action now!](https://actionnetwork.org/letters/support-good-paying-energy-jobs-for-ibew-members-tell-your-member-of-congress-to-oppose-the-gop-tax-bill?source=direct_link&)

* * *

## 🛑 What’s at Stake?

The GOP’s new tax bill, currently moving to the House floor, includes **provisions to eliminate the clean-energy tax credits** established under the Inflation Reduction Act (IRA)—credits that have:

- **Supercharged the growth** of wind, solar, nuclear, and geothermal energy across the U.S.
- **Created tens of thousands of jobs**, many for union electricians and tradespeople
- **Fueled local economies**, especially in **GOP-held districts**, where the IRA has spurred the most investment

Repealing these credits doesn’t just slow development—it **kills jobs** and **jeopardizes the U.S.’s energy leadership** in an increasingly global race for clean power.

* * *

## 💼 The Real Cost: Jobs

According to Cooper, repealing these tax incentives will **destroy tens of thousands of energy jobs**—many of which currently support IBEW members in nearly every state.

That includes:

- Utility-scale solar and wind technicians
- Nuclear retrofit crews
- High-voltage electrical infrastructure teams
- Emerging geothermal and battery storage roles
- And more, all at risk

These are **family-sustaining union jobs**, often located in rural or economically hard-hit regions where such work has been transformational.

[ACTION NETWORK – Take action now!](https://actionnetwork.org/letters/support-good-paying-energy-jobs-for-ibew-members-tell-your-member-of-congress-to-oppose-the-gop-tax-bill?source=direct_link&)

* * *

## ⚡ Energy Demand Is Rising—Why Cut Now?

The surge in **AI development and data center construction** across the U.S. is driving **skyrocketing demand for reliable domestic energy**. Repealing the IRA tax credits now sends a dangerous signal: **that the U.S. is backing away from its energy future.**

Instead of:

- Building up domestic capacity
- Becoming a global leader in clean-tech manufacturing
- Fortifying the grid with high-skilled labor

…the GOP plan **cuts funding and gives tax breaks to billionaires like Elon Musk**—at the direct expense of American workers.

* * *

## 🔄 Twisting the Narrative

Some Republicans are claiming that keeping IRA labor protections intact is a “win” for workers. But Cooper makes it clear:

> “If there are no jobs left to protect, working people still lose.”

Labor standards only matter if projects exist. No jobs = no protections. It’s that simple.

* * *

## 🧭 What IBEW Stands For

The IBEW fights every day for:

✅ **Good jobs**

✅ **Energy security**

✅ **Fair wages and labor protections**

✅ **A future built by American hands**

This tax proposal threatens all of it—for nothing more than a billionaire windfall.

* * *

## 🗣️ Our Call to Action

IBEW Local 212 stands with President Cooper and the 860,000 active and retired IBEW members across the country in calling on **Congress to reject this anti-worker, anti-energy legislation.**

This isn’t about politics—it’s about:

- **Jobs in our community**
- **Training our apprentices for the future**
- **Powering America with union-made energy**

We urge every member to stay informed, contact your representatives, and **stand up for union jobs and energy progress.**

* * *

📣 **Let your voice be heard. The fight for clean, union-built energy is a fight for our future.**
