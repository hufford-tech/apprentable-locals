# Update Contact Information

Change of address – New Phone number – Email changes

#### what you need to get it done

## The process is easy!

Changing your address or updating your personal information with IBEW Local 212 is a multi-step process, use the following guide to make sure you update your address and personal information with IBEW Local 212 and all affiliated Plan Administrators and Benefit Providers.

1

### use the Local 212 webApp

Update your personal contact information with IBEW Local 212 using the Local 212 WebApp on your mobile device or by accessing it directly here – [IBEW Local 212 WebApp.](https://ibew212.workingsystems.com/wkrstatus)

Navigate to the <Personal Info> section to update your information.

Updating your information using the Local 212 WebApp will only update your personal information for the Union Hall.

**IBEW Local 212 partners with additional Benefit and Plan Administrators. You must provide updates to your personal information using their approved methods.**

**These providers are covered in Step 2.**

2

### Additional Benefit Providers

The following benefit administration providers require you change your personal information using their approved methods:

- IBEW International Office
  - [Change of Address Forms (ibew.org)](https://www.ibew.org/Tools/Change-of-Address)
- National Electrical Benefit Fund and the National Electrical Annuity Plan (NEBF/NEAP)
  - Create an account then login to the [NEBF/NEAP benefits portal](https://www.nebf.com/).
  - Navigate to the Change Your Address page to update your personal information.
- National Electrical 401k Plan (NEFP) this plan is optional, and you will only need to update your information if you participate in the 401k plan.
  - Create an account to login to the [NEFP benefits portal.](https://nefp.empower-retirement.com/participant/#/login)
- [Zenith American Solutions](https://edge.zenith-american.com/landingPage.php)
- [Delta Dental](https://www.deltadentaloh.com/Member/Using-Your-Benefits/Online-Tools/Member-Portal)
- [Anthem Vision](https://www.anthem.com/account-login/)
- [Apprenticeship Office](https://www.electricaltc.org/)
- DECA Credit Union 513-541-2480

3

### You are Almost Done!

Don’t forget to update your other important contacts with your new information.

[Official USPS® Change-of-Address Form](https://moversguide.usps.com/mgo/address-info)

## a comprehensive change of address checklist to help you manage the process smoothly:

### 1\. **Personal Information & Records**

- **Update with the Postal Service:** Submit a change of address form at your local post office or online at the USPS website.
- **Driver’s License/ID:** Update your address with the Department of Motor Vehicles (DMV).
- **Voter Registration:** Update your voter registration with your new address.
- **Social Security Administration:** If you receive benefits, update your address with the SSA.
- **IRS:** Update your address with the IRS to ensure tax documents reach you. Use Form 8822 for this.
- **Medical Records:** Inform your doctors, dentist, and other healthcare providers.
- **Schools/Colleges:** Notify your children’s schools and your alma mater if applicable.

### 2\. **Financial Accounts**

- **Banks & Credit Unions:** Update your address for checking, savings, and loan accounts.
- **Credit Card Companies:** Notify all credit card issuers.
- **Investment Accounts:** Update your address with brokers, mutual funds, and retirement accounts.
- **Insurance Policies:** Notify health, life, auto, and home insurance providers.

### 3\. **Utilities & Home Services**

- **Electric, Gas, Water:** Arrange for the transfer or cancellation of services at your old address and set up services at your new address.
- **Internet, Cable, Phone:** Transfer or cancel services and set up new services if needed.
- **Home Security:** Update your address and set up service at your new home.
- **Trash & Recycling:** Set up services at your new address.

### 4\. **Subscriptions & Memberships**

- **Magazines & Newspapers:** Update your address for any subscriptions.
- **Streaming Services:** Update your billing address.
- **Gym Memberships:** Notify your gym or consider transferring or canceling your membership.
- **Clubs & Organizations:** Update your address with any social, professional, or alumni organizations.

### 5\. **Work-Related**

- **Employer:** Update your address for payroll and benefits.
- **HR Department:** Ensure your new address is on file for any work-related documents.

### 6\. **Government Agencies**

- **State Tax Agency:** Update your address with your state’s tax department.
- **Veterans Affairs:** If applicable, notify the VA of your new address.
- **Immigration Services:** If you are a non-citizen, notify USCIS of your change of address using Form AR-11.

### 7\. **Others**

- **Friends & Family:** Send out a change of address notification to close friends and family.
- **Pet Microchip Company:** Update your pet’s microchip information with your new address.
- **Loyalty Programs:** Update your address with any retail or airline loyalty programs.
