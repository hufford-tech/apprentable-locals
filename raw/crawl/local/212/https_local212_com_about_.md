# About Us

Empowering electricians and strengthening communities since 1901

IBEW Local 212 is your trusted partner in excellence

## Vision

To continue our legacy as the leading provider of skilled electrical professionals in the Greater Cincinnati area, fostering a community where excellence in electrical work supports local economic growth and enhances the quality of life for our members and the community at large.

## Mission Statement

To train, support, and employ top-tier electricians and telecommunications technicians, ensuring safety, innovation, and quality in every project we undertake, while promoting union values and fair labor practices.

## Purpose

To significantly enhance the lives of our members and the broader communities we serve by providing comprehensive, high-quality training programs, ensuring the highest standards of safe working conditions, and relentlessly advocating for fair wages, robust benefits, and the overall well-being of our workforce. We are committed to fostering professional growth, safety, and economic stability for all our members, while also contributing positively to the local economy and community development.

## Goals

Expand the IBEW Local 212 membership and apprentice base to accommodate the growing demand for skilled labor in the region.

Foster partnerships with local businesses and educational institutions to create more opportunities for our members.

Increase community engagement and awareness about the benefits and importance of unionized labor.

## A TRADITION OF EXCELLENCE

IBEW Local 212 has a long-standing tradition of excellence in the electrical industry. We are committed to providing our members with the highest quality training and support. Our focus is on maintaining high standards of professionalism and skill, ensuring our members are among the best in the field.
