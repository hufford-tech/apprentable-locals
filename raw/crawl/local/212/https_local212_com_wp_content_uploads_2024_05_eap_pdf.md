## Help for Managing Life’s Challenges & Opportunities

TriHealth EAP is a prepaid benefit available to you as an employee of IBEW and to your family at no 
cost, even if you opt out of your employer’s health insurance plan. Our confidential services are 
designed not only to assist you in times of crisis, but also to help you thrive in all aspects of your 
life.

IBEW employees, their spouses/partners and dependents in the home are eligible for up to 6
confidential counseling sessions per issue with a licensed counselor. Be assured your employer 
does not receive names or any information about your participation.

• To schedule a confidential appointment, call 513 891 1627 or 1 800 642 9794,
or access “Request-a-Counselor” via <u>TriHealthEAP.com</u>

• To speak with a licensed clinician for urgent situations any time 24/7, call 1 800 642 9794.
