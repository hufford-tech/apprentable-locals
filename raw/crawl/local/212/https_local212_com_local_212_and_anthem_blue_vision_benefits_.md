# Local 212 and Anthem Blue Vision Benefits

![](https://local212.com/wp-content/uploads/2025/01/anthem-vision.png)

- [January 27, 2025](https://local212.com/2025/01/27/)
- [Chad](https://local212.com/author/wpadminuser/)

The following information is for the Inside Wireman and Inside Apprentice classifications, for help determining your IBEW Local 212 classification contact the Union Hall at 513-559-0200.

For more information regarding this benefit, please contact Anthem at 877-635-6403.

### Finding Vision Providers and plan details for Anthem Blue View Vision

Navigating vision care options can be overwhelming, but with Anthem’s Blue View Vision, it’s simple to find the right care. Whether you’re looking for routine eye exams, stylish eyewear, or specialty lens treatments, Blue View Vision offers extensive coverage through a vast network of providers. Here’s how you can make the most of your vision plan and find a provider that fits your needs.

[Find Vision Providers Here.](http://anthem.com/find-care)

* * *

#### What Blue View Vision Does for You.

Anthem Blue View Vision provides access to **multiple eye doctors** at **multiple locations** nationwide. This expansive network includes independent eye doctors, popular regional and national retail stores, and online providers. With flexible hours, including evenings and weekends, you can schedule care at your convenience, ensuring that vision health fits seamlessly into your busy life.

* * *

#### Steps to Find a Vision Provider in Your Network

Finding an eye doctor or retailer within the Anthem Blue View Vision network is quick and straightforward. Follow these steps:

1. **Visit Anthem’s Find Care Tool**:

   - Go to [anthem.com/find-care](http://anthem.com/find-care) or log into the Sydney Health mobile app.
   - You can search as a member or guest. Members can log in for a personalized search experience, while guests can use a basic search option.
2. **Specify Your Plan and Location**:

   - Select the type of care you need (vision) and the state where you’re located.
   - Enter your city, county, or ZIP code to find local providers.
3. **Browse Results**:

   - Results can be filtered by location, provider type, or other factors. You can view options in either list or map format for convenience.
4. **Make a Selection**:

   - Choose from options like LensCrafters®, Pearle Vision®, Target Optical®, or independent providers. Many of these retailers offer on-site labs for same-day glasses and services.

* * *

#### Vision Providers in the Network

Anthem’s network includes trusted names such as:

- **LensCrafters®**
- **Pearle Vision®**
- **Target Optical®**
- **America’s Best**
- **MyEyeDr.**
- **Eyeglass World**
- **_and many more, see additional documents below._**

Additionally, online retailers such as Glasses.com® and ContactsDirect® make it easy to shop for eyewear from the comfort of your home.

* * *

#### Lens and Eyewear Options

With Blue View Vision, you’ll have access to a wide array of lens options, including:

- **Anti-Reflective Coatings**: Reduce glare and enhance clarity.
- **Scratch-Resistant Coatings**: Keep lenses durable.
- **Polycarbonate and UV Lenses**: Protect eyes from impact and harmful rays.
- **Transitions® Lenses**: Adjust to changing light conditions, offering dual functionality as prescription sunglasses.

* * *

#### Additional Benefits of Blue View Vision

- **Cost Savings**: Members enjoy negotiated discounts on popular lens treatments and complete pairs of glasses.
- **Global Access**: Care is available at 20 international locations, with translation support in 160 languages and emergency eyewear delivery within 24 hours.
- **Comprehensive Health Benefits**: Eye doctors may detect early signs of chronic conditions such as diabetes and high blood pressure, ensuring holistic care.

Review the documents provided below for more detailed information.

* * *

#### Start Prioritizing Your Vision Today

Don’t let finding vision care be a hassle. With Anthem Blue View Vision, you’ll benefit from a large network, affordable options, and advanced tools to keep your vision—and overall health—in focus. Visit [anthem.com](http://anthem.com/), and [anthem.com/find-care](http://anthem.com/find-care) or download the [Sydney Health mobile app](https://www.anthem.com/member-resources/sydney-app) to find a provider and make your next vision appointment today.
