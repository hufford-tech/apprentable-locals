## A TRADITION OF EXCELLENCE

IBEW Local 212 has a long-standing tradition of excellence in the electrical industry. We are committed to providing our members with the highest quality training and support. Our focus is on maintaining high standards of professionalism and skill, ensuring our members are among the best in the field.

[About Us](https://local212.com/about/)

![](https://local212.com/wp-content/uploads/2024/05/logo-dark.webp)

IBEW Local 212 is dedicated to supporting and advancing the careers of skilled electricians. We offer top-tier training programs, excellent benefits, and a strong community committed to excellence in the electrical industry. Whether you're an experienced electrician or just starting out, we provide the resources and support you need to succeed.
