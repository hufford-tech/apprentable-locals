**Organizing: Wages**

IBEW Local 7 has over one hundred Electrical and Telecommunication contractors that hire highly skilled and trained Journeymen and apprentices in the Electrical and Voice Data Video fields. We at IBEW Local 7 feel that you should earn a wage that allows you to provide for your family, and not worry about your families’ future by having a good Health Insurance and Pension Plans for you and your Family. For more information on wages and benefits, click on the calculator below. Click the join tab in the menu to submit your application. We want to hear from you.
