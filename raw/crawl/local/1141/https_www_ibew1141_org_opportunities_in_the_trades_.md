# **Want a career in the skilled trades?**

The IBEW is a great career move for people looking to have a challenging, exciting work life and a good wage and benefit package for their families.

If you are **just starting out**, we recommend you connect with an organizer to see how we can benefit you.

**Get in touch** with us in one of the following ways:

1: If you live reasonably close to the greater **OKC** area, contact Organizer Allen Bresemann by [phone](tel:1-405-543-7512) or [text](sms:1-405-543-7512) now.

2: If you are in the **Lawton** area, or in a location near Pauls Valley or south of that region, please contact Organizer Steven Wicker by [phone](tel:1-580-695-0450) or [text](sms:1-580-695-0450).

3: If you’d like more information on the **current job picture**, in our jurisdiction as well in as the surrounding states, contact Dispatcher John Kirkland via [phone](tel:405-651-5200) or [text](sms:1-405-651-5200) now.

4: If you’d instead like us to **reach out to you**, [click here to fill out a short call-back form](https://www.ibew1141.org/schedule-a-call/).

If you are still exploring your options with various trades, check out the **Oklahoma Building Trades** website at [https://okbctc.org/](https://okbctc.org/). You can also learn about many **different apprenticeship paths** toward a rich and satisfying career.
