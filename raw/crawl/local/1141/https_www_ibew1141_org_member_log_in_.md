**How to Log in to the IBEW 1141 Member Portal**

Here’s a quick refresher for the website’s “members only” section.

1\. You have two fields to complete: “Username” and “Password.”

2\. Your Username is your email address we have on file with the hall. Please type in the entire address. For example: “HenryMiller@ibew.org” or “GeorgeWashington@gmail.com”.

3\. If this is your first time logging in, your password is your card number. PASSWORDS ARE CASE-SENSITIVE. If your card number is “D12345”, do NOT enter “d12345,” or it will not let you in.

4\. You can change your password at any time. At the login prompt, click on where it says “Forgot your password?” You will be redirected to a new page to submit your email address, and a link to update your password will be emailed to you.

5\. If you get stuck, please feel free to email IBEW 1141 Communications Assistant Lucas directly at [media@ibew1141.org](mailto:media@ibew1141.org) and he will be happy to help you.
