## **Make Payments the Easy Way**

If you are wanting to make a dues or death benefit payment, please text the IBEW Local 1141 office at [844-337-5808](sms:1-844-337-5808). Laura will call you from (405) prefix “670” to take your payment (the Hall’s landline phone number), and then mail your receipt to your home address.

We have enhanced our credit card processing to enable more convenient dues and death benefit payment options. A brief explanation is that it involves “tokenization,” which is a secure way to store credit or debit card information off-site at the bank.

If you would like to sign up for this feature, we ask you to text the office at [844-337-5808](sms:1-844-337-5808) with “I, (your name), give IBEW permission to store my credit card information.” **PLEASE DO NOT text your credit card information to the Local 1141 Office.** Laura will get this information verbally from you when she calls.

If you choose this new feature, the last Wednesday of every month, you will receive a text letting you know how much your dues and death benefit payment will be for the following month. **WE WILL NOT CHARGE YOUR CARD until you reply “YES” to the text.** If you want to pay additional month(s)/death(s), you would reply “YES” with the number of months and death benefits you wish to pay.

You will receive your credit card receipt via email within 48 hours and your dues receipt will be mailed.
