# **Need CEUs? This list can help.**

[OSHA 10 Hour](https://www.360training.com/course/osha-10-hour-outreach-construction-free-study-guide?utm_source=google&utm_medium=cpc&utm_campaign=610355519&utm_content=146396524255&utm_term=online%20osha%2010%20hour%20construction%20training&gclid=Cj0KCQjwz8emBhDrARIsANNJjS5iWbea3sa8QZrfJ91YjQ8J7LUYplPiepJSjxSucN_bj8e3H3p2lAoaAskTEALw_wcB)

[OSHA 30 Hour](https://www.360training.com/osha-campus/osha-30-hour-online)

[First Aid](https://www.nationalcprfoundation.com/?gclid=CjwKCAjwpuajBhBpEiwA_ZtfhV61NkLYJOVD6MAyGJ9QERfzKeBoVVSF-7g9UIAQw7Ukd65TKSR3ThoCKmcQAvD_BwE#)

When you receive your completion certificate, send a copy of your certification and receipt to the Hall — either in person, by mail, or email\*. You will receive a reimbursement check in the mail in about 30 days. Code Update classes are not reimbursable.
