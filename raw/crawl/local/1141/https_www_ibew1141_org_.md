# **The UNION Difference:**

### **Union members work together to negotiate and enforce a contract with management that guarantees the things you care about —** **like decent raises, affordable health care, job security, and a stable schedule.**

## **[![](https://i0.wp.com/www.ibew1141.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/kisspng-computer-icons-money-wallet-icon-design-money-border-5b3ca95d8b38f6.3971894715307021735703.png?resize=75%2C75&ssl=1)](https://www.ibew1141.org/more-than-just-a-paycheck-why-ibew-1141-electricians-come-out-ahead/)**

## **HIGHER WAGES**

**$191 more per week** higher than their unrepresented counterparts across industries.

## **[![](https://i0.wp.com/www.ibew1141.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/400fe99bb9e1d29f61659b61473a20bb.png?resize=96%2C74&ssl=1)](https://www.ibew1141.org/the-benefit-of-benefits-why-union-health-care-is-worth-it/)**

## **BETTER BENEFITS**

More likely to have employer-provided **pensions** and **health insurance**.

## **[![](https://i0.wp.com/www.ibew1141.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/07/ad6f049210f1472cb6c81226cbf6037b.png?resize=61%2C68&ssl=1)](https://www.ibew1141.org/the-union-advantage-for-electricians/)**

**Safe working conditions** that prevent death, illness, and injury.

### **Powering Oklahoma City, Lawton, and Beyond**

IBEW 1141 represents **highly-trained**, highly-skilled power professionals in the OKC metro and more.

Our collectively bargained employment agreements mean that you, your spouse, and your children can **enjoy quality health care coverage** – something not all other electrical contractors are willing to offer you.

**Make the switch**. [Connect with IBEW 1141 today](tel:405-670-4777).
