One of the best benefits of being a member of LU 1141 is our amazing health plan. Our members and their families pay no health care premiums and enjoy low co-pays and deductibles. The IBEW-NECA Southwestern Health and Benefit Fund offers a web site where you can get information about your eligibility, work history, and claims history.

**To access plan and claim information:**

1. Visit [https://www.zenith-american.com/](https://www.zenith-american.com/)
2. Click on “Log In”
3. At the drop-down menu, where it says “Account Type,” select the first option for “Participant Edge.”
4. At the next “LOGIN” option on the new screen that opens, select “Participant Edge” one more time.
5. If you are a first time user, you must click “register” in order to create a username, password and setup your security questions and answer. Otherwise, log in to your account in the window.

Use the links below to view Summary Plan Description documentation:

**[Main Plan](https://www.ibew1141.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Main-Plan.pdf)**– For JWs, Apprentices, and CEs

**[Sub Plan B1](https://www.ibew1141.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Sub-Plan-B1.pdf)**– For CW1s, CW2s, and CW3s

**[Sub Plan B2](https://www.ibew1141.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Sub-Plan-B2.pdf)** – For CW4s

To find a doctor, hospital or other provider in-network, [click here](http://provider.bcbs.com/). Note: at the prompt that reads “Find your plan by prefix [(click here to view example)](https://www.ibew1141.org/health-benefits/#example),” type the letters “ **SWH**”, then continue to follow the search instructions.
