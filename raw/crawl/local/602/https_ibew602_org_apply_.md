# Apply

Even Artificial Intelligence Needs Electricity...

#### Welcome to the IBEW Local 602 Application Page. Whether you are just starting out in the electrical industry or you have previous experience, we have opportunities for you. This page provides links to our apprenticeship program for beginners and a short application for experienced individuals.

## **No Experience? No Problem!**

If you have no prior experience in the electrical industry, our apprenticeship program is the perfect place to start. We offer comprehensive training that combines classroom instruction with hands-on experience, preparing you for a successful career as an electrician. Click the link below to learn more and apply for our apprenticeship program.

[Get Electrical Training](https://wtxjatc.org/ "Get Electrical Training")

![](https://ibew602.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/03/IBEW-602-Earn-While-You-Learn.png)

## **Have Experience?**

If you already have experience in the electrical industry, you can apply online through the links below. Each application will open in a new tab or window so you can complete it and return to this page at any time. Our experienced applicant forms are powered by the Trades Backer web app, making it easy to securely apply from your phone, tablet, or computer. Please select the application that best matches your trade experience.

[Inside Electrical Application](https://www.tradesbacker.com/projects/ibew-602-amarillo-tx/apply?src=direct&job=ibew-602-amarillo-tx-electrical-electrical-application&kid=8d952f50fe6c980589f16f94 "Inside Electrical Application")

[Outside Line Application](https://www.tradesbacker.com/projects/ibew-602-amarillo-tx/apply?src=direct&job=ibew-602-amarillo-tx-utility-utility-application&kid=8d952f50fe6c980589f16f94 "Outside Line Application")

#### **Why Join IBEW Local 602?**

- **Career Opportunities:** Gain access to a wide range of employment opportunities within the electrical industry.
- **Support and Resources:** Benefit from the support, training, and resources provided by our union.
- **Community:** Join a community of skilled professionals dedicated to excellence and solidarity.

#### **For any questions or additional information, please feel free to [contact us](https://www.ibew602.org/contact-us/).**

#### **We look forward to welcoming you to IBEW Local 602 and supporting your career in the electrical industry.**
