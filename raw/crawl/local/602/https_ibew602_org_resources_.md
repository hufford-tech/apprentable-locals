# Member Resources

Tailored by Members

#### Welcome to the Useful Resources page of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local Union 602(IBEW 602). This central hub provides our members with easy access to a wealth of information and valuable tools designed to support your career and enhance your membership experience.

#### Here, you can explore a variety of resources:

- **New Member Orientation:** Get started with essential information tailored for new members of our community.
- **Member Portal:** Your comprehensive resource for managing your membership details
- **Newsletter Articles:** Stay informed with the latest news, updates, and insights relevant to our industry and union activities.
- **Calendar:** Keep track of union events, meetings, and important dates with our comprehensive calendar.
- **Affiliates:** Discover our network of affiliated organizations and partners.
- **Discounts:** Access exclusive member-only discounts on products and services.
- **Member Benefits:** Learn about the full range of benefits available exclusively to our members.
- **Bylaws:** Review the rules and regulations that govern our operations and member activities.
- **Collective Bargaining Agreements:** Understand your rights and the terms of employment negotiated on your behalf.

#### This page is your gateway to staying connected and informed. We encourage you to explore these resources and make the most of your membership.
