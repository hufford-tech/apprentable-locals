# Dispatch

Available-for-Work List Procedures

#### Welcome to the Dispatch page of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local Union 602(IBEW 602). This central hub provides a starting point designed to support you and enhance your employment experience through IBEW Local 602.

#### **Sign the Books for the First Time**

**New Members** and **Travelers** from other locals can complete the initial sign-in to the available-for-work list online by clicking the link below. After being added to the available-for-work list, you will gain access to the Local 602 member portal for re-signs and all future book signings.

[Sign Books](https://www.ibew602.org/books/ "Sign Books")

#### **Dispatch Rules and Procedures**

**DISPATCH IS AT 9:00 A.M. EACH WEEKDAY MORNING.**

**AVAILABLE-FOR-WORK LIST SIGN-IN TIME: ALL DAY**

**OFFICE HOURS:** 8:00 A.M. TO 5:00 P.M., MONDAY THROUGH FRIDAY

**Sign-In:** Complete the original [sign-in online](https://www.ibew602.org/dispatch/books/) by submitting the required documentation. After the initial sign-in, you will receive an email providing further instructions regarding access to the Local 602 member portal for bidding on jobs, re-signs and future book signings.

Alternatively, you may sign in person during business hours (8:00 A.M. to 5:00 P.M.), Monday through Friday at 200 S. Fannin in Amarillo or 405 50th Street in Lubbock.

**Re-sign:**

Failure to re-sign between the 10th and 16th will result in removal from the available-for-work list. Purging occurs the next business day after the 16th.

**Job Call Procedures:**

- Sign your appropriate book only with confirmation of termination from your last employer.
- All calls for manpower are posted on the website the evening prior to dispatch.
- To view job listings, check “Jobs Available” in the [Member Portal](https://ibew602.workingsystems.com/workeropenjobs) or LaborPower app.
- Indicate your interest by bidding on jobs through the Member Portal or LaborPower app between 5:00 P.M. and 9:00 A.M. on business days and anytime on weekends and holidays, or call the Amarillo Hall at (806) 376-9945 or the Lubbock Hall at (806) 744-4062 between 8:00 A.M. and 9:00 A.M. the morning after a call is posted.

For detailed dispatch procedures, click the link below:
