# New Member Orientation

## Welcome Brothers and Sisters!

#### Embark on your journey with the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers at Local 602 through our comprehensive orientation pages. Each section is meticulously tailored to guide new members across our various bargaining units, offering a foundational understanding of what it means to be a part of our dynamic community.

#### **Dive into a Wealth of Information**

- **Discover Our Essence:** Uncover the core principles, missions, and values that shape the IBEW and Local 602. Understand the ‘how’s and ‘why’s of our organization, fostering a deep sense of belonging and purpose.
- **Explore Membership Perks:** Familiarize yourself with the myriad of programs, benefits, and opportunities exclusively available to our members. From health to career growth, we’ve got you covered.
- **Know Your Rights and Responsibilities:** Equip yourself with essential knowledge about your rights as a member and the responsibilities that come with it, ensuring a harmonious and productive environment for all.
- **Our Proud History:** Journey through the rich history of the IBEW and Local 602, learning how our past has sculpted our present and future.
- **Get Involved:** Discover various avenues to actively participate and contribute to our community, enhancing both personal growth and the collective strength of our union.

#### **A Glimpse into Each Realm**

While each orientation page serves as a valuable resource, offering snapshots of various topics, they are intentionally concise. For those seeking more detailed information or access to actual documents, our [Member Resources Page](https://www.ibew602.org/resources/) is your next destination. There, you’ll find links to additional perks, and official documents that further enrich your understanding and involvement in our union.
