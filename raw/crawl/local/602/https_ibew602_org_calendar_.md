## Regular Monthly ScheduleChanges Reflected in Upcoming Events

### **Main Meeting:**

IBEW Local 602 General Membership meetings are held on the **2nd Wednesday** of each month at **6:30pm**. The Meeting Hall doors open at **6:00pm**.

200 S Fannin St, Amarillo, TX 79106, USA

### **Lubbock Division Meeting:**

IBEW Local 602 Lubbock General Membership meetings are held on the **1st Thursday** of each month at 6 **:30pm**. The Meeting Hall doors open at 6 **:00pm**.

405 50th St, Lubbock, TX 79404, USA

### **Executive Board Meeting:**

IBEW Local 602 Executive Board meetings are held on the **4th Wednesday** of each month at **6:00pm**. _Elected E-Board Members Only_

200 S Fannin St, Amarillo, TX 79106, USA

### **Amarillo Labor/Management Cooperation Committee:**

IBEW602/WTXNECA Amarillo LMCC meetings are held on the **4th Monday** of each month at **12:00pm**, unless moved.

_Location determined by the committee members._

### **Lubbock Labor/Management Cooperation Committee:**

IBEW602/WTXNECA Lubbock LMCC meetings are held on the **2nd Monday** of each month at **12:00pm**, unless moved.

_Location determined by the committee members._

### **SPS Meetings:**

IBEW Local 602 SPS Employee Unit meetings vary. **Contact your steward** for current dates and times.

### **MTC Meeting:**

Metal Trades Council of Amarillo & Vicinity meetings are held on the **2nd Monday** of each month at **6:00pm**.

200 S Fannin St, Amarillo, TX 79106, USA

### **PGU Meeting:**

Pantex Guards Union meetings are held on the **4th Tuesday** of each month at **6:40pm**, and on the **4th Wednesday** of each month at **12:00pm** and **6:40pm**.

200 S Fannin St, Amarillo, TX 79106, USA

### **IUOE Meeting:**

International Union of Operating Engineers meetings are held on the **1st Thursday** of each month at **5:00pm**.

200 S Fannin St, Amarillo, TX 79106, USA

### **West Texas JATC Meeting:**

West Texas Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee main meetings are held on the **1st Wednesday** of each month at **12:00pm**, unless moved.

_Location determined by the committee members._

### **Amarillo JATC Sub Committee:**

West Texas Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee Amarillo Sub meetings are held on the **1st Thursday** of each month at **5:00pm**, unless moved.

100 Bowie St, Amarillo, TX 79106

### **Lubbock JATC Sub Committee:**

West Texas Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee Lubbock Sub meetings are held on the **3rd Tuesday** of each month at **5:00pm**, unless moved.

405 50th St, Lubbock, TX 79404, USA

### **Women’s Committee:**

IBEW602 Women’s Committee meetings are held on the **2nd Thursday** of each month at **6:00pm**, unless moved.

200 S Fannin St, Amarillo, TX 79106, USA
