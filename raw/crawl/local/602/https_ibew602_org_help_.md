# Help

Welcome to the IBEW Local 602 Video Tutorial Center — your one-stop resource for learning how to apply, sign the books, and navigate the LaborPower Mobile App and Member Portal. Whether you’re a current member, an applicant preparing to join, or a traveler looking to understand our process, these short video guides walk you through every step.

Here you’ll find easy, practical tutorials on how to:

- Apply for membership or apprenticeship

- Log in to the Member Portal
- Sign or re-sign the out-of-work list

- View and bid on job calls

- Update your personal information

- Pay dues and manage your membership

- Stay informed and connected with Local 602


Our goal is to make your experience smooth, transparent, and accessible — empowering you to take full advantage of the tools available to every IBEW member.
