# Payments

Dues and Death Benefits

#### Welcome to the IBEW Local 602 Payments page. Here, members can access the member portal to manage their status, make payments, and update personal information. Our streamlined system ensures you can efficiently handle your dues and benefits from the convenience of your home or office.

#### **Member Portal Access**

The member portal is your comprehensive resource for managing your membership details. Through the portal, you can:

- **Check Member Status:** Stay up-to-date with your membership standing.
- **Make Monthly Dues Payments:** Ensure your membership remains in good standing by paying your monthly dues online.
- **Pay Death Benefits:** Handle any death benefit payments promptly and securely.
- **Update Contact Information:** Keep your contact details current to receive important communications and updates.

Access the member portal by clicking the link below:

[Member Portal](https://ibew602.workingsystems.com/ "Member Portal")

[![](https://ibew602.org/wp-content/uploads/2025/11/Video-Help-1-150x150.png)](https://ibew602.org/help/ "Video Help")

![](https://ibew602.org/wp-content/uploads/2024/06/Labor-Power-App.png)

#### **How to Use the Member Portal**

1. **Log In:** Use your card number as your username to log into the portal.
2. **Navigate:** Once logged in, use the navigation menu to find the relevant sections for dues payments and updating contact information.
3. **Make Payments:** Follow the prompts to make secure payments for your monthly dues or any death benefits.
4. **Update Information:** Ensure your contact information is accurate and up-to-date.

Our member portal is designed to provide a user-friendly experience, making it easy for you to manage your membership responsibilities efficiently.

#### **For any assistance or questions regarding the member portal or payment processes, please feel free to [contact](https://www.ibew602.org/contact-us/) our office during business hours.**
