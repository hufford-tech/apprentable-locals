# Our History

> “I believe it well to consider the farsightedness of our leaders in the past, for present achievement. To hold a membership card in our organization is an asset to any man working in the electrical industry.”

– Brother Al E. Danielson, June 1929 IBEW Journal

### IBEW 340 By the Year

**1912**

IBEW Local 340 was issued a charter from the International office

**1917**

50 members

**1929**

80 members

**1930**

125-150 members

**1938**

IBEW Local 340’s Apprenticeship Program was recognized

**1942**

Charter was amended to incorporate Local Union #36 (Lineman’s Local in Sacramento, CA) and #1188 (Redding, CA)

**1953**

Welfare Plan was enacted, thought to be the first for electrical workers in California

**1960**

1,000 members

**1967**

Pension Plan instituted

**1998**

Charter was amended to incorporate IBEW Local Union 442 (Redding, CA)

**2017**

1,927 members
