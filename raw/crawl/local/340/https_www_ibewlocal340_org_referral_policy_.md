# IBEW Local 340 Referral Policy

### JOB CALL RECORDING FOR THE NEXT WORKING DAY

**Job Line (updated after 6 p.m.):**(916) 927-4160   **Website:** [**www.ibewlocal340.org**](http://205.196.220.11/)

**IBEW LOCAL 340’S MONTHLY RE-SIGN PROCEDURES**

**BEGINS: 8 a.m. on the 10** **th** **of the month**

**ENDS: 5 p.m. on the 16th of the month**

To maintain your place on Local 340’s Out-of-Work Lists, you must adhere to the following Monthly Re-Sign Procedures. The easiest way to re-sign is either in person, or via the Local’s website. However, if you choose to re-sign via mail, email, or fax, the following information must be provided: Full Name, IBEW Card No., Book type (Inside, Book #, Full Address, Phone Numbers, and Email Address.

### Instructions For Local 340 Re-Sign

**_VIA THE WEBSITE_**

- Visit: [www.ibewlocal340.org](http://205.196.220.11/)
- Click on “Member Login” you will be prompted to enter your username & password (see instructions on page if it is your first time or you Forgot Your Password).
- Once logged in you will click on Registration (a drop-down menu will display) click on Re-Sign.

**_MAIL, EMAIL OR FAX_** – MUST BE RECEIVED (Not Postmarked!) in the Business Office with all required information by 5 p.m. on the 16th of the month.

**_VIA EMAIL_** – Email the “Re-Sign” form or an email with all the required information to: [dispatch@ibewlocal340.org](mailto:dispatch@ibewlocal340.org). Type “Re-Sign” in the subject box.

**_U.S. MAIL_** – Mail Local 340’s Re-Sign Form or a letter with all information provided to:

**_IBEW LU 340 Re-Sign_**

**_10240 Systems Parkway, Ste.100_**

**_Sacramento, CA 95827_**

DO NOT SEND ANYTHING ELSE IN THIS ENVELOPE!

**_VIA FAX_** – Fax Local 340’s Re-sign form or a letter with all the required information to 916-927-1074.

**_IN PERSON_** – At both Halls (SACRAMENTO & REDDING) – any weekday during the re-sign days (excluding Holidays) between the hours of 8:00AM and 4:45PM Pacific Time.

_\*Please note the halls are temporarily available by appointment only due to COVID restrictions._
