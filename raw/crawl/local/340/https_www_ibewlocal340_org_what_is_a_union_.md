# What is a Union?

### What is a Union?

A union is an organization of workers joined together for a common purpose, for mutual aid and protection, to engage in concerted activity and collective bargaining, to elevate their conditions of life and labor.

It is an organization by which ordinary people come together to accomplish extraordinary things.

### What We Believe

Everyone has the right to work, to free choice of employment, to just and favorable conditions of work and to protection against unemployment. Everyone, without discrimination, has the right to equal pay for equal work. We believe that strength that comes from joining together to work toward common goals.

### We Are IBEW – International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers

IBEW is the largest, strongest, and oldest union devoted to electrical workers in the world. With more than 750,000 active and retired members, [IBEW](http://www.ibew.org/) local unions are spread throughout the United States and Canada.

### IBEW Local 340 – Your Local Union

We are your local union. You and your coworkers elect the officers of Local 340, attend union meetings where the members set policy for your officers to carry out, and vote on contracts that your representatives negotiate.

Local 340 has the main responsibility of enforcing your rights under the union contract.

Join IBEW Local 340

### NECA and IBEW – Working Together

The [National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA), Greater Sacramento Chapter](http://www.necasac.org/) and IBEW Local 340 are industry partners with a shared vision focused on: partnering, establishing the electrical standard, commitment to customer satisfaction, honesty, quality, public safety and continually seeking and pursuing opportunities to build better communities.

Our mission is to exceed our customer expectations by providing a quality electrical installation on time and within budget, while utilizing the IBEW Local 340 workforce.
