# Contractors

IBEW Local 340 is proud to work in partnership with the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA), Greater Sacramento Chapter and its 40+ electrical contractor members. Together, we lead the electrical industry through training, promotion of higher standards, quality workmanship and the opportunity to enhance our communities.

Learn more about becoming a partner contractor, contact Mike Franklin.
