## Union of the Year!

Last month our Local was recognized by
the Contra Costa Central Labor Council as
“Union of the Year”. This award was received
by our Business Manager Julian Vinatieri at
the CLC’s annual Labor to Labor dinner.

The CLC is comprised of over 85 affiliated Union’s
throughout our county. Our Local was selected
because of our political engagement last year. We
were recognized for our efforts in leaflet drops,
phone banking, lobbying at the capitol, and for our
support in helping Shanelle Scales-Preston get
elected as County Supervisor.

# Calling All Retirees!

Are you retired? Are you retiring soon? Did you know that there is a club where you can continue to engage
in the camaraderie of your “toolies”?

The IBEW Local 302 Retiree’s Club members are waiting to welcome you. Our club meets for a short
meeting and then we enjoy our fantastic potluck lunch every second (2nd) Thursday of the month (except
December) at 11:00 am at the IBEW Local 302 hall.

Usually once a quarter, we also meet for a field trip. We will either go out to eat someplace special or visit
some type of fun entertainment venue. The club pays a portion of these adventures.

Retired Local 302 members and their “significant others” -be they spouse, partner, or friend- are all
welcome! Annual dues are currently only $20.00 per person, $40.00 per couple (paid Jan/Feb of each year).
Birthdays and anniversaries are recognized with a card. Our “Holiday Dinner 2025” seasonal celebration, in
lieu of a December meeting, is being held at “The Greenery” in Walnut Creek.

So come check us out. It really is a fun time. The more - the merrier!

Text me or call me and please leave a message (Sorry, I don’t answer calls unless you are in my contacts) or
email me to find out more about the club. I will return your inquiry.

We are all looking forward to you joining us.

## A Message from your Business Manager:

Dear 302 Members,

First and foremost, I want to thank all of our members for placing their trust in me during
the recent election. I truly appreciate everyone who took the time to show up and make their
voices heard. I look forward to leading our Local into its next chapter.

I’d also like to thank our Election Judge, J.J. Sevilla. The role isn’t an easy one, but J.J. did an
excellent job. Special thanks as well to our Election Tellers—Russ McNally, Aran Rogers, and
Arturo Morfin—for stepping up and serving with integrity.

Now that I’ve had a couple of months to settle into this role, I wanted to reach out and
reconnect. We’ll be bringing back our regular newsletter to keep everyone informed. Our
goal is to publish it quarterly. This is your Local, and you deserve to know what’s happening.
While Local meetings remain a great way to stay involved, I understand that not everyone
can attend regularly.

A few updates to share: The Executive Board recently approved a $20 merchandise credit for
members who pay their dues for a full calendar year. This credit can be used toward any of
our merchandise items. This merchandise credit must be redeemed in person. In addition,
we’ll begin tracking attendance at meetings and volunteer events. These records will be used
for an end-of-year raffle or giveaway—details to come soon.

In closing, thank you for your hard work, your dedication, and your patience as we move
forward together. I’m proud to represent you and excited for what’s ahead for Local 302.

In Solidarity,
