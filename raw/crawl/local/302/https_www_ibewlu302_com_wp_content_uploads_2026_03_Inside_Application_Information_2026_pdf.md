## APPLICATION ANNOUNCEMENT

## The following information can also be found on our website at www.ibewlu302.com

Position: Apprentice Electrician (Inside Wireman) Starting Wage currently is $27.30 an hour. This is a five-year apprenticeship program. Apprentices work during the day and attend classes two nights per week. A Journeyman certificate is awarded upon successful completion.

When: Applications are not being accepted currently. Should there be any changes, information will be posted on our website at www.ibewlu302.com

Tests: Applicants meeting the minimum requirements will be scheduled for the following steps. Notification will be verbal/or by mail. Eligibility will be determined by the Committee.

1. The NJATC Aptitude Test (including Algebra and Functions and a Reading Comprehension test) (see: www.electricaltrainingalliance.org website for more information)
2. Oral interview only if a qualifying score is achieved on the aptitude test.

## Minimum Requirements: Each applicant must:

1. Be 18 years of age or older, with proof of identification, such as a drivers’ license or identification card issued by the D.M.V. (Must have a valid drivers’ license at time of indenture).
2. Be a High School graduate or equivalent.
3. Have completed one full year (2 semesters) of algebra in High School or one semester of algebra in College or Adult School with a passing grade of “C” or better.
4. Applicants will be required to pass a pre-employment drug/alcohol test.
5. Women and minorities are encouraged to apply.

## What to turn in with your completed application:

1. Proof of identification.
2. An official sealed copy of your High School Transcripts signed by the Registrar’s Office (No Diplomas) which we will check to verify that it includes your graduation date and proof of meeting the algebra requirement. If you did not graduate from High School, you will also need to provide an official GED Certificate as proof of High School Equivalency Graduation. If you did not take algebra in High School, you must also provide your College or Adult School Transcripts as proof of the algebra requirement. The burden of proof is the applicant’s responsibility. (NJATC online Tech Math Course meets the algebra requirement http://electricaltrainingalliance.org/Learning/OnlineTechMath)
3. All records must be presented in English.
