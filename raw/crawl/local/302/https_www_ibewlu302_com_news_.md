## Inside Allocation Results:   $1.00 H&W, $0.50 JATC, $1.00 Pension, $2.00 Check

**SOUND & COMMUNICATIONS NEWS**

The Sound & Communications members approved a new four-year agreement on Saturday, October 4, 2025, by all members in attendance. The members allocated $1.65 to wages, $1.25 to Health & Welfare, $1.00 to Pension and $0.35 to the HRA for an effective date of December 1, 2025. Local 302 is under Schedule E of the 9th District/Northern California Sound & Communications Agreement Addendum #2 and the increases will be 90% of the actual Wage Increase.
