# Register with IBEW Local 302

Register with ibewlu302.com to stay up to date on important issues affecting you and our family. You will have full access to the members resources and the ability to manage your own settings. You will also have the option to be added to relevant mailing lists in order to receive important e-mails on current issues.

You must register to use the Member Only section of the website. Please complete the form below to register. Information will be used for internal union business only, IBEW Local 302 will not share your information with any outside sources or entities.

Make sure you read and understand the Terms of Use-Privacy Policy.

It will take 24-48 hours to process & approve your registration, if you do not receive confirmation within 48 hours please call (925) 228-2302.

This protected area web site is for the convenience of members and any information herein should be kept confidential.
