### **IBEW 972 ~ Working Hand in Hand for a strong tomorrow**

_**Providing quality electricians to the Mid-Ohio Valley since 1919**_

**The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local Union 972 is a proud part of the Mid-Ohio Valley. Chartered June 19th 1919, we currently represent 261 Union Electricians that live and work within the jurisdiction for a variety of customers, and locations.**

[NECA](https://www.necanet.org/) [Health Fund](http://www.4thdistricthealthfund.com/) [Affiliated Links](https://www.ibew972.com/972AffiliatedLinks/)

### **Why should you join ibew local 972**

The National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) jointly sponsor apprenticeship training programs that offer you the opportunity to earn wages and benefits while you learn the skills needed for a trade that can be both challenging and rewarding.
