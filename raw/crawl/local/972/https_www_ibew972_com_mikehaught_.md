Michael E Haught

Business Manager / Financial Secretary - IBEW Local Union # 972

& Executive Training Director - Marietta Electrical JATC

Mike has been a member of IBEW Local Union # 972 since July of 2007.

He served as Organizer under past Business Manager Brother Ryan Dyar.

He became Business Manager in September 2022.

Mike resides in Fleming with his wife Brenda and loves to spoil his Granddaughters.

On behalf of the IBEW he serves on the following committees:

Athens County Building Trades Association

Central LMC, Inc (Board of Directors)

IBEW 972 Pension & Vacation Fund (Trustee)

Marietta Electrical JATC (Secretary/Treasurer)

Ohio State Electrical Workers Association E-Board

Parkersburg-Marietta Contractors & Trades Building Trades Association

Southeastern Ohio Business Managers Association

Southeastern Ohio AFL-CIO AFL & CLC

WV-OH Supplemental Health Fund (Trustee)

4th District IBEW Health Fund (Trustee)

Central Regional LMCC (Chairman)
