**Sue Huck**

**Office Administrator**

Sue has a variety of different responsibilities including all of the bookkeeping and assisting in overseeing the financials for the IBEW Local Union 972, Electrical Workers Home Inc. and the Marietta Electrical JATC.

For questions regarding the Pension or Vacation Funds, she is who you will need to contact.

Sue can also assist members with most of their health care insurance inquiries or direct them to someone who can. For instance, getting the necessary forms to change beneficiaries or to add your new addition(s) to your health plan.

She has been with us since Sept. 2010 and will help you in any way she can.

**Sue can be reached at the office 740-373-5054 x 114or by email [sue@ibew972.org](mailto:sue@ibew972.org).**

Sue resides in Churchtown with her husband Rob on their farm.  Sue has 2 adult children and 1 granddaughter + 2 grandpup's.
