July 29th, 2026

To:  All IBEW Local Union # 972 Stewards and Members

Subject:  Collection for Brother Travis McCutcheon

Brothers & Sisters,

I need to bring to your attention that Brother Travis McCutcheon will be off work for several weeks after surgery.

It has been the policy of this local union to help its members in need.  Therefore, I ask that a collection be taken by each project Steward and at

the Union Hall to assist him during this hard time.

For those who do not recognize Travis, he has been a member with our Local since October 1995 and currently a Journeyman.  Thank you

in advance for your generosity and compassion for your brother Travis.

United We Stand,

Michael E Haught

Business Manager

To:  All IBEW Local Union # 972 Stewards and Members

Brother & Sisters,
