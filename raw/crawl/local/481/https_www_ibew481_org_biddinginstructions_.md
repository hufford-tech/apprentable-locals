**BIDDING INSTRUCTIONS _(for non-local 481 members)_**

1\. Get on the Out-of-Work List

You must be on the Out-of-Work List before you can bid. To be added, your home local must fax our office with your information. Our fax number is 317 923 6662.

2\. Log In

Log in to the website (ibew481.org) after 4:30 PM.

3\. View Available Jobs

Click the “View All Jobs” button.

4\. Submit Your Bid

Select the jobs you want to bid on, rank them in order of preference, and submit your bid.

5\. Confirmation Email

You will receive a confirmation email if your bid was successfully submitted.

6\. Next Steps

The referral agent will contact members who are awarded jobs the next morning. If you do not receive a call, log back in after 4:30 PM to submit new bids.
