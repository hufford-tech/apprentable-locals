# NEXT STEP

Aptitude Test

Aptitude tests are one of the most commonly used assessments in measuring a candidate’s suitability for a role.

The IBEW wants to ensure that their candidates have a solid ability to comprehend written text, basic algebra, and have some mechanical aptitude.

The IBEW aptitude test consists of two parts – math and reading.

1. The math portion covers algebra and functions.
2. The reading portion covers comprehension.

APTITUDE TEST FEE

**Please note: Tests are scheduled throughout the year and once you have applied, you will receive a letter regarding the next test date.  Depending on the timing of your application, it may take up to four months before you receive a test date letter.**

**\-\-\-----------**

In order to take the aptitude test for this Apprenticeship program, a non-refundable test fee of $22 is to be paid at the time you receive the test notification letter. The test payment confirms your spot for that test.
