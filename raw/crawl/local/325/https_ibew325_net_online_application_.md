# Apprenticeship Application

## Apprenticeship Application

The apprenticeship program requires that you are either a High School graduate or you have a GED. You must have also completed and received credit for Algebra I (or some higher math) in high school. These items must be provided at time of Indenture. Failure to provide substantiated documents will result in your name being removed from the list of eligible applicants.
