. Dixie has volunteered to set up a smoker following the regular union meeting Scheduled for December 20 at 5:00pm.

Work at this time is OK but not fantastic, we do have members working in sister locals. There has been a slowdown of projects to bid and 
getting material is proving to be challenging to say the least. The Bluestone wind project has been experiencing the delays in material and 
has not been able to get things going as anticipated. On a good note, it appears that the project LeChase was going to bring in a non-union 
electrical contractor to do, has reconsidered and Schuler-Haas is scheduled to do the work tentatively with an early 2022 start date. We 
were not so lucky in getting the new sports center on Airport road. I did have a phone conversation with the owner but in the end he decided to go with a non-union firm. There is a new mixed-use parking housing structure planned to replace the existing structure on Water 
Street. Feel free to send the Mayor a friendly email (MayorDavid@cityofbinghamton.com) that we would like to see our brothers & sisters 
of the IBEW bringing this project to completion as so many projects in the past. Take care of each other.

Fraternally yours, Kelvin
