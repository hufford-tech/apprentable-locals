# HAPPY HOLIDAYS!

Greetings Brothers and Sisters,

Here we are in the last month of 2021 already. Seasons Greetings to all and I hope everyone got to have some sort of 
Thanksgiving celebration to whatever extent it may have been with positive COVID cases throughout the area. I am very exrd
cited with the upcoming Christmas party for the Union Families on December 3 at 5:00pm. It is going to be a fun time for all 
and Jessica has put a lot of thought and hard work into this being a spectacular event. This years Dinner Dance was very well
attended and I got to talk to so many people all evening long. It was great to see and speak to as many members as I did and 
catch up on things.

COVID. IBEW Local 325 was recognized for it’s gain in market share and we’re tied for 20 place in all of the IBEW, USA & 
Canada! I want to thank our signatory contractors for going after this work and I can only hope that together we will make another advancement next year when the report comes out.  We had a 13% increase in the CW/CE program and that is a big 
part of getting some of this work back. I also want to thank the members for showing up and doing the work, because every 
hour that is paid to a union member goes into figuring market share.

Work in the area is still ok, with some members still working in sister Local 43 Syracuse helping them fulfill job calls. The Fed-
Ex building is moving forward with walls & steel going up so there will be additional manpower needed to keep that project on
schedule. The solar projects we have are facing difficulties in getting material so that is causing disruption with work in that
segment of the vast types of projects we do. Also there are big plans in place for the former IBM site in Endicott and projects 
projected to get underway in early 2022 with Ubiquity Solar & Imperium 3 ion lithium battery manufacturing and to be fully 
functional by the end of the year .

There is also a large project that will be starting at the Fraser substation near Delhi in January. It is in the eastern portion of 
our territory but as members we need consider if we aren’t doing this work the non-union is and they will get our rate and our 
portion of the market share that goes with those hours.

Fraternally yours,

Kelvin

Reminder: Any health claims under $100 can be 
submitted for payment in the month of  December.
