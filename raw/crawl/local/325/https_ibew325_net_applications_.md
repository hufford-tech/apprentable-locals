## Equal Opportunity Employment

### [Equal Opportunity Employment](https://ibew325.net/journey-level-workers/)

**This application is voluntary only.** We are dedicated to strict policy on non-discrimination in employment on any basial including: race, creed, color, age, sex, religion, national origin, disability, marital status, military status, or arrest record. **We do not ask for your name, or contact details, and your answers remain anonymous.**

We provide our contractors with the highest quality of skilled and trained electricians, apprentices and technicians in the electrical construction industry, all of whom receive ongoing, continuous training. We pride ourselves on our ability to keep Apprentices and Journeyman Wireman up-to-date and knowledgeable on the latest technologies and work processes.
