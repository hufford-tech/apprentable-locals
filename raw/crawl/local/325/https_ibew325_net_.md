## Representing Five New York Counties

**IBEW Local 325 represents nearly 500 hard-working men and women in five counties:**

[**Broome**](https://www.gobroomecounty.com/) **:** All Townships

[**Chenango**](http://www.co.chenango.ny.us/)**:** Townships of Afton, Bainbridge, Coventry, German, Greene, Guilford, Lincklaen, McDonough, North Norwich, Norwich, Ostelic, Oxford, Pharslia, Pitcher, Plymouth, Preston, Smithville, and Smyrna.

[**Delaware**](https://www.delcony.us/)**:** Townships of Davenport, Delhi, Deposit, Franklin, Hamden, Masonville, Meredith, Sidney, Tompkins, Walton, and that portion of Colchester and Hancock north of the East Branch of the Delaware River.

[**Otsego**](https://www.otsegocounty.com/)**:**Townships of Butternuts, Harwick, Laurens, Maryland, Milford, Morris, Oneonta, Otego, Unadilla and Westford.

[**Tioga**](https://www.tiogacountyny.com/)**:** Township of Berkshire, Newark Valley, Owego, Richford and Tioga.

[**IBEW Code of Excellence**](http://www.ibew.org/Portals/37/documents/Code%20of%20Excellence/Outside%20Construction/Handout%2016%20COE%20Commitment%20Statement%20-%20Outside.pdf)

The heart of training is our **Joint Apprenticeship and Training Program (JATC)**, a regimented training program that assures competency in the standardized skills needed to be an electrician.

[Learn More](https://ibew325.net/jatc/)

We provide our contractors with the highest quality of skilled and trained electricians, apprentices and technicians in the electrical construction industry, all of whom receive ongoing, continuous training. We pride ourselves on our ability to keep Apprentices and Journeyman Wireman up-to-date and knowledgeable on the latest technologies and work processes.
