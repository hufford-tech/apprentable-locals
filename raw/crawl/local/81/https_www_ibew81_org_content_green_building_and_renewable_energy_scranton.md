# Green Building and Renewable Energy in Scranton

For over 100 years, the [IBEW-NECA](http://necanet.org/) trained electricians of Local 81 have brought safe, reliable power and energy to Scranton and its businesses and citizens.

Now, in 2018, working with  our affiliated Local 81 contractors means our customers  are able to consistently 1) reduce their ongoing energy and operating costs, and 2) create healthier, safer, and more productive working environments for their workers. This enables our customers or "end users" to improving their firms own productivity and profitability, all while helping the Scranton community.

Please register on this site so that we may become a resource to you and your business, and answer any questions you may have.

As more and more developers, contractors, realtors, hospitals, and other businesses in and around Scranton  strive for energy efficiency via the [LEED Certification](http://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Leadership_in_Energy_and_Environmental_Design), IBEW 81's Electricians and Electrical Contractors are ready and trained to deliver these benefits to you.

Our goal is to ensure that the highest standards of professionalism in electrical and energy work are always brought to your job and-or facility, 100% of the time.
