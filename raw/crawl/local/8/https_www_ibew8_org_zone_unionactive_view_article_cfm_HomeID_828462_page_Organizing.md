| **Career Opportunities**

**If you're interested in applying for one of the programs listed below,**

**download the [Personal Contact Sheet](https://www.ibew8.org/Application%20-%20Personal%20Contact%20Sheet%202025.pdf).  You must have**

**[Adobe Acrobat](https://get.adobe.com/reader/) in order to do this. Click on Fill & Sign**

**on the right-hand side of the form, fill it out and email it to us at**

**contact [@ibew8.org](mailto:info@ibew8.org).**

**Keep in mind that applying for any of the apprenticeship programs, must**

**also be done in person at the apprenticeship school.**

**\\_\\_\\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_**

**No experience?**

**No sweat!**

**There are several on the job combined with classroom training options**

**Earn while you Learn in the following**

**[Residential Apprenticeship](http://bit.ly/2Me7G05)**

**[Teledata Apprenticeship](http://bit.ly/2Md9g2n)**

**[Inside/ Commercial Apprenticeship](http://bit.ly/3eSlycF)**

**[Construction Wireman/ Electrician](http://login.themagicbluebox.com/s/5Xu)**

\\_\\_\\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_\_

**Already in the Electrical Industry** **?**

**Looking for a realistic retirement option that will not break**

**the bank while you are working?**

[**National Electrical Benefit Fund (NEBF)**](https://www.nebf.com/nebf/)

**Looking for medical coverage that actually covers?**

**Not just you but the entire family!**

**[Family Medical Care Plan (FMCP)](https://www.nebf.com/fmcp/)**

**If you are already doing the work, why not demand the best wages,**

**healthcare, and retirement for you and your family?**
