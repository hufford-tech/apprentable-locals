# ORGANIZE YOUR WORKPLACE

Are you a non-union electrical worker who wants better wages, benefits, and working conditions? Bring IBEW Local 113 to your shop. We'll help you and your coworkers organize — confidentially and at no cost to you.

## WHY JOIN THE IBEW?

### Better Wages

Union electricians earn significantly more than their non-union counterparts.

### Comprehensive Benefits

Health insurance, dental, vision, and retirement plans that protect you and your family.

### Ongoing Training

Access to world-class training programs and continuing education at no cost.

### Job Security

Collective bargaining agreements protect your employment rights.

### Brotherhood

Join a community of skilled professionals who support each other.

### Fair Treatment

Grievance procedures ensure workplace disputes are resolved fairly.

## HOW ORGANIZING WORKS

### Initial Contact

Reach out to our organizing team. All conversations are confidential.

### Build Support

Connect with coworkers who share your interest in better conditions.

### Authorization Cards

Employees sign cards indicating their desire for union representation.

### NLRB Election

The National Labor Relations Board conducts a secret ballot election.

### Negotiate Contract

After winning, we negotiate a collective bargaining agreement.
