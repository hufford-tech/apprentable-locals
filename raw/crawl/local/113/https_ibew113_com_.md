WHO WE ARE

## POWERING THE PIKES PEAK REGION

Local Union 113, of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, has been proudly representing the skilled electrical workers of Colorado Springs and the Pikes Peak region for over 125 years.

[Learn about our history](https://ibew113.com/about) [View our officers & staff](https://ibew113.com/about#leadership)

## ORGANIZE YOUR  WORK PLACE

IBEW, Local Union 113 is a labor union committed to advancing the ideals of the Labor Movement. Our members uphold the belief that every working individual deserves equal wages and benefits. Without collective action, both wages and working conditions are likely to persist unchanged. Federal laws and boards safeguard your right to establish a union in your workplace.
