# APPRENTICESHIP & TRAINING

Earn while you learn. IBEW Local 113 represents two apprenticeship paths — Inside (Wireman) and Outside (Lineman). Choose your path below to learn how to get started.

## CHOOSE YOUR PATH

There are two apprenticeship programs. Pick the one that fits the career you want. Each is run by its own training center — that's who you apply to.

Inside — Wireman

## Apprentice Wireman

Inside Wiremen are the electricians who install and maintain the wiring, controls and electrical systems in commercial, industrial and residential buildings. The Inside apprenticeship is a paid, earn-while-you-learn program combining on-the-job training with classroom instruction, run by the Colorado Springs Electrical JATC.

![Colorado Springs Electrical JATC](https://ibew113.com/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fapprenticeship%2Fcsejatc-logo.webp&w=640&q=75)

### Classifications

Trades you can train for through this apprenticeship.

- Inside Wireman



Install and maintain the wiring, controls and electrical systems in commercial, industrial and residential buildings.

- VDV / Telecommunications Installer Technician



Install and service low-voltage voice, data and video systems — structured cabling, networks and fiber.

Outside — Lineman

## Apprentice Lineman

Outside Linemen build and maintain the overhead and underground power lines that make up the electrical grid. The Outside apprenticeship is run by MSLCAT — the Mountain States Line Constructors Apprenticeship & Training program — serving a multi-state region. The closest training center to Colorado Springs is in the Denver area (Brighton, CO).

![MSLCAT — Mountain States Line Constructors Apprenticeship & Training](https://ibew113.com/_next/image?url=%2Fimages%2Fapprenticeship%2Fmslcat-logo.avif&w=640&q=75)

### Classifications

Trades you can train for through this apprenticeship.

- Journeyman Lineman



Build and maintain overhead and underground power lines — the backbone of the electrical grid.

- Line Equipment Operator



Operate the cranes, diggers and heavy equipment used in line construction.

- Substation Technician



Construct, wire and maintain the substations that step power up and down across the grid.

- Traffic Signal Technician



Install and service traffic signals, street lighting and intelligent transportation systems.

## HELMETS TO HARD HATS

Helmets to Hard Hats is a national program that connects transitioning active-duty military, veterans, National Guard and Reserve members with career training in the building and construction trades — including the IBEW/NECA electrical apprenticeship. It's free to join, and the skills and discipline you built in the service translate directly to a career in the trade.

Veterans interested in an electrical career are encouraged to explore Helmets to Hard Hats, then apply through the JATC (Inside — Wireman) or MSLCAT (Outside — Lineman) as described above.

[Visit helmetstohardhats.org](https://www.helmetstohardhats.org/)
