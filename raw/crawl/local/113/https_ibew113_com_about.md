# IBEW LOCAL 113

ABOUT US

## NEED HELP? LOOK NO FURTHER THAN IBEW

Together with the Southern Colorado Chapter of NECA, IBEW Local 113 delivers the region's highest-trained, most productive electrical workforce — committed to quality, value, and safety on every job.

### Partnership for the Future

IBEW Local Union #113 and the Southern Colorado Chapter of the National Electrical Contractors Association (NECA) are dedicated to creating an environment in which our partnership can flourish — built on a shared vision, shared risk taking, common objectives, mutual respect, trust, and a team approach. Partnering helps us create avenues to accommodate the needs of our customers and community.

### Our Vision

IBEW Local Union #113 and the Southern Colorado Chapter of NECA shall lead the electrical industry by excelling in all future opportunities. This cooperative effort is committed to customer satisfaction and safety.

### Our Mission

To be recognized as the leader in the electrical industry. Through partnering, we provide the best quality product and value to the customer, utilizing the highest trained and most productive workforce.

## 126 YEARS OF SERVICE

A proud tradition of excellence in the electrical trade

1900

IBEW Local 113 founded in Colorado Springs

1930s

Survived the Great Depression, maintained member benefits

1950s

Post-war expansion, growth in residential and commercial work

1970s

Major infrastructure projects across the Pikes Peak region

2000s

Expanded training programs and JATC partnership

2025

Celebrating 125 years of service to Colorado Springs
