# AGREEMENTS & WAGE SCALES

IBEW Local 113 negotiates fair wages and working conditions for electrical workers.

## WAGE CLASSIFICATIONS

### Journeyman Wireman (Inside)

Commercial and industrial electrical work

- Full journeyman rate
- Health & welfare
- Pension
- Annuity

### Journeyman Lineman

Outside line construction and maintenance

- Full journeyman rate
- Health & welfare
- Pension
- Annuity

### Residential Wireman

Residential electrical construction

- Competitive rate
- Health & welfare
- Pension
- Training

### VDV Technician

Voice, data, and video installation

- Competitive rate
- Health & welfare
- Pension
- Training

### Apprentice

Percentage of journeyman rate based on year

- Progressive increases
- Health & welfare
- Training provided
- Book costs covered

For specific wage rates, contact the union hall at [(719) 633-3872](tel:7196333872)
