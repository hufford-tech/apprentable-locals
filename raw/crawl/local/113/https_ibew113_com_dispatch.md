## HOW TO RE-SIGN

You must re-sign the out-of-work list each calendar quarter to keep your position on the books. How you re-sign depends on which book you're on.

### Book 1

Re-sign online through the Working Systems member portal.

[Re-Sign on the Member Portal](https://ibew113.workingsystems.com/)

### Books 2, 3 & 4

Email your information to dispatch (Book 1 may also re-sign this way if preferred). Include:

- Your name
- Your Local #
- Book #
- Your email (optional)

[dispatch@ibew113.com](mailto:dispatch@ibew113.com)

### Quarterly Schedule

Re-sign on the first working day of each new calendar quarter — January, April, July, and October. There is a 15-day grace period: mailed re-signs must be received between the 1st and close of business on the 15th of the quarter.

### In Person or by Mail

You may also re-sign by walk-in during regular office hours, or by mail to:

IBEW Local 113

2150 Naegele Rd

Colorado Springs, CO 80904

## HOW DISPATCH WORKS

### Sign the Books

Register at the union hall.

### Bid on the Call You Would Like to Take

Review the open calls and bid on the jobs you want. Calls are awarded based on your position on the books.

### Respond Promptly

When called, respond within 15 minutes to accept or decline the job.
