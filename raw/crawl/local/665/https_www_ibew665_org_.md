INTERNATIONAL BROTHERHOOD OF ELECTRICAL WORKERS LOCAL 665

IBEW 665 was founded in Lansing in 1926 by a group of ten area electricians. Since then, we have grown considerably and trained some of the best and brightest electricians and communication technicians that can be found in the Midwest. We would be delighted to put our experience to work for you.
