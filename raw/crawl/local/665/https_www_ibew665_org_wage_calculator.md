# Wage and Benefit Calculators

## **Excellent wages, benefits + retirement. Join the IBEW and change your future.**

IBEW 665 represents the very best electricians in the Lansing, Michigan area. IBEW 665 electricians are provided with comprehensive healthcare packages that include an HSA to cover out-of-pocket expenses for you and your family. Local 665 electricians have higher wage packages, negotiated wage increases, time off & overtime, and benefit from extensive upgrade training throughout their career.

**Are you making what you are worth?** Find out with our wage calculators below.
