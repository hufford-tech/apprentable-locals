**Email address** ñ **cujatc@601jatc.com** **Summer Hours for applications:** **Wednesdays from 12:30 pm – 3:30 pm** **Please bring all documents with you to apply** **All Applicants must fully meet the following requirement to qualify:** **Must pay a $30.00 application fee.** **Must have a copy of your BIRTH CERTIFICATE.** **Must have an official High School Transcript or a GED and it must reflect one full** **credit of High School Algebra with a passing grade or one post High School** **Algebra Course with a passing grade.** **Must possess and maintain a valid DRIVERS LICENSE.** **Must live within our jurisdictional area.** **Must pass a three (3) hour APTITUDE TEST.**

**Champaign-Urbana JATC will not discriminate against apprenticeship applicants or** **apprentices based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex (including pregnancy and** **gender identity), sexual orientation, genetic information, or because they are an individual** **with a disability or a person 40 years old or older. Champaign-Urbana JATC will take** **affirmative action to provide equal opportunity in apprenticeship and will operate the** **apprenticeship program as required under title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations, part 30.** **At another date the Applicants who meet the first five requirements will be given an Aptitude** **Test. Upon passing the aptitude test the applicant will be interviewed by the Committee.** **Apprentices will be selected in order of their ranking resulting from the rating of the Aptitude** **Test and interview. All requirements should be submitted with the application.** **If you are interested in the apprenticeship, the following website may be of interest to you:** ***[http://www.electricianapprenticehq.com/how-to-join-ibew-apprenticeship/***](http://www.electricianapprenticehq.com/how-to-join-ibew-apprenticeship/***)
