# ELECTRICAL APPRENTICESHIP OPPORTUNITY

## IBEW Local Union 596 Clarksburg JATC 1001 North 12th Street, Clarksburg, WV 26301

## 304-622-0151 ext. 15

www.ibew596.com

The IBEW-NECA Clarksburg Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee takes applications year-round but only on the **first Tuesday** of each month. Those interested should apply at the IBEW 596 Union Hall at 1001 North 12th Street, in Clarksburg between the hours of 8AM and 4PM. The cut-off for the 2027 incoming class will be **June 1, 2027.**

The four-year program teaches all aspects of the electrical trade for low, medium, and high voltage systems including general wiring, conduit/cable tray, motor controls, transformers, communications cabling, security and fire alarm systems, solar power, safety, and a complete understanding of all aspects of the trade.

Apprentices learn on the job under the supervision of journeymen and attend classes 2 nights per week, from September through May.

#### Requirements to Apply

**17** years old (18 at time of interviews), valid **WV Driver’s License**, **Birth Certificate**, **High School Diploma** or **GED**, **Transcripts** (showing at least 1 year of Algebra), **DD-214** (if military), **$25** Application Fee

Applicants **must** reside within the jurisdiction of IBEW Local 596: Barbour, Doddridge, Harrison, Lewis, Marion, Monongalia, Pendleton, Pocahontas, Preston, Randolph, Taylor, Tucker, or Upshur County.

Applicants meeting the basic requirements will be scheduled for a math and reading comprehension aptitude test. Those who meet or exceed the minimum accepted score will be scheduled for an interview by the committee.

Clarksburg Electrical JATC will not discriminate against apprenticeship applicants or apprentices based on race, color, religion, national origin, sex (including pregnancy and gender identity), sexual orientation, genetic information, or because they are an individual with a disability or a person 40 years old or older.

Clarksburg JATC will take affirmative action to provide equal opportunity in apprenticeship and will operate the apprenticeship program as required under Title 29 of the Code of Federal Regulations, Part 30, as amended.
