# IBEW Local 131 History

![singlePostImage](https://www.ibew131.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmedia.linkedunion.com%2Fapps%2Fibew131%2Fnews%2FFounding_Fathers.jpg&w=3840&q=75)

The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) was founded on November 28, 1891 in St. Louis, Missouri. There were ten delegates (pictured below) representing 286 members in attendance. Henry Miller was elected as the first International President, and J.T. Kelly as the first International Secretary-Treasurer.

Kalamazoo received it's charter on September 14th, 1916 and was signed by the seventh International President F.J McNulty. Kalamazoo is the fourth local to use the number 131. The first local was Columbia, South Carolina (1900-1901), the second was Traverse City, Michigan (1901-1908), the third was Twin Falls, Idaho (dates unknown).

For more history about the I.B.E.W., **[CLICK HERE](https://ibew.org/our-history-museum/)** to visit the International site.

# Member Assistance Program

![singlePostImage](https://www.ibew131.com/_next/image?url=https%3A%2F%2Fmedia.linkedunion.com%2Fapps%2Fibew131%2Fnews%2F147cab6e-dd82-4192-8879-ee6855b56185.png&w=3840&q=75)

### **We're Here for You**

IBEW Local 131 is committed to supporting our members both on and off the job. We understand that life can be overwhelming—whether you're dealing with work stress, family challenges, mental health concerns, or just need someone to talk to. That's why we've partnered with Ulliance to provide a confidential Member Assistance Program (MAP) available to all members and their families, 24/7.

This free resource offers professional counseling, crisis support, and guidance whenever you need it. You don't have to face tough times alone. If you have any questions or need help accessing the MAP, please reach out to the union hall—we're here to make sure every member has the support they deserve.

[Visit The MAP](https://lifeadvisorwellness.com/UserLogin/EAPLogin/?lm=cpy&cGUID=e1a73799-fc59-40cf-ad53-d3a051affc92)
