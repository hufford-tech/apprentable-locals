**About Us**

The Charleston Electrical JATC is an affiliate of the electrical training ALLIANCE (etA). The electrical training ALLIANCE developed uniform standards that are adopted and used nationwide to select and train thousands of qualified men and women each year. Over the years, we have continually trained the best electrical workers in the industry without cost to the taxpayers. Students in the electrical training ALLIANCE training programs earn while they learn by doing apprenticeships.   Apprentices receive excellent wages, paid health benefits, pensions, guaranteed wage advancement, and an employment atmosphere with safe working conditions. Today, the electrical training ALLIANCE has developed into the largest apprenticeship and training program of its kind, having trained over 350,000 apprentices to journey-worker status through local affiliate programs.

**Mission Statement**

The mission of the Charleston Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee is to educate the members of Local Union 466 of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers and the West Virginia - Ohio Valley National Electrical Contractors Association; ensuring that we are providing the Electrical Construction Industry with the most highly trained and highly skilled workforce possible.

**Our Committee**

The Charleston Electrical Joint Apprenticeship Training Committee consists of a Committee of Trustees. The Committee is made up of six members: three management members that represent the West Virginia-Ohio Valley Chapter of NECA, and three labor members that represent Local Union 466 of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers.

**Location**

The Charleston Electrical JATC is located at: 810 Indiana Ave, in Charleston, West Virginia.
