## Safety Inspection: Buckingham BuckGrab Rope Length Adjusting Device (LAD)

Please be advised, we have been informed that Buckingham has issued a Safety Inspection Notice on their LAD and we ask all of our members and represented utilities to please check to see if you have any of these in use. Click here to see the three (3) page PDF Inspection Notice.
