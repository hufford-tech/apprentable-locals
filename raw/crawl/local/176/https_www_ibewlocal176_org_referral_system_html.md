## **Referral Guidelines & Procedure**

## **LOCAL 176 IS IN-PERSON BOOK SIGNING ONLY**

![Picture](https://www.ibewlocal176.org/uploads/8/9/0/2/89025372/referralcard1.png)

To view referrals, book placement, bid on calls, and work history, change your address or phone number, click on the "Member Access" tab at the top of the web page or [click here](https://members.ibewlocal176.org/wsiscript/jobrefisapi.dll/). Follow the instructions on the web page to sign in initially. Once on the site, you will need to set up your personal password in order to view everything available. If you forget your password, call the hall, we can retrieve it for you.

**You will be able to bid on jobs from 5:00 p.m. to 9:30 a.m.** the following morning. Please refer to the bidding instructions and referral procedure below:

* * *

## **How to Bid on Jobs**

_**EFFECTIVE 9/24/2025**_

You can place job requests in **three ways**:

**A. By Phone**

- Call in, follow the prompts, and leave a voicemail with:
  - Your **card number**
  - **Sign date** (for long calls) or **short call sign date** (for short calls)
  - **Contractor name** and **job site**
- You can request multiple jobs, **listing them in order of preference** (1st request = top choice).
- Requests accepted **from 5:00 p.m. to 9:30 a.m. the following morning**.

**B. Online**

- Log in at [**www.ibewlocal176.org**](http://www.ibewlocal176.org/) and bid using the online system.

**C. In Person**

- Submit your name **7:30 – 9:30 a.m.** at the hall.
- Bidding closes at **9:30 a.m.** and calls are filled based on book position.
- If the referral agent contacts you for a job, you **must respond by 11:00 a.m.** or the job will go to the next person, and **you will be rolled off the book**.​

## **Referral Roll Procedure Update**  **EFFECTIVE AUGUST 10, 2026**

- If your number on the book is **50 or lower**, you will remain on the list for **one (1) month** without having to take a call.

- At least **five job calls** will be offered during your 1 month period before removal.

- **After 1 month, you'll be removed from the book** \- in order to be back on the book​ you will have to come back, in person, to sign the bottom of the book.

**Referral Hotline Number: (815) 744-0176** or **(815) 744-0177**
