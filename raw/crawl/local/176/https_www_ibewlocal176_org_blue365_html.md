## Blue365  MEMBER DISCOUNT PROGRAM

## Learn how to further use your benefits through  **Blue365**®

|     |     |
| --- | --- |
| **Blue365** is a program that helps you stay healthier for less. This programs offers discounts for members to save on products and services for a well balanced lifestyle. <br>These " **Blue365 Deals**" are different than the healthcare benefits through your Blue Cross Blue Shield plan. Blue365 partners with vendors nationwide to offer savings and help promote a healthier life.  ​<br>Members will register for an online account to start browsing the Blue365 Deals that can be redeemed online or in-person (for those applicable).<br>_Frequently Asked Questions_ from the website may be found below: | ![Picture](https://www.ibewlocal176.org/uploads/8/9/0/2/89025372/published/redeem.png?1741024705) |

​**What Types of Deals Are Offered through Blue365?**

The Blue365 Deals are categorized into 6 categories:

Apparel & Footwear         Fitness             Hearing & Vision

Home & Family                 Nutrition           Personal Care

**How Long Are Blue365 Deals Valid?**

Blue365 Deals are valid for different lengths. Some are short term "Featured Deals", and some can be long term "Ongoing Deals". Deals that are about to expire will have an icon on the top right corner.

**Can I Recommend a Deal?**

Yes, Blue365 would love to hear more about what participating members would like see. You may submit a form [by **clicking** **on this link**](https://www.blue365deals.com/BCBSIL/tell-us-what-deals-youd-see-blue365).

**What Else Should I Know?**

Blue365 Deals cannot be combined with any other promotions.

**How it Works**

## **_Finding Deals_**

You may browse all current deals on the Blue365 website. For a more specific search, deals can be broken down by category, keyword, or through sort & refine options.

![Picture](https://www.ibewlocal176.org/uploads/8/9/0/2/89025372/blue-365-search_orig.png)

## **_Redeeming Deals_**

Once logged in, the redemption instructions will appear at the top of all deal pages.

Deals may be redeemed **Online** or **In-Person**.

_Online_: Discounts will be redeemed through the partner's websites. When there is a promo code, you can copy the code                    before visiting the partner's website.

_In-Person_: Use the Print feature to print the deal's details and bring it to the retail location.
