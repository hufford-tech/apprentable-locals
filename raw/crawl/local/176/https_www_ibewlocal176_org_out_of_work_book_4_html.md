## Out-of-Work   ​Book 4

## **LOCAL 176 NO LONGER ACCEPTS WALK INS FOR BOOK 4**  **PLEASE SEE INSTRUCTIONS BELOW FOR THE PROCESS TO BE APPROVED TO SIGN BOOK 4**

**Effective J** **une 24, 2026**, IBEW Local 176 has suspended walk-in Book 4 registrations while we review and update our registration process.

**Effective July 22, 2026**

To qualify for Book 4:

- **IBEW members:** must present a current dues receipt (dues ticket).
- **Non-IBEW members:** must live within IBEW Local 176's jurisdiction.
- Both members and non-members must provide proof of at least 2,000 hours of electrical work experience.

**Before you are eligible to sign Book 4, you must submit the required information listed below to our hall via email.** Paperwork cannot be filled out or submitted in person at our hall. All completed submission will be reviewed by Local 176 for approval - this can take some time, please don't expect an immediate response. Once approved, you will be notified and able to come sign Book 4 in person.

Individuals seeking to register for Book 4 may submit their name, contact information, qualifications, license/status information, work history, and hours by email to [**dispatch@ibewlocal176.org**](mailto:dispatch@ibewlocal176.org). Any IBEW Members should also submit home local/classification information. The Book 4 Questionnaire found below should also be completed in full and submitted to the same email.

Local 176 will maintain a record of inquiries received during this period and will contact applicants with further instructions once the updated registration process is available.

​This temporary procedure is administrative in nature and is intended to improve the efficiency, accuracy, and consistency of the Book 4 registration process.

[CLICK HERE FOR BOOK 4 QUESTIONNAIRE (ENGLISH)](https://www.ibewlocal176.org/uploads/8/9/0/2/89025372/local_176_book_4_questionnaire_.docx)

**A partir del 24 de junio de 2026**, IBEW Local 176 suspenderá temporalmente las inscripciones presenciales para el Libro 4 mientras revisa y actualiza su proceso de registro.

**A partir del 22 de julio de 2026**

**Para calificar para el Libro 4:**

- **Los miembros de IBEW** deben presentar un comprobante vigente de pago de cuotas sindicales (dues ticket).
- **Las personas que no sean miembros de IBEW** deben vivir dentro de la jurisdicción de IBEW Local 176.
- Presentar comprobante de al menos **2,000 horas de experiencia laboral en trabajo eléctrico**.

Durante este período temporal, las personas que deseen registrarse en el Libro 4 podrán enviar por correo electrónico su nombre, información de contacto, calificaciones, información sobre licencias o estatus, y experiencia laboral a [dispatch@ibewlocal176.org](mailto:dispatch@ibewlocal176.org), o mediante un formulario en línea una vez que esté disponible.

Local 176 mantendrá un registro de las consultas recibidas durante este período y se comunicará con los solicitantes con instrucciones adicionales una vez que el proceso de registro actualizado esté disponible.

​

Este procedimiento temporal es de carácter administrativo y tiene como objetivo mejorar la eficiencia, precisión y consistencia del proceso de registro del Libro 4.
