## Payments

![Picture](https://www.ibewlocal176.org/uploads/8/9/0/2/89025372/money-card-business-credit-card-50987.jpeg?326)

We have implemented an additional method of quarterly membership dues payments. The addition of Credit Card or Debit Card payment will now be offered. This is another method our members may use, other than payment by cash or check. The use of Credit or Debit Card will have a processing fee of **_2.5%_** of the total amount of the payment. This fee is due to the vendor charge from the Credit Card Companies for card use.

​Credit Cards that will be accepted are: VISA, MASTERCARD and DISCOVER. The use of the Credit or Debit Card can be implemented in person or over the phone. We will accept Credit Cards for Merchandise also. If you have any questions or need more information, please call Simoane Spell, Treasurer or Stan Rutherford, Financial Secretary at the hall (815-729-1240). The Health and Welfare office is now accepting Credit Cards for Insurance payments.
