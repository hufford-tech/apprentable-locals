## ALAViDA PROGRAM

![Picture](https://www.ibewlocal176.org/uploads/8/9/0/2/89025372/alavida-logo-edited_orig.png)

## ALAViDA _Substance Use_ provides easy access to substance use care:   wherever you are, whenever you need it.    ​You set your goals. We help you meet them.

IBEW Local 176 members and their adult dependents have access to the **ALAViDA TRAiL**, a confidential on-demand health benefit for substance use management.

**What?**

ALAViDA offers confidential, evidence-based substance use support from the privacy of your smartphone. The TRAiL is a secure platform where you can track your journey, access self-guided resources available 24/7, and text or meet your care team. No awkward conversations, no shame and without interruption to your work or personal life.

**Who?**

ALAViDA is for everyone who would like to reevaluate their relationship with alcohol and substance use and for folks who are supporting a loved one who is struggling. Whether you are looking to become more mindful of your consumption, cut back or quit altogether, ALAViDA is tailored to your needs.

The TRAiL offers three different pathways:

- Substance Use Care: for all substances and goals: abstinence or harm reduction
- Smoking Cessation: abstinence
- Loved Ones: for those supporting someone with a substance use issue

**Resources Offered:**

- Substance use awareness self-assessments

- Daily notifications and self-tracking tools

- A personalized learning plan with iCBT modules (Internet-based Cognitive Behavioral Therapy)
- Evidence-based resources available 24/7
- Progress and health outcome reporting
- Ongoing check-ins and asynchronous messaging with a proactive care team
- A dedicated coach for guidance and accountability
