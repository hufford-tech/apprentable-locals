## Benefits Overview

Notice to all members:

The following information is for your file. This will help you to summarize the different benefits available. Please check your agreement for the benefits that apply to you.

## **Local 176 Business Office**

**NECA-IBEW PENSION TRUST FUND - (Decatur Pension)**-5 year vesting. Early Retirement with reduction - age 55-59. Full benefits - age 60 - Current contribution (paid by contractors)

**NEBF PENSION - (National Electrical Benefit Fund)** \- 5 year vesting (3% of gross earnings) - current retirement rate $32.00 per month per year of credited service ($33.00 per month beginning in 2025) - Early Retirement with reduction - age 60 & 61. Full benefits - age 62.  (paid by contractors)

**IBEW PENSION - (International Pension)** \- 20 year vesting - paid through dues - current retirement rate - $5.50 per month for each full year of continuous good standing as an "A" member-$4.50 per month (2022-1997)-$3.50 per month prior to 1997. Early retirement with reduction - age 62-64. Full benefits- age 65. _MEMBER MUST CONTINUE TO PAY UNION DUES UNTIL APPROVED FOR THIS PENSION._

_ALL ABOVE IS GENERAL INFORMATION - INDIVIDUALS MAY HAVE VARIANCES_

**​** **NUMARK CREDIT UNION - (Vacation Fund)** \- Current contribution rate (deducted from wages)

**STANDARD LIFE INSURANCE - $8,500.00 life insurance** \- paid through dues $100.56 yearly (effective 4/1/2023)

**IBEW DEATH BENEFIT  - $6,250.00-$3,000.00** \- this benefit changes based on how long pension has been collected. Amounts double if death is accidental

For questions, please contact Missy at (815) 729-1240, option 1.

* * *

## **Health & Welfare Office**

**​NECA-IBEW LOCAL 176 PENSION PLAN - (Local Pension)** \- immediate vesting - Current contribution rate (paid by contractors) - Eligible at age 55

**NECA-IBEW LOCAL 176 HEALTH & WELFARE FUND** \- (Medical, dental, vision and MAP) Current contribution rate (paid by contractors)

**SUPPLEMENTAL UNEMPLOYMENT BENEFITS FUND - (SUB Fund)** \- Current contribution rate (paid by contractors)

**AMALGAMATED LIFE- Life Insurance $25,000.00 (2/1/15)** \- eligible to employees covered under Health & Welfare until age 65

**AMALGAMATED LIFE - Accidental Death & Dismemberment Ins. $25,000.00 (2/1/15)** \- eligible to employees covered under Health & Welfare until age 65

​

For questions, please contact Nichole at (815) 729-2666, option 3.

**Please remember to change beneficiaries with each office listed. This cannot be done automatically. It must be requested by you!**
