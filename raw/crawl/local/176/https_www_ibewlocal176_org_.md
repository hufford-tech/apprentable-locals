## Who We Are

The **International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers** ( **IBEW**) is a labor union which represents nearly 873,000 workers and retirees in the electrical industry in the United States, Canada, Panama, Guam and several Caribbean island nations; particularly electricians, or inside wiremen, in the construction industry and linemen and other employees of public utilities. The union also represents some workers in the computer, telecommunications, broadcasting, and other fields related to electrical work. It was founded in 1891 shortly after homes and businesses in the United States began receiving electricity. Its International President is Kenneth Cooper. The IBEW is affiliated with the AFL-CIO.
