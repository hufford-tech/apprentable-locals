# Welcome to IBEW Local 269

Welcome to the website for IBEW Local 269.  Chartered in 1912, we are proud members of the Mercer, Burlington, and Bucks County (PA) community. Throughout Central New Jersey and Eastern Pennsylvania we’ve be working on high profile projects.

IBEW Local 269 has jurisdiction for Inside, Commercial Outside and Line, Residential, and Telecommunications.  We work everywhere from the New Jersey State House, Justice Complex, and State Police Facilities like “The Rock” to the CURE Insurance Arena and Waterfront Park.  We work on major industrial and commercial facilities right down to the ceiling fan in your home.  We’ve helped provide electricity to improve your quality of life and we’re proud to be working on projects of all sizes.

Throughout our website you’ll be able to learn a great deal of information [About Us](https://ibew269.com/about-us/).  From our [History](https://ibew269.com/ibew-history/), to our [Training Program](https://ibew269.com/training/) and videos, from how to join to [Government Information](https://ibew269.com/government-information/).
