### IBEW Local 307- now accepting Credit Cards  You will be able to use your credit card at the Union hall for DUES and merchandise purchases.  DUES must be paid in 3 month increments when using your credit card as your payment method.   In person credit card use will be charged a 3% convenience fee to cover bank fees.    Credit card payments will be accepted over the phone for DUES ONLY, at 301- 724-3403, with 4% convenience fee to cover bank fees.
