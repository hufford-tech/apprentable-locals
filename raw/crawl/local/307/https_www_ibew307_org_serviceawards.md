# Local 307 Service Awards

![](https://static.wixstatic.com/media/ddf543_10a2acd3d58a43afb2fd751c559c7713~mv2.jpg/v1/fill/w_295,h_293,al_c,q_80,usm_0.66_1.00_0.01,enc_avif,quality_auto/ddf543_10a2acd3d58a43afb2fd751c559c7713~mv2.jpg)

Every Year Local 307 honors and thanks our members at our Annual Members Smoker Party.  Here you are recognized for your years of service to the IBEW. The International and Local Union provide and present members with a commemorative Pin for every 5 years of Service.

Below is the Official list for this years' Service award recipients. IF you were unable to attend the Smoker this year, you may pickup your Service Award Pin at the Union Hall.

​

THANK YOU FOR YOUR SERVICE TO THE IBEW AND TO LOCAL 307.

Those of you who have gone before us, through your dedication have prepared the way for the current membership. I hope that the up and coming generations take the same pride as you have to help maintain our heritage.

​
