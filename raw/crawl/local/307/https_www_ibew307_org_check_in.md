# Check-In

It is your responsibility as an IBEW Member to Check In with the Local that you are visiting. Local 307 covers Garrett County, Allegany County and Washington County in Maryland. Local 307 also covers Grant County, Hardy County, Mineral County, Hampshire County, Morgan County, Berkeley County and Jefferson County in West Virginia.

--  Please reference the map below.

​

\\*\\*\\* You may only CHECK-IN electronically after your company has done its initial sign in to the union hall in-person. IF you are not sure if this applies to you and your company, please call the Union Hall at 301-724-3403.
