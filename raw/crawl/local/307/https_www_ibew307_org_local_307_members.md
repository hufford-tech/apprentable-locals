### Credit Union Deductions  - AVAILABLE TO ALL UNION MEMBERS

Credit Union Deductions will no longer be made to ACT 1st FCU; the language in the agreement has been changed to read “AmeriServ Bank”. The reason for this change is ACT 1st FCU chose to use non-union labor to construct their facility in Romney, WV and AmeriServ chose to use union labor on their entire project.  The Local Union has removed all funds from ACT 1st FCU as well.

If you currently have a credit union deduction, and wish to continue to do so, you will need to complete the enclosed application to open an account with AmeriServ Bank, or go to [www.ameriserv.com/new-account-inquiry/](http://www.ameriserv.com/new-account-inquiry/) .

The employers will then make their deposit to AmeriServ Bank on your behalf.   You may also contact them at address/phone number on the top of the application.  Also, if you have any questions concerning this, do not hesitate contacting the hall as well.
