**Retirement Process**

Below we have listed the steps in the retirement process to help ease the burden of how confusing it may feel for some individuals.  Listed is a step by step process to help you when you are getting closer to retirement age.  Please call the Hall with any questions!  We can assist you throughout the process if needed.  We are here to help!

**_Step by step process to retire from all 3 pensions:_**

--Make a decision regarding a future retirement date.

--Keep in mind it will takes 60-90 days to process each claim

--Call the Hall to request the applications to be emailed to you, printed and mailed to your home or picked up.  You will then fill out all applications at home and will call the Hall to make an appointment for them to be reviewed before submission.

~ _Here are the steps below if you would like to submit the applications without total assistance from the Hall._

**_Local 141 Profit Sharing_ _:_**

1. Call **Jessica Jarvis** at American Benefit Corporation. Her phone number is: **(304) 525-0331, Ext. 365**

2. Give Jessica your retirement date.  Jessica will send you the retirement application to your home.

_\*\*If you have problems understanding the application, call Jessica at ABC or Heather at the Hall._

3. Gather your documentation, complete the application and copy all paperwork for yourself. If needed, bring the paperwork to the Hall and we will copy it for you and place a copy in your file.  \*Remember the 60 to 90 days needed for processing.

Make a copy for yourself and mail to:

**American Benefits****3150 US Route 60****Ona, WV  25545-9507**

**_Pension Benefit Fund (PBF) - International_**

_Based on the number of years paying dues_ _$4.50/year_

_As of 1-1-23 it is $5.50/year going forward_

If you do not have a printer, call the Hall and Heather will print out the application for you. We will then set up an appt for a future date to review the entire application.

_To print the application at home:_

1. Go to the Home page of [www.ibew.org](http://www.ibew.org/) You can simply CLICK on this link to go to the website.
2. Click on Local Connections then click on Departments.
3. Click on Pension and Reciprocity
4. Scroll down the page, click "PBF Secure Login"
5. Enter your Card # and last 4 of SS# then click Login
6. Checkmark with or without Spousal calculations, add spouse's birthdate then click "calculate"
7. Scroll down to the word "Apply" in purple and pick the appropriate one that suits your age
8. Click "Yes" and open PDF to print at home

This is an easy and clear application. You should have no problems answering the questions or finding your documentation.  Once it is completed call Heather to set up an appointment for review. It will then need to be read at the closest Union Meeting and signed by the Business Manager.

We will email your application directly to the International Office the following Monday after the Union Meeting.

A copy will be maintained in the file.

_Based on years of service._ _$32.00/year with 300 qualified hrs_
