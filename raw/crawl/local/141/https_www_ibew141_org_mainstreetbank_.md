**Main Street Bank**

Main Street bank handles the deposits from ABC (American Benefits Corporation) for your Vacation Fund.

This account is called a "Vacation Savings Account". It takes 2 1/2 full months before you can withdraw your funds.  For example, if you started work January 1st then your funds will not be available until March 15th or so. After the first deposit there will be a reoccurring monthly deposit until you are done with that job. You can withdraw up to $500.00 with your debt card at any ATM (fees may apply) per day or you can go in to any branch with your valid Driver's License to take out any necessary amount.  If you are a traveler and your job is complete and you leave the location, contact the bank and they will send you a check to your home address.  There is an annual $125.00 fee from American Benefits Corp which is withdrawn on the 1st deposit of the year (March for January work month). There is a $5 monthly fee if your balance drops below $200. There is a $5 monthly fee if your account goes dormant for 3 years.

If there are any questions regarding a late deposit please contact your employer first, then Main Street bank and then our Vacation Fund department at ABC if there is a true emergency.
