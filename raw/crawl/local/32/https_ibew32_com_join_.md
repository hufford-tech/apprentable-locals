## **It’s Time To Earn What You Are Worth!**

Join IBEW Local 32 and start your new career as a union electrician today. Enjoy excellent wages and benefits as a member of IBEW Local 32. We are part of the [International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers](https://www.ibew.org/). With 750,000 members, the IBEW is the largest organization of electrical workers in North America. Our work is is interesting and varied; we work in construction, utilities, data centers, telecommunications, manufacturing, health care, education, and more.

We also have an apprenticeship program that combines classroom instruction with on-the-job experience to give you the skills you need to become a journeyman electrician. Learn more about our apprenticeship program by clicking [here](https://limajatc.com/).

For more information, contact Membership Development Coordinator Jared Parsley at [jared@ibew32.com](mailto:jared@ibew32.com) or [419-305-0678](tel:4193050678).

Join us today!
