# Employee Assistance Program

## Employee Assistance Program from T.E.A.M

T.E.A.M. provides a variety of services to assist employees and their family members with personal problems, both large and small, that interfere with their day-to-day activities including work performance. T.E.A.M. can assist with short-term mental health concerns, drug and alcohol issues, financial stress, legal concerns, and child / elder care. Speak confidentially with a T.E.A.M. counselor 24/7 by calling (651) 642-0182 or (800) 634-7710 to address the next steps needed to resolve your daily living concerns. This benefit is provided your union at no cost to you!
