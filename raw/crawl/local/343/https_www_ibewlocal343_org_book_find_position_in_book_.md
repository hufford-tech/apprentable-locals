# Book Position

## NEW – PLEASE READ AND FOLLOW DIRECTIONS BELOW (ANY REGISTERED MEMBER OF IBEW LOCAL 343)

- I.B.E.W Work History (only electronic records from 2005)
- Book 1 & 2 (L/C and S/C) Position

## Instructions for First Time Users:  Default login for members is your IBEW card number, and your password is your last name along with your last 4 digits of your social security number.

## Example:  Username: C569402 Default Password: Smith1234

When you first login to the website, you will be prompted to give an email address, and to change your password from the default. After this, you can login with either your email address OR card number, along with the password you have provided.
