## Our Mission

To provide the most qualified and highly trained craftspeople,

Local 343 and its contractors provide a joint apprenticeship training program as well as continual upgrade training.

[Learn more](https://ibewlocal343.org/about/#history)

## Free service for    members and families

Short term, face-to-face or telehealth counseling sessions FREE to you and eligible family members to solve personal problems and workplace issues that affect your ability to work safely and effectively.
