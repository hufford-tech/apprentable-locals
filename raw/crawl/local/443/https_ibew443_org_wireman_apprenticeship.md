# Montgomery Electrical Training Alliance

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/bf24eaf3-a5ee-493b-98a4-f5d3a0f23c5d/MEJATC.jpg/:/rs=w:600,cg:true,m)

#### Our Mission

The Montgomery Electrical Training Alliance trains highly skilled and qualified electricians.

All of our apprentices obtain their Alabama State Journeyman Electrician License prior to their graduation.

![](https://img1.wsimg.com/isteam/ip/bf24eaf3-a5ee-493b-98a4-f5d3a0f23c5d/blob-716d980.png/:/cr=t:16.67%25,l:0%25,w:100%25,h:66.67%25/rs=w:600,h:300,cg:true)

#### Our Program

We offer training with no out of pocket tuition cost.

The only cost to you is your books and personal tools for the job.

Our 4 year program is an earn while you learn, meaning that you get paid to learn to become an electrician!

## Journeyman Wireman Apprenticeship Requirements

- **High School Diploma or GED**
- **Valid Drivers License**
- **Birth Certificate**
- **Proof of passing one year of Algebra (High school or college transcripts)**
- **Application Fee**
- **DD-214 (Military Only)**
