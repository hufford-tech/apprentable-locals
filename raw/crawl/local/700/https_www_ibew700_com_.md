# Welcome message from the Business Manager and Financial Secretary of Local 700:

We have developed this web site to help communicate with all our members and their families, as well as non-members and others interested in our union and our work in the Northwest Arkansas area. It is our goal to make accessible to you the latest news and to keep an archive of past news for reference.

If you need to contact the hall please take a look at our contact page. (To navigate our web site, use the navigation buttons on the left hand side of this page for the electrical construction industry and the right side for Exide Manufacturing). We have listings of who to contact at Local 700.

I encourage all members to take an active role in the union. Visit this site for information and updates, and email your thoughts and suggestions. This site is for your benefit and we want to make it the best we can to serve your needs.
