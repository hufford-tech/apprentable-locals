| **Business Manager's Corner** |

|     |
| --- |
| We're fortunate to have opportunities both here at home and across the<br>country. The large data center projects are providing excellent wages and<br>valuable experience, and there's nothing wrong with traveling when that's where<br>the work is.<br>But we also have to remember what keeps Local 480 strong. The projects in<br>our jurisdiction are what sustain our local union, our contractors, and our<br>future. These signatory contractors support our agreements year after year,<br>even when work isn't booming. They deserve our support when work is available.<br>If we continually leave local work for higher-paying jobs elsewhere, we risk<br>weakening the very market that provides us with long-term stability.<br>Contractors who can't staff their jobs lose opportunities, and over time that<br>can mean fewer projects, fewer market gains, and less leverage at the<br>bargaining table.<br>The data center work won't last forever. When those projects are complete,<br>we all want to come home to a Local that is stronger than when we left—not one<br>that has lost market share because we didn't support the work in our own<br>backyard.<br>Our Brotherhood has always been built on helping each other. Sometimes that<br>means traveling to answer another local's call. Other times, it means staying<br>home to ensure our own local remains strong for the next generation.<br>Let's continue to support our local contractors, organize more electrical<br>workers, grow our market share, and build a Local 480 that provides good-paying<br>work for years to come.<br>Thank you for your continued support |
