Electronic Reciprocal Transfer System

**Travelers.** If and when you travel outside the Local 480 jurisdiction you must be signed up with ERTS. You can 
do this by coming into the hall and register. Even if you work for a local contractor, but travel to another area, your 
benefits will be reported in that jurisdiction. If you are not signed up, they are not liable to return your benefits to 
your home local.

## What is ERTS?

The *IBEW/NECA Electronic Reciprocal Transfer System* (ERTS). The ERTS system has been designed to help 
you assist plan participants to expeditiously transfer Pension and Health & Welfare money back to their selected 
home funds no matter where they may be working. ERTS will make it a lot easier for all concerned (Fund offices, 
plan participants, and assisting Local Union offices) by affording the timely and accurate transfer of information 
and money via ACH from participating fund to home fund.

**If you ever have a question about whether your benefits were sent in, please feel free to contact the plan office. 
Please allow time for hours and funds to be reciprocated.
