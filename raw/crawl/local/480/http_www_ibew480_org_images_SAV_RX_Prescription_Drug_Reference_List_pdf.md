## HIV/AIDS THERAPY*
Presently, all brand name drugs without generics specifically indicated for the treatment of HIV and its opportunistic infections are on Formulary, subject to plan parameters and applicable copays.

## ANTINEOPLASTIC AND IMMUNOSUPPRESSIVE AGENTS*

All oral FDA-approved antineoplastic and immunosuppressive agents are eligible for coverage under the prescription drug benefit, subject to clinical review, plan parameters and applicable copays.
