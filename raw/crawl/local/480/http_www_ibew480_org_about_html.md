| ## History of IBEW Local Union 480 |

|     |
| --- |
| Local 480 was first chartered in Charleston, WV on February 1, 1906, <br>where it subsequently lasted 2 years and became non-operational in May, <br>1908.From Charleston, WV, Local 480 was <br>chartered in Portland, OR on June 11, 1912, and was there until they were merged <br>with Local 317 to form Local 48 on May 21, 1913.Thecharter was reissued to Marshall, TX on May 3, 1921 where it became <br>non-operational in September, 1922.<br>On March 29, 1927, Local <br>Union 480, Jackson, MS was officially established with the signing of the <br>Charter by then IBEW President James P. Noonan and IBEW Secretary Gustave M. <br>Bugniazet.The original Local 480 <br>Officers on the Charter are as follows:<br>C.E. Bardwell                        B.L. Haves<br>Pat   Biggs                             C.L. Horton<br>O.T. "Claude" Blackmon        J.T."Red" Martin<br>T.J. Bullion, Jr.                     W.A. Moses<br>Roy Frizell                             A.U. Tompkins<br>The first home for Local <br>480, Jackson, Mississippi was in a motor rewinding shop on State Street.The second home was an office space on the <br>second floor above the Walgreen’s Drug Store located then at Capitol Street and <br>Lamar Street, across the street from South Central Bell.The third home was in the Carpenter’s Labor <br>Temple on State Street.The fourth home <br>was in an old house on Rankin Street.The fifth home for Local Union 480 was 230 W. Rankin Street, the first <br>owned building.The sixth home and our <br>present location is 4767 I-55 South, Jackson, Mississippi.<br>The Charter is proudly displayed in our Meeting Hall.Local Union 480 has been a growing and <br>thriving force in the Central Mississippi Labor movement for over 98 years.With thehistory of our organization and our Local Union 480, we have a <br>responsibility to those that left it to us and those that follow behind us of <br>passing it on better and stronger than we found it. | |
