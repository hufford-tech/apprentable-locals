### IBEW 681

# Benefit Golf Scramble (Oct 3, 2026)

[« All Events](https://ibew681.org/events/)

## October 3 @ 8:00 am \- 5:00 pm

- [« Union Meeting](https://ibew681.org/event/union-meeting-10/)
- [Union Meeting »](https://ibew681.org/event/union-meeting-11/)

![](https://ibew681.org/wp-content/uploads/2026/07/Back-of-Shirt-scaled.png)

**Brotherhood-Fund Benefit Golf Scramble** is scheduled for **Saturday, Oct 3, 2026**, at **River Creek Golf Course** in Wichita Falls. The event will operate on a traditional scramble format with shotgun starts, and registration begins early on the day of the tournament. Entry options are available on a per-person or per-team basis, with early registration incentives for those who sign up before Oct. 2, 2026. Alongside player registration, a variety of sponsorship levels are offered, enabling local businesses and supporters to contribute through hole sponsorships that include team entries and course signage.

[CLICK HERE](https://ibew681.org/brotherhood-fund-benefit-golf-scramble-oct-3-2026/) for more information on:

- Registering your team.
- Becoming a sponsor.
