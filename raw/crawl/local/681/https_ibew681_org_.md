### Together We Achieve

# Wiring The Future

IBEW represents its members in negotiations with employers over wages, benefits, working conditions, and other employment-related matters. This includes collective bargaining to secure contracts that outline these terms.

Our mission is to ensure that our members have the resources, support, and opportunities needed to excel in their careers and lives. We strive to provide top-notch training, excellent benefits, and a strong community for our members.

**Join IBEW 681 for exceptional benefits, including comprehensive health care, retirement plans, training, legal support, and strong advocacy.**
