PALS Training

**Decisions get made every day that affect wages, job safety, and benefits.**

This interactive Politics, Activism & Livelihood (PAL) workshop connects real-life ‘bread-and-butter’ issues—work, wages, healthcare, retirement—to the decisions made in government

What you will learn

- **What “livelihood” really includes** (wages, healthcare, paid time off, pension, training, safety, and more).
- **How unions secure those things**—and why “the union difference” matters.
- **How politics and government decisions impact your everyday life** (not just elections every few years).
- **Why money isn’t the whole game**: corporate spending vs. “boots on the ground,” and the power of voting and activism.
- **How endorsements are made** and how to make informed decisions using practical tools.

What to expect

- **Interactive, not a lecture**: small-group discussion, quick activities, and a “Reality Check” quiz.
- **Respectful ground rules**: respect differences; debate issues, not people; assume good intent.
- **Practical takeaways**: voter registration handouts and Government Affairs resources for next steps.
