# Jobsite Watchdog’s

Brothers and Sisters of Local 538. In an effort to compile data and make it easier for you to report what you know to the hall, we have created the form below to do the following;

1. Allow you to report both Union and Non-Union contractors you may have seen working in our jurisdiction.
2. Allow you to report a jobsite you may have seen under construction
3. Allow you to pass along information regarding upcoming projects that you may have heard about in our jurisdiction

**This form is solely for the purpose of tracking both the work in the area as well as the contractors that may be involved.**

This form is **not for filing complaints** that you may have with a current contractor, if you need assistance regarding this please contact the hall via the normal channels.
