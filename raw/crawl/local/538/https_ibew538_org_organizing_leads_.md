# Organizing Leads

Brothers and Sisters of Local 538. In an effort to compile data and make it easier for you to report what you know to the hall, we have created the form below to do the following.

1. Allow you to notify the organizer if you have **spoken with someone** who is **interested in joining the union**. Provide contact information and tell us about the conversation that you had.
2. Allow you to notify the organizer if you have been contacted by someone working for a **company (doesn’t have to be an electrical outfit)** that is interested in getting our union involved to represent them. Provide contact information and tell us about the conversation that you had with them.

* * *

**This form is solely for the purpose of helping us keep track of interested people and potential companies that we can follow up with**

* * *

If you spoke with a person who has electrical experience, please have them go to the [“Join Us”](https://ibew538.org/join/) section of the website and fill out our form so that I can setup a meeting with them.
