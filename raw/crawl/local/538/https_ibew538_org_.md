We are Industrial, Commercial, Residential and Teledata Electricians.

Our Jurisdiction covers **100% of Fountain, Vermillion, and Warren Counties in Indiana, as well as 100% of Vermilion County in Illinois and part of Iroquois County (Illinois)**.

       The IBEW along with our contractors association (NECA) jointly sponsor an apprenticeship program (Danville NECA IBEW Electrical JATC) that provide excellent education to both apprentices and seasoned electricians.

       Local 538 is the oldest building trades union based in Danville and Vermilion County, beginning with nine charter members on Jan. 30, 1907 – 15 years after the IBEW was formed.

       We invite all workers belonging to our trade to come forward, join our ranks and help increase our numbers until there shall be no one working at our trade outside of the brotherhood. If you would care to join us, contact Joseph Stelzer for more information.
