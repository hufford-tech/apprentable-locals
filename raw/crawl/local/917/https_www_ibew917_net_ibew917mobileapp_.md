**IBEW LOCAL 917 MOBILE APP**

**COMING SOON! AN APP FOR IBEW 917 MEMBERS**

Local IBEW 917 new mobile app will be available to our members. It will be available in the App Store & Google Play Store. If you have an iPhone or Android Phone you will be able to download for free.

As a member you will be able to enter your last name and card number to access member features and receive notifications from IBEW 917. You will also be able to pay dues, view and bid on jobs, re-sign books, update your contact information, view secure documents and register for events.
