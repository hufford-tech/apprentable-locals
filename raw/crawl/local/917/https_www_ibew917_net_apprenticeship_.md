**ELECTRICAL TRAINING ALLIANCE - IBEW NECA**

**APPRENTICESHIP**

Thank you for considering our Apprenticeship Program. Our five-year apprenticeship program is for qualified applicants who wish to begin a career in the electrical field. To apply for the apprenticeship program, visit our local at any time. Here is the documentation needed in order to apply.

The program offers a combination of classroom instruction and practical experience under the guidance of experienced professionals. Apprentices learn about electrical codes, technical blueprints, wiring installation, and equipment maintenance. They also earn a paycheck while working and gaining the education they need to get their Journeyman Ticket.

**• A Sealed High School Transcript (proving a passing grade for Algebra 1)**

**• A copy of GED if you did not graduate High School**

**• A copy of your Birth Certificate**

**• A Valid Driver's License**

**• Social Security Card**

**• $50.00 for the Application Fee**

Once we have received all of the listed documentation you can fill out the application and wait for the hiring process.
