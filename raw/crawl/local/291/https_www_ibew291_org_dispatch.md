## Dispatch calls for Inside Construction.

## Monday, August 10, 2026

# ‍

‍

**Re-sign is between the 10th and the 16th of each month.**

**The Job's Board is updated by 5:00pm each day. Information is current at that time, please check the board for updated information. Prior to this time the Office does not have updated information about the numbers of Calls available. Thanks.**

**For questions related to dispatch, please email:** _DispatchAdmin@Ibew291.org_ **\- Please use this email for all Dispatch Inquires. Due to volume, answering the phone during regular hours is difficult, please be patient.**

**For any questions not related to Dispatch, Book Numbers, or signing the Books, please email** _Questions@Ibew291.org._ **Items such as schedules, Travel Pay, licensing, etc go to this email address. If you email DispatchAdmin asking questions about a jobsite, conditions, pay, etc, you will not receive a response.**

**To bid for a Call:**

**(1) Click the " Bid For Referral " tab to the right, complete all fields ( Card# Optional ). Online bids may be placed - _AFTER-_ 5pm MST the day of posting and must be completed - _BY-_ 8:00am MST on the day of the Bid for Referral. Any Bids placed prior to 5pm MST and after 8:00am MST are considered invalid.**

##### **(2) Upon clicking the "Bid for Referral" tab, you will now be redirected to a new log-in site. At this point follow the instructions to register onto the new system, once registered you will be able to Bid for jobs. If you have issues, please show up during regular business hours and Dispatch will assist you after regular dispatch is complete. The Dispatch office does not open before 8:00am. The Hall is unable to assist with any registration or bidding issues prior to 8:00am or after 5:00pm.**

##### **(3) "Daily Bid Sheet" will be placed out after morning Dispatch for remaining calls** [**available**](https://www.bing.com/search?q=available&FORM=AWRE) **.**

**(4) Anyone accepting a bid must report to Dispatch by 4:00pm on the day prior to the reporting date requested by the employer. Anyone who rejects their referral after previously accepting will be removed from the Out of Work List.**

**(5) Only those who are awarded a Bid will be contacted. If you do not hear from Dispatch, you did not receive a bid for employment**
