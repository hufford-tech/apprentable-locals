# Political Action

let your voice be heard

## Contact Your Elected Officials

IBEW Local 291 is committed to improving the lives of our rank and file members through the power of collective bargaining and our never-ending commitment to make sure that those in the legislative and political community understand our issues and stand behind our members.

Together, our voices must be heard by our elected officials. By contacting your legislative representatives, you are doing your part to ensure that our voices are heard.
