# DUES INCREASE REMINDER

March 11, 2025

July 1, 2024

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab2d5/66feb8cbea51dfe2145ca490_111.png)

\*All Apprentices classifications, CW and CE's pay $1.00 less

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab2d5/66feb8cbd01026e531f2a313_1112.png)

‍

Please make sure you are sending in the proper payment amount starting **July 1, 2024**. If you are on automatic recurring bill payment with your bank you will want to have this amount adjusted accordingly. If you are on recurring bill payment with the Hall, please call us directly to adjust your recurring draft amount.

‍

Also note that if you are paid in advance, any time beyond July 1, 2024, that you will owe a difference in dues. To account for this change and will see a balance owed at the top of your next dues receipt. If you would like to know if you have any additional monies owed at this time, please call the Hall.

**Union Hall phone number: 208-343-4861 (Monday-Friday 8am-5pm)**
