# First Time Signing The Books

**Please fill out this form if this is your first time signing the Out of Work Books for IBEW Local 291 in Boise, ID.** If you have previously signed our books, you will need to click “Sign the Books” and complete the form.

**IF YOU ARE NOT A MEMBER OF AN IBEW LOCAL UNION YOU MUST COME TO 225 N 16TH ST, BOISE ID 83702 IN ORDER TO SIGN OUR OUT OF WORK LIST \| THE BOOKS**

In order to be placed on the Out of Work Books, you are required to submit a current Dues Receipt and a Travel Letter no more than one year old to [DispatchAdmin@Ibew291.org](mailto:DispatchAdmin@Ibew291.org). Failure to complete this form or submit the required documents will result in a delay in your registration.

Screenshots with Proof of Dues Payment are not accepted in lieu of a Yellow Dues Receipt.

**If you have any questions or concerns, you may email** [**Questions@Ibew291.org**](mailto:Questions@Ibew291.org) **.**
