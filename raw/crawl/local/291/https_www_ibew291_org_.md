## Membership Benefits

As a member of IBEW Local 291, you will have a range of exclusive benefits tailored to advance your career, ensure your financial security, and support your family.

[  Join Local 291](https://www.ibew291.org/join)

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab2c2/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab505_megaphone.svg)

### a voice at work

When workers unite, they gain a collective voice over their wages, benefits, and policies.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab2c2/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab506_worker.svg)

### job security

An IBEW contract ensures job security and supports a better work-life balance.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab2c2/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab507_money-bag.svg)

### better wages

A union contract empowers workers to ensure they receive fair compensation.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab2c2/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab50c_diagram.svg)

### opportunity to thrive

The IBEW offers top-notch training that opens up more career opportunities for you.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab2c2/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab50a_financial-security.svg)

### Retirement Security

Union workers have greater retirement security than non-union workers.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab2c2/66e43b3aeffbd044ea3ab50b_health-insurance.svg)

### Great Healthcare

The IBEW negotiates comprehensive, affordable family healthcare, so workers get the care they need without financial stress.
