### About Us

**IBEW Local 64** provides highly skilled and highly trained journeymen and apprentice electrical workers to our signatory contractors throughout Mahoning county, and parts of Columbiana and Trumbull counties, Ohio. We are committed to helping our members prosper by providing them with opportunities to work for our signatory contractors, who want to employ the region's most efficient, experienced, and safest workforce.
