## The Region’s Most Highly Trained And Highly Experienced Electrical Workforce

**IBEW Local 64** services our contractors and their customers in a Tri-county region _(throughout Mahoning County and parts of Columbiana and Trumbull Counties)_ with a highly skilled electrical workforce of inside wiremen – while also providing our members with all the benefits of belonging to the world’s most progressive trade union, the _International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers_.

### representation

As a nonprofit labor union that maintains a firm but friendly relationship with the employer, we work hard to fairly protect the interests of our members while helping both the employer and employees accomplish their goals.

As union members, we bargain collectively with our employers over wages, benefits and rights. Most of us have very limited bargaining power as one person, but as a group, we are strong. And with a good negotiated contract, we have legal protections we would not have otherwise.

[Join the IBEW Local 64](https://ibew64.org/join-union/)

### Training

**IBEW Local 64** members and apprentices are trained through the [Youngstown Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee](http://yjatc.org/), whose programs are taught at a state-of-the art training center and are registered with the Department of Labor, Bureau of Apprenticeship and training.

Thousands of successful electricians started their careers in the **Youngstown Area Electrical J.A.T.C.** Apprenticeship Training Program.

Training is low cost and Local 64 apprentices earn a salary while they learn.

[Become an Apprentice](https://ibew64.org/apprenticeship/)

## Discover the difference of a Union workforce

Become signatory to **IBEW Local 64** and employ highly skilled and highly trained industrial, commercial, residential electricians and low voltage installers and technicians. Ensure your projects will be finished on time and on or under budget.

There are numerous advantages of employing IBEW electricians:

#### A Knowledgeable and Well-Trained Workforce

The [Youngstown Area Electrical Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee (JATC)](http://yjatc.org/) provides journeyman upgrade classes and continuing education classes to ensure **IBEW Local 64** members have the education and skills to expertly complete the work asked of them – no matter the size or scope of it.

#### A Safe Workforce

Members of **IBEW Local 64** undergo industry-leading safety courses to ensure their safety and the safety of those who work around them on the jobsite. A safe workforce helps to prevent workplace fatalities and reduce injuries, which ultimately limit unplanned work stoppages. This helps keep the project on schedule and also helps reduce Workers’ Compensation costs.

#### Access to a Workforce to Meet Your Manpower Needs

Local 64 signatory contractors can request an electrical worker with a specific skill or certification, and have the flexibility to man-up or man-down according to specific job site needs. **IBEW Local 64** provides signatory contractors with certified electrical workers at zero cost, saving affiliated contractors from wasting money on advertising, screening, and training.

[Contact us for more information about becoming a Signatory Contractor](https://ibew64.org/contact-us/)

### About Us

**IBEW Local 64** provides highly skilled and highly trained journeymen and apprentice electrical workers to our signatory contractors throughout Mahoning county, and parts of Columbiana and Trumbull counties, Ohio. We are committed to helping our members prosper by providing them with opportunities to work for our signatory contractors, who want to employ the region's most efficient, experienced, and safest workforce.
