## IBEW Partners

Through a partnership with multiple organizations, **IBEW Local 64** provides comprehensive training that allows journeymen to stay up-to-date on technologies, service contractors and their customers with highly skilled electrical workers, and enforce best safety practices among contractors.

[![ibew](https://ibew64.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/ibewLogo250-150x150.png)](http://www.ibew.org/)

#### [International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW)](http://www.ibew.org/)

The **IBEW** represents approximately 750,000 members who work in a wide variety of fields, including utilities, construction, telecommunications, broadcasting, manufacturing, railroads, and government. The IBEW has members in both the United States and Canada and stands out among the American unions in the AFL-CIO because it is among the largest and has members in so many skilled occupations.

[![wrbt](https://ibew64.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/wrbt2-150x150.png)](http://www.wrbctc.org/home)

#### [Western Reserve Building Trades](http://www.wrbctc.org/home)

The Western Reserve Building Trades Council represents quality craft workers that have been building quality structures on time and on a budget in the Mahoning Valley for over 100 years. Comprised of over 21 local unions and district councils, the WRBCTC serves Columbiana, Mahoning, and Trumbull counties.

[![neca-ibew](https://ibew64.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/neca-ibew-300x121.jpg)](http://necaibewelectricians.com/)

#### [NECA-IBEW Electricians](http://necaibewelectricians.com/)

NECA-IBEW Electricians provides world-class services through a highly trained union workforce in the _Youngstown-Warren area_. We’re a partnership of local units of the National Electrical Contractors Association and the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers.

[![jatc](https://ibew64.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/12/jatc-150x134.png)](http://www.yjatc.org/)

#### [Youngstown Area Electrical J.A.T.C.](http://www.yjatc.org/)

YJATC provides training to electrical apprentices in Mahoning, Trumbull and Columbiana Counties. The mission of the _Joint Apprenticeship and Training Committee_ is to educate the members of the IBEW thereby providing the electrical construction industry with the most highly trained and highly skilled workforce possible.

[![MVNECA](https://ibew64.org/wp-content/uploads/2022/04/MVNECA.jpg)](https://www.mvneca.org/)

#### [NECA-IBEW Electricians](https://ibew64.org/links-resources/Mahoning%20Valley%20NECA)

Working in partnership with International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers (IBEW) Local Unions **#64** **in Youngstown**, Ohio and [**#573**](https://www.ibew573.org/) **in Warren**, Ohio. Mahoning Valley NECA and its contractors bring the highest levels of training, experience, quality, reliability and safety to each construction project.

### About Us

**IBEW Local 64** provides highly skilled and highly trained journeymen and apprentice electrical workers to our signatory contractors throughout Mahoning county, and parts of Columbiana and Trumbull counties, Ohio. We are committed to helping our members prosper by providing them with opportunities to work for our signatory contractors, who want to employ the region's most efficient, experienced, and safest workforce.
