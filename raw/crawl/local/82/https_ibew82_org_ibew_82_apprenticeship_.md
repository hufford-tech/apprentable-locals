# IBEW 82 APPRENTICESHIP

## JOIN IBEW LOCAL 82

**IBEW 82 JATC Electrical Apprenticeship**

The members of IBEW Local 82, through joint labor-management partnerships, invest thousands of dollars every year to train the Miami Valley’s next generation of Electrical Tradespeople. Training is tuition-free. Apprentices earn union-scale, competitive wages with full benefits packages.

Thousands of successful electricians started their careers in the Dayton Electrical Trades Apprenticeship Training Program. If you’d like to learn more about becoming an IBEW Local 82 Apprentice, visit [82JATC.com](https://www.82jatc.com/) or contact [Anthony Henderson](https://ibew82.org/ibew-82-apprenticeship/anthony@dejatc.org), Training Director.
