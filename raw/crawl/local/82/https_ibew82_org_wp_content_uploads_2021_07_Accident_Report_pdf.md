# IBEW Local 82 – Form 173 Supplemental

## Instructions to Worker, Steward or Supervisor

1. Fill out this form immediately in case of a serious lost time accident or fatality.

2. This form must be filled out by the injured worker or if impaired, by his / her Steward or
   immediate field supervisor.

3. Return to Local 82 Safety Director within 36 hours of the incident.


Note: This form is required by the IBEW Constitution (Article XV, Section 15).
