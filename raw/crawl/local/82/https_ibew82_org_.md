## WE POWER DAYTON

In 1916, the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers Local 82 was chartered in Dayton, Ohio. For over 100 years, the men and women of IBEW Local 82 have powered Dayton and the Miami Valley Region. IBEW 82 electrical tradespeople are the highest-trained, safest in the business. From industrial and commercial construction to power facilities and green energy expansion, IBEW 82 members have the training and expertise to build and maintain our communities for the next century.

IBEW Local 82 members are proud to live and work in Ohio’s Miami Valley region. We provide:

- The most highly skilled, best trained workforce in the electrical construction industry.
- 100% drug-free workforce with drug-free training.
- Construction work that meets the highest safety and quality standards, for the benefit of the citizens of the Greater Dayton and Miami Valley Region.
- Volunteer service and charitable support when there is need in our community.
