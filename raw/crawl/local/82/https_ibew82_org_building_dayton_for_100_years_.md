### BUILDING DAYTON FOR 100 YEARS

![](https://ibew82.org/wp-content/uploads/2016/08/proclamation_2016.jpg)Chartered on January 20, 1916, IBEW Local 82 has built Dayton for the past century, training each new generation of electrical workers through our [Local 82 JATC Electrical Training Center](https://www.82jatc.com/). Dayton Mayor Nan Whaley proclaimed June 25, 2016 as IBEW Local 82 Day in Dayton.

IBEW 82 journeymen, retirees, and apprentices joined our union leadership at a gala commemorating our Centennial:
