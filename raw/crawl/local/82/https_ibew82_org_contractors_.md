## CONTRACTORS

The IBEW Code of Excellence for the electrical industry is designed to ensure the highest standards of excellence on each and every IBEW workplace. Leaders and members commit to demonstrating the IBEW’s core values in everything they do. Those core values are known as SPARQ:

- SAFETY
- PROFFESIONALISM
- ACCOUNTABILITY
- RELATIONSHIPS
- QUALITY
