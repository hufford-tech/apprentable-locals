Re-Sign Period

### RE-SIGN PERIOD

January 10-16 and July 10-16

Effective January 1st, short calls will change to 14 calendar days. For more information on resign or if you are interested in working out of town, please call:

Johnny Morris (937) 898-4239

or 1 (888) 568-8282

If working out of town, make sure you are registered in the ERTS system. Current drug card is needed to take a job call.
