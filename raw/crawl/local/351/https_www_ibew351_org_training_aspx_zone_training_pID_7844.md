## **Apprenticeship Recruitment**

**[Equal Opportunity In Apprenticeship Training](https://www.ibew351.org/ULWSiteResources/ibew351/Resources/file/Training/Equal%20Opportunity.pdf)**

**2027 JOURNEYMAN WIREMAN AND TELEDATA APPRENTICESHIPS**

Sponsored by Local Union 351 IBEW & South Jersey NECA.

**Applications are accepted every Wednesday from 10:00 AM- 2:00 PM.**

**Applications must be filled out in person at:**

JATC LU 351 IBEW

1125 Black Horse Pike

Folsom, NJ  08037

(609) 561-8515

**Requirements for day of applying:**

- Must be 18 years or older with a high school diploma or equivalent
- Must live in jurisdictional boundary

[See Jurisdictional Page for More information.](https://ibew351.org/ULWSiteResources/ibew351/Resources/file/About/NJ%20Inside%20Map.pdf)
- Must present a valid Driver’s License
- Must pay $100 Application Fee (Cash, Check or Money Order. No Cards)

**Requirements to be submitted within 30 days of applying:**

- Birth Certificate
- High School Diploma or GED
- Transcripts of grades, to prove you passed Algebra 1 or higher (to be sent directly from HS to JATC, or in a sealed envelope hand delivered)
- Proof of Address (something other than license, like a piece of mail)


Once you complete your application with the required documents above, you will be scheduled for an aptitude test (45 minute section of Reading Comprehension and a 45 minute section of Algebra 1).  If you pass the aptitude test, you will be scheduled for an interview in 2027.

If you have any questions or concerns, please do not hesitate to contact the JATC at 609-561-8515.

## About IBEW 351

Southern New Jersey. This means you receive the highest quality
craftsmanship with on-time and on-budget projects.
