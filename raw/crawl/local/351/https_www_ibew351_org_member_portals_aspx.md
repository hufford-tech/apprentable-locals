## IBEW Local 351 Member Portals

In an effort to help you find where you need to go we wanted to explain your options related to the Member Portals.  As a member of IBEW Local 351 you have access to TWO (2) Separate Portals for Members that require you to login with a user name and password.  The are as follows:

1)  Online Dues Payment Portal, Referral and Dispatch and also your Monthly Resign.

**[CLICK HERE](https://referral.ibew351.org/members/lpx351.dll/)** for Online Dues, Referral and Dispatch Portal (Login and Password Required)

**[CLICK HERE](https://www.ibew351.org/referral-and-resign.aspx)** for the Monthly Resign procedure which is found on our IBEW Local 351 website.

2)  Members Only Section on the IBEW Local 351 Website (Login and Password Required)

**[CLICK HERE](https://www.ibew351.org/membersOnly/default.aspx)** to access information for the Benefits Office, Employee Assistance Program, Recently Deceased, Photo Gallery, Scholarships, Retirees Homepage, Political Action, Training Classes, and the Business Manager's Report.

## About IBEW 351

IBEW Local 351 provides a highly-skilled Electrical Workforce to all of
Southern New Jersey. This means you receive the highest quality
craftsmanship with on-time and on-budget projects.
