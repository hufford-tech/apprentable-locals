## **Office Closings**

The Union Office is closed the following holidays:

New Year’s Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Presidential Election Day, Veteran’s Day, Thanksgiving, Christmas. Holidays falling on a Sunday will be recognized on Monday.

Please check the cover page of our website for any additional closings.

## **Membership Meetings**

Monthly Union Meetings are held on the Third Tuesday of each month at 7 PM.  The meetings are held at The Forum (formerly the Palace) located at 109 N Black Horse Pike, Blackwood, NJ 08012.

Please check the cover page of our website for any meeting cancellations.

## About IBEW 351

IBEW Local 351 provides a highly-skilled Electrical Workforce to all of
Southern New Jersey. This means you receive the highest quality
craftsmanship with on-time and on-budget projects.
