**Are you a licensed electrician leaving money on the table?**



**Your electrical skills are in high demand, and you should be getting a salary and benefits that match your worth.**







**Starting wage: $63/hr Plus over $27/hr in benefits. With many employers paying above scale.**











**Leadership positions start at $69.30/hr and go up to $81.90/hr minimum.**



**Insurance for your whole family, not just you.**



**And a retirement that you can actually retire on.**
