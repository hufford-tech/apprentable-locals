| **Electrical (Construction) Apprenticeship Program**<br>**Term:**<br>Five (5) Years<br>**Training:**<br>On-The-Job-Training - Minimum 8000 Hours.<br>**Paid Related Instruction**\- Minimum 700 hours attending Paid Related Instruction. Classes are attended one full day (eight hours) every two (2) weeks for ten (10) semesters during the school year. Apprentices are paid their normal wage rate while attending day school. <br>**Unpaid Related Instruction**<br>Apprentices are required to attend a minimum of 200 hours of class instruction as designated by the Committee, on their own time. This is usually accomplished in a night school setting.<br>**Wages:**<br>The Electrical (Construction) Apprenticeship Program is divided into six (6) Pay Periods. The wage at each level is a designated percentage of the Journeyworker Electrician's Base Wage Rate. Advancement to each subsequent level is determined by the successful completion of hours and training acquired.<br>**Completion:**<br>Upon completion of the Wisconsin River Valley Area Electrical (Construction) Apprenticeship Program, apprentices are advanced to Journeyworker Electrician status.<br>![](https://www.ibewlocal388.org/images/ap2.jpg)<br>**TeleCommunication (Voice, Data, Video) Apprenticeship Program**<br>**Training:**<br>On-The-Job-Training - Minimum 4800 Hours.<br>**Paid Related Instruction**\- Minimum 432 hours attending Paid Related Instruction. Classes are attended one full day (eight hours) every two (2) weeks during the school year.<br>**Unpaid Related Instruction**<br>Apprentices are required to attend class instruction as designated by the Committee, on their own time. This is usually accomplished in a night school setting.<br>**Wages:**<br>The TeleCommunications (Voice, Date, Video) Apprenticeship Program is divided into six (6) Pay Periods. The wage at each level is a designated percentage of the Installer-Technician's Base Wage Rate. Advancement to each subsequent level is determined by successful completion of hours and training acquired.<br>**Completion:**<br>Upon completion of the TeleCommunications (Voice, Data, Video) Apprenticeship Program, apprentices are advanced to Installer-Technicians status. |
