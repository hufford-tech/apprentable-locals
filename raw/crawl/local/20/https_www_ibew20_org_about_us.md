about us

# proudly representing electrical workers throughout the Dallas / Ft. Worth Metroplex.

The International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, Local 20 was chartered on July 20, 1998. Our membership has grown to serve men & women throughout the Dallas / Ft. Worth Metroplex.

We are located at:

**684 West Tarrant Rd.**

**Grand Prairie, TX 75050**

**Office: 972-263-1122**

**Fax: 972-263-1112**

## The Power of Union Benefits

As a member of the International Brotherhood of Electrical Workers, you gain access to the **highest standards of compensation, benefits, and retirement security in the electrical industry.** Unlike non-union employers, IBEW contractors offer more than just a paycheck — they invest in you and your future.

[get started](https://www.ibew20.org/organize)
