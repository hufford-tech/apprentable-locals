# never forgotten

We honor the memory of our brothers and sisters who have passed, and recognize their lasting contributions to Local 20 and the trade.

## Anthony Loria Sr.

Anthony Loria Sr. passed away on July 28, 2026. Brother Loria Sr. was a member of IBEW Local Union 20 for 66 years. Visitation will be held Aug. 11 at 4pm-8pm at Calvary Hill. A Mass of Christian burial will be held Aug. 12 at 9am at Calvary Hill.
