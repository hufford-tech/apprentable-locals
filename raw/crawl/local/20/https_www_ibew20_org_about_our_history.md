A legacy of service

# serving the dfw area since 1998

For generations, the IBEW has stood at the forefront of the electrical industry, advancing the rights, safety, and livelihoods of skilled workers across North America. Local 20 carries that tradition forward in North Texas—fighting for fair treatment, quality training, and the respect every electrician deserves.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/687501ee3e70f0c828562612/687faf7a3f8255aa531f6b95_writing.svg)

## local 20 charter

**Chartered on July 20, 1998**, IBEW Local 20 has grown to represent electricians across the Dallas–Fort Worth Metroplex. Guided by the mission of the IBEW, our local is dedicated to fair wages, safe working conditions, and strong benefits for all members. We stand for unity, opportunity, and a higher standard of living for the men and women who power our communities.
