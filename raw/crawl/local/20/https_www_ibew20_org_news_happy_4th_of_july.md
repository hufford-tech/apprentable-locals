# Happy 4th of July

June 30, 2026

For those working under the Inside Agreement:

July 4th falls on Saturday, so the holiday will be observed on Friday, July 3rd. The Business Office will be closed on Friday, July 3rd.

For those working under any other Agreement (Teledata, NMA, GPPMA, etc.) to holiday is observed on Saturday, July 4th.

Have a safe and happy July 4th weekend Brothers and Sisters!
