# Apprenticeship program

## You Have Opportunities

### Why Choose Our Apprenticeship Program?

- **College Credit While You Train**

Our apprenticeship program is affiliated with **Dallas College North Lake Campus**, so you’ll earn college credits while completing the program.
- **No Student Loans Needed**

Tuition and books are affordable, and you’ll be **working full time while going to school**.
- **Earn While You Learn**

Get classroom instruction combined with **paid on-the-job training**.
- **Regular Pay Increases**

Apprentices receive scheduled raises as they advance through the program.
- **Full Benefits**

Once you start working, your employer provides **Health Insurance and Retirement benefits**—on top of your hourly pay.

## [**Click HERE to apply online now**](https://ntejatc.org/index.php/applicant)

## Start Your Career

![Three individuals working on electrical wiring setups on a training wall in a classroom with desks and chairs.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/687501ee3e70f0c828562612/690e2a9941084c3c0c8b6dc6_Screenshot%202025-11-07NTET%20Apprenticeship%20in%20electrical%20construction.webp)

### Current Pay Rates

- **Apprentices** start at **$20.33/hour**, plus an additional **$2.91/hour** contributed into Insurance & Retirement.
- **Journeymen** earn **$81,000+ per year**, plus over **$24,000 annually** in Insurance & Retirement contributions.
