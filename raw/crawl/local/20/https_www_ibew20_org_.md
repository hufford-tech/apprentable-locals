## discover the IBEW advantage

### Competitive Wages

**The IBEW ensures that members earn competitive wages that reflect their skill, training, and dedication to the trade.** Through collective bargaining, we secure fair compensation that helps support a **better quality of life** for members and their families.

![](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/687501ee3e70f0c828562612/6a628364e0fbc7b99c2baef7_Image-1.jpg)

### Comprehensive Benefits

**As an IBEW member, you’ll receive a comprehensive benefits package** designed to support your well-being. From f **ull health coverage for you and your family** to **retirement plans** like pensions and annuities, IBEW members are protected both on and off the job.

![Group of workers wearing orange safety vests gathered inside a large industrial warehouse.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/687501ee3e70f0c828562612/68dec486f7aee256918181e3_1000000855.avif)

### Continuous Training

**The IBEW is committed to providing members with ongoing training to stay ahead in a constantly evolving industry.** From registered apprenticeships to journeyman upgrade programs, IBEW members have access to high-quality instruction and hands-on experience that **keep their skills sharp and their careers moving forward.**

![Group of diverse people standing in a workshop, two men holding orange measuring tools while others watch.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/687501ee3e70f0c828562612/68c30c89d3723aec49f7bf7f_IMG_2073.avif)

### Job Placement Assistance

**The IBEW helps connect members with reliable contractors and steady job opportunities across the industry.** Through strong relationships with signatory employers and a nationwide network, IBEW members gain access to **consistent work and pathways for long-term career growth.**

![Electrical control panel with multiple variable frequency drives, circuit breakers, and wiring organized on metal shelves.](https://cdn.prod.website-files.com/687501ee3e70f0c828562612/69245e5df899490304f29636_IMG_20150531_133011.webp)

## thinking about organizing?

**The IBEW helps electrical workers take control of their future.** If you’re working non-union, **you may be missing out** on fair wages, real benefits, and the protection of a strong contract. **Organizing your workplace is the first step toward a safer, more secure, and more respected career.**
